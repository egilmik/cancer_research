## High

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers
- **FURLONG: furmonertinib vs gefitinib first line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (double-blind randomised phase 3, n=358, China, Lancet Respir Med).** IRC-assessed PFS was 20.8 vs 11.1 months (HR 0.44, 95% CI 0.34–0.58), with fewer grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs (11% vs 18%). It is a third-generation TKI over gefitinib, not osimertinib, and OS is immature. Shi Y, Chen G, Wang X et al. Lancet Respir Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35662408/
- **BEVERLY: bevacizumab plus erlotinib vs erlotinib first line in EGFR-mutant non-squamous NSCLC (randomised phase 3, n=160, Italy, JTO).** PFS was 15.4 vs 9.6 months (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.47–0.92). Benefit was confined to current or former smokers (interaction p=0.03), and grade ≥3 hypertension (24% vs 5%) and rash were higher. The comparator is no longer standard. Piccirillo MC, Bonanno L, Garassino MC et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35659580/
- **WJOG9717L: osimertinib plus bevacizumab vs osimertinib first line (randomised phase 2, n=122, Japan, JTO).** PFS was 22.1 vs 20.2 months (HR 0.862, 95% CI 0.531–1.397), so the trial is negative. Grade ≥3 AEs were 56% vs 48%, and pneumonitis was less frequent with the combination (3% vs 18%). Adding bevacizumab to osimertinib is not supported. Kenmotsu H, Wakuda K, Mori K et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35636696/
- **KRYSTAL-1: adagrasib in previously treated KRAS G12C NSCLC (registrational phase 2 cohort, n=116, NEJM).** ORR was 42.9%, median DoR 8.5 months, PFS 6.5 months and OS 12.6 months. Intracranial ORR was 33.3% in 33 patients with treated, stable CNS metastases. Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 44.8%, including 2 deaths. It is single-arm. Jänne PA, Riely GJ, Gadgeel SM et al. N Engl J Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35658005/
- **Befotertinib in T790M NSCLC after first- or second-generation TKIs (single-arm phase 2, n=466, China, JTO).** At 75–100 mg, IRC ORR was 67.6% and IRC PFS 16.6 months, with intracranial ORR about 56%. It is a T790M setting that first-line osimertinib now pre-empts. A phase 3 is underway. Lu S, Zhang Y, Zhang G et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35724798/
- **Limertinib in T790M NSCLC (single-arm phase 2b, n=301, China, JTO).** IRC ORR was 68.8% and PFS 11.0 months. With CNS metastases (n=99), ORR was 64.6% and PFS 9.7 months. It shares the same limitation as befotertinib. Shi Y, Li B, Wu L et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35659581/
- **TATTON part B: osimertinib plus selumetinib after EGFR TKI progression (phase Ib, n=47, Clin Cancer Res).** After a T790M-directed TKI, ORR was 22.9% and PFS 2.8 months. After first- or second-generation TKIs, ORR was 66.7%. Diarrhoea occurred in 89%. Activity after osimertinib is limited. Yang JC, Ohe Y, Chiu CH et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35735998/
- **SWOG S1403 ctDNA kinetics on afatinib ± cetuximab (trial-embedded, n=106, Clin Cancer Res).** Clearance of EGFR ctDNA by 8 weeks predicted PFS (15.1 vs 4.6 months, HR 0.23) and OS (32.6 vs 15.6 months, HR 0.44), outperforming RECIST. It is prognostic and has not been tested as a guide to treatment. Mack PC, Miao J, Redman MW et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35713632/
- **Entrectinib in ROS1-positive NSCLC, updated integrated analysis (n=168 TKI-naive, JTO Clin Res Rep).** ORR was 68%, DoR 20.5 months, PFS 15.7 months and OS 47.8 months, with intracranial ORR 80% (n=25). Activity after CNS-only progression on crizotinib was modest (ORR 11%). The data are pooled single-arm trials. Drilon A, Chiu CH, Fan Y et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35663414/
- **CRBN-recruiting EGFR L858R/T790M PROTAC degraders (preclinical, Eur J Med Chem).** They are selective (>1,700-fold over wild-type cells, DC50 13.2 nM) and inhibited H1975 xenograft growth. The work is preclinical, with no C797S data. Zhang W, Li P, Sun S et al. Eur J Med Chem 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35691176/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-toxicity
- **Lung-MAP S1800A: ramucirumab plus pembrolizumab vs standard of care after ICI and chemotherapy (randomised phase II, n=136, JCO).** OS was 14.5 vs 11.6 months (HR 0.69, 80% CI 0.51–0.92; one-sided p=0.05). PFS and ORR were similar, and grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs were lower (42% vs 60%). It is phase 2 with a lenient alpha, and it led to the phase 3 Pragmatica-Lung. Reckamp KL, Redman MW, Dragnev KH et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35658002/
- **WJOG9616L: nivolumab retreatment after prior ICI benefit and an ICI-free interval of at least 60 days (phase 2, n=61, Clin Cancer Res).** ORR was only 8.5% and PFS 2.6 months. A longer ICI-free interval was the only predictor. ICI rechallenge after acquired resistance has little value. Akamatsu H, Teraoka S, Takamori S et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35762926/
- **Consolidation durvalumab duration in the US VA system (n=1,006, Eur J Cancer).** Median treatment was 7 months, and only 31% completed 12 months. Marginal structural models suggested 9 months gave PFS similar to 12 months, while 6 months was inferior. It is observational and hypothesis-generating for de-escalation. Bryant AK, Sankar K, Zhao L et al. Eur J Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35704975/
- **AMBITIOUS: atezolizumab in pretreated NSCLC with idiopathic interstitial pneumonia (phase II, n=17, Oncologist).** The trial was terminated early: grade ≥3 pneumonitis was 23.5% (all-grade 29.4%). One-year survival was 53.3% and OS 15.3 months. It quantifies the pneumonitis risk in IIP. Ikeda S, Kato T, Kenmotsu H et al. Oncologist 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35759340/
- **ALTER-L042: concurrent anlotinib with chemoradiotherapy in stage III NSCLC (phase 1, n=7, JTO Clin Res Rep).** It was terminated after 2 of 7 patients died (pneumonitis, haemoptysis) and 2 more had grade 3 radiation pneumonitis. The authors advise against combining anti-angiogenics with concurrent thoracic RT. Zhu H, Jia W, Jing X et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35663415/
- **Nurse-led telephone primary palliative care in newly diagnosed lung cancer (multisite RCT, n=151, US veterans, J Palliat Med).** There was no improvement in FACT-L TOI at 3 months (+1.03, 95% CI −3.98 to 6.04) or in satisfaction. The population was 98% male and 36% stage I–II. Primary palliative care by nurses did not reproduce the benefit of specialist palliative care. Reinke LF, Sullivan DR, Slatore C et al. J Palliat Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35649214/
- **SITC clinical practice guideline on immunotherapy for lung cancer and mesothelioma (JITC).** It updates the 2018 NSCLC guideline and adds SCLC and mesothelioma (testing, treatment selection, irAEs, QoL). The abstract does not state which recommendations changed. Govindan R, Aggarwal C, Antonia SJ et al. J Immunother Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35640927/
- **Chinese Medical Association guideline for lung cancer, 2022 edition (Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi).** It is a national multidisciplinary guideline for China. The abstract names no specific changed recommendation. Oncology Society of Chinese Medical Association. Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35754224/

### sclc
- **SRS vs WBRT for SCLC brain metastases (systematic review and meta-analysis, 31 studies, Lancet Oncol).** In 7 comparative studies (about 18,000 patients), OS was longer after SRS than after WBRT ± SRS boost (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.75–0.97). Median OS after SRS was 9.0 months. The studies are observational with high heterogeneity (I² 72%) and confounding by tumour burden. Gaebe K, Li AY, Park A et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35644163/
- **Hippocampal-avoidance PCI vs conventional PCI: MRI substudy of a randomised phase 3 trial (NCT01780675, about 100 patients, Neuro Oncol).** HA-PCI reduced hippocampal atrophy (3.0% vs 5.8% at 12 months), but both arms showed substantial brain injury. Atrophy was not associated with memory decline, so the clinical value of HA-PCI remains debated. de Ruiter MB, Groot PFC, Deprez S et al. Neuro Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35640975/

### lung-repurposed
- **CBM588 (Clostridium butyricum) with immune checkpoint blockade in NSCLC patients on PPIs (retrospective, n=118, Oncoimmunology).** PPI use was associated with reduced ICB efficacy, and CBM588 was associated with restored efficacy and longer OS, including in patients on PPIs and antibiotics. It is retrospective, single-country and confounded. Tomita Y, Goto Y, Sakata S et al. Oncoimmunology 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35655708/

### car-t-solid
- **TBI-1301: affinity-enhanced NY-ESO-1 TCR-T with siRNA knockdown of endogenous TCR (first-in-human phase I, n=9, JITC).** Three of six high-dose patients responded, and three had early CRS. One grade 3 lung injury was linked to TCR-T infiltration. It is small. Ishihara M, Kitano S, Kageyama S et al. J Immunother Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35768164/

### thoracic-other
- **VIM: oral vinorelbine plus active symptom control vs ASC alone in relapsed pleural mesothelioma (randomised phase 2, n=154, UK, EClinicalMedicine).** PFS was 4.2 vs 2.8 months (HR 0.60, 80% CI upper limit 0.7; one-sided p=0.002). BRCA1 did not predict resistance, and neutropenia was the main grade 3–4 AE. It supports vinorelbine as a second-line option; OS data are not in the abstract. Fennell DA, Porter C, Lester J et al. EClinicalMedicine 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35706488/
