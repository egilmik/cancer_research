### nsclc-drivers / egfr-nsclc
- **[Brief Report: Characterization of the Potentially Actionable TP53 Y220C Mutation in Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer. A Multicenter, European Thoracic Oncology Platform Study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42901386/)** — Retrospective multicentre ETOP study of NGS data from 28,244 NSCLC patients; TP53 Y220C was found in 195 (0.7%), above the 0.4% solid-tumour average, and about half had no known driver. It was more prevalent in EGFR-mutant than EGFR-wild-type tumours (1.7% vs 0.7%, p=0.029), and 17.0% carried a second TP53 mutation. It sizes the potential population for Y220C reactivators such as rezatapopt, whose phase 1 PYNNACLE trial enrolled no NSCLC. Limitations: retrospective, no treatment or outcome data, selection by centre. Molina-Vila MA, Tsourti Z, Stenzinger A et al., J Thorac Oncol 2026.
