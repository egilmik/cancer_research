### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **EMPOWER-Lung 1, 35-month follow-up (open-label phase 3, n=712, Lancet Oncol).** In patients with verified PD-L1 ≥50%, cemiplimab vs chemotherapy gave median OS 26.1 vs 13.3 months (HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.46–0.71) and PFS 8.1 vs 5.3 months (HR 0.51). In 64 patients, continued cemiplimab plus chemotherapy after progression gave PFS 6.6 and OS 15.1 months. Treatment-related deaths occurred in 3%. The beyond-progression analysis is exploratory, and the population was 85% male. Özgüroğlu M, Kilickap S, Sezer A et al. Lancet Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37591293/
- **TASUKI-52 updated OS (randomised phase 3, n=550, Japan/Korea/Taiwan).** Nivolumab vs placebo added to carboplatin-paclitaxel-bevacizumab in driver-negative non-squamous NSCLC gave median OS 30.8 vs 24.7 months (HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.58–0.94) at minimum 19.4 months' follow-up. The population is East Asian only, and the regimen is little used in Norway. Kim HR, Sugawara S, Lee JS et al. Cancer Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37641544/
- **Bintrafusp alfa vs pembrolizumab, first line PD-L1-high (adaptive phase 3, n=304).** The trial was stopped at interim for futility: PFS 7.0 vs 11.1 months (HR 1.23, 95% CI 0.89–1.71) and OS HR 1.20. Grade 3–4 TRAEs were 42.4% vs 13.2%. It is a clearly negative trial for TGF-β trap/PD-L1 fusion. Cho BC, Lee JS, Wu YL et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37597750/
- **TD-FOREKNOW (randomised phase 2, n=94, two Chinese centres, JAMA Oncol).** Three cycles of neoadjuvant camrelizumab plus nab-paclitaxel/platinum vs chemotherapy alone in resectable stage IIIA/IIIB (T3N2) NSCLC gave pCR 32.6% vs 8.9% (OR 4.95) and MPR 65.1% vs 15.6%. EFS was not reached at 14 months' follow-up. It is small, with a surrogate primary endpoint. Lei J, Zhao J, Gong L et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37535377/
- **Ivonescimab (AK112, PD-1/VEGF bispecific) plus chemotherapy (open-label phase II, n=83, 11 Chinese hospitals).** Confirmed ORR was 53.5% first line (12-month PFS 59.1%), 68.4% after EGFR-TKI failure (PFS 8.5 months) and 40.0% after chemo plus PD-(L)1 (PFS 7.5 months). Grade ≥3 TRAEs were mainly haematological. Cohorts are small and single-arm. Zhao Y, Chen G, Chen J et al. EClinicalMedicine 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37593227/
- **TQB2450 (anti-PD-L1) with or without anlotinib after prior therapy (randomised double-blind phase Ib, n=101).** In driver-negative NSCLC, the combination gave PFS 8.7 vs 2.8 months and ORR 30.9% vs 3.0% vs TQB2450 plus placebo. It is a small early-phase study, and anlotinib is not available in Norway. Zhang W, Wang J, Wang Q et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37647728/
- **Atezo-Brain (GECP17/05, single-arm phase II, n=40, J Clin Oncol).** Atezolizumab plus carboplatin-pemetrexed in non-squamous NSCLC with untreated brain metastases (55% on steroids) gave 12-week PFS 62.2%, intracranial ORR 42.7%, intracranial PFS 6.9 months and OS 11.8 months. Grade 3–4 neurological events occurred in 12.5%. There is no control arm. Nadal E, Rodríguez-Abreu D, Simó M et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37603816/
- **5-year survival on ICI monotherapy in Denmark (multicentre real-world, n=729, Acta Oncol).** At 48.7 months' median follow-up, 5-year OS was 13% first line and 11% in later lines, lower than in the RCTs. Among PS 2 patients, 2-year OS was 32% with PD-L1 ≥50% vs 5% below 50%. It is mostly retrospective. Bjørnhart B, Mouritzen MT, Kristiansen C et al. Acta Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37534770/

### nsclc-drivers

- **Divarasib (GDC-6036), KRAS G12C (phase 1, n=137, N Engl J Med).** In 60 NSCLC patients, confirmed ORR was 53.4% (95% CI 39.9–66.7) and median PFS 13.1 months. Grade 3 TRAEs occurred in 11%, with no dose-limiting toxicity. It is phase 1 and non-randomised. Sacher A, LoRusso P, Patel MR et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37611121/
- **Envonalkib vs crizotinib, first-line ALK+ NSCLC (open-label phase III, n=264, China).** IRC PFS was 24.9 vs 11.6 months (HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.34–0.64) and CNS ORR 79% vs 24%. Grade ≥3 TRAEs were 55.7% vs 42.9%. OS is immature (HR 0.84), and crizotinib is an outdated comparator. Yang Y, Min J, Yang N et al. Signal Transduct Target Ther 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37574511/
- **ALTA-3: brigatinib vs alectinib after crizotinib (open-label phase 3, n=248).** BIRC PFS was 19.3 vs 19.2 months (HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.66–1.42), and the trial met its futility criterion. Patients with ctDNA-detectable ALK fusion at baseline had shorter PFS (11.1 vs 22.5 months, exploratory). OS is immature. Yang JC, Liu G, Lu S et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37574132/

### lung-nonpharm / lung-screening

- **Move For Surgery (blinded single-site RCT, n=102, Br J Surg).** Home-based, wearable-supported preconditioning (exercise goals, breathing, nutrition, sleep and smoking advice) before lung cancer resection reduced hospital stay >5 days from 24% to 7% (P = .021). It is small and single-centre. Patel YS, Sullivan KA, Churchill IF et al. Br J Surg 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37643916/
- **SYMPRO-Lung (multicentre stepped-wedge RCT, n=515, J Natl Cancer Inst).** Weekly PRO symptom monitoring improved the QLQ-C30 summary score at 15 weeks vs usual care (B = 4.85, 95% CI 1.96–7.73) and physical functioning (B = 7.00). Patient-alert and clinician-alert approaches did not differ. It is unblinded and confounder-adjusted. Billingy NE, Tromp VNMF, Aaronson NK et al. J Natl Cancer Inst 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37603720/
- **Smoking abstinence before NSCLC diagnosis and survival (ILCCO pooled cohorts, n=42,087, Lancet Public Health).** Compared with current smoking, quitting 1–3 years before diagnosis was associated with OS aHR 0.92 (0.87–0.97), and >5 years with aHR 0.90; NSCLC-specific survival improved at >5 years (aHR 0.87). It is observational, with smoking data mostly self-reported. Fares AF, Li Y, Jiang M et al. Lancet Public Health 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37633678/
- **Tobacco treatment in lung screening (SMART RCT, n=636, three US health systems, JAMA Netw Open).** Among early non-responders, adding medication-therapy management did not improve 6-month prolonged abstinence at 18 months (17.8% vs 16.4%, aOR 1.13). Stepping down to quarterly contact in early responders lowered abstinence (43.6% vs 58.6%, not significant). The overall quit rate was 24%. Abstinence was self-reported. Fu SS, Rothman AJ, Vock DM et al. JAMA Netw Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37615989/
- **Lifetime gained with cancer screening (meta-analysis of RCTs, >2.1 million participants, JAMA Intern Med; Norwegian authors).** CT lung screening gave an estimated 107 days (95% CI −286 to 430), not significant; only sigmoidoscopy reached significance (110 days). Trials were not powered for all-cause mortality, and the lung estimate rests on few trials with 10-year follow-up. Bretthauer M, Wieszczy P, Løberg M et al. JAMA Intern Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37639247/
- **PulmoSeek Plus for pulmonary nodules (multicentre model development and validation, n=1,380, 24 Chinese hospitals, Lancet Digit Health).** Combining clinical, imaging and cfDNA methylation biomarkers gave sensitivity 0.98 at fixed specificity 0.50 for 5–30 mm nodules, with an AUC gain of 0.05 over either component. It has no clinical-outcome endpoint, and follow-up for one cohort is incomplete. He J, Wang B, Tao J et al. Lancet Digit Health 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37567793/

### lung-local-therapy

- **RAVAL early results (blinded multicentre RCT, n=164 analysed, Canada/US/France, Ann Surg).** Robotic vs VATS lobectomy gave a better 12-week health utility score (0.85 vs 0.80, P = .02), more lymph nodes sampled (10 vs 8), and an ICER of about $14,900 per QALY at 12 months. Survival endpoints are pending. Patel YS, Baste JM, Shargall Y et al. Ann Surg 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37551615/

### thoracic-other

- **IASLC thymic epithelial tumour staging, 9th edition T proposal (9,147 patients, J Thorac Oncol).** T1 is split into T1a (≤5 cm) and T1b (>5 cm) regardless of mediastinal pleura invasion. T2 now covers pericardium, lung or phrenic nerve, and T3 covers brachiocephalic vein, SVC, chest wall or extrapericardial pulmonary vessels. The data are retrospective and registry-based. Okumura M, Marino M, Cilento V et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37634808/
- **European guideline for BAP1 tumour predisposition syndrome (Delphi, 34 experts, Eur J Hum Genet).** It sets testing and surveillance recommendations for carriers, who are at risk of pleural and peritoneal mesothelioma among other tumours. The recommendations rest largely on expert opinion. Lalloo F, Kulkarni A, Chau C et al. Eur J Hum Genet 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37607989/
