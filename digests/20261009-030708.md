## High

### lung-key-evidence
- **Canakinumab with chemoradiation and durvalumab consolidation in unresectable stage III non-small cell lung cancer: the phase I/II CHORUS trial.** CHORUS, an open-label single-arm phase I/II trial (n=41 enrolled, 32 in efficacy population), added canakinumab (anti-IL-1β) to concurrent chemoradiation and durvalumab consolidation in unresectable stage III NSCLC. 24-month PFS was 67% (95% CI 52-88), above the 46% historical benchmark; 24-month OS 73%, ORR 81%, median follow-up 25 months. Grade 3-4 canakinumab-related events included neutropenia (n=5); pneumonitis of any grade occurred in 24%. Single-arm with historical comparison, so randomised testing is needed. Shaverdian N, Offin M, Toumbacaris N et al., Nat Commun, 2026. [PMID 42844242](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42844242/)
- **FIND: a phase-II study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of erdafitinib in FGFR-altered NSCLC.** FIND, a phase II Simon two-stage study of erdafitinib in FGFR-altered NSCLC, enrolled 22 treated patients (73% squamous). In the high-confidence fusion cohort 2/7 had partial response (one unconfirmed); the mutation cohort's best response was stable disease. Hyperphosphatemia occurred in 45.5%. Very small, stage I only; activity limited to fusions. Nogova L, Malchers F, Hillmer AM et al., Clin Cancer Res, 2026. [PMID 42848441](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42848441/)
- **Efficacy and Safety of Alectinib in Pediatric and Adult Patients with ALK-Altered Advanced Solid Tumors: a phase II TACKLE Trial (NCCH1712/MK003).** TACKLE, an open-label phase II of alectinib in ALK-altered solid tumours (n=26, 11 tumour types, mostly IMT and neuroblastoma, not lung), met its primary endpoint with centrally confirmed ORR 43.8% in Cohort A (n=16); median PFS 24.9 months, grade ≥3 related AEs 15.4%. Small, heterogeneous, non-lung population. Okuma HS, Arakawa A, Hirakawa A et al., Clin Cancer Res, 2026. [PMID 42848320](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42848320/)
- **Trial-specific absolute disease-free survival effects of postoperative PD-1/PD-L1 blockade after complete resection of NSCLC: A systematic review.** Systematic review of four phase III adjuvant PD-(L)1 trials (n=4,336) reconstructing Kaplan-Meier data: 36-month RMST gains of 1.34-1.97 months and 2.3-6.1 additional patients event-free per 100. Confidence intervals included zero for several estimates (KEYNOTE-091 crossed zero under alternative reconstruction); OS reconstructions allowed benefit or harm and serious AEs were more frequent with checkpoint blockade. Reconstructed data, not patient-level. Yao Z, Wang X, Zhang H et al., Lung Cancer, 2026. [PMID 42843316](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42843316/)
- **Clinical Practice Guidelines for Pleural Effusion and Bronchoalveolar Lavage Fluid-Based Circulating Tumor DNA Testing in Lung Cancer.** Korean consensus guideline for pleural effusion/BALF ctDNA testing: 15 recommendations from a review of 27 studies, a survey (21% of 109 respondents already implement it) and a two-round Delphi of eight experts. Evidence is dominated by pleural effusion; BALF data are sparse. Rated high rather than practice-changing given the national scope and small panel. Lee J, Kim JS, Kim HS et al., Ann Lab Med, 2026. [PMID 42844226](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42844226/)

### nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **Drug-specific resistance mechanisms to RET inhibitors in RET fusion-positive NSCLC.** LentiMutate screening and Ba/F3 validation identified drug-specific RET resistance mutations (V804E/M for selpercatinib; L730I/V and A883V, novel, for pralsetinib; G810X for both). One retrospective patient with G810X after selpercatinib responded durably to pralsetinib. Preclinical with an anecdotal clinical component. Liu X, Paula AG, Nilsson MB et al., Clin Cancer Res, 2026. [PMID 42848316](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42848316/)

### lung-top-journals
- **A preinvasive regulatory T cell axis for lung cancer interception.** Multi-omic profiling of patients with preinvasive lesions found BATF+ effector Tregs accumulating in high-grade lesions, clonally related to circulating Tregs, and marking rapid progression in screen-detected early NSCLC; PI3Kδ inhibition reduced lung tumour incidence and size in mice. Interception data are murine only. Gamble S, Whiteman ZE, Peinador-Marín C et al., Nature, 2026. [PMID 42844481](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42844481/)
