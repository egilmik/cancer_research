### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / lung-ctdna-mrd
- **CTONG1104 final OS: adjuvant gefitinib vs vinorelbine-cisplatin in resected stage II–IIIA (N1–N2) EGFR-mutant NSCLC (phase 3, n=222).** At a median follow-up of 80 months, median OS was 75.5 vs 62.8 months (HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.62–1.36; P=0.674). 5-year OS was 53.2% vs 51.2%. The early DFS advantage disappeared: 5-year DFS was 22.6% vs 23.2%. In a post-hoc analysis, subsequent targeted therapy contributed most to OS. Gefitinib for 24 months improved DFS but not OS, which supports the view that DFS gains with adjuvant first-generation TKIs do not translate into OS. The trial was small, and the subsequent-therapy analysis is post hoc. Zhong WZ, Wang Q, Mao WM et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33332190/
- **ARCHER 1050 updated OS: dacomitinib vs gefitinib first line (phase 3, n=452).** At a median follow-up of 47.9 months, OS HR was 0.748 (95% CI 0.591–0.947), with median OS 34.1 vs 27.0 months. Dacomitinib had substantial toxicity: diarrhoea 87.7%, paronychia 61.7% and acneiform dermatitis 49.3%. This updated analysis is ad hoc, patients with CNS metastases were excluded, and the comparator gefitinib has been replaced by osimertinib as first-line standard. Mok TS, Cheng Y, Zhou X et al. Drugs 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33331989/
- **JO25567 final OS: erlotinib + bevacizumab vs erlotinib first line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (randomised phase II, n=152).** The PFS benefit held (HR 0.52, 95% CI 0.35–0.76; median 16.4 vs 9.8 months). OS did not differ (HR 0.81, 95% CI 0.53–1.23; median 47.0 vs 47.4 months), although 5-year OS was numerically higher (41% vs 35%). The trial was Japanese-only, phase II and not powered for OS. Yamamoto N, Seto T, Nishio M et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33279874/
- **ETCTN 9903: osimertinib + navitoclax (BCL-2/BCL-xL inhibitor) after prior EGFR-TKI (single-arm phase Ib, n=27).** No dose-limiting toxicities occurred, and the recommended phase 2 dose was osimertinib 80 mg with navitoclax 150 mg. Thrombocytopenia (37%) and lymphopenia (37%) were common. In the T790M-positive expansion cohort (n=9), ORR was 100% and median PFS 16.8 months. These patients were osimertinib-naive and T790M-positive, so the result does not address post-osimertinib resistance, and the cohort is tiny. Bertino EM, Gentzler RD, Clifford S et al. Clin Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33376097/
- **WBRT with or without concurrent erlotinib for NSCLC brain metastases (randomised phase III, n=224, China).** Intracranial PFS was 11.2 vs 9.2 months (P=0.601), with no difference in PFS or OS. In EGFR-mutant patients, iPFS was 14.6 vs 12.8 months (P=0.164). Cognitive decline (MMSE) did not differ. Concurrent erlotinib was safe but added nothing to WBRT. Patients were not selected for EGFR status, and follow-up was short (11.2 months). Yang Z, Zhang Y, Li R et al. Neuro Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33331923/
- **Liquid-Lung-A: afatinib for patients selected by EGFR mutation in ctDNA (single-arm phase II, n=21).** Plasma EGFRm had 59% sensitivity against tissue. ORR was 74% (14/19), and median PFS was 12.0 months, similar in patients with mutations found only in ctDNA and in those with concordant tissue results. This supports starting a TKI on a plasma result alone, but the study is very small and uses a single assay (PANA Mutype). Park CK, Lee SY, Lee JC et al. Thorac Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33270375/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals
- **CameL: camrelizumab + carboplatin-pemetrexed vs chemotherapy in non-squamous NSCLC without EGFR/ALK alterations (phase 3, n=419, China).** At the interim analysis (median follow-up 11.9 months), median PFS was 11.3 vs 8.3 months (HR 0.60, 95% CI 0.45–0.79). Grade ≥3 neutropenia was 38% vs 30%. The trial supported camrelizumab approval in China. The comparator, chemotherapy alone, is no longer standard where pembrolizumab-based regimens are available, the OS analysis is interim, and the open-label trial was limited to China. Zhou C, Chen G, Huang Y et al. Lancet Respir Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33347829/
- **IMpower132 final analysis: atezolizumab + platinum-pemetrexed vs platinum-pemetrexed in non-squamous NSCLC (phase 3, n=578).** PFS improved (median 7.6 vs 5.2 months; HR 0.60, 95% CI 0.49–0.72), but the co-primary OS endpoint was missed (final median 17.5 vs 13.6 months; HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.71–1.06). Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs were 54.6% vs 40.1%. The result explains why this regimen did not become a standard alongside KEYNOTE-189. Nishio M, Barlesi F, West H et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33333328/
- **CheckMate 078 2-year follow-up: nivolumab vs docetaxel in pretreated, mainly Chinese NSCLC (phase 3, n=504).** Median OS was 11.9 vs 9.5 months (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.61–0.93), and 2-year OS 28% vs 18%. Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs were 12% vs 47%. These are longer-term confirmatory data for second-line nivolumab in Asian patients, a setting largely replaced by first-line immunotherapy. Lu S, Wang J, Cheng Y et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33321441/
- **Camrelizumab + apatinib in chemo-pretreated non-squamous NSCLC (phase Ib/II, n=105).** At the RP2D (apatinib 250 mg), ORR was 30.9% (95% CI 21.7–41.2), median PFS 5.7 months and median OS 15.5 months. Responses were seen across PD-L1 and TMB subgroups, and appeared better in STK11/KEAP1-mutant tumours (ORR 42.9% vs 28.1%, exploratory). The trial was single-arm with no comparator. Zhou C, Wang Y, Zhao J et al. Clin Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33323401/
- **Quavonlimab (anti-CTLA-4, MK-1308) + pembrolizumab first line in NSCLC (phase 1, n=134 in the NSCLC dose-confirmation phase).** ORR ranged from 27.5% to 40.0% across dose arms. Grade 3–5 treatment-related AEs were 30–57%. 25 mg every 6 weeks was chosen as the RP2D. These are early, non-comparative data on a new CTLA-4 combination. Perets R, Bar J, Rasco DW et al. Ann Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33276076/
- **Carboxyamidotriazole + cisplatin-vinorelbine vs chemo + placebo in advanced NSCLC (randomised double-blind phase III, n=495, 3:1, China).** Median PFS was 134 vs 98 days (HR 0.690, 95% CI 0.539–0.883), and ORR 34.6% vs 25.0%. OS did not differ, and grade ≥3 AEs were more frequent (68.1% vs 55.2%). The comparator, chemotherapy without immunotherapy, is outdated, and the absolute PFS gain is about five weeks. Si X, Wang J, Cheng Y et al. Ther Adv Med Oncol 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33281949/
- **TROG 11.03: palliative RT (36 Gy/12) vs concurrent chemo + palliative RT (40 Gy/20) for symptomatic intrathoracic NSCLC (randomised phase 3, n=76 recruited, 68 analysed).** The symptom burden index improved in both arms at 6 weeks, with no difference between arms. Chemotherapy added no palliative benefit. The trial is small and appears to have under-recruited. Lehman M, Bernard A, See A et al. Pract Radiat Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33271352/
- **Chinese expert consensus on antiangiogenic drugs for advanced NSCLC, 2020 edition (CSCO expert committees).** It updates the 2019 consensus on standardised use of antiangiogenic agents. The abstract does not say which recommendations changed. It is relevant to Chinese practice only. Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33342166/
- **Canadian rapid consensus on consolidation durvalumab outside PACIFIC eligibility (Delphi, virtual tumour board).** It recommends durvalumab after chemoradiation for inoperable stage IIB, recurrent mediastinal disease and gross residual disease after attempted surgery. It recommends against durvalumab after R0/R1 resection of stage III. No consensus was reached for oligometastatic disease, LCNEC or combined SCLC. This is expert opinion, not trial evidence. Robinson DAG, Snow S, Brade A et al. Cancer Treat Res Commun 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33310367/

### lung-repurposed / lung-nordic / lung-toxicity
- **Metformin use and lung cancer survival in Norway (population cohort, n=22,324, Cancer Registry of Norway linked to the Prescription Database).** Post-diagnostic metformin was associated with better lung cancer-specific survival overall (HR 0.83, 95% CI 0.73–0.95), with the strongest association in regional-stage SCC (HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.38–0.86), and with a dose-response. Pre-diagnostic use was not associated with survival overall. The study is observational, with risk of confounding by indication and residual confounding (diabetes control, smoking), and it does not report EGFR or immunotherapy subgroups. Brancher S, Støer NC, Weiderpass E et al. Br J Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33262518/
- **Randomised phase II of gemcitabine-carboplatin + controlled diet with or without metformin in EGFR/ALK wild-type advanced NSCLC (n=164, Korea).** Metformin did not improve PFS (HR 1.01, 95% CI 0.72–1.42) or OS (HR 0.95, 95% CI 0.67–1.34). An exploratory subgroup (SCC with high FDG uptake) showed benefit (OS HR 0.42), but it is hypothesis-generating only. This negative randomised result contrasts with the observational signal above. Lee Y, Joo J, Lee YJ et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33278671/
- **High-intensity statins and PD-1 inhibitor outcomes in mesothelioma and NSCLC (retrospective multicentre, n=253 evaluable).** Statin use was associated with higher ORR (32% vs 18%), longer PFS (HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.39–0.83) and longer OS (HR 0.67, 95% CI 0.45–1.00). The association was limited to high-intensity statins and was absent in a chemotherapy-treated mesothelioma control cohort. The study is retrospective and small, with likely confounding by indication and healthy-user bias. Cantini L, Pecci F, Hurkmans DP et al. Eur J Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33326868/
- **Cardiac events with immune checkpoint inhibitors: nationwide Danish study (25,573 lung cancer patients, 743 treated with PD-1 inhibitors).** The 1-year absolute risk of cardiac events (arrhythmia, myo/pericarditis, heart failure or CV death) was 9.7% (95% CI 6.8–12.5). The HR vs no ICI was 2.14 (1.50–3.05) within 6 months and 2.26 (1.27–4.02) after. The absolute risk is much higher than in trial reports. It is registry-based, with possible confounding from comorbidity and selection for ICI. D'Souza M, Nielsen D, Svane IM et al. Eur Heart J 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33291147/

### lung-nonpharm
- **Multimodal prehabilitation vs rehabilitation for lung cancer surgery (RCT, n=95).** Patients received 4 weeks of home exercise, whey protein and anxiety reduction either before surgery (n=52) or for 8 weeks after (n=43). 6MWT functional capacity did not differ at any time point, and >75% in both groups returned to baseline by 8 weeks. Within an ERAS pathway, starting before surgery added nothing over starting after. The study was small, and functional capacity was the only primary outcome (no complication endpoint). Ferreira V, Minnella EM, Awasthi R et al. Ann Thorac Surg 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33321089/
- **Inspiratory muscle training + aerobic exercise after VATS (RCT, n=63, 54 completed).** At 12 weeks, the intervention improved respiratory muscle strength, lung expansion volume and 6MWT (402 vs 360 m at week 12). The trial was single-centre and small, with no clinical outcomes such as complications or survival. Liu JF, Kuo NY, Fang TP et al. Clin Rehabil 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33307766/
- **Breathing retraining + psychosocial support for cancer-related dyspnoea (multicentre RCT, n=144, Brisbane).** The primary outcome, "worst" dyspnoea, did not improve. Average dyspnoea, perceived control over dyspnoea and anxiety improved over 8 weeks. Groups were unbalanced (81 vs 63), and the trial was registered in 2007. Yates P, Hardy J, Clavarino A et al. Front Oncol 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33335858/

### lung-vaccines-cell / car-t-solid
- **UV1 telomerase peptide vaccine in stage III/IV NSCLC: long-term phase I outcomes (Norway, n=18).** It was well tolerated, and 67% had T-cell responses, with responses and OS rising with dose. Median PFS was 10.7 months, median OS 28.2 months and 4-year OS 39%, or 83% in the 700 µg group. The trial had no comparator, the number of patients is very small and selected (no progression after prior therapy), and the dose-group survival data are exploratory. Brunsvig PF, Guren TK, Nyakas M et al. Front Immunol 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33324397/
- **CD133-directed CAR-T (CART-133) in advanced hepatocellular carcinoma (single-arm phase I/II, n=21).** Median OS was 12 months (95% CI 9.3–15.3) and median PFS 6.8 months. There was 1 partial response and 14 cases of stable disease. The most common high-grade AE was hyperbilirubinaemia. Outcome correlated with baseline VEGF/sVEGFR2/SDF-1 and endothelial progenitor cell counts. The response rate was low, there was no comparator and the study was small. Dai H, Tong C, Shi D et al. Oncoimmunology 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33312759/

### sclc / thoracic-other / nsclc-drivers
- **CASPIAN updated results: durvalumab ± tremelimumab + platinum-etoposide vs platinum-etoposide in ES-SCLC (phase 3, n=805).** At a median follow-up of 25.1 months, durvalumab + EP sustained its OS benefit (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.62–0.91; median 12.9 vs 10.5 months). Adding tremelimumab did not meet its OS primary endpoint (HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.68–1.00; p=0.045 against a stricter threshold; median 10.4 vs 10.5 months). Grade ≥3 neutropenia was 24% with durvalumab + EP vs 33% with EP. The update confirms durvalumab + EP as a first-line standard and closes the door on adding tremelimumab. The trial was open-label, and the absolute gain is about 2.4 months. Goldman JW, Dvorkin M, Chen Y et al. Lancet Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33285097/
- **Trilaciclib before carboplatin-etoposide-atezolizumab in ES-SCLC (randomised double-blind phase II, n=105).** Mean duration of severe neutropenia in cycle 1 was 0 vs 4 days, and severe neutropenia occurred in 1.9% vs 49.1% of patients. Red cell, platelet and quality-of-life measures also improved, and grade ≥3 AEs were fewer. Antitumour efficacy was comparable. This trial underpins trilaciclib's myeloprotection label. Efficacy equivalence was not formally tested. Daniel D, Kuchava V, Bondarenko I et al. Int J Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33348420/
- **FDA approval summary: lurbinectedin for metastatic SCLC after platinum (accelerated approval, 15 June 2020).** The approval rests on the single-arm Study B-005 SCLC cohort (n=105): investigator-assessed ORR 35% (95% CI 26–45), median DOR 5.3 months. The label warns of myelosuppression, hepatotoxicity and embryo-fetal toxicity. It includes platinum-resistant disease and was the first new second-line SCLC approval in over 20 years. It is a journal summary of an earlier decision, so it is rated high. Confirmatory randomised data are pending. Singh S, Jaigirdar AA, Mulkey F et al. Clin Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33288660/
- **Gavreto (pralsetinib) FDA approval for metastatic RET-fusion NSCLC (journal news brief).** This is a short overview of FDA approvals between 24 July and 4 September 2020. The item gives no trial data. It is a secondary report of the September 2020 decision. Am Health Drug Benefits 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33343822/
- **Radical hemithoracic RT vs palliative RT in non-metastatic pleural mesothelioma after non-radical lung-sparing surgery and chemotherapy (randomised phase 3, n=108, Italy).** 2-year OS was 58% vs 28% (HR 0.54, 95% CI 0.31–0.95; P=0.031), at a median follow-up of 14.6 months. Toxicity was notable: 11 patients had grade ≥3 acute toxicity, 17 had grade 3–4 late toxicity and 9 had grade ≥2 pneumonitis, including 1 death. This is a rare positive randomised trial of radiotherapy in mesothelioma. But it is small, follow-up is short, the comparator is palliative RT, it was done in an expert setting, and it predates first-line nivolumab + ipilimumab. Trovo M, Relevant A, Polesel J et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33259933/
