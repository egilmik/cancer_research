## High

### lung-key-evidence / sclc
**Patterns of progression with durvalumab vs placebo after cCRT in LS-SCLC (ADRIATIC, post-hoc).** Post-hoc exploratory analysis of the randomised phase 3 ADRIATIC (durvalumab n=264, placebo n=266). Progression occurred in 47.7% vs 59.4%. Extrathoracic progression was less frequent (20.1% vs 30.1%; HR for time to progression or death 0.68, 95% CI 0.49–0.93), and brain progression was 6.8% vs 12.4% (HR 0.64, 0.40–1.01). Intrathoracic progression was not clearly different (HR 0.81, 0.61–1.08). Limitations: post-hoc, not powered for these endpoints, and the CIs for the intrathoracic and brain comparisons include 1. Brief report, J Thorac Oncol 2026. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42971506/)
