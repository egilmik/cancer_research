## High
### nsclc-drivers
- **Divarasib outperforms first-generation KRAS-G12C inhibitors in NSCLC.** This is a Nature Reviews Clinical Oncology research highlight on the head-to-head phase 3 Krascendo 1 trial (divarasib vs sotorasib or adagrasib in previously treated KRAS G12C NSCLC), presented at ESMO 2026. The item has no abstract, so it gives no effect sizes, follow-up or toxicity data, and no efficacy numbers are claimed here. It matters because it is the first randomised comparison between KRAS G12C inhibitors and could change which G12C inhibitor is preferred. Check the primary publication for PFS, HR with CI and OS maturity before re-rating. Romero D, Nat Rev Clin Oncol, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43003982/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43003982/) (doi:10.1038/s41571-026-01233-z)
