### ERS/ESTS/EACTS/ESTRO guidelines for the management of malignant pleural mesothelioma (2020)
This joint task force of four European societies (ERS, ESTS, EACTS, ESTRO) updates the 2009 ERS/ESTS guideline. It is based on a systematic review of the 2009–2018 literature, with evidence graded by GRADE. Its key recommendations:

- **Diagnosis:** pleural biopsy, usually by thoracoscopy, remains the gold standard. Image-guided needle biopsy is reserved for pleural symphysis or poor performance status.
- **Pathology:** specific markers, including BAP-1 and CDKN2A (p16), should be used to separate atypical mesothelial proliferation from MPM, since standard staining is insufficient in about 10% of cases.
- **Staging:** the 8th TNM classification is advised, with an algorithm for pre-treatment assessment.
- **Treatment:** chemotherapy has limited efficacy, and only selected patients are candidates for radical surgery. Patients considered for multimodal treatment that includes radical surgery should be treated within clinical trials in MPM-dedicated centres.

The abstract gives no effect sizes, and the literature review ends in 2018. It therefore predates first-line nivolumab plus ipilimumab (CheckMate 743), and the systemic-therapy recommendations were soon out of date. The most durable changes are the diagnostic (BAP-1/CDKN2A) and surgical (trial-only radical surgery) recommendations. Applicability in Norway depends on how the national handlingsprogram for mesothelioma adopts them. The same guideline was also published in *Eur J Cardiothorac Surg* (listed under High).
Scherpereel A, Opitz I, Berghmans T et al. *Eur Respir J*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32451346/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers
- **Osimertinib + bevacizumab first-line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase 1/2, MSKCC, n=49).** 12-month PFS was 76% (95% CI 65–90), ORR 80% and median PFS 19 months (95% CI 15–24). All six patients with measurable CNS disease responded. Persistent EGFR ctDNA at 6 weeks predicted shorter PFS (9.8 vs 16.2 months) and OS. Resistance included squamous transformation and acquired L718Q and C797S. The single-arm PFS does not clearly exceed osimertinib alone (median 18.9 months in FLAURA), and the randomised data are needed. Yu HA, Schoenfeld AJ, Makhnin A et al. *JAMA Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32463456/
- **VISION: tepotinib in METex14 NSCLC (phase 2, *NEJM*, n=152 treated; 99 with ≥ 9 months' follow-up).** ORR by independent review was 46% (95% CI 36–57), with median duration of response 11.1 months. Response was similar whether METex14 was found by liquid biopsy (48%) or tissue (50%). Grade ≥ 3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 28%, mainly peripheral oedema (7%), and 11% discontinued. It is the registration dataset for tepotinib and supports plasma-based METex14 testing. It is single-arm, with no comparator. Paik PK, Felip E, Veillon R et al. *N Engl J Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32469185/
- **Tepotinib: First Approval (*Drugs* profile).** Tepotinib was approved in Japan in March 2020 for MET-altered NSCLC, after FDA breakthrough designation in 2019. This is the first approval of tepotinib anywhere. It is a journal profile, not the regulatory decision itself, so it is rated high. Markham A. *Drugs*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32361823/
- **ALEX final PFS and updated OS: alectinib vs crizotinib first-line in ALK-positive NSCLC (phase 3, n=303).** Mature investigator PFS was 34.8 vs 10.9 months (HR 0.43, 95% CI 0.32–0.58). The 5-year OS rate was 62.5% vs 45.5% (stratified HR 0.67, 95% CI 0.46–0.98), but OS was immature (37% of events) and not formally powered. The benefit held with and without baseline CNS metastases. It confirms alectinib as a first-line standard. The comparator (crizotinib) is no longer standard, and the OS analysis is descriptive. Mok T, Camidge DR, Gadgeel SM et al. *Ann Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32418886/
- **JIPANG: adjuvant pemetrexed–cisplatin vs vinorelbine–cisplatin in resected stage II–IIIA non-squamous NSCLC (phase 3, Japan, n=804).** RFS was not superior (38.9 vs 37.3 months, HR 0.98, 95% CI 0.81–1.20). Pemetrexed–cisplatin was much better tolerated: grade 3–4 neutropenia 22.7% vs 81.1% and febrile neutropenia 0.3% vs 11.6%. It supports pemetrexed–cisplatin as an equally effective, less toxic adjuvant option, but the trial was a failed superiority design rather than a formal non-inferiority test. Kenmotsu H, Yamamoto N, Yamanaka T et al. *J Clin Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32407216/
- **Low-dose erlotinib (50 mg) in frail or elderly EGFR-mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, Japan, n=80, median age 80).** ORR by independent review was 60.0% (90% CI 50.2–69.2), median PFS 9.3 months and median OS 26.2 months, with few grade ≥ 3 AEs. It supports dose de-escalation in frail patients. It is single-arm and uses a first-generation TKI, which limits relevance now that osimertinib is standard. Miyamoto S, Azuma K, Ishii H et al. *JAMA Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32407455/
- **Osimertinib in T790M-positive NSCLC with PS 2–4 (single-arm phase II, Japan, n=18).** Median PFS was 7.0 months, ORR 53% and median OS 12.7 months, and performance status improved in 72%. Three patients stopped because of ILD. It supports osimertinib in poor-PS patients, but the sample is small. Nakashima K, Ozawa Y, Daga H et al. *Invest New Drugs*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32424780/
- **Amivantamab in EGFR exon 20 insertion models (*Cancer Discov*).** In Ba/F3 cells, patient-derived cells, organoids and xenografts, the EGFR–MET bispecific downregulated EGFR and MET and engaged immune effector mechanisms. In vivo it was more active than cetuximab or poziotinib, and two patients responded. It is the preclinical basis for the amivantamab exon 20 programme. Clinical data are limited to two patients. Yun J, Lee SH, Kim SY et al. *Cancer Discov*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32414908/
- **Brigatinib + cetuximab for EGFR T790M–cis-C797S after osimertinib (retrospective, n=15).** Five patients with 19del–T790M–cis-C797S received brigatinib + cetuximab: ORR 60%, DCR 100%, median PFS 14 months, with no grade 3–4 AEs. Ten patients who received chemotherapy had ORR 10% and PFS 3 months. It is a signal for a post-osimertinib C797S strategy, but the series is very small, non-randomised and single-country. Wang Y, Yang N, Zhang Y et al. *J Thorac Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32353596/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence
- **Neoadjuvant atezolizumab + carboplatin/nab-paclitaxel in resectable NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, USA, n=30, 77% stage IIIA).** Major pathological response was 57% (95% CI 37–75), and 87% had R0 resection. Grade 3–4 neutropenia occurred in 50%, and there were no treatment-related deaths. It is an early signal for neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy, later tested in phase 3 trials. It is single-arm, with short follow-up (12.9 months) and no survival data. Shu CA, Gainor JF, Awad MM et al. *Lancet Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32386568/
- **Chinese Medical Association guideline for lung cancer diagnosis and treatment (2019 edition).** This is a national multidisciplinary guideline covering screening, diagnosis, pathology, molecular testing, treatment and follow-up, adapted to Chinese conditions. The abstract does not state which recommendations changed, so it is rated high, not practice-changing. Chinese Medical Association, Oncology Society of CMA. *Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32375445/

### sclc
- **KEYNOTE-604: pembrolizumab + etoposide/platinum first-line in ES-SCLC (phase 3, n=453).** PFS improved (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.61–0.91), with 12-month PFS 13.6% vs 3.1%. OS improved numerically (HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.64–0.98; 24-month OS 22.5% vs 11.2%), but the prespecified significance threshold (p=0.0128) was not met (p=0.0164). Grade 3–4 AEs were 76.7% vs 74.9%. Pembrolizumab was not approved for first-line SCLC on this basis, unlike atezolizumab (IMpower133) and durvalumab (CASPIAN). Rudin CM, Awad MM, Navarro A et al. *J Clin Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32468956/
- **SC-002, a DLL3-directed ADC, in relapsed SCLC/LCNEC (phase 1, n=35).** Partial response occurred in 14%. Serious AEs occurred in 66% (37% related), mainly effusions and hypoalbuminaemia, with one grade 5 event. It is a negative early-phase result that mirrors the rovalpituzumab tesirine experience with DLL3 ADCs. It is a small dose-escalation study. Morgensztern D, Johnson M, Rudin CM et al. *Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32438272/
- **Repurposed desipramine in SCLC and high-grade neuroendocrine carcinoma (phase IIa, n=6).** The tricyclic antidepressant was poorly tolerated, with colonic pseudo-obstruction and neurocognitive AEs. Median PFS was 1.2 months and median OS 2.7 months. There was no benefit, and the trial was terminated. It is a negative repurposing trial; the doses reached may have been below the preclinical active range. Riess JW, Jahchan NS, Das M et al. *Cancer Treat Res Commun*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32413603/

### lung-nonpharm / lung-screening
- **Supervised exercise during chemotherapy for advanced inoperable lung cancer (RCT, Copenhagen, n=218).** A 12-week twice-weekly programme of aerobic, strength and relaxation training did not improve the primary endpoint, VO2 peak. Muscle strength, social well-being (FACT-L), anxiety and depression improved compared with usual care. It supports offering supervised exercise for function and mood, not fitness, in advanced disease. It was open-label, with drop-out typical of this population. Quist M, Langer SW, Lillelund C et al. *Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32416432/
- **Secondhand smoke and lung cancer in never smokers (PLCO cohort, n=49,569).** Adult secondhand-smoke exposure at work for most of the time was associated with higher lung cancer incidence (HR 2.04, 95% CI 1.31–3.16), as was exposure at home (HR 1.81). Exposure for most of adult life was associated with higher lung cancer mortality (HR 1.93, 95% CI 1.04–3.58). It supports passive smoking as a risk factor relevant to never-smoker screening. Exposure is self-reported, and event numbers are modest. Abdel-Rahman O. *Clin Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32389507/

### car-t-solid
- **CAR-GPC3 T cells in advanced hepatocellular carcinoma (two phase I trials, China, n=13).** CRS occurred in 9 patients, one of them grade 5, and there was no grade 3–4 neurotoxicity. There were two partial responses. OS was 50.3% at 6 months, 42.0% at 1 year and 10.5% at 3 years, and one patient with stable disease was alive at 44 months. Expansion tended to track with response. It is the first published clinical data for GPC3 CAR-T in HCC. The sample is small and uncontrolled, and there was a CRS death. Shi D, Shi Y, Kaseb AO et al. *Clin Cancer Res*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32371538/
- **Human CAR macrophages (CAR-M) for solid tumours (preclinical, *Nat Biotechnol*).** A chimeric adenoviral vector made engineering of primary human macrophages possible and kept them in an M1 phenotype. In two xenograft models, a single CAR-M infusion reduced tumour burden and prolonged survival. In humanised mice, CAR-Ms remodelled the microenvironment and boosted T-cell activity. It is the foundational CAR-M paper behind HER2 CAR-M clinical development. These are mouse data only. Klichinsky M, Ruella M, Shestova O et al. *Nat Biotechnol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32361713/
