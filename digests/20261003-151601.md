### Studies and papers
- **The effect of common medications on the efficacy of immune checkpoint inhibitors.** Retrospective VHA cohort of 3739 stage IV NSCLC patients on ICI vs 6585 on chemotherapy used propensity-weighted Cox models and a chemotherapy negative-control cohort; 14 of 20 medication classes (including antihistamines, NSAIDs, statins, metformin as assessed) showed no association with OS or time-to-next-treatment and the associations seen were non-specific, suggesting prior reports reflect unmeasured confounding; limitation is retrospective claims-type data with residual confounding.
  Brinzevich D, Falvello V, Rehmani SS et al. — Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41390939/
- **The impact of β-blockers on outcomes of immune checkpoint inhibitors therapy in advanced lung cancer: a multicenter real-world study.** Multicentre retrospective study of 462 advanced lung cancer patients on ICIs (318 after propensity matching); beta-blocker use was associated with longer PFS (15.8 vs 11.8 months, HR 0.67) and higher ORR (51.1% vs 35.2%) but not OS (HR 1.38, NS); limitations are retrospective design, only 88 beta-blocker users, possible confounding by indication and cardiovascular comorbidity.
  Chang L, Zhang H, Li Y et al. — Front Immunol 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41194930/
- **Repurposing Cardiac Glycosides to Potentiate CD47 Blockade through Calreticulin-mediated Phagocytic Effects for Lung Cancer Treatment.** Preclinical study showing ouabain and digoxin (cardiac glycosides) combined with anti-CD47 antibody reduced tumour volume in several lung cancer in vivo models and increased macrophage phagocytosis about 5-fold via calreticulin translocation; authors state clinical translation is supported; limitation is mouse and cell models only, no human data.
  Ye ZH, Yu WB, Huang MY et al. — Adv Sci (Weinh) 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40985476/
- **Synergistic anti-tumor effect of fenbendazole and diisopropylamine dichloroacetate in immunodeficient BALB/c nude mice transplanted with A549 lung cancer cells.** In vivo nude-mouse A549 xenograft study of fenbendazole (anthelmintic) plus diisopropylamine dichloroacetate: the combination gave greater tumour regression and complete tumour loss than either single agent over 60 days, with a stated call for a clinical study; limitation is small immunodeficient xenograft model, no human data.
  Nguyen TQ, Phan UTT, Can MV et al. — Transl Lung Cancer Res 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40799435/
- **Post-diagnosis statin use and survival among lung cancer patients in the Military Health System.** Retrospective Military Health System cohort of 5096 NSCLC patients (864 post-diagnosis statin users) using time-dependent Cox models (addresses immortal-time bias); each additional year of statin use gave adjusted HR 0.82 (0.75-0.90) for overall survival; limitations are observational design, residual confounding by indication/healthy-user effect and a largely military-beneficiary population.
  Lin J, Browning RF, Shriver CD et al. — Cancer Epidemiol 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40743913/
- **Association Between Metformin Use and Mortality Among Individuals With Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer Receiving Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors: A Retrospective Cohort Study.** SEER-Medicare retrospective cohort of 1123 metformin vs 362 sulfonylurea/DPP-4 users with NSCLC starting ICI, with stabilised IPTW and competing-risk models; no significant difference in cancer-specific or all-cause mortality (adjusted rate difference about 1 and 4 per 100 person-years); null result with active-comparator design, limited by claims data, older patients and residual confounding.
  Sun X, Heyward J, Murray JC et al. — J Immunother 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40548761/
- **Morphine and metformin impact immunotherapy benefit in patients with NSCLC: Results of the real-world study IFCT-1502 CLINIVO-SNDS.** Post hoc analysis of nationwide French retrospective cohort IFCT-1502 CLINIVO (753 advanced NSCLC patients on nivolumab) adjusting for comorbidity and disease aggressiveness; metformin was associated with better overall survival while morphine and high-dose corticosteroids were associated with worse OS and PFS; limitations are retrospective design, second-line-plus nivolumab only and residual confounding.
  Gobbini E, Langlais A, Missy P et al. — Eur J Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40532471/
- **Statin and Immune-Related Cardiovascular Events in Lung Cancer Patients Receiving Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors.** Propensity-matched TriNetX cohort of 4379 lung cancer patients on ICIs (from 16,650) found statin use associated with lower 12-month MACE (HR 0.87) and lower all-cause mortality (HR 0.83) without increased serious irAEs; primary outcome is cardiovascular rather than tumour response, and database design carries confounding by indication and coding limitations.
  Song J, Chi KY, Jeon H et al. — Oncology 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40398400/
- **Disulfiram potentiates cisplatin-induced apoptosis in small cell lung cancer via the inhibition of cystathionine β-synthase and H2S.** Disulfiram identified by drug screen as a sensitiser of cisplatin in SCLC cells via CBS/H2S inhibition, with significant tumour reduction in nude-mouse SCLC xenografts for the combination; authors call for immediate clinical assessment; limitation is cell lines and xenograft only.
  Wang Y, Du R, Gao R et al. — Am J Cancer Res 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40371164/
- **Antihistamines Improve the Survival of Lung Cancer: A 10-Year Cohort Study of Tertiary Hospital in Taiwan.** Single-hospital registry cohort of 1886 stage IV lung cancer patients (594 post-diagnosis antihistamine users); post-diagnosis users surviving more than 90 days had median OS 28.4 vs 15.1 months (HR 0.49), pre-diagnosis use showed no benefit; the 90-day landmark partly addresses immortal-time bias but confounding by indication, single-centre data and a very large effect size warrant caution.
  Hsu CH, Chen CF, Yin CH et al. — Oncology 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40159404/
- **Enhancing PD-1 blockade in NSCLC: Reprogramming tumor immune microenvironment with albumin-bound statins targeting lipid rafts and mitochondrial respiration.** Albumin-bound fluvastatin nanoformulation (Abstatin) amplified anti-PD-1 efficacy in murine and humanised PDX NSCLC models by lowering hypoxia and lipid-raft integrity, with minimal toxicity; directly relevant to statin plus ICI; limitation is preclinical only and a novel formulation rather than the approved drug.
  Chen N, Li Z, Liu H et al. — Bioact Mater 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40124597/
- **Statin Use With Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors and Survival in Nonsmall Cell Lung Cancer.** SEER-Medicare retrospective cohort of 1401 older NSCLC patients on ICIs; time-updated concurrent statin use was associated with lower lung-cancer-specific (HR 0.59, 0.35-0.99) and overall mortality (HR 0.62), adjusted with a propensity score; wide CIs, limited anti-PD-L1 numbers, observational with residual confounding and claims-based exposure.
  Marrone MT, Reuss JE, Crawford A et al. — Clin Lung Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39818516/
