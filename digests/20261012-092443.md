### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-top-journals

- **KEYNOTE-042, 5-year update (phase 3).** 1,274 patients with PD-L1 ≥1% NSCLC received first-line pembrolizumab or chemotherapy. OS HRs were 0.68 (TPS ≥50%), 0.75 (≥20%) and 0.79 (≥1%), with 5-year OS of 21.9%, 19.4% and 16.6% on pembrolizumab. ORR was 84% in those who completed 35 cycles. de Castro G, Kudaba I, Wu YL et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36306479/
- **CheckMate 227 part 1, 5-year update (phase 3).** 5-year OS with nivolumab+ipilimumab vs chemotherapy was 24% vs 14% (PD-L1 ≥1%) and 19% vs 7% (PD-L1 <1%). About two-thirds of 5-year survivors were off treatment without further therapy. 5-year OS was 39% among those who stopped for toxicity. This confirms durable benefit, including in PD-L1-negative disease. Brahmer JR, Lee JS, Ciuleanu TE et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36223558/
- **NEPTUNE (phase 3, negative).** 823 patients were randomised to first-line durvalumab+tremelimumab or chemotherapy. In the bTMB ≥20 primary population (n=129), OS was 11.7 vs 9.1 months (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.49–1.05, p=0.081), so the primary endpoint was not met. Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 20.7% vs 33.6%. Blood TMB again fails as a selector for ICI without chemotherapy. de Castro G, Rizvi NA, Schmid P et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36240972/
- **CHOICE-01 (phase 3).** First-line toripalimab+chemotherapy vs placebo+chemotherapy improved PFS (8.4 vs 5.6 months, HR 0.49, 95% CI 0.39–0.61) and interim OS (HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.53–0.92), with similar grade ≥3 AEs. Chemotherapy alone is no longer the comparator in practice, and the trial was China-only. Wang Z, Wu L, Li B et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36206498/
- **RATIONALE-303 (phase 3).** Tislelizumab vs docetaxel after platinum: median OS 16.9 vs 11.9 months in the ITT population (HR 0.66) and 19.3 vs 11.5 months with PD-L1 ≥25% (HR 0.53). This is another PD-1 agent beating docetaxel in ICI-naive patients. Zhou C, Huang D, Fan Y et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36184068/
- **IFCT-1601 IONESCO (neoadjuvant durvalumab, closed early).** 46 patients received three doses of neoadjuvant durvalumab. The R0 rate was 89% and MPR 19%, and MPR was associated with DFS. The trial was stopped early after four unexpected postoperative deaths within 90 days, none attributed directly to durvalumab. Wislez M, Mazieres J, Lavole A et al. J Immunother Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36270733/
- **Perioperative adebrelimab + chemotherapy (phase 1b).** 37 patients with resectable stage II–III NSCLC received adebrelimab with nab-paclitaxel-carboplatin. MPR was 51.4%, pCR 29.7% and 12-month EFS 77.8%. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 78%. The trial is small and single-arm. Yan W, Zhong WZ, Liu YH et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36191882/
- **KROG 09-03 (phase 3, negative).** Accelerated 2.4 Gy concomitant-boost RT vs conventional 2 Gy, both with concurrent chemotherapy, in stage III NSCLC. There was no difference in OS (27 vs 26 months), PFS or local control, and no difference in grade ≥3 pneumonitis or oesophagitis. Conventional fractionation remains standard. Kim YH, Ahn SJ, Moon SH et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36280151/
- **TBNA plus mediastinal cryobiopsy (multicentre RCT, 271 patients).** Adding cryobiopsy raised diagnostic yield from 81% to 93% (RR 1.15). The gain came from benign disorders, with no gain for mediastinal metastasis (99% in both arms). This is relevant to diagnostics, less so to lung cancer staging. Fan Y, Zhang AM, Wu XL et al. Lancet Respir Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36279880/
- **International consensus on radiotherapy in metastatic NSCLC.** The consensus covers palliative, oligometastatic, consolidative and cranial radiotherapy alongside targeted therapy and ICI. It names open questions on sequencing and patient selection, but no changed recommendation. Zhu Z, Ni J, Cai X et al. Transl Lung Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36248338/
- **International expert consensus on immunotherapy for early-stage NSCLC.** This item has no abstract, so the recommendations cannot be summarised from it. Liang W, Cai K, Cao Q et al. Transl Lung Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36248334/
- **Chinese clinical practice guideline on image-guided thermal ablation of lung tumours (2022 edition).** It covers indications, contraindications, technique, response assessment and complications. The abstract does not state any changed recommendation. Ye X, Fan W, Wang Z et al. J Cancer Res Ther 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36204866/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers / lung-ctdna-mrd

- **TATTON final analysis (phase 1b).** Patients with MET-amplified, EGFR-mutant NSCLC progressing on an EGFR-TKI received osimertinib+savolitinib. ORR was 33–67% and median PFS 5.5–11.1 months in Part B (n=138), and 62% and 9.0 months in Part D (n=42). Activity was greater with MET copy number ≥10, and ctDNA clearance predicted longer PFS. It is single-arm, with mixed prior TKIs. Hartmaier RJ, Markovets AA, Ahn MJ et al. Cancer Discov 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36264123/
- **Telisotuzumab vedotin + erlotinib (phase 1b).** In c-Met+ NSCLC, ORR was 32.1% in EGFR-mutant TKI-pretreated patients (n=28) and 52.6% in c-Met-high EGFR-mutant patients. Median PFS was 5.9 months. Neuropathy affected 57%. The trial is small and early-phase. Camidge DR, Barlesi F, Goldman JW et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36288547/
- **LC-SCRUM-Liquid (prospective, 1,062 paired samples).** Plasma NGS had 77% positive agreement with tissue for mutations (EGFR 78%, KRAS 75%), but only 47% for fusions (ALK 46%, ROS1 18%). Patients positive by plasma alone, mostly because tissue failed, had 85% response to matched therapy. Plasma is good for mutations, but tissue is still needed for fusions and METex14. Sugimoto A, Matsumoto S, Udagawa H et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36201167/
- **Aprepitant vs desloratadine for EGFR-TKI pruritus (randomised phase 2).** In 130 patients, the 1-week response rate was 53.2% with aprepitant vs 23.7% with desloratadine, with faster response and mild toxicity. The trial was open-label and single-country, with short follow-up. Zhou T, Zhang Y, Ma Y et al. Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36197287/
- **SPIRAL-0 (phase 2, elderly).** 38 elderly patients received first-line osimertinib. 1-year PFS was 59.4%, which missed the primary endpoint threshold, and pneumonitis occurred in 17.5%. Osimertinib was tolerable, but the pneumonitis rate in older Japanese patients needs watching. Chihara Y, Takeda T, Goto Y et al. Oncologist 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36181763/
- **Crizotinib in East Asian ROS1+ NSCLC, final OS (phase 2).** Median OS was 44.2 months in 127 patients, with median follow-up of 56 months. QoL benefit was maintained and toxicity was low. The authors call it a new OS benchmark for East Asian patients. Wu YL, Lu S, Yang JC et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36247019/
- **Savolitinib in METex14 NSCLC, final analysis (phase 2, China).** Median OS was 12.5 months in 70 patients: 19.4 months pretreated, 10.9 months treatment-naive and 10.6 months with sarcomatoid histology. The trial is small and single-arm, and the population is enriched for sarcomatoid tumours. Lu S, Fang J, Li X et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36217329/

### sclc / lung-screening

- **ATLANTIS (phase 3, negative).** 613 patients with relapsed SCLC were randomised to lurbinectedin+doxorubicin or topotecan/CAV. Median OS was 8.6 vs 7.6 months (HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.82–1.15), and treatment-related deaths were 2 vs 10. The combination does not improve survival. Aix SP, Ciuleanu TE, Navarro A et al. Lancet Respir Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36252599/
- **BMS-986012 (anti-fucosyl-GM1) ± nivolumab, first-in-human phase 1/2.** In relapsed SCLC, ORR was 38% with nivolumab (n=29) vs 4% as monotherapy (n=77), and median OS 18.7 vs 5.4 months. Pruritus occurred in 90%. The combination cohort is small and non-randomised. Chu Q, Leighl NB, Surmont V et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36275912/
- **EarlyCDT-Lung autoantibody test in EPIC and NSHDS (nested case-control).** In 154 ever-smoker cases and 154 controls, sensitivity was 8.4% and specificity 92%, and a high-risk result did not raise lung cancer odds (OR 1.09). The study does not support the test for selecting people for screening. The sample is small and the blood was pre-diagnostic. Wu WY, Haider Z, Feng X et al. Int J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36305647/

### car-t-solid / thoracic-other

- **BrainChild-03: intraventricular B7-H3 CAR-T for DIPG (phase 1, first three patients).** The three patients received 40 infusions with no DLTs. One had sustained clinical and radiographic improvement for 12 months, and CAR-T cells persisted in CSF. This is feasibility data only. Vitanza NA, Wilson AL, Huang W et al. Cancer Discov 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36259971/
- **Palbociclib in refractory thymic epithelial tumours (KCSG LU17-21, phase 2).** In 48 patients (23 thymic carcinoma), ORR was 12.5%, median PFS 11.0 months and OS 26.4 months. Grade 3–4 neutropenia occurred in 41.7%. The trial is single-arm with a heterogeneous population. Jung HA, Kim M, Kim HS et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36307042/
