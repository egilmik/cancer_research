### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-top-journals / lung-local-therapy

- **ASCO living guidelines, version 2022.2: stage IV NSCLC with and without driver alterations (two items).** These are scheduled updates of ASCO's living guidelines. An external summary says the driver version adds recommendations for HER2-mutant and KRAS G12C NSCLC and an updated later-line algorithm, and the non-driver version adds one recommendation. Neither abstract states the recommendation wording, so both are rated high, not practice-changing. Owen DH, Singh N, Ismaila N et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36534938/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36534935/
- **VIOLET RCT, full HTA report.** 503 patients in nine NHS hospitals were randomised to VATS or open lobectomy. Physical function at 5 weeks was better with VATS (mean difference 4.65, 95% CI 1.69–7.61). VATS also gave a shorter hospital stay (4 vs 5 days), less prolonged pain (RR 0.82) and similar nodal upstaging, OS and PFS. The primary results were already published, and follow-up was 1 year. Lim E, Harris RA, McKeon HE et al. Health Technol Assess 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36524582/
- **SAMURAI (phase 2, Japan).** 59 patients with stage III NSCLC received cisplatin/S-1 with 60 Gy, then durvalumab, and 86% proceeded to durvalumab. 1-year PFS was 72.5% (95% CI 59.1–82.2), meeting the primary endpoint, and 1-year OS was 91.5%. Any-grade pneumonitis occurred in 78% (grade ≥3 in 2%). The trial is single-arm with short follow-up. Tanaka H, Tanzawa S, Misumi T et al. Ther Adv Med Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36570411/
- **TELMA (single-arm phase 2, Spain).** 38 patients with treatment-naive non-squamous NSCLC with TMB ≥10 and no EGFR/ALK/STK11/MDM2/ROS1 alterations received first-line atezolizumab plus bevacizumab. 12-month PFS was 51.3% (95% CI 34.2–66.0), meeting the primary endpoint, with median PFS 13.0 months and ORR 42%. The trial is small and uncontrolled, and TMB is not an established selection marker. Provencio M, Ortega AL, Coves-Sarto J et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36520426/
- **LungMate 002 (single-arm phase 2).** 50 patients with stage II–III EGFR/ALK-negative NSCLC received 2–4 cycles of neoadjuvant toripalimab plus carboplatin doublet. ORR was 76%, 37 patients had surgery (all R0), MPR was 55.6% and pCR 27.8%. One treatment-related death occurred (haemoptysis). The trial is single-arm, and survival is not reported. Zhu X, Sun L, Song N et al. BMC Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36581917/
- **Early SBRT to the primary tumour with EGFR-TKI (prospective single-arm phase 2).** 41 patients with advanced EGFR-mutant NSCLC on gefitinib or icotinib received SBRT to the primary tumour about 1.5 months into treatment. Median PFS was 15.2 months and OS 27.6 months, and most failures were at new sites. There were no grade ≥3 AEs. The trial has no control arm and uses a first-generation TKI. Shi Y, Xu H, Raynor WY et al. Life (Basel) 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36556319/
- **Spanish national consensus (SEAP/SEOM) on predictive biomarker testing in NSCLC.** This updated consensus considers EGFR, BRAF, KRAS and MET mutations, ALK, ROS1, NTRK and RET rearrangements, and PD-L1 essential. It recommends NGS, which also picks up HER2 and other emerging markers. It is consensus-based. Isla D, Lozano MD, Paz-Ares L et al. Clin Transl Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36571695/
- **Chinese expert consensus on molecular testing of NSCLC in malignant effusions.** This is from the Cytology Section of the Chinese Society of Pathology. There is no abstract. Zhonghua Bing Li Xue Za Zhi 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36480826/
- **ESTS/AATS 2022 guideline on VTE prophylaxis in thoracic cancer surgery.** This GRADE guideline makes 24 recommendations, mostly conditional and based on low or very low certainty evidence:
  - parenteral anticoagulation plus mechanical prophylaxis after anatomic lung resection or oesophagectomy;
  - parenteral anticoagulants over DOACs;
  - extended prophylaxis for 28–35 days at moderate or high risk;
  - VTE screening after pneumonectomy.

  Shargall Y, Wiercioch W, Brunelli A et al. Eur J Cardiothorac Surg 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36519935/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers / lung-ctdna-mrd / lung-toxicity

- **CROWN 3-year update (phase 3, unplanned analysis).** 296 patients were randomised to lorlatinib or crizotinib. Median PFS by BICR was not reached vs 9.3 months (HR 0.27, 95% CI 0.18–0.39), and 3-year PFS was 64% vs 19%. With baseline brain metastases, the HR for time to intracranial progression was 0.10. The analysis is descriptive, OS is immature, and the comparator is no longer preferred. Solomon BJ, Bauer TM, Mok TSK et al. Lancet Respir Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36535300/
- **Resistance to first-line osimertinib (MSK, 327 patients).** Atypical EGFR (median PFS 5.8 months) and TP53/RB1 co-alteration (10.5 months) predicted shorter PFS. Among 95 post-progression biopsies, a resistance mechanism was found in 52%, mainly MET amplification, SCLC transformation and EGFR amplification. Tailoring second-line therapy to the biopsy was associated with longer post-progression survival (HR 0.09). The cohort is single-institution, retrospective and subject to selection bias. Choudhury NJ, Marra A, Sui JSY et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36494075/
- **Erlotinib ± bevacizumab (randomised phase 2, Korea, n=127).** PFS was 17.5 vs 12.4 months (HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.51–1.08, not significant), with grade ≥3 AEs in 56.6% vs 20.6%. The benefit in patients with brain metastases was an unplanned subgroup finding, and erlotinib is no longer the standard backbone. Lee Y, Kim HR, Hong MH et al. Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36451343/
- **EGFR testing in pleural and pericardial effusion cfDNA (prospective diagnostic study, n=171).** ddPCR on effusion cfDNA found sensitizing EGFR variants with 97% sensitivity and 97% specificity, against 74% sensitivity for plasma. It found T790M more often than cell blocks did (51% vs 25%), but specificity for T790M was only 60%. The study is from two Hong Kong centres. Lee KWC, Li MSC, Gai W et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36580285/
- **Osimertinib rechallenge after pneumonitis (multicentre retrospective, n=33).** Most first episodes were grade 1. After rechallenge, 15% had a mild relapse and median PFS was not reached. The cohort is small and selected, and grade ≥2 events are under-represented. Imaji M, Fujimoto D, Sato Y et al. Eur J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36470023/
- **BI 1810631 (zongertinib) phase Ia/Ib design.** This is a HER2-selective covalent TKI that spares EGFR. Phase Ib will enrol pretreated HER2 TKD-mutant NSCLC. Only the design is reported. Heymach J, Opdam F, Barve M et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36528522/
- **Glesatinib phase 1 (MET/AXL).** 108 patients were treated in dose escalation plus 71 in expansion. ORR in NSCLC with MET-activating mutations was 30%, and all six partial responses were in MET exon 14 tumours. Diarrhoea occurred in 75–83%. The evidence is early-phase. Kollmannsberger C, Hurwitz H, Bazhenova L et al. Target Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36459255/
- **Resistance to MET TKIs and switching from type I to type II.** Eight paired biopsies showed off-target KRAS and HER2 alterations and second-site MET D1246N/Y1248H. A patient with a KIF5B-MET fusion responded again after the switch. This is a very small series. Riedel R, Fassunke J, Tumbrink HL et al. Eur J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36521334/
- **Bintrafusp alfa in ICI-refractory NSCLC (phase 1 expansion, n=83).** ORR was 4.8% and grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 23%, so this is a negative result for TGF-β/PD-L1 trapping after ICI. Barlesi F, Isambert N, Felip E et al. Oncologist 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36571770/
- **Tremelimumab: First Approval (drug profile).** The profile covers the US approval in November 2022 with durvalumab and platinum chemotherapy for metastatic EGFR/ALK-negative NSCLC, based on POSEIDON. It is a journal profile, not the agency's own record. Keam SJ. Drugs 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36571670/

### lung-nordic

- **Norway, stage IV adenocarcinoma 2010–2020 (Cancer Registry of Norway).** The cohort is 6,351 patients. Median survival rose for women aged 0–69 from 6.7 to 12 months, and 1-year relative survival rose from 32.4% to 51.2% in women and from 25.4% to 44.5% in men. Gains persisted after excluding EGFR/ALK-positive patients, coinciding with the introduction of immunotherapy. The study is registry-based without treatment-level data, so the gains cannot be attributed causally. Børø S, Thoresen S, Helland Å. Front Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36523970/

### thoracic-other

- **Neoadjuvant durvalumab vs durvalumab+tremelimumab in resectable mesothelioma (randomised phase 2 window, n=20).** The CD8/Treg primary endpoint was not changed. Median OS was not reached with the combination vs 14.0 months with monotherapy, and grade ≥3 immunotoxicity was 27% vs 8%. Major pathological response occurred in 2 of 17. The trial is tiny, and OS was not its primary comparison. Lee HS, Jang HJ, Ramineni M et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36469573/
- **DIADEM (single-arm phase 2, n=69).** Durvalumab in pretreated pleural mesothelioma gave a 16-week PFS rate of 28.3%, which is not meaningful activity. Canova S, Ceresoli GL, Grosso F et al. ESMO Open 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36463732/
- **IMIG consensus statement on imaging of pleural mesothelioma.** It sets harmonised technical standards for radiography, CT, FDG-PET and MRI, for care and for trials. Katz SI, Straus CM, Roshkovan L et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36549385/
