## Practice-changing

- **ADAURA: overall survival with adjuvant osimertinib (double-blind phase 3, NEJM 2023).**
  - **Design:** 682 patients with completely resected EGFR-mutant (ex19del/L858R) stage IB–IIIA NSCLC, with or without prior adjuvant chemotherapy, were randomised to osimertinib 80 mg daily for up to 3 years or placebo.
  - **Primary endpoint and this analysis:** DFS in stage II–IIIA was the primary endpoint and was already reported. This is the planned final analysis of OS, a key secondary endpoint.
  - **Results:** In stage II–IIIA, 5-year OS was 85% vs 73% (HR 0.49, 95.03% CI 0.33–0.73, P < .001). In the overall IB–IIIA population it was 88% vs 78% (HR 0.49, 95.03% CI 0.34–0.70).
  - **Safety:** It was consistent with the primary analysis, with one new serious AE (COVID-19 pneumonia) unrelated to the drug. The 3-year safety analysis in the previous run showed ILD as the main reason for discontinuation.
  - **Still open:**
    - Optimal duration beyond 3 years is untested.
    - The trial predates routine perioperative immunotherapy.
    - Reimbursement status in Norway is not covered by this record.
  - Tsuboi M, Herbst RS, John T et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37272535/
- **KEYNOTE-671: perioperative pembrolizumab for resectable stage II–IIIB (N2) NSCLC (double-blind phase 3, NEJM 2023).**
  - **Design:** 797 patients were randomised to 4 cycles of neoadjuvant cisplatin-based chemotherapy with pembrolizumab or placebo, then surgery and up to 13 cycles of adjuvant pembrolizumab or placebo.
  - **Primary endpoints (EFS and OS, dual):** At the first interim analysis (median follow-up 25.2 months), 24-month EFS was 62.4% vs 40.6% (HR 0.58, 95% CI 0.46–0.72, P < .001). 24-month OS was 80.9% vs 77.6%, which had not yet crossed the significance boundary.
  - **Pathological response:** MPR was 30.2% vs 11.0% and pCR 18.1% vs 4.0%.
  - **Toxicity:** Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 44.9% vs 37.3%, with grade 5 events in 1.0% vs 0.8%.
  - **Since then:** This primary EFS report supports the perioperative regimen. The later OS analysis (HR 0.72) is already in the index.
  - **Still open:** Whether the adjuvant phase adds to neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy alone, and the choice between perioperative and neoadjuvant-only approaches (CheckMate 816).
  - Wakelee H, Liberman M, Kato T et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37272513/
- **England: national targeted lung cancer screening programme announced (BMJ news, June 2023; national screening programme decision).**
  - **The decision:** The UK Government and NHS England announced a national targeted LDCT screening programme for people aged 55–74 with a smoking history in their GP record, with smoking cessation offered alongside.
  - **Basis:** It follows the UK National Screening Committee recommendation (September 2022) and scales up the Targeted Lung Health Check pilot, with full coverage planned by about 2029–2030.
  - **Source note:** The BMJ item has no abstract. These details come from the government announcement and related coverage I found by web search, not from the item itself.
  - **Still open:** The programme rests on NELSON/NLST-type mortality evidence. Its relevance for Norway is as a European precedent for Helsedirektoratet's later screening assessment.
  - Mahase E. BMJ 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37369389/

## High

### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **NADIM II (open-label randomised phase 2, n=86, NEJM).** Perioperative nivolumab plus chemotherapy vs chemotherapy alone was tested in resectable stage IIIA–IIIB NSCLC. pCR was 37% vs 7% (RR 5.34). 24-month PFS was 67.2% vs 40.9% (HR 0.47) and OS 85.0% vs 63.6% (HR 0.43, 95% CI 0.19–0.98), with surgery in 93% vs 69%. The trial is small, randomised phase 2, and pCR-powered, so it is supportive rather than definitive. Provencio M, Nadal E, González-Larriba JL et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37379158/
- **GEMSTONE-302 interim OS (phase 3, n=479).** Sugemalimab plus platinum chemotherapy vs placebo plus chemotherapy, first line, gave OS 25.4 vs 16.9 months (HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.50–0.84). The comparator lacks immunotherapy and is no longer standard. Sugemalimab is not available in Norway. Zhou C, Wang Z, Sun M et al. Nat Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37322367/
- **IMscin001 Part 2 (phase 3, n=371).** Subcutaneous atezolizumab (1,875 mg) vs IV (1,200 mg) in pretreated NSCLC met PK non-inferiority (Ctrough GMR 1.05, AUC GMR 0.87), with similar PFS (HR 1.08) and ORR (12% vs 10%). It was the basis for SC formulations, but the endpoint is PK, not efficacy. Burotto M, Zvirbule Z, Mochalova A et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37268157/
- **Duration of first-line immunotherapy (retrospective US cohort, n=706 progression-free at 2 years).** Stopping at 2 years (n=113) vs continuing (n=593) gave 2-year OS 79% vs 81% (adjusted HR 1.33, 95% CI 0.78–2.25, not significant). It is observational with a small fixed-duration group, and supports, but does not prove, stopping at 2 years. Sun L, Bleiberg B, Hwang WT et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37270700/
- **IL-6R blockade for irAEs (multicentre retrospective study, n=92, 8% lung).** Tocilizumab or sarilumab, mostly after steroids or DMARDs failed, brought irAEs to grade ≤1 in 73% with no loss of ORR (66% before vs 66% after). It is retrospective and mainly inflammatory arthritis. Fa'ak F, Buni M, Falohun A et al. J Immunother Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37328287/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / lung-ctdna-mrd

- **LASER301 (phase 3, n=393).** Lazertinib vs gefitinib, first line, gave PFS 20.6 vs 9.7 months (HR 0.45, 95% CI 0.34–0.58) and DoR 19.4 vs 8.3 months. OS is immature (18-month 80% vs 72%, HR 0.74, P = .12). The comparator is a first-generation TKI. Cho BC, Ahn MJ, Kang JH et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37379502/
- **ICOMPARE (randomised phase II, n=109).** 2 years vs 1 year of adjuvant icotinib in stage II–IIIA EGFR-mutant adenocarcinoma gave DFS 48.9 vs 32.9 months (HR 0.51) and OS HR 0.34. The trial is small, uses a first-generation TKI with no chemotherapy, and is exploratory. Lv C, Wang R, Li S et al. ESMO Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37348348/
- **Plasma EGFR clearance on treatment (exploratory analysis of AURA3 and FLAURA).** Week-3 clearance predicted longer PFS on osimertinib: 19.8 vs 11.3 months in FLAURA and 10.9 vs 5.7 months in AURA3. It is retrospective and not yet a guide for treatment changes. Gray JE, Ahn MJ, Oxnard GR et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37379430/
- **Longitudinal ctDNA in resected EGFR-mutant NSCLC (prospective cohort, n=278, ddPCR).** 3-year DFS was 84% when baseline ctDNA was negative, 78% when positive but cleared after surgery, and 50% when MRD-positive. MRD preceded radiological relapse in 69% with ex19del but only 20% with L858R. It is a single-institution cohort. Jung HA, Ku BM, Kim YJ et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37308037/
- **Zipalertinib in EGFR exon 20 insertions (phase 1/2a, n=73).** Confirmed PR was 38% overall and 41% at 100 mg BID. There was no grade ≥3 rash or diarrhoea at ≤100 mg. It is an early-phase trial in a pretreated population. Piotrowska Z, Tan DS, Smit EF et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37384848/
- **JMT101 plus osimertinib in EGFR exon 20 insertions (phase 1b, n=121).** ORR was 36.4% and PFS 8.2 months (34% and 9.2 months if platinum-refractory). Intracranial DCR was 87.5%. Rash (77%) and diarrhoea (64%) were common, and it is single-arm. Zhao S, Zhuang W, Han B et al. Nat Commun 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37308490/
- **Datopotamab deruxtecan, TROPION-PanTumor01 NSCLC cohort (phase I, n=210).** At 6 mg/kg (n=50), ORR was 26%, DoR 10.5 months, PFS 6.9 months and OS 11.4 months. Drug-related ILD occurred in 6%, and responses were independent of TROP2. It is single-arm and early-phase. Shimizu T, Sands J, Yoh K et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37327461/
- **Dacomitinib skin-toxicity prophylaxis (single-arm phase II, n=41).** Comprehensive prophylaxis including minocycline did not meet its goal: grade ≥2 skin toxicity occurred in 43.9%. It is a negative prophylaxis trial. Iwasaku M, Uchino J, Chibana K et al. Cancer Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37269194/
- **CSCO consensus on EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC (2023).** It is a Chinese expert consensus. Zhongguo Fei Ai Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37316442/

### nsclc-drivers

- **VISION long-term follow-up (phase 2, n=313).** Tepotinib in METex14 NSCLC gave ORR 51.4%. In treatment-naive patients ORR was 57.3% with DoR 46.4 months, vs 45.0% and 12.6 months in pretreated patients. Oedema occurred in 67% (11% grade ≥3). It is single-arm. Mazieres J, Paik PK, Garassino MC et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37270698/
- **PHAROS: encorafenib plus binimetinib in BRAF V600E NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=98).** ORR was 75% in treatment-naive and 46% in pretreated patients, with PFS not reached and 9.3 months respectively. TRAEs led to discontinuation in 15%, with one fatal intracranial haemorrhage. It is single-arm. Riely GJ, Smit EF, Ahn MJ et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37270692/
- **KRYSTAL-1: adagrasib with untreated CNS metastases (n=25).** Intracranial ORR was 42% and DCR 90%, with PFS 5.4 months and OS 11.4 months. Grade 3 TRAEs occurred in 40%. It is a small cohort. Negrao MV, Spira AI, Heist RS et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37327468/
- **Unecritinib in ROS1-positive NSCLC (phase I/II, n=111 in phase II).** IRC-assessed ORR was 80.2% and PFS 16.5 months. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 47%, and ocular and neurotoxicity were common but low-grade. It is single-arm and China-only. Lu S, Pan H, Wu L et al. Signal Transduct Target Ther 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37385995/
- **Pralsetinib, ARROW Chinese cohort (n=68).** ORR was 83.3% in treatment-naive and 66.7% in pretreated patients, with PFS 12.7 and 11.7 months. Grade 3/4 anaemia occurred in 35% and neutropenia in 34%. It is single-arm. Zhou Q, Zhao J, Chang J et al. Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37282666/
- **Selpercatinib intracranial activity, LIBRETTO-321 (n=8 with brain metastases).** 4 of 5 with measurable CNS lesions responded, including 1 CR. It is a very small case series. Cheng Y, Huang D, Zhou J et al. JCO Precis Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37315261/
- **FDA Approval Summary: tissue-agnostic selpercatinib (21 September 2022).** It was an accelerated approval for RET fusion-positive solid tumours, based on LIBRETTO-001 (ORR 44% in 41 non-lung, non-thyroid tumours). It is a journal summary of an earlier decision. Duke ES, Bradford D, Marcovitz M et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37265412/
- **TAPUR: pertuzumab plus trastuzumab in ERBB2-altered lung cancer (n=28).** DC was 37% and ORR 11%, mostly in exon 20 insertions. Activity was modest. Ganti AK, Rothe M, Mangat PK et al. JCO Precis Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37315265/
- **KRAS G12C in the Swedish national registry (n=6,183 with NGS KRAS status).** G12C was found in 16% of adenocarcinomas. It was enriched in women (65%) and in stage IV patients with CNS metastases (28%). Stage IV OS was 5.8 months for G12C vs 6.4 months for KRAS wild-type. It is registry data, mostly from before G12C inhibitors. Isaksson J, Berglund A, Louie K et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37296038/

### lung-local-therapy / guidelines

- **ASTRO/ESTRO guideline: oligometastatic NSCLC.** It makes conditional recommendations for definitive local therapy (radiation or surgery only) when all sites (≤5) can be treated safely, in synchronous, metachronous, oligopersistent and oligoprogressive settings, with SBRT dose and fractionation guidance. It is explicitly limited by the lack of phase 3 evidence. Iyengar P, All S, Berry MF et al. Pract Radiat Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37294262/
- **AATS 2023 expert consensus on staging and multidisciplinary management of early-stage NSCLC.** It emphasises accurate staging and early molecular testing with perioperative targeted and immunotherapy. The abstract gives no specific recommendations. Expert Consensus Panel, Kidane B, Bott M et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37306641/
- **ESTS expert consensus on technical standards of segmentectomy.** There is no abstract. Brunelli A, Decaluwe H, Gonzalez M et al. Eur J Cardiothorac Surg 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37267148/
- **Chinese consensus on wedge resection of nodules ≤2 cm (2023).** Hu J, Chen J, Chen C et al. Zhongguo Fei Ai Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37316443/
- **ESMO Open consensus on pathologist-initiated reflex biomarker testing in NSCLC.** Gosney JR, Paz-Ares L, Jänne P et al. ESMO Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37356358/
- **SRS vs WBRT for 4–10 brain metastases (prospective SRS cohort n=40 vs propensity-matched historical WBRT n=70).** OS was 10.4 vs 6.5 months (HR 0.65, P = .07), so the primary endpoint was not met. There was no grade III toxicity with SRS. It is non-randomised. Bodensohn R, Kaempfel AL, Boulesteix AL et al. Radiother Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37330054/

### car-t-solid

- **Fatal pulmonary toxicity with high-dose mesothelin CAR-T (2 cases from a phase 1 trial).** Both patients had hypoxaemia within 48 hours with CRS features, and one died of respiratory failure. Autopsy showed CAR-T accumulation in lung, with low MSLN expression in benign pneumocytes. This is on-target off-tumour toxicity relevant to MSLN-directed therapy in mesothelioma and lung cancer. Haas AR, Golden RJ, Litzky LA et al. Mol Ther 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37312454/
- **T4 immunotherapy: intratumoral pan-ErbB CAR-T in HNSCC (phase 1, n=15).** There were no DLTs (all TRAEs grade ≤2) and no CAR-T leakage into blood. Disease stabilised at 6 weeks in 60%. It is single-arm dose escalation. Papa S, Adami A, Metoudi M et al. J Immunother Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37321663/
- **IV RNA-electroporated cMET CAR-T in melanoma and TNBC (phase I, n=7).** There was no grade ≥3 toxicity. The best response was stable disease (4 of 7), and no CAR-T cells were found in tumour. It is very small. Shah PD, Huang AC, Xu X et al. Cancer Res Commun 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37377890/

### thoracic-other

- **NIVOTHYM: nivolumab in pretreated B3 thymoma and thymic carcinoma (EORTC-ETOP phase II, n=55).** 6-month PFS rate was 35%, below the target, with ORR 12%, PFS 6.0 months and OS 21.3 months. There were 2 grade 4 myocarditis cases. It is single-arm and negative on its primary endpoint. Girard N, Ponce Aix S, Cedres S et al. ESMO Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37285717/
- **Nintedanib in recurrent mesothelioma (phase II, n=20).** There were no responses. 4-month PFS was 13% (primary endpoint missed) and OS 4.2 months. It is negative. Wozniak AJ, Schneider B, Kalemkerian GP et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37301693/
