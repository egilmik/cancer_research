## Practice-changing

- **Lung ART: postoperative radiotherapy (PORT) vs no PORT after complete resection of pN2 NSCLC (open-label randomised phase 3, n=501, Lancet Oncol).** Patients were enrolled from 64 centres in five European countries between 2007 and 2018. Most were staged with FDG-PET (91%) and had neoadjuvant or adjuvant chemotherapy. They were randomised to mediastinal 3D-conformal PORT, 54 Gy in 27–30 fractions, or no PORT. The primary endpoint, 3-year DFS, was 47% (95% CI 40–54) with PORT vs 44% (37–51) without (median 30.5 vs 22.8 months; HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.68–1.08; p=0.18), at a median follow-up of 4.8 years. PORT added grade 3–4 pneumonitis (5% vs <1%) and late grade 3–4 cardiopulmonary toxicity (11% vs 5%). Two deaths from pneumonitis, partly attributed to radiotherapy and infection, both occurred in the PORT arm. The trial is negative for its primary endpoint and shows added toxicity, which argues against routine PORT after complete resection of N2 disease. Open questions: OS data are not reported in the abstract, PORT may still have a place in selected high-risk patients (e.g. incomplete resection, many involved stations), and the trial began before routine adjuvant immunotherapy or osimertinib. Le Pechoux C, Pourel N, Barlesi F et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34919827/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers
- **WJOG8515L: nivolumab vs carboplatin-pemetrexed after EGFR-TKI resistance without T790M (randomised phase II, n=102, CCR).** PFS was 1.7 vs 5.6 months (HR 1.92, 60% CI 1.61–2.29) and ORR 9.6% vs 36.0%. OS was similar (20.7 vs 19.9 months; HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.53–1.47). No subgroup, including high TMB, clearly benefited from nivolumab. It confirms that PD-1 monotherapy should not replace chemotherapy after EGFR-TKI failure; the trial is small. Hayashi H, Sugawara S, Fukuda Y et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34921023/
- **Lazertinib 240 mg in T790M-positive NSCLC after prior EGFR TKI (integrated phase 1/2, n=78, Korea, JTO).** ORR was 55.3% (95% CI 44.1–66.4) and median PFS 11.1 months, with OS not reached at 22 months. Intracranial ORR was 85.7%, but in only 7 patients. Rash, pruritus and paraesthesia were the most common AEs, mostly mild. It is single-arm, in a setting osimertinib already covers. Cho BC, Han JY, Kim SW et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34958928/
- **RELAY safety analysis: ramucirumab plus erlotinib vs placebo plus erlotinib first line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (phase 3, n=446 safety population, Drug Saf).** Grade ≥3 AEs were more frequent with the combination, mainly grade 3 hypertension, plus acneiform dermatitis and diarrhoea. These were detected through routine monitoring and managed with dose adjustments. It is a secondary analysis, and the comparator (erlotinib) is no longer the first-line standard. Nadal E, Horinouchi H, Shih JY et al. Drug Saf 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34928484/
- **T-DM1 in pretreated HER2 exon 20 insertion NSCLC (phase II, n=22, Japan, EJC).** ORR was 38.1% (90% CI 23.0–55.9), but median DoR was 3.5 months, PFS 2.8 and OS 8.1 months. Toxicity was mild. Responses are short-lived, and trastuzumab deruxtecan is the more active HER2 ADC. Iwama E, Zenke Y, Sugawara S et al. Eur J Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34959152/
- **Canadian consensus recommendations on MET-altered NSCLC (Curr Oncol).** An expert panel addressed testing, first- and later-line therapy for MET exon 14 skipping and de novo MET amplification, acquired MET amplification after EGFR TKIs, and resistance to MET inhibitors. The abstract lists the questions but not the recommendations. Cheema PK, Banerji SO, Blais N et al. Curr Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34898564/
- **FDA approval summary: sotorasib for pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC (Clin Cancer Res).** Accelerated approval was granted on 28 May 2021, based on CodeBreaK 100 (n=124): ORR 36% (95% CI 28–45) and median DoR 10.0 months. Common adverse reactions were diarrhoea, musculoskeletal pain, nausea, fatigue, hepatotoxicity and cough. A dose-comparison study is a post-marketing requirement. It is a journal profile of an approval already recorded, not the decision itself. Nakajima EC, Drezner N, Li X et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34903582/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy / sclc
- **CameL-Sq: camrelizumab plus carboplatin-paclitaxel vs placebo plus chemotherapy first line in advanced squamous NSCLC (double-blind phase 3, n=389, China, JTO).** PFS was 8.5 vs 4.9 months (p<0.0001) and OS not reached vs 14.5 months (p<0.0001). No unexpected immune-related AEs occurred. ctDNA clearance after two cycles was associated with outcome. The comparator, chemotherapy alone, is no longer standard; HRs are not given in the abstract. Ren S, Chen J, Xu X et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34923163/
- **ASCO guideline: management of stage III NSCLC (JCO).** Recommendations on staging, surgery, neoadjuvant and adjuvant therapy, and unresectable disease are based on 127 studies. The abstract does not spell out the recommendations or what changed. Daly ME, Singh N, Ismaila N et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34936470/
- **ASCO-SNO-ASTRO guideline: treatment of brain metastases (JCO).** It is based on 32 randomised trials. Symptomatic brain metastases should get local therapy whatever the systemic therapy. SRS alone should be offered for 1–4 unresected metastases (not SCLC) when there are no systemic options, and SRS to the cavity for 1–2 resected metastases. Memantine and hippocampal avoidance should be offered with WBRT. The abstract does not say which recommendations are new. Vogelbaum MA, Brown PD, Messersmith H et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34932393/
- **NCCN Guidelines: small cell lung cancer, version 2.2022 (JNCCN).** This selection focuses on extensive-stage disease and strongly promotes smoking cessation. The abstract refers to the update summary but does not name the changed recommendations. Ganti AKP, Loo BW, Bassetti M et al. J Natl Compr Canc Netw 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34902832/

### lung-screening / lung-nonpharm
- **ILST: USPSTF2013 criteria vs the PLCOm2012 risk model for screening eligibility (prospective cohort, interim analysis, n=5,819, Canada/Australia/Hong Kong/UK, Lancet Oncol).** For the same number selected (4,540), PLCOm2012 (≥1.70% risk over 6 years) found more lung cancers than USPSTF2013 (162 vs 135; sensitivity difference 15.8%, 95% CI 10.7–22.1). Those selected by PLCOm2012 were older, with more comorbidity. Mean follow-up is only 2.3 years, and incidence among those excluded by both was modelled from PLCO data. Tammemägi MC, Ruparel M, Tremblay A et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34902336/
- **Persistent smoking after surgery for stage I NSCLC (retrospective VA cohort, n=7,489, Chest).** Of 4,562 patients smoking at surgery, 58% still smoked a year later, and 19.6% of non-smokers had relapsed. Persistent smoking was associated with shorter OS (aHR 1.29, 95% CI 1.20–1.39) but not with DFS (aHR 0.99). It supports continued smoking-cessation support after resection; it is observational. Heiden BT, Eaton DB, Chang SH et al. Chest 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34919892/
- **Condensed (3-week) vs standard (5-week) prehabilitation before surgery in NSCLC (randomised trial, J Physiother).** Both arms had 15 supervised sessions. The 3-week regimen gave a similar or better gain in VO2peak (MD 1.2 ml/kg/min, 95% CI −0.1 to 2.6), with no loss of adherence or rise in adverse events. It suggests prehabilitation fits short preoperative windows. Fitness is a surrogate endpoint, and effects on complications are uncertain. Gravier FE, Smondack P, Boujibar F et al. J Physiother 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34952813/

### lung-ctdna-mrd
- **LUNGCA-1: perioperative ctDNA MRD in resected stage I–III NSCLC (prospective multicentre cohort, n=330, China, CCR).** Preoperative ctDNA positivity predicted shorter RFS (HR 4.2). Postoperative MRD (day 3 and/or 1 month) predicted relapse strongly (HR 11.1) and outweighed TNM stage. MRD-positive patients given adjuvant therapy had better RFS (HR 0.3), while MRD-negative patients did not benefit. The adjuvant comparison is non-randomised. Xia L, Mei J, Kang R et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34844976/

### lung-vaccines-cell / car-t-solid
- **DCVAC/LuCa dendritic-cell vaccine with carboplatin-pemetrexed first line in driver-negative non-squamous NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=61, China, ESMO Open).** Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 13.3%, with no vaccine-induced AEs. In the mITT population (n=44), 2-year OS was 52.6%, median PFS 8.0 months and ORR 31.8%. It is uncontrolled, and the regimen has no checkpoint inhibitor, which is now standard; a phase III is planned. Zhong R, Ling X, Cao S et al. ESMO Open 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34959168/
- **HBV-specific TCR-T cells in advanced HBV-related HCC (phase I dose escalation, n=8, Hepatol Int).** One grade 3 liver AE occurred. One patient had a partial response lasting 27.7 months, and most had falling HBsAg/HBV DNA, indicating on-target effect. It is very small. Meng F, Zhao J, Tan AT et al. Hepatol Int 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34850325/
