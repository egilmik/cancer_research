### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **LUNAR (open-label pivotal phase 3, n=276, Lancet Oncol).** Tumor Treating Fields added to standard second-line therapy (docetaxel or ICI) after platinum progression improved median OS from 9.9 to 13.2 months (HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.56–0.98, P = .035). Serious AEs occurred in 53% vs 38%, and device-related events were mostly grade 1–2 skin reactions. Limitations: there was no sham device, the ICI subgroup is small, and the docetaxel-alone control is now rare after first-line chemo-IO. Leal T, Kotecha R, Ramlau R et al. Lancet Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37657460/
- **JAVELIN Lung 100 (phase 3, n=1,214, J Thorac Oncol).** First-line avelumab vs platinum doublet in PD-L1-high NSCLC did not meet its primary endpoint. OS HR was 0.85 (95% CI 0.67–1.09) with Q2W dosing, and 0.79 with weekly dosing. PFS improved (HR 0.71). It is a negative trial for avelumab. Reck M, Barlesi F, Yang JC et al. J Thorac Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37748693/
- **ATALANTE-1: OSE2101 vaccine vs chemotherapy after ICB failure (randomised phase 3, n=219, HLA-A2+, Ann Oncol).** Overall OS was not significantly improved (HR 0.86). In the 118 patients with secondary resistance, OS was 11.1 vs 7.5 months (HR 0.59, 95% CI 0.38–0.91). Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 11.4% vs 35.1%. The positive finding is in a subgroup, so it is hypothesis-generating. Besse B, Felip E, Garcia Campelo R et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37704166/
- **NeoCOAST (randomised phase II platform, n=83, Cancer Discov).** One neoadjuvant cycle of durvalumab plus oleclumab (anti-CD73) or monalizumab (anti-NKG2A) gave higher MPR rates than durvalumab alone, with similar safety. Arms had 16–26 patients, MPR is a surrogate, and there is no chemotherapy backbone. Cascone T, Kar G, Spicer JD et al. Cancer Discov 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37707791/
- **JIPANG 5-year OS (phase 3, n=783, J Clin Oncol).** Adjuvant pemetrexed-cisplatin vs vinorelbine-cisplatin in resected stage II–IIIA non-squamous NSCLC gave 5-year OS 75.0% vs 75.6% (HR 1.04, 95% CI 0.81–1.34) and RFS HR 0.95. It supports pemetrexed-cisplatin as an equally effective, better-tolerated adjuvant option. The population is Japanese. Kenmotsu H, Yamamoto N, Misumi T et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37656928/
- **TARGET-TP (RCT, n=328, 39% lung cancer, JAMA Oncol).** In patients at high risk by a biomarker-based score, enoxaparin thromboprophylaxis reduced thromboembolism from 23% to 8% (HR 0.31, NNT 6.7) with no excess major bleeding. Six-month mortality was 13% vs 26% (HR 0.48). The mortality effect is a secondary finding in a small open-label trial. Alexander M, Harris S, Underhill C et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37733336/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers

- **HERTHENA-Lung01 (phase II, n=225, J Clin Oncol).** Patritumab deruxtecan (HER3-DXd) 5.6 mg/kg after EGFR-TKI and platinum gave BICR ORR 29.8%, median PFS 5.5 months and OS 11.9 months, with CNS ORR 33.3% in untreated brain metastases. Activity was seen across HER3 levels and resistance mechanisms. It is single-arm, and the phase 3 (HERTHENA-Lung02) is ongoing. Yu HA, Goto Y, Hayashi H et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37689979/
- **DESTINY-Lung02 (randomised phase II, n=152, J Clin Oncol).** T-DXd 5.4 vs 6.4 mg/kg in pretreated HER2-mutant NSCLC gave ORR 49.0% vs 56.0%. Adjudicated drug-related ILD occurred in 12.9% vs 28.0%, and grade ≥3 TRAEs in 38.6% vs 58.0%, favouring 5.4 mg/kg. It is not powered to compare doses formally. Goto K, Goto Y, Kubo T et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37694347/
- **Amivantamab plus lazertinib after osimertinib (CHRYSALIS cohort E, phase 1, Nat Med).** In 45 chemotherapy-naive patients enrolled without biomarker selection, ORR was 36% (95% CI 22–51), DoR 9.6 months and PFS 4.9 months; grade ≥3 events were reported in 4%. High EGFR/MET expression may enrich for response (exploratory). It is single-arm. Cho BC, Kim DW, Spira AI et al. Nat Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37710001/
- **AfaBev-CS (randomised phase II, n=100, Japan, Lung Cancer).** Afatinib plus bevacizumab vs afatinib alone first line gave PFS 16.3 vs 16.1 months (HR 0.87, P = .55) and no OS difference, with more grade ≥3 toxicity in the combination. It is negative. Ninomiya T, Ishikawa N, Kozuki T et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37651927/

### lung-ctdna-mrd

- **PROPHET tumour-informed ctDNA MRD (181 prospectively enrolled early-stage NSCLC patients, 760 samples, Cancer Cell).** Deep sequencing of 50 patient-specific variants (limit of detection 0.004%) gave 45% baseline sensitivity and a median lead time of 299 days before radiological recurrence, outperforming fixed panels. The study proposes a TNM-B classification. It is observational, with no ctDNA-guided intervention. Chen K, Yang F, Shen H et al. Cancer Cell 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37683638/
- **Upfront plasma NGS at the first visit (randomised study, Taiwan, n=180 enrolled, Eur J Cancer).** Liquid NGS from the first visit shortened median time to treatment from 28 to 20 days and found driver mutations in 42.6% (20/47) of tissue-negative cases. ORR and PFS did not differ. 57% had EGFR mutations, which limits transfer to a Nordic population. Yang CY, Shih JY, Liao WY et al. Eur J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37722270/

### lung-local-therapy

- **DOLPHIN (single-arm phase 2, n=35, Japan, JAMA Oncol).** Durvalumab with concurrent radiotherapy, without chemotherapy, in PD-L1-positive unresectable stage III NSCLC gave 12-month PFS 72.1% and median PFS 25.6 months. Grade 3–4 AEs occurred in 52.9%, grade 5 in 5.9%, and pneumonitis of any grade in 67.6%. It is small and non-randomised. Tachihara M, Tsujino K, Ishihara T et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37676681/
- **iSABR (nonrandomised phase 2, 217 patients and 285 tumours, JAMA Oncol).** SABR dose was individualised by tumour volume, most often 25 Gy in one fraction. One-year freedom from local recurrence was 94–97%, 5-year 83–93%, and grade 3–5 toxicity 5%. There is no randomised comparator, and tumours were mixed primary and metastatic. Gensheimer MF, Gee H, Shirato H et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37707820/

### lung-nordic

- **Vulnerable patients not receiving guideline treatment (Danish Lung Cancer Registry, n=14,597, Acta Oncol).** 21% of stage I–IIIA patients did not receive curative treatment, and 10% of stage IIIB–IV received no oncological therapy. Associated factors were age ≥80 (OR 0.59), PS 2 (OR 0.33), comorbidity, living alone (OR 0.79) and, in advanced disease, distance to hospital. It is observational. Langballe R, Jakobsen E, Iachina M et al. Acta Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37656828/
- **Stage III NSCLC in Denmark 2010–2018 (retrospective cohort, n=851, Acta Oncol).** 70% were offered curative-intent treatment, mostly chemoradiotherapy, with median OS 24.4 months. Diagnosis in 2016–2018 was associated with better OS. The period is largely before durvalumab consolidation. Meldgaard P, Kristensen M, Conte S et al. Acta Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37699061/

### thoracic-other

- **ONCOS-102 plus pemetrexed-platinum in pleural mesothelioma (randomised phase 2, n=31, J Immunother Cancer).** This oncolytic adenovirus expressing GM-CSF was originally developed in Norway (Targovax). There was no significant efficacy difference. In chemotherapy-naive patients, median OS was 20.3 vs 13.5 months (30-month OS 34.1% vs 0%, n=17), with T-cell infiltration in injected tumours. It is very small and exploratory, and first-line nivolumab-ipilimumab is now standard. Ponce S, Cedrés S, Ricordel C et al. J Immunother Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37661097/
- **IASLC thymic epithelial tumour staging, 9th edition: stage classification and N/M proposals (J Thorac Oncol).** T1 is split at 5 cm, the mediastinal pleura is dropped as a descriptor, and lung or phrenic nerve invasion moves to T2. N and M categories and stage groups are unchanged; the T proposal paper appeared in the previous run. Ruffini E, Huang J, Cilento V et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37689391/; Fang W, Girard N, Cilento V et al. J Thorac Oncol 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37774950/
- **NCCN Guidelines: Mesothelioma: Peritoneal, version 2.2023 (J Natl Compr Canc Netw).** These are recommendations for workup, diagnosis and treatment of primary and previously treated peritoneal mesothelioma. The abstract notes updated pathology markers and that "malignant" is no longer used, since all mesotheliomas are malignant. The specific treatment changes are not stated in the abstract. Ettinger DS, Wood DE, Stevenson J et al. J Natl Compr Canc Netw 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37673108/
