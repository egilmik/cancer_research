## Practice-changing

### ASCO guideline: exercise, diet and weight management during cancer treatment (JCO, 2022)
This is a new ASCO guideline built on a systematic review of 52 systematic reviews and 23 additional RCTs (2000–May 2021) across cancer types; lung was among the most studied. Exercise during treatment improved cardiorespiratory fitness, strength, fatigue and other patient-reported outcomes. In lung cancer, preoperative exercise shortened hospital stay and reduced postoperative complications. The recommendations: oncology providers *should* recommend regular aerobic and resistance exercise during curative-intent treatment, and *may* recommend preoperative exercise before lung cancer surgery. Neutropenic diets are not recommended, and evidence for other diet or weight-loss interventions was too limited. Limitations: the lung-specific recommendation is permissive ("may"), the underlying prehabilitation trials are small and heterogeneous, and the guideline gives no survival benefit. Implementation in Norway depends on whether thoracic surgery pathways fund prehabilitation. Ligibel JA, Bohlke K, May AM et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35576506/

Strong candidates, rated high:

- **CAPSTONE-1 (adebrelimab plus chemotherapy in ES-SCLC):** a positive phase 3 OS result, but against placebo plus chemotherapy, which is no longer standard where atezolizumab or durvalumab is available.
- **AENEAS (aumolertinib vs gefitinib):** a positive phase 3, but against a comparator that osimertinib has replaced.

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers
- **AENEAS: aumolertinib vs gefitinib first line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (double-blind randomised phase 3, n=429, China, JCO).** PFS was 19.3 vs 9.9 months (HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.36–0.60), with similar ORR (73.8% vs 72.1%) and grade ≥3 AEs (36.4% vs 35.8%), and less rash and diarrhoea. It establishes a third-generation TKI over gefitinib, not over osimertinib, and OS is not reported. Lu S, Dong X, Jian H et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35580297/
- **CROWN post hoc analysis: lorlatinib vs crizotinib by baseline brain metastases (phase 3, JCO).** Twelve-month PFS was 78% vs 22% with baseline brain metastases and 78% vs 45% without. Twelve-month cumulative CNS progression was 7% vs 72% and 1% vs 18%. CNS AEs occurred in 35%, mostly grade 1, and 56% resolved. It is post hoc. Solomon BJ, Bauer TM, Ignatius Ou SH et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35605188/
- **Lorlatinib for CNS-only relapse on second-generation ALK TKIs (phase II, n=23, JCO Precis Oncol).** Intracranial disease control at 12 weeks was 95%, intracranial ORR 59%, and median intracranial PFS 24.6 months. Hyperlipidaemia was near-universal, and cognitive (52%) and mood (43%) effects were common. Two patients died of respiratory events. It is small and single-arm. Dagogo-Jack I, Oxnard GR, Evangelist M et al. JCO Precis Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35584349/
- **JMTO LC12-01: continuing gefitinib with docetaxel or pemetrexed beyond progression in elderly EGFR-mutant NSCLC (randomised phase II, n=22, Thorac Cancer).** The trial was terminated early. PFS was longer with gefitinib continuation (5.6 vs 1.6 months, HR 0.40, 95% CI 0.16–0.99), but grade ≥3 AEs doubled (90.9% vs 45.5%). It is too small to guide practice and was done in the gefitinib era. Asami K, Ando M, Nishimura T et al. Thorac Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35562327/
- **HJM-561, an oral EGFR PROTAC (preclinical, Mol Cancer Ther).** It selectively degrades Del19/T790M/C797S and L858R/T790M/C797S EGFR, spares wild-type, and is active in osimertinib-resistant CDX and PDX models. It is preclinical only. Du Y, Chen Y, Wang Y et al. Mol Cancer Ther 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35499406/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy / lung-ctdna-mrd
- **CITYSCAPE: tiragolumab (anti-TIGIT) plus atezolizumab vs atezolizumab first line in PD-L1-positive NSCLC (randomised double-blind phase 2, n=135, Lancet Oncol).** ORR was 31.3% vs 16.2% and PFS 5.4 vs 3.6 months (HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.37–0.90). Serious treatment-related AEs were 21% vs 18%, with two treatment-related deaths with tiragolumab. It is phase 2 with short follow-up (5.9 months at the primary analysis), and phase 3 confirmation is needed. Cho BC, Abreu DR, Hussein M et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35576957/
- **NADIM: 3-year OS and ctDNA after neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy in stage IIIA NSCLC (single-arm phase II, JCO).** Three-year OS was 81.9% (95% CI 66.8–90.6) in the ITT population. PD-L1 and TMB did not predict survival. Undetectable ctDNA after neoadjuvant treatment predicted OS (HR 0.04, 95% CI 0.00–0.55) better than RECIST (C-index 0.82 vs 0.72). It is single-arm and small. Provencio M, Serna-Blasco R, Nadal E et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35576508/
- **Longitudinal MRD after resection of stage I–III NSCLC (prospective cohort, n=261, 913 samples, Cancer Discov).** Only 3.2% with longitudinally undetectable MRD recurred (NPV 96.8%), and longitudinally detectable MRD had a PPV of 89.1% with a 3.4-month lead time. Brain-only recurrence was poorly detected (1 of 5). In subgroups, MRD-negative patients appeared not to benefit from adjuvant therapy, which is hypothesis-generating. Zhang JT, Liu SY, Gao W et al. Cancer Discov 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35543554/
- **Neoadjuvant sintilimab, 3-year follow-up (phase 1b, single-arm, JTO).** After two doses of sintilimab and R0 resection, 3-year OS was 88.5% and DFS 75.0%. PD-L1 ≥1% was associated with better outcomes (HR 0.275). It is a small single-arm study of monotherapy. Zhang F, Guo W, Zhou B et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35550174/
- **SABR to oligoprogressive sites while continuing anti-PD-1 in NSCLC and melanoma (prospective multicentre observational, 50 analysed, IJROBP).** ORR was 42% (30% complete), median PFS 14.2 months and OS 37.4 months from SABR, with abscopal response in 65% of evaluable patients. It is non-randomised with mixed tumour types. Chicas-Sett R, Zafra J, Rodriguez-Abreu D et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35595158/
- **Functional lung avoidance with 3He-MRI during chemoradiotherapy (double-masked RCT, 27 of 64 planned, IJROBP).** The trial closed early for slow accrual. Three-month FACT-L LCS was the same (17.4 vs 17.3), and grade ≥2 pneumonitis occurred in 2 per arm. It is underpowered and inconclusive. Yaremko BP, Capaldi DPI, Sheikh K et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35550405/
- **NCCN NSCLC Guidelines version 3.2022 (JNCCN).** This selection covers targeted therapy for metastatic NSCLC with actionable alterations. The abstract does not name which recommendation changed, so it is rated high rather than practice-changing. Ettinger DS, Wood DE, Aisner DL et al. J Natl Compr Canc Netw 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35545176/

### sclc
- **CAPSTONE-1: adebrelimab (anti-PD-L1) or placebo plus carboplatin-etoposide first line in ES-SCLC (randomised double-blind phase 3, n=462, China, Lancet Oncol).** OS was 15.3 vs 12.8 months (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.58–0.90; one-sided p=0.0017) at a median follow-up of 13.5 months. Treatment-related serious AEs were 39% vs 28%. It confirms the PD-L1 plus chemotherapy class effect, but the comparator is no longer standard. Wang J, Zhou C, Yao W et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35576956/
- **Durvalumab with concurrent chemoradiotherapy in limited-stage SCLC (single-arm phase II, n=51, Eur J Cancer).** Median PFS was 14.4 months, 24-month PFS 42.0% and 24-month OS 67.8% at 26.6 months' median follow-up. PD-L1 did not predict outcome, and PCI reduced brain-first failure (13.6% vs 42.9%). Randomised validation is needed. Park S, Noh JM, Choi YL et al. Eur J Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35500460/

### lung-repurposed / lung-screening
- **Antibiotics around the start of a TKI in NSCLC and melanoma (retrospective single centre, n=168, ESMO Open).** Antibiotics given from 2 weeks before to 6 weeks after TKI start were independently associated with shorter PFS (HR 1.57, 95% CI 1.05–2.34) and OS (HR 2.19, 1.44–3.32), most strongly at PS ≥2. It is retrospective with residual confounding by illness severity. Tinsley N, Zhou C, Nahm S et al. ESMO Open 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35489288/
- **Household solid fuel, secondhand smoke and lung cancer mortality in never-smokers (China Kadoorie Biobank, n=323,794, 10.2 years, AJRCCM).** Mortality rose 4% per 5 years of solid-fuel exposure (HR 1.04, 95% CI 1.01–1.06), and 40–50 years of exposure gave HR 1.53 (1.13–2.07). Secondhand smoke showed no significant association. Exposure is self-reported. Cheng ES, Chan KH, Weber M et al. Am J Respir Crit Care Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35616543/

### car-t-solid / lung-vaccines-cell / lung-nordic
- **CT041, a claudin18.2 CAR-T, in GI cancers (phase 1 interim, n=37, Nat Med).** ORR was 48.6% overall and 57.1% in gastric cancer, with 6-month OS 81.2% in gastric cancer. All patients had grade ≥3 haematological toxicity, and 94.6% had grade 1–2 CRS, with no grade ≥3 CRS or neurotoxicity. Durability is limited (6-month DoR rate 44.8%), and it is single-arm. Responses were faster and more frequent with ipilimumab (melanoma) than with UV1 alone (lung). These are immunogenicity data only. Ellingsen EB, Aamdal E, Guren T et al. J Immunother Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35613827/

Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35598797/
