### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **EVIDENCE: adjuvant icotinib for 2 years vs 4 cycles of platinum doublet after complete resection of stage II–IIIA EGFR-mutant NSCLC (open-label phase 3, n=322, 29 Chinese centres, Lancet Respir Med).** At a preplanned interim with a median follow-up of 24.9 months, median DFS was 47.0 vs 22.1 months (HR 0.36, 95% CI 0.24–0.55), and 3-year DFS was 63.9% vs 32.5%. OS is immature (HR 0.91, 0.42–1.94). Treatment-related serious AEs were 1% vs 14%. The comparator lacks adjuvant osimertinib, the current standard in many settings (see the ADAURA item below). He J, Su C, Liang W et al. Lancet Respir Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34280355/
- **FDA approval summary: adjuvant osimertinib (CCR, Project Orbis).** The FDA approved osimertinib on 18 December 2020 for resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC with EGFR Ex19del/L858R, based on ADAURA (n=682). DFS HR was 0.20 (95% CI 0.15–0.27), and median DFS was not reached vs 27.5 months. OS was immature at approval. This is a journal summary of an earlier decision; ADAURA results were also posted on ClinicalTrials.gov this run (NCT02511106). Koch AL, Vellanki PJ, Drezner N et al. Clin Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34301748/
- **Amivantamab: First Approval (Drugs profile).** The FDA approved amivantamab, an EGFR×MET bispecific, on 21 May 2021 for EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC progressing on or after platinum. The drug was in pre-registration in the EU and elsewhere. This is a profile, not the decision itself. Syed YY Drugs 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34292533/
- **Atezolizumab, bevacizumab, carboplatin and pemetrexed after EGFR TKI failure (single-arm phase II, n=40, Hong Kong).** 57.5% had progressed on osimertinib. ORR was 62.5%, median PFS 9.4 months and 1-year OS 72.5%. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 37.5%. This is a small single-arm study, with no comparator to chemotherapy alone. Lam TC, Tsang KC, Choi HC et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34303276/
- **Personalised neoantigen peptide vaccination in advanced NSCLC (phase I, 24 vaccinated, J Immunother Cancer).** Median PFS was 6.0 months and OS 8.9 months, and there were 7 objective responses, 1 complete. All 7 responders had EGFR-mutant tumours, 4 of them on concurrent TKI. Vaccine-induced T-cell responses to EGFR L858R/T790M neoantigens were seen. The study is small, early phase and uncontrolled. Li F, Deng L, Jackson KR et al. J Immunother Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34244308/
- **Dacomitinib after first-line osimertinib (phase II pilot, n=12).** ORR was 17% (95% CI 5–45) and median PFS 1.8 months. None of the 3 patients with C797S/G724S responded. The study shows limited benefit. Choudhury NJ, Makhnin A, Tobi YY et al. JCO Precis Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34250398/
- **Erlotinib plus trametinib after EGFR TKI resistance (phase I/II, n=23).** ORR was 4% and median PFS 1.8 months, with diarrhoea and rash in 87%. The combination was not efficacious in unselected patients, though BRAF-fusion cases may be a subset. Luo J, Makhnin A, Tobi Y et al. JCO Precis Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34250388/
- **LUX-Lung 8 final analysis: afatinib vs erlotinib after platinum in squamous NSCLC (phase 3, n=795).** OS was 7.8 vs 6.8 months (HR 0.84, 95% CI 0.73–0.97). ERBB family mutations were more frequent among long-term responders. This is a pre-immunotherapy setting of limited current relevance. Goss GD, Cobo M, Lu S et al. EClinicalMedicine 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34195574/

### nsclc-drivers / lung-ctdna-mrd
- **BFAST ALK-positive cohort: blood-first NGS then alectinib in treatment-naive advanced NSCLC (phase 2, JTO).** Of 2,219 patients screened, blood NGS gave results in 98.6% and identified ALK in 5.4%. In 87 treated, investigator ORR was 87.4% (IRF 92.0%), CNS ORR 91.4% and 12-month PFS 78.4%. This supports a plasma-first genotyping approach; it is single-arm, with short follow-up. Dziadziuszko R, Mok T, Peters S et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34311110/
- **Ensartinib in ROS1-positive NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, n=59, China, JTO).** ORR was 27.0% (95% CI 13.8–44.1), median DoR 4.8 months and median PFS 4.6 months. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 25.4%. Efficacy was limited compared with established ROS1 TKIs. Ai X, Wang Q, Cheng Y et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34265433/
- **IASLC 2021 consensus statement on liquid biopsy for advanced NSCLC (JTO).** It updates the 2018 statement after FDA approval of two ctDNA assays. It addresses 'plasma first' vs 'tissue first' testing and gives evidence-based recommendations on test choice and interpretation. The abstract does not state the specific recommendations. Rolfo C, Mack P, Scagliotti GV et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34246791/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy
- **IMpower150 final OS (phase 3, n=1,202).** Atezolizumab-carboplatin-paclitaxel vs bevacizumab-carboplatin-paclitaxel was not significant (median 19.0 vs 14.7 months, HR 0.84, 95% CI 0.71–1.00). Adding atezolizumab to bevacizumab-chemotherapy kept its OS benefit (19.5 vs 14.7 months, HR 0.80, 0.67–0.95). This is an update of an existing option. Socinski MA, Nishio M, Jotte RM et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34311108/
- **IMpower110 updated OS: atezolizumab vs chemotherapy in PD-L1-selected first-line NSCLC (phase 3).** In the high-or-intermediate PD-L1 group, OS was 19.9 vs 16.1 months (HR 0.87, p=0.31, not significant), so the any-PD-L1 group was not formally tested. The exploratory high-PD-L1 HR was 0.76 (0.54–1.09). This supports use only in high PD-L1. Jassem J, de Marinis F, Giaccone G et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34265434/
- **Lung-MAP S1400I: nivolumab plus ipilimumab vs nivolumab in pretreated, IO-naive squamous NSCLC (phase 3, n=252).** The trial closed for futility. OS HR was 0.87 (95% CI 0.66–1.16), with median 10 vs 11 months. The trial is negative. Gettinger SN, Redman MW, Bazhenova L et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34264316/
- **SAKK 16/14: neoadjuvant cisplatin-docetaxel, then durvalumab, with 1 year of adjuvant durvalumab in resectable IIIA(N2) NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=67, JCO).** 1-year EFS was 73% (90% CI 63–82) vs a historical 48%. MPR was 62% and pCR 18% among those resected, and 93% had R0 resection. Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 88%. This is single-arm and compared against a historical control. Rothschild SI, Zippelius A, Eboulet EI et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34251873/
- **OCOG-ALMERA: metformin added to CRT in stage III NSCLC (randomised phase 2, n=54, stopped early).** 12-month treatment failure was 69.2% vs 42.9%. 1-year PFS was 34.8% vs 63.0% (HR 2.42) and OS 47.4% vs 85.2% (HR 3.80), with more grade ≥3 AEs. Metformin worsened outcomes; the trial is small. Tsakiridis T, Pond GR, Wright J et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34323924/
- **NRG-LU001: metformin added to CRT in stage III NSCLC (randomised phase 2, n=167).** 1-year PFS was 51.3% vs 60.4% (HR 1.15, 95% CI 0.77–1.73) and 1-year OS about 80% in both arms. The trial is negative and, with ALMERA, closes this repurposing question for stage III. Skinner H, Hu C, Tsakiridis T et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34323922/
- **Nimotuzumab added to docetaxel-cisplatin CRT in unresectable stage III squamous NSCLC (single-centre randomised phase 2, n=122).** OS was 24.9 vs 23.5 months (p=0.655) and PFS 12.1 vs 13.7 months. Brain metastasis risk was lower (sHR 0.099), an exploratory finding. The trial is negative on survival. Qiu B, Wang D, Li Q et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34229051/
- **CHEST 2021 guideline: Screening for Lung Cancer (Chest).** A systematic review of 75 additional studies yielded 7 graded recommendations and 9 consensus statements on eligibility, imaging quality, management of findings and smoking cessation. The abstract does not give the eligibility criteria, so it cannot be said here what changed. Mazzone PJ, Silvestri GA, Souter LH et al. Chest 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34270968/
- **Chinese Medical Association Oncology Society guideline for diagnosis and treatment of lung cancer (2021).** It is a multidisciplinary guideline from screening to follow-up. The abstract does not name changed recommendations. Oncology Society of Chinese Medical Association, Chinese Medical Association Publishing House Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34289551/

### lung-nonpharm / lung-nordic / thoracic-other
- **Postdiagnosis smoking cessation and outcomes in early-stage NSCLC (prospective cohort, n=517 current smokers, Moscow, Ann Intern Med).** Over an average of 7 years, quitting was associated with longer median OS (6.6 vs 4.8 years). It was also associated with lower all-cause mortality (HR 0.67, 95% CI 0.53–0.85), cancer-specific mortality (HR 0.75) and progression (HR 0.70). Smoking status was self-reported, and this is observational. Sheikh M, Mukeriya A, Shangina O et al. Ann Intern Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34310171/
- **Pleural and peritoneal mesothelioma in Norway, 2000–2019 (Cancer Registry of Norway, n=1,509, Acta Oncol).** Male pleural incidence fell from 1.7 to 1.1 per 100,000. Median survival was 15.8 months for epithelioid vs 5.4 months for sarcomatoid pleural disease, and 43.3 vs 5.1 months for peritoneal disease. These are national pre-immunotherapy baseline data for Norway. Brustugun OT, Nilssen Y, Eide IJZ Acta Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34313510/
- **Atezolizumab plus bevacizumab in pretreated advanced peritoneal mesothelioma (phase 2, n=20, Cancer Discov).** ORR was 40% (95% CI 19.1–64.0) with median DoR 12.8 months. 1-year PFS was 61% and OS 85%. Responses occurred regardless of TMB or PD-L1, and EMT expression was associated with resistance. The study is small and single-arm. Raghav K, Liu S, Overman MJ et al. Cancer Discov 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34261675/

### lung-vaccines-cell / car-t-solid
- **SLU01: DCVAC/LuCa (autologous dendritic cell vaccine) plus carboplatin-paclitaxel vs chemotherapy in stage IV NSCLC (randomised phase I/II, n=112 across 3 arms).** mITT median OS was 15.5 vs 11.8 months (HR 0.54, 95% CI 0.32–0.91). Neutropenia and fatigue were more frequent. The trial is small, uses an mITT population and a chemotherapy-only comparator, and one arm was discontinued. Zemanova M, Cernovska M, Havel L et al. Cancer Treat Res Commun 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34284344/
- **Intrapleural mesothelin CAR-T, then pembrolizumab, in malignant pleural disease (phase I, n=27, 25 MPM, Cancer Discov).** Doses of 0.3M–60M cells/kg were safe, and CAR-T persisted >100 days in 39%. In 18 MPM patients who also received pembrolizumab, median OS was 23.9 months and 1-year OS 83%. Two had complete metabolic responses. The trial is early phase and uncontrolled. Adusumilli PS, Zauderer MG, Rivière I et al. Cancer Discov 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34266984/
- **IL-7/CCL19-secreting ('7×19') CAR-T targeting GPC3 or mesothelin (J Hematol Oncol).** The armoured CAR-T outperformed conventional CAR-T in xenografts. In the phase 1 (NCT03198546), one HCC patient had complete tumour disappearance after intratumoural injection and one pancreatic cancer patient a near-complete response after IV infusion. These are case-level clinical data. Pang N, Shi J, Qin L et al. J Hematol Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34325726/
- **BrainChild-01: repeated locoregional HER2 CAR-T in children and young adults with CNS tumours (phase 1 interim, n=3, Nat Med).** There was no dose-limiting toxicity, and there was evidence of local CNS immune activation (CXCL10, CCL2). Efficacy is not yet assessable. Vitanza NA, Johnson AJ, Wilson AL et al. Nat Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34253928/
- **First-in-human EphA2 CAR-T in recurrent glioblastoma (n=3 at the starting dose, intravenous).** Grade 2 CRS with pulmonary oedema occurred in 2 patients. There was 1 SD and 2 PD, with OS of 86–181 days. Activity is minimal at this dose. Lin Q, Ba T, Ho J et al. Front Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34235085/
