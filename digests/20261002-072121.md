### egfr-nsclc

- **Induction and concurrent aumolertinib with radiotherapy for EGFR-mutated stage III NSCLC: results of phase III ADVANCE trial and real-world validation.** Phase III ADVANCE trial in unresectable stage III EGFR-mutant NSCLC compared induction plus concurrent aumolertinib with radiotherapy against concurrent chemoradiotherapy. Only 43 patients were randomised (24 vs 19) before early termination for loss of equipoise and slow accrual; median PFS was 34.0 vs 7.4 months (HR 0.15) and OS was not reached vs 30.5 months (HR 0.32, P = 0.09), with a real-world cohort (n = 125) supporting the direction. Chemo-free TKI + RT strategy is a candidate for this setting, but the trial is small, open-label, stopped early and OS is not significant.  
  Bi N, Wang J, Jiang W et al., Signal Transduct Target Ther, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42764281/

- **Evaluation of MET Detection Methods and Cutoffs in EGFR-Mutated Non-small Cell Lung Cancer Following Disease Progression on Osimertinib in the phase II SAVANNAH study.** SAVANNAH biomarker analysis defined MET cutoffs for savolitinib plus osimertinib after osimertinib progression: confirmed ORR was 49% with MET IHC3+/≥90% and/or FISH10+ vs 9% without, with 34% of patients meeting that cutoff (62% for the looser IHC3+/≥50% and/or FISH5+). These cutoffs will define eligibility in the phase III SAFFRON study. Limitation: single-arm phase II data, post-hoc cutoff derivation, no randomised comparison.  
  Ahn MJ, de Marinis F, Cho BC et al., Clin Cancer Res, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42752799/

- **Adjuvant Osimertinib in Resected EGFR-Mutated Stage IB-IIIA Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: Exploratory 8-year Overall Survival Landmark Update from the ADAURA Trial [161/175].** Exploratory 8-year overall survival update of the ADAURA phase III trial of adjuvant osimertinib vs placebo. In stage II-IIIA, OS HR 0.53 (95% CI 0.38-0.75), 8-year OS 74% vs 58%; in stage IB-IIIA HR 0.52, 8-year OS 79% vs 64%; HR by Ex19del 0.45 vs L858R 0.72 (95% CI 0.46-1.11). Strengthens the established adjuvant benefit; limitations are the exploratory nature of the late analysis and subgroup HRs with wide intervals.  
  Wu YL, Majem M, John T et al., J Thorac Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42732874/

- **Long-term safety of first-line osimertinib plus platinum-pemetrexed in EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC: FLAURA2.** FLAURA2 safety analysis by treatment period (induction, pemetrexed maintenance, osimertinib-only). Grade ≥3 AE onset frequency fell over successive periods, renal AEs were more frequent during maintenance (19%, all grade 1/2 except one grade 3 in induction), and AEs leading to osimertinib discontinuation were ≤8% per period. Informs tolerability of the intensified first-line regimen; descriptive, post hoc, and only among patients who remained on treatment.  
  Planchard D, Jänne PA, Yang JC et al., Lung Cancer, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42728193/

- **Clinical utility of molecular residual disease detection in early-stage resected EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer.** Tumour-informed MRD (MinerVa Prime) analysis in 175 patients from a randomised trial of adjuvant icotinib vs chemotherapy in resected stage II-IIIA EGFR-mutant NSCLC (1,352 plasma samples). MRD positivity at landmark predicted inferior DFS (HR 4.44), longitudinal MRD HR 7.82, NPV 91.3% with median lead time 169 days before clinical recurrence; icotinib improved DFS over chemotherapy in both MRD subgroups. Supports ctDNA-guided adjuvant strategies, but is a retrospective biomarker analysis in one Chinese population and does not test MRD-driven treatment change.  
  Zhou F, Su C, Liang W et al., Nat Commun, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42706268/

### nsclc-resistance-nextgen

- **Systemic and intracranial activity of antibody-drug conjugates in patients with non-small cell lung cancer and brain metastases: A systematic review and meta-analysis.** Meta-analysis of 13 studies (3,191 patients, 905 with brain metastases) of ADCs in NSCLC: pooled systemic ORR 40%, intracranial ORR 33% (IC-DCR 78%), HER2-directed ADCs highest (systemic ORR 54%, IC-ORR 44%), with no significant difference in systemic ORR by brain-metastasis status. Supports CNS activity of ADCs; pooled largely single-arm, heterogeneous studies and the abstract gives no per-drug survival data.  
  Zhou S, Tan J, Patel V et al., Lung Cancer, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42815252/

### lung-top-journals

- **Tambotatug Pelitecan in Small-Cell Lung Cancer after Platinum-Based Therapy.** Phase 3 TAISHAN-302 randomised 451 patients with SCLC relapsed after platinum to the B7-H3 ADC tambotatug pelitecan or topotecan. At the prespecified interim analysis OS was 13.3 vs 9.4 months (HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.35-0.62), PFS 7.4 vs 2.8 months (HR 0.29), ORR 59.1% vs 9.7%, and grade ≥3 AEs 55.4% vs 77.9%. A strong second-line SCLC result; open-label, interim analysis, and the control is topotecan rather than tarlatamab or lurbinectedin.  
  Zhao Y, Liu H, Meng X et al., N Engl J Med, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42734213/

- **Final Biomarker and Efficacy Analyses of Lorlatinib in Patients With ALK-Positive Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer in a Phase 2 Study.** Final 5-year analysis of the phase 2 lorlatinib study in ALK-positive NSCLC (treatment-naive and previously treated cohorts). Activity and prolonged OS were maintained regardless of ALK fusion subtype or co-mutation, TP53 mutation was associated with shorter OS, and acquired ALK mutations were not seen in treatment-naive patients at progression. Useful resistance/biomarker data for a non-EGFR driver; single-arm phase 2 with exploratory subgroup analyses.  
  Bauer TM, Martini JF, Besse B et al., J Thorac Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42749050/

- **Oral KRAS G12D inhibitor GFH375 for previously treated advanced solid tumors with KRASG12D mutations: a phase 1 trial.** Phase 1 trial of the oral KRAS G12D inhibitor GFH375 in previously treated KRAS G12D solid tumours. Confirmed ORR was 35.7% (5/14) in NSCLC (median PFS 5.5, OS 13.4 months) and 35.1% (13/37) in pancreatic cancer; treatment-related adverse events occurred in 36.5% of 74 patients. Encouraging for a new lung driver subgroup, but the NSCLC cohort is only 14 patients and the phase 2 expansion is ongoing.  
  Ai X, Song Z, Wu L et al., Nat Med, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42745055/

- **Patient-reported symptom monitoring in patients with lung cancer (SYMPRO-Lung trial): long-term, post-hoc survival results of a multicentre, stepped-wedged, cluster-randomised clinical trial.** Post-hoc long-term survival analysis (up to 5 years) of the stepped-wedge cluster-randomised SYMPRO-Lung trial (14 Dutch centres) of weekly patient-reported symptom monitoring in stage I-IV lung cancer. Median OS was 5 months longer with monitoring, with the authors reporting a meaningful long-term OS improvement; adjusted PFS showed no significant difference (HR 0.86, 0.70-1.07). Relevant to non-drug care; limitations are that survival was not the primary outcome (post hoc), stepped-wedge design and possible time-period confounding.  
  Hassing MJ, Billingy NE, van den Hurk CJG et al., Lancet Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42727592/

- **Induction and consolidation atezolizumab with stereotactic body radiation therapy versus radiation alone in high-risk, early-stage non-small-cell lung cancer (SWOG/NRG S1914): a multicentre, open-label, superiority, phase 3, randomised controlled trial.** Phase 3 SWOG/NRG trial of atezolizumab with SBRT vs SBRT alone in 402 evaluable patients with higher-risk inoperable early-stage NSCLC. The trial closed for futility at first interim analysis: OS HR 1.04 (95% CI 0.69-1.58), 2-year OS 82% in both arms, grade ≥3 AEs 12% vs 3% with two grade 5 respiratory events. Negative result that argues against adding atezolizumab to SBRT here; open-label, with limited follow-up.  
  Daly ME, Simone CB, Redman MW et al., Lancet, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42702214/

- **Daraxonrasib for Previously Treated RAS-Mutant Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer.** Phase 1 (RMC-6236-001) results of the pan-RAS inhibitor daraxonrasib in previously treated RAS-mutant NSCLC at doses ≤300 mg: ORR 31-37% across dose levels, grade ≥3 AEs in 54% (pneumonia 10%, diarrhoea 9%, rash 8%), four grade 5 events. Shows activity in a hard-to-treat population; early-phase, non-randomised, with high toxicity and no comparator.  
  Arbour KC, Punekar S, Luo J et al., N Engl J Med, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42685317/
