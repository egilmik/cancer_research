### egfr-nsclc / lung-local-therapy / lung-key-evidence
- **Effectiveness of local ablative therapy combined with first-line tyrosine kinase inhibitors in epidermal growth factor receptor-mutated oligometastatic non-small cell lung cancer: a systematic review and meta-analysis** Pooled 9 studies (n=1077; 525 TKI + local ablative therapy) in EGFR-mutant oligometastatic NSCLC, mean follow-up 29.3 months: OS HR 0.50 (95% CI 0.42–0.59) and PFS HR 0.44 (0.34–0.58) favouring TKI + LAT, with no statistically significant excess toxicity. Supports local consolidation alongside first-line TKI as a hypothesis, but included studies are largely non-randomised, so selection and immortal-time bias likely inflate the effect. Bisht R, Desai A, Bhardwaj A et al., Clin Exp Metastasis, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42891869/

### sclc / lung-key-evidence
- **Rethinking the Platinum Standard in SCLC: A Meta-Analysis of Limited- and Extensive-Stage Disease in the Immunotherapy Era** Meta-analysis of 7 first-line chemoimmunotherapy RCTs (n=3059) by platinum backbone. In extensive-stage SCLC (n=1843) the pooled OS HR for immunotherapy was 0.71 (0.57–0.89) with cisplatin and 0.76 (0.68–0.85) with carboplatin, i.e. comparable. In limited-stage disease (n=1216) immunotherapy improved OS (HR 0.71, 0.54–0.92) and PFS (HR 0.70, 0.56–0.87) in carboplatin-treated patients; the cisplatin result was truncated in the abstract extract. Relevant to platinum choice, but the comparison is indirect across trials. Meirson T, Ditzian Kugler H, Hanovich E et al., JTO Clin Res Rep, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42893056/
