### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **IMPACT (WJOG6410L): adjuvant gefitinib vs cisplatin-vinorelbine in resected EGFR-mutant stage II–IIIA NSCLC (phase III, n=234, Japan, JCO).** Median DFS was 35.9 vs 25.1 months, but the curves crossed around 4 years (HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.67–1.28). OS did not differ (HR 1.03; 5-year OS 78.0% vs 74.6%). There were no treatment-related deaths with gefitinib vs 3 with chemotherapy. The trial is negative, it was not a non-inferiority design, and the comparator predates adjuvant osimertinib. Tada H, Mitsudomi T, Misumi T et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34726958/
- **ADAURA: postoperative chemotherapy use and outcomes (exploratory, n=682, JTO).** 60% received adjuvant chemotherapy, more often if <70 years, stage II–IIIA or in Asia. The osimertinib DFS benefit held with chemotherapy (HR 0.16, 95% CI 0.10–0.26) and without it (HR 0.23, 0.13–0.40), regardless of stage. It is a non-randomised comparison of subgroups. Wu YL, John T, Grohe C et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34740861/
- **ETOP 10-16 BOOSTER: osimertinib plus bevacizumab vs osimertinib in EGFR T790M-positive NSCLC (randomised phase II, n=155, Ann Oncol).** At a median follow-up of 33.8 months, PFS was 15.4 vs 12.3 months (HR 0.96, 95% CI 0.68–1.37) and OS 24.0 vs 24.3 months (HR 1.03). An exploratory interaction with smoking history was reported. The trial is negative. Soo RA, Han JY, Dafni U et al. Ann Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34839016/
- **APOLLO updated results: aumolertinib in EGFR T790M-positive NSCLC after first/second-generation TKI (registrational phase 2, n=244, JTO).** Central ORR was 68.9% (95% CI 62.6–74.6), median DoR 15.1 months and PFS 12.4 months. CNS ORR was 60.9% in 23 patients. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 16.4%, mainly CPK elevation. It is single-arm; this is the basis of the NMPA approval mentioned in the title. Lu S, Wang Q, Zhang G et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34801749/
- **Abivertinib in EGFR T790M-positive NSCLC after prior EGFR inhibitors (phase I/II, n=227 at RP2D 300 mg twice daily, CCR).** ORR was 52.2% (95% CI 45.2–59.1), median DoR 8.5 months, PFS 7.5 months and OS 24.9 months. Treatment-related serious AEs occurred in 13.7%. It is single-arm. Zhou Q, Wu L, Hu P et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34740925/
- **Mefatinib, a second-generation pan-EGFR inhibitor, first line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (phase Ib/II, n=106, STTT).** ORR was 84.9%, median PFS 15.4 months (primary endpoint) and OS 31.6 months. In brain metastases (n=31) ORR was 87.1% and PFS 12.8 months. Skin and GI toxicities predominated. It is single-arm, with no osimertinib comparator. Wang P, Li Y, Lv D et al. Signal Transduct Target Ther 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34719670/
- **Osimertinib 80 mg in EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC (phase I/II, 12 evaluable, Japan, Lung Cancer).** There were no objective responses (7 SD, 5 PD), median PFS was 3.8 months and OS 15.8 months. Exploratory PK suggested higher doses might help some insertion types. Activity at the standard dose is limited. Yasuda H, Ichihara E, Sakakibara-Konishi J et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34808485/

### nsclc-drivers
- **ZENITH20-2: poziotinib in previously treated HER2 exon 20 insertion NSCLC (phase 2, n=90, JCO).** ORR was 27.8% (95% CI 18.9–38.2), DCR 70.0%, PFS 5.5 months and DoR 5.1 months. Grade ≥3 rash occurred in 48.9%, diarrhoea in 25.6% and stomatitis in 24.4%, and 76.7% needed dose reduction. Activity is modest and toxicity substantial. Le X, Cornelissen R, Garassino M et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34843401/
- **Pyrotinib in HER2-amplified advanced NSCLC (prospective single-arm, n=27, China, CCR).** The 6-month PFS rate was 51.9%, median PFS 6.3 months, OS 12.5 months and ORR 22.2%. 30.8% of those progressing on EGFR-TKI responded. Grade 3 TRAEs occurred in 22.2%, mainly diarrhoea. It is small. Song Z, Lv D, Chen SQ et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34753778/
- **VISION subgroups: tepotinib in METex14 NSCLC (phase II, n=152 efficacy, CCR).** ORR was 44.7% overall, 48.8% under 75 and 39.7% at 75 or over. It was 44.9% treatment-naive and 44.6% pretreated. 13/15 with brain metastases had intracranial disease control, and grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 25.1%. These are single-arm subgroup analyses. Le X, Sakai H, Felip E et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34789481/
- **Acquired resistance to sotorasib (paired biopsies from 43 patients, Nature).** Treatment-emergent alterations appeared in 27 patients (KRAS, NRAS, BRAF, EGFR, FGFR2, MYC and others), often subclonal. In models, targeting ERK signalling intermediates enhanced G12C inhibition when RAS/BRAF mutations were acquired. It is translational and not yet tested clinically. Zhao Y, Murciano-Goroff YR, Xue JY et al. Nature 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34759319/
- **CLIP1-LTK fusion as a new oncogenic driver in NSCLC (LC-SCRUM-Asia, Nature).** The fusion was found in 0.4% of NSCLCs, mutually exclusive with other drivers, and was constitutively active and transforming. Lorlatinib inhibited it in vitro, and one patient responded well to lorlatinib. Clinical evidence is a single case. Izumi H, Matsumoto S, Liu J et al. Nature 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34819663/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy
- **CCTG BR34: durvalumab-tremelimumab with or without platinum chemotherapy first line in metastatic NSCLC (randomised phase 2, n=301, JTO).** OS was 16.6 vs 14.1 months (HR 0.88, 90% CI 0.67–1.16; p=0.46), PFS 7.7 vs 3.2 months (HR 0.67) and ORR 42.4% vs 29.3%. Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 82% vs 70%. Adding chemotherapy did not improve survival. Leighl NB, Laurie SA, Goss GD et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34800700/
- **Vibostolimab (anti-TIGIT) alone or with pembrolizumab (first-in-human phase 1, Ann Oncol).** In PD-(L)1-naive NSCLC (n=39, combination) ORR was 26%, with responses regardless of PD-L1. In PD-(L)1-refractory NSCLC, activity was minimal with monotherapy and combination. Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 9–17% in part A. It is early phase. Niu J, Maurice-Dror C, Lee DH et al. Ann Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34800678/
- **INTEPI: stopping ICI after ≥18 months of disease control in advanced NSCLC (multicentre retrospective, France, CII).** Of 107 patients controlled at ≥18 months, 54 stopped (median treatment 26 months). From discontinuation, 24-month OS was 84% and PFS 63%. 12-month PFS was 76% after CR/PR vs 22% after SD. It is small and retrospective but informs treatment-duration decisions. Bilger G, Girard N, Doubre H et al. Cancer Immunol Immunother 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34821950/
- **AEGEAN design: phase III double-blind neoadjuvant durvalumab plus chemotherapy, then adjuvant durvalumab, vs placebo in resectable stage II–III NSCLC (about 800 patients, Clin Lung Cancer).** It is a design paper with no results. Heymach JV, Mitsudomi T, Harpole D et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34819266/
- **JCOG2007 NIPPON design: phase III of platinum chemotherapy plus pembrolizumab vs plus nivolumab-ipilimumab first line (n=422 planned, Japan, Clin Lung Cancer).** The primary endpoint is OS. It is a design paper with no results. Shiraishi Y, Hakozaki T, Nomura S et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34802879/
- **NIRVANA-Lung design: phase 3 adding multisite RT to first-line pembrolizumab-chemotherapy (n=460, France, Clin Lung Cancer).** The primary endpoint is 1-year OS. It is a design paper with no results. Doyen J, Besse B, Texier M et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34810130/
- **4DCT-ventilation functional avoidance thoracic RT (multi-institutional phase 2, n=67, IJROBP).** Grade ≥2 radiation pneumonitis occurred in 14.9% (upper 95% CI 24.0%), meeting phase 2 criteria. The mean reduction in functional lung V20 was 3.5%. It is single-arm with short follow-up (median 312 days). Vinogradskiy Y, Castillo R, Castillo E et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34767934/

### lung-screening / lung-nonpharm
- **UKLS: single LDCT screen vs usual care (randomised, n=4,055, UK) and international meta-analysis (Lancet Reg Health Eur).** At a median follow-up of 7.3 years, lung cancer deaths were 30 vs 46 (RR 0.65, 95% CI 0.41–1.02; p=0.062). The meta-analysis of 9 RCTs gave a pooled RR of 0.84 (0.76–0.92). UKLS alone is underpowered and not significant, and the pooled result is a meta-analysis. Field JK, Vulkan D, Davies MPA et al. Lancet Reg Health Eur 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34806061/
- **Duration of preoperative smoking cessation and complications after surgery for stage I NSCLC (retrospective VA cohort, n=9,509, Ann Surg).** Of 6,168 smoking at diagnosis, only 10.7% quit before surgery. Each additional week of cessation lowered the odds of major complication or death (aOR 0.919 per week). Quitting ≥3 weeks before surgery gave odds similar to never-smokers, while quitting within 3 weeks did not (aOR 1.70, 95% CI 1.20–2.40). It is observational. Heiden BT, Eaton DB, Chang SH et al. Ann Surg 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34793352/

### lung-ctdna-mrd
- **ctDNA for dynamic recurrence risk and adjuvant chemotherapy benefit after NSCLC resection (cohort, Nat Commun).** Postsurgical and post-adjuvant ctDNA positivity predicted worse RFS. In stage II–III, ctDNA-positive patients benefited from adjuvant chemotherapy, while ctDNA-negative patients had low relapse risk with or without it. ctDNA preceded radiological recurrence by a median of 88 days. It is observational, without randomised de-escalation. Qiu B, Guo W, Zhang F et al. Nat Commun 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34799585/
- **Clinical validation of Guardant360 CDx as a plasma companion diagnostic for sotorasib (CodeBreaK, Lung Cancer).** Against tissue PCR in 189 patients, positive percent agreement was 0.71 and negative 1.00. ORR was similar whether patients were selected by plasma or tissue. The authors say plasma testing should be augmented by tissue testing as described on the product label. Bauml JM, Li BT, Velcheti V et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34838325/

### car-t-solid
- **4SCAR-GD2 CAR-T in refractory/recurrent paediatric neuroblastoma (phase I, 10 evaluable, J Cancer Res Clin Oncol).** All had PD before infusion. 6/10 had SD at 6 months and 4 remained SD at 1 year, alive at 3–4 years. Median OS was 25 months and PFS 8 months. CRS was grade 1–2 and haematological toxicity was mainly from lymphodepletion. No objective responses were reported, and it is small. Yu L, Huang L, Lin D et al. J Cancer Res Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34724115/

### thoracic-other
- **PrE0505: durvalumab plus platinum-pemetrexed first line in unresectable pleural mesothelioma (phase 2, n=55, Nat Med).** The primary endpoint was met, with median OS 20.4 months vs 12.1 months for historical cisplatin-pemetrexed controls. Immune-related AEs were all grade ≤2. Higher immunogenic mutation burden, a diverse T-cell repertoire and germline DNA-repair alterations were linked to longer survival. The comparison is with historical controls. Forde PM, Anagnostou V, Sun Z et al. Nat Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34750557/
