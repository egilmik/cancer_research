## Practice-changing

### ADAURA: adjuvant osimertinib in resected EGFR-mutant NSCLC (NEJM)
This double-blind phase 3 trial randomised 682 patients after complete resection of stage IB–IIIA EGFR-mutant (ex19del/L858R) NSCLC to osimertinib 80 mg daily or placebo for 3 years. The primary endpoint was investigator-assessed DFS in stage II–IIIA. At 24 months, 90% vs 44% were alive and disease-free (HR 0.17, 99.06% CI 0.11–0.26; P<0.001). In the whole IB–IIIA population the figures were 89% vs 52% (HR 0.20, 99.12% CI 0.14–0.30). CNS disease-free survival at 24 months was 98% vs 85% (HR 0.18). OS was immature (29 deaths: 9 vs 20), so whether the DFS gain becomes a cure benefit or delays recurrence was still open at this report. No new safety concerns were reported. This report does not settle the open questions: the 3-year duration, use in stage IB, cost, and Norwegian reimbursement through Nye Metoder. It is the first adjuvant targeted-therapy standard in NSCLC and makes EGFR testing of resected non-squamous tumours necessary. Wu YL, Tsuboi M, He J et al. *N Engl J Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32955177/

### Carboplatin-etoposide rechallenge vs topotecan in sensitive relapsed SCLC (phase 3, Lancet Oncol)
This open-label French phase 3 trial (GFPC) randomised 164 patients whose SCLC had responded to first-line platinum-etoposide and relapsed at least 90 days after its completion. Patients received six cycles of carboplatin-etoposide or oral topotecan, which the authors describe as the only drug approved in Europe for second line. Centrally reviewed PFS, the primary endpoint, was 4.7 vs 2.7 months (stratified HR 0.57, 90% CI 0.41–0.73; p=0.0041), with median follow-up 22.7 months. Grade 3–4 neutropenia (14% vs 22%) and febrile neutropenia (6% vs 11%) were less frequent with the doublet. There were two treatment-related deaths with topotecan and none with the doublet. The abstract reports no OS benefit, so the gain is in PFS and tolerability rather than survival. The trial was done before chemo-immunotherapy became first-line standard, so applicability to patients relapsing after platinum-etoposide plus a PD-L1 inhibitor is uncertain. Baize N, Monnet I, Greillier L et al. *Lancet Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32888454/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-drivers / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **RELAY East Asian subset: ramucirumab + erlotinib vs placebo + erlotinib (phase 3, prespecified, n=336).** PFS was 19.4 vs 12.5 months (HR 0.64, 0.49–0.83), consistent with the global trial. Grade ≥3 AEs were more frequent (70.7% vs 49.4%), and OS was immature. The comparator, erlotinib, is no longer the first-line standard since osimertinib. Nishio M, Seto T, Reck M et al. *Cancer Sci*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32954593/
- **Almonertinib first-in-human phase 1 (n=120) in EGFR-TKI-pretreated NSCLC.** In 94 T790M-positive patients in dose expansion, ORR was 52% (95% CI 42–63) and median PFS 11.0 months. Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs were mainly CPK rise (10%). It establishes a third-generation EGFR TKI later used widely in China. The trial is single-arm with no comparator. Yang JC, Camidge DR, Yang CT et al. *J Thorac Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32916310/
- **GEOMETRY mono-1: capmatinib in MET-dysregulated NSCLC (phase 2, NEJM, n=364).** In MET exon 14 skipping, ORR was 41% (29–53) in pretreated patients (n=69) and 68% (48–84) in untreated patients (n=28), with median DoR 9.7 and 12.6 months. MET amplification responded only with gene copy number ≥10 (29% pretreated, 40% untreated). Peripheral oedema (51%) and nausea (45%) were common but mostly grade 1–2. The design is single-arm and cohorts are small, especially untreated exon 14. Wolf J, Seto T, Han JY et al. *N Engl J Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32877583/
- **CodeBreaK 100 phase 1: sotorasib in KRAS G12C solid tumours (NEJM, n=129).** In 59 heavily pretreated NSCLC patients, confirmed ORR was 32.2%, DCR 88.1% and median PFS 6.3 months. Colorectal cancer responses were 7.1%. There were no dose-limiting toxicities, and grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 11.6%. It is the first clinical proof that KRAS G12C can be targeted. It is a phase 1 trial with small cohorts. Hong DS, Fakih MG, Strickler JH et al. *N Engl J Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32955176/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals
- **NADIM: neoadjuvant carboplatin-paclitaxel + nivolumab then 1 year of adjuvant nivolumab in resectable stage IIIA NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, Spain, n=46).** At 24 months, PFS was 77.1% (95% CI 59.9–87.7), and 35 of 41 resected patients were progression-free. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 30% and did not delay surgery. It is a key forerunner of perioperative chemo-immunotherapy. It is single-arm and small. Provencio M, Nadal E, Insa A et al. *Lancet Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32979984/
- **CheckMate 153: continuous vs 1-year fixed-duration nivolumab in pretreated NSCLC (phase 3b/4, exploratory randomisation, n=252).** In patients without progression, PFS was 24.7 vs 9.4 months (HR 0.56, 0.37–0.84). OS from randomisation favoured continuing (ITT HR 0.62, 0.42–0.92). It argues against stopping PD-1 therapy at 1 year in this setting. It is an exploratory analysis of a community study with small arms. Waterhouse DM, Garon EB, Chandler J et al. *J Clin Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32910710/
- **Pembrolizumab + chemotherapy in PD-L1-negative NSCLC: pooled KEYNOTE-021G/189/407 (n=444).** OS HR was 0.63 (0.50–0.79) and PFS HR 0.68 (0.56–0.83) vs chemotherapy. This supports chemo-immunotherapy regardless of PD-L1. It is a descriptive, exploratory pooled analysis with no α assigned. Borghaei H, Langer CJ, Paz-Ares L et al. *Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32914866/
- **IFCT-1201 MODEL: switch maintenance vs observation after carboplatin-weekly paclitaxel in patients aged 70–89 (phase 3, n=632, 328 randomised).** OS, the primary endpoint, was not improved (14.0 vs 14.1 months; p=0.72), although PFS was 5.7 vs 2.7 months. The trial is negative, and the authors advise against switch maintenance in elderly patients. It was done in the pre-immunotherapy era. Quoix E, Audigier-Valette C, Lavolé A et al. *Eur J Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32898792/
- **KEYNOTE-158 prospective TMB analysis: pembrolizumab in ten advanced solid tumour types (phase 2, Lancet Oncol).** ORR was 29% (21–39) in tTMB-high tumours (≥10 mut/Mb, n=102) vs 6% (5–8) in non-high tumours (n=688). Cohorts included SCLC and mesothelioma. It is the basis for tumour-agnostic TMB-high use of pembrolizumab. It is non-randomised, uses one assay and a single cut-off, and includes few lung cases. Marabelle A, Fakih M, Lopez J et al. *Lancet Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32919526/

### sclc
- **CASPIAN patient-reported outcomes: durvalumab + platinum-etoposide vs platinum-etoposide in ES-SCLC (phase 3).** Time to deterioration was longer with durvalumab for all key symptoms (for example appetite loss HR 0.70, 0.54–0.90; chest pain 0.76) and for functioning and global QoL. It shows the OS gain does not come at a QoL cost. PROs were a secondary endpoint in an open-label design. Goldman JW, Garassino MC, Chen Y et al. *Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32961445/
- **Pembrolizumab with concurrent chemoradiotherapy in LS-SCLC (phase 1/2, single centre, n=40).** RT was 45 Gy twice daily. Median PFS was 19.7 months and median OS 39.5 months at 23.1 months of follow-up. Pneumonitis occurred in 15% (grade 3 in 3 patients), with no grade 5 events. This was hypothesis-generating at the time for randomised trials of consolidation immunotherapy in LS-SCLC. It is single-arm and small. Welsh JW, Heymach JV, Guo C et al. *J Thorac Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32916308/

### lung-screening
- **LUSI (German Lung Screening Intervention Trial): overdiagnosis estimate (RCT, n=4,052, 9.8 years of follow-up).** There were 90 vs 74 lung cancers. The excess in the screening arm was 25.4% (95% CI −11.3 to 64.3) of screen-detected cancers, driven mostly by adenocarcinoma. Modelling suggests that 33% of screen-detected tumours have a lead time of at least 6 years. Overdiagnosis is therefore substantial in people with short life expectancy, which bears on upper age limits. The trial is small, and the confidence interval crosses zero. González Maldonado S, Motsch E, Trotter A et al. *Int J Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32930386/
- **CXR-LC: deep learning on routine chest radiographs to select smokers for CT screening (Ann Intern Med).** The model was developed in PLCO (n=41,856) and validated in PLCO and NLST. Its AUC for incident lung cancer was 0.755 vs 0.634 for CMS eligibility, similar to PLCOm2012 (0.751), and it missed 30.7% fewer cancers at equal screening volume. It uses only data available in the electronic record. It was validated in trial populations, not in clinical practice. Lu MT, Raghu VK, Mayrhofer T et al. *Ann Intern Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32866413/
