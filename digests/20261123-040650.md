## Practice-changing

**FDA approval of zidesamtinib (Jideytro, Nuvalent) for ROS1-positive NSCLC.** On 22 July 2026 the FDA approved zidesamtinib for adults with locally advanced or metastatic ROS1-positive NSCLC who have received at least one prior ROS1 TKI, ahead of the 18 September goal date. The approval rests on the single-arm ARROS-1 trial: 117 patients in the efficacy population, ORR 44%, and 82% of responders with responses lasting at least 6 months (69% at least 12 months). There is no comparator and no PFS or OS data in what I saw. Dose is 100 mg orally once daily. The drug is described as CNS-penetrant. I did not see a toxicity table, so the main safety trade-off is not characterised here. Still open: approval or reimbursement in Norway and EU, and comparison with other ROS1 TKIs. This is a regulatory decision first surfaced by this monitor on this run, not a new event this week. WCLC 2026 coverage (secondary) reported a 94% ORR for zidesamtinib in ARROS-1 in a different population, so the figures should be checked against the label.
Source: FDA oncology approval notifications, https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-zidesamtinib-ros1-positive-non-small-cell-lung-cancer (the stored item URL is the generic notifications page).
