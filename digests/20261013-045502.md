### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **Tislelizumab plus chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone as first-line treatment for advanced squamous non-small-cell lung cancer: final analysis of the randomized, phase III RATIONALE-307 trial.** RATIONALE-307 final analysis (phase 3, n=360, first-line squamous NSCLC): tislelizumab plus paclitaxel or nab-paclitaxel and carboplatin kept PFS benefit vs chemotherapy (HR 0.45 and 0.43); OS HRs 0.68 (0.46-1.01) and 0.75 (0.50-1.12), not significant; Chinese population, OS secondary and immature at 16.7 months. Lu S, Wang J, Yu Y et al. ESMO Open 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39461773/

### sclc

- **Five-year survival in patients with extensive-stage small cell lung cancer treated with atezolizumab in the Phase III IMpower133 study and the Phase III IMbrella A extension study.** IMpower133 plus IMbrella A extension (exploratory, ES-SCLC, median follow-up 59.4 months): atezolizumab + carboplatin/etoposide gave 3-, 4- and 5-year OS of 16%, 13% and 12%; first 5-year OS data for chemo-immunotherapy in ES-SCLC; single-arm extended follow-up without a comparator, only 18 patients rolled over. Reck M, Dziadziuszko R, Sugawara S et al. Lung Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39306923/
- **Effect of FDG PET-CT for Staging and Radiotherapy Planning - A Comparison of Cohorts From Two Randomized Trials of Thoracic Radiotherapy in Limited-Stage SCLC.** Norwegian HAST and THORA trial cohorts (LS-SCLC, n=149): PET-CT staging with selective nodal irradiation had less grade 3-4 oesophagitis (18% vs 33%) and pneumonitis than CT with elective nodal irradiation; cross-trial comparison, not randomised on this question. Graabak G, Grønberg BH, Killingberg KT et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39286339/

Gouliaev A, Berg J, Khalil AA et al. Eur Clin Respir J 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39391702/
