## High

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **Combined amivantamab, lazertinib, and bevacizumab in EGFR-mutant advanced NSCLC with acquired resistance to a third-generation EGFR TKI (ETOP 18-21 AMAZE-lung)**: single-arm phase 2, 61 patients enrolled (first 60 evaluable for primary endpoint) after osimertinib/lazertinib progression. 12-week ORR 33% (95.6% CI 21–47), rejecting the ≤20% null; 39% had partial response overall; median follow-up only 9.0 months. Grade 3–4 TRAEs 43%, treatment-related VTE 17%, no treatment-related deaths. Chemotherapy-sparing option after third-gen TKI, but limitations: single-arm, no comparator (MARIPOSA-2 chemo arms not tested head-to-head), short follow-up, PFS/OS not reported in the abstract. Soo RA, Popat S, Dafni U et al. Lancet Respir Med 2026. [PubMed 42833236](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42833236/) (DOI 10.1016/s2213-2600(26)00203-1). Funding: ETOP IBCSG Partners Foundation and Janssen. (Companion comment, medium: PMID 42833237.)
- **Comparative profiling of osimertinib and fourth-generation EGFR inhibitors on EGFR-mutant proteins and cell lines**: preclinical biochemical/cellular comparison of BLU-945, JBJ-09-063 and silevertinib vs osimertinib. BLU-945 had the broadest activity across variants with/without T790M/C797S; JBJ-09-063 was most potent in engineered L858R+T790M+C797S H1975 cells and binds independently of osimertinib; silevertinib showed >200-fold mutant selectivity but reduced activity against T790M-containing mutants. In vitro only, no in vivo or clinical data. Melis JJTM, Kevenaar KJW, Ytsma J et al. Front Oncol 2026. [PubMed 42835192](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42835192/)

New trial registrations: none this run.
