### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers

- **Updated overall survival and circulating tumor DNA analysis of ensartinib for crizotinib-refractory ALK-positive NSCLC from a phase II study.** Updated phase II (n=180, median follow-up 53.2 months): ensartinib after crizotinib in ALK-positive NSCLC gave median OS 42.8 months (95% CI 29.3-53.2). Single-arm; the comparator setting (post-crizotinib) is no longer typical where alectinib/lorlatinib are first line. Zheng J, Wang T, Yang Y et al.. Cancer Commun (Lond) 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38421881/
- **Overall survival and central nervous system activity of crizotinib in ROS1-rearranged lung cancer-final results of the EUCROSS trial.** EUCROSS final analysis (single-arm phase II, n=34, median follow-up 81.4 months): crizotinib in ROS1-rearranged lung cancer gave median OS 54.8 months and PFS 19.4 months; co-occurring TP53 aberrations predicted worse outcome. Small; next-generation ROS1 TKIs have better CNS activity. Michels S, Massutí B, Vasyliv I et al.. ESMO Open 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38350336/
- **Repotrectinib: First Approval.** Drug profile of repotrectinib's first approval (FDA, November 2023) for ROS1-positive NSCLC, based on the single-arm TRIDENT-1 trial. Dhillon S. Drugs 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38279972/

### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **First-line durvalumab in patients with PD-L1 positive, advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with a performance status of 2 (PS2). Primary analysis of the multicenter, single-arm phase II trial SAKK 19/17.** SAKK 19/17 single-arm phase II (n=48): first-line durvalumab in PD-L1 >=25% advanced NSCLC with performance status 2 gave 6-month OS 60%, median OS 8.5 months, ORR 17%. Eur J Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38330766/

### sclc

- **Myeloprotection with trilaciclib in Chinese patients with extensive-stage small cell lung cancer receiving chemotherapy: Results from a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase III study (TRACES).** Randomised double-blind phase III bridging study in China (n=83 in Part 2): trilaciclib before chemotherapy in ES-SCLC cut cycle-1 duration of severe neutropenia (mean 0 vs 2 days, P=.0003), with OS 12.0 vs 8.8 months (HR 0.69, 0.40-1.22, not significant). Small, supportive-care endpoint; trilaciclib is not approved in Europe. Cheng Y, Wu L, Huang D et al.. Lung Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38224653/

### lung-screening / lung-nonpharm / lung-nordic

- **ACR Lung-RADS v2022: Assessment Categories and Management Recommendations.** Summary of ACR Lung-RADS v2022 (released November 2022): new guidance for atypical pulmonary cysts, juxtapleural, airway-centred and potentially infectious findings, clarified growth definitions and stepped management for stable or shrinking nodules. A US reporting standard, relevant to any Norwegian screening pilot. Christensen J, Prosper AE, Wu CC et al.. Chest 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38300206/
- **Rates of Downstream Procedures and Complications Associated With Lung Cancer Screening in Routine Clinical Practice : A Retrospective Cohort Study.** Retrospective cohort of 9,266 people screened with LDCT in routine US practice (Ann Intern Med): 15.9% had abnormal baseline scans, PPV 9.5%, sensitivity 92.7%. Complications after invasive procedures were much more common than in NLST (any 30.6% vs 17.7%; major 20.6% vs 9.4%). Outcomes come from procedure coding. Rendle KA, Saia CA, Vachani A et al.. Ann Intern Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38163370/
- **Risk of Venous Thromboembolic Events After Surgery for Cancer.** Swedish nationwide cohort (432,218 cancer surgery patients vs 4.0 million comparators, JAMA Netw Open): 1-year pulmonary embolism risk after lung cancer surgery was 2.61 percentage points higher than in comparators, among the highest of all cancers. Bears on the duration of postoperative thromboprophylaxis; observational. Björklund J, Rautiola J, Zelic R et al.. JAMA Netw Open 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38306100/

### lung-local-therapy

No results. Kamigaichi A, Hamada A, Tsuboi M et al.. Clin Lung Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38360496/

### thoracic-other

- **Pegargiminase Plus First-Line Chemotherapy in Patients With Nonepithelioid Pleural Mesothelioma: The ATOMIC-Meso Randomized Clinical Trial.** ATOMIC-Meso randomised double-blind phase 2/3 (n=249, JAMA Oncol): adding pegargiminase (ADI-PEG20, arginine depletion) to platinum-pemetrexed in non-epithelioid pleural mesothelioma improved OS (9.3 vs 7.7 months, HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.55-0.93) and PFS (HR 0.65). Grade 3-4 AEs 28.8% vs 16.9%. The comparator (chemotherapy alone) is no longer standard for non-epithelioid disease, where nivolumab-ipilimumab is preferred; no approval. Szlosarek PW, Creelan BC, Sarkodie T et al.. JAMA Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38358753/
