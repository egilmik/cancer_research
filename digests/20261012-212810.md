### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers

- **Prevalence and Therapeutic Targeting of High-Level ERBB2 Amplification in NSCLC.** Institutional NGS series (5,769 samples) finds high-level ERBB2 amplification (>=6 copies) in 0.9% of lung adenocarcinomas, reliably predicting HER2 overexpression, either as sole driver in smokers or co-occurring with other drivers. Trastuzumab deruxtecan was active in preclinical models and in two patients. The clinical evidence is anecdotal. Odintsov I, Makarem M, Nishino M et al.. J Thorac Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38154514/
- **A phase Ib/II study of cadonilimab (PD-1/CTLA-4 bispecific antibody) plus anlotinib as first-line treatment in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer.** Phase Ib/II (n=69): first-line chemotherapy-free cadonilimab (PD-1/CTLA-4 bispecific) plus anlotinib in driver-negative NSCLC gave confirmed ORR 51.0% at 15 mg/kg and 60.0% at 10 mg/kg. Grade >=3 TRAEs were 59.2% and 25.0%. It is single-arm and dose cohorts are small. Chen B, Yao W, Li X et al.. Br J Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38110665/
- **Sunvozertinib for patients in China with platinum-pretreated locally advanced or metastatic non-small-cell lung cancer and EGFR exon 20 insertion mutation (WU-KONG6): single-arm, open-label, multicentre, phase 2 trial.** WU-KONG6 single-arm phase 2 (n=104, 97 evaluable, 37 Chinese centres): sunvozertinib 300 mg in platinum-pretreated EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC gave IRC-confirmed ORR 61% (95% CI 50-71), all partial responses. Grade >=3 TRAEs included CPK rise 17% and diarrhoea 8%; ILD 5%. Follow-up is short (7.6 months) and a phase 3 vs chemotherapy is ongoing. Wang M, Fan Y, Sun M et al.. Lancet Respir Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38101437/
- **A phase II study of atezolizumab with bevacizumab, carboplatin, and paclitaxel for patients with EGFR-mutated NSCLC after TKI treatment failure (NEJ043 study).** NEJ043 phase II (n=60): atezolizumab-bevacizumab-carboplatin-paclitaxel (ABCP) after EGFR-TKI failure gave PFS 7.4 months, OS 23.1 months and ORR 55.9%. PFS was shorter after first-line osimertinib (HR 1.93) and with brain or liver metastases. Single-arm. Watanabe S, Furuya N, Nakamura A et al.. Eur J Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38061214/
- **CNS Efficacy of Osimertinib With or Without Chemotherapy in Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor-Mutated Advanced Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer.** FLAURA2 CNS analysis (phase III, CNS BICR): in patients with baseline CNS metastases (n=222), osimertinib plus chemotherapy vs osimertinib gave CNS progression-or-death HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.33-1.01) and CNS CR 59% vs 43%. It is a secondary analysis, not powered for CNS endpoints. Jänne PA, Planchard D, Kobayashi K et al.. J Clin Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38042525/
- **Canakinumab Versus Placebo in Combination With First-Line Pembrolizumab Plus Chemotherapy for Advanced Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer: Results From the CANOPY-1 Trial.** CANOPY-1 double-blind phase III (n=643): adding canakinumab (anti-IL-1beta) to first-line pembrolizumab-platinum chemotherapy did not improve PFS (6.8 vs 6.8 months, HR 0.85) or OS (20.8 vs 20.2 months, HR 0.87). Negative. Tan DSW, Felip E, de Castro G et al.. J Clin Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38039427/
- **[Chinese expert consensus on the diagnosis and treatment of advanced RET fusion-positive non-small cell lung cancer (2023 edition)].** Chinese expert consensus on diagnosis and treatment of advanced RET fusion-positive NSCLC. Society of Cancer Precision of Chinese Anti-Cancer Association;, Lung Cancer Expert Group of Chinese Medical Journal;, Chinese Society of Clinical Oncology, Expert Committee on Non-small Cell Lung Cancer. ### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **Olaparib maintenance versus placebo in platinum-sensitive non-small cell lung cancer: the Phase 2 randomized PIPSeN trial.** PIPSeN double-blind randomised phase II (n=60 randomised, terminated early when anti-PD-(L)1 was approved): olaparib maintenance vs placebo in platinum-sensitive NSCLC gave no PFS (2.9 vs 2.0 months, p=0.99) or OS benefit. Negative and underpowered. Postel-Vinay S, Coves J, Texier M et al.. Br J Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38097741/
- **Neoadjuvant nivolumab with or without platinum-doublet chemotherapy based on PD-L1 expression in resectable NSCLC (CTONG1804): a multicenter open-label phase II study.** CTONG1804 phase II (n=52): neoadjuvant nivolumab alone or with chemotherapy chosen by PD-L1 gave MPR 50% and pCR 25%; with PD-L1 >=50%, pCR was 16.7% with nivolumab alone vs 66.7% with chemo-nivolumab. Post-neoadjuvant ctDNA negativity was linked to 18-month EFS 93.8% vs 47.3% (HR 0.15). Small and nonrandomised. Liu SY, Dong S, Yang XN et al.. Signal Transduct Target Ther 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38057314/
- **Canadian Consensus Recommendations for the Management of Operable Stage II/III Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer: Results of a Modified Delphi Process.** Canadian consensus recommendations for operable stage II/III NSCLC in the era of perioperative immunotherapy and targeted therapy; a national consensus, not a new trial. Tankel J, Spicer J, Chu Q et al.. Curr Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38132389/
- **Atezolizumab and Platinum Plus Pemetrexed With or Without Bevacizumab for Metastatic Nonsquamous Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Phase 3 Randomized Clinical Trial.** Japanese open-label phase 3 (n=412): adding bevacizumab to atezolizumab-carboplatin-pemetrexed did not improve BICR PFS (9.6 vs 7.7 months, HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.70-1.07). A prespecified subgroup with driver alterations after TKI did better (HR 0.67). Negative primary result; subgroup is hypothesis-generating. Shiraishi Y, Kishimoto J, Sugawara S et al.. JAMA Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38127362/
- **The International Association for the Study of Lung Cancer Lung Cancer Staging Project: Proposals for the Revisions of the T-Descriptors in the Forthcoming Ninth Edition of the TNM Classification for Lung Cancer.** IASLC Staging Project T-descriptor analysis for the 9th TNM edition (33,982 clinical, 30,715 pathological cases): no changes proposed to the 8th-edition T descriptors. Van Schil PE, Asamura H, Nishimura KK et al.. J Thorac Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38070600/

### lung-screening / lung-nonpharm / lung-nordic

- **Effectiveness of Aerobic Exercise and Tai Chi Interventions on Sleep Quality in Patients With Advanced Lung Cancer: A Randomized Clinical Trial.** Assessor-blinded RCT (n=226, Hong Kong, JAMA Oncol): 16 weeks of aerobic exercise or tai chi vs guidelines alone improved sleep quality in advanced lung cancer, with tai chi better than aerobic exercise (PSQI difference -1.49 at 16 weeks) and a reported survival improvement. Survival was a secondary outcome and needs confirmation. Takemura N, Cheung DST, Fong DYT et al.. JAMA Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38060250/
- **Low-dose CT screening among never-smokers with or without a family history of lung cancer in Taiwan: a prospective cohort study.** TALENT prospective cohort (n=12,011 never- or light-smokers with risk factors, Taiwan, Lancet Respir Med): baseline LDCT found lung cancer in 2.6% (77.4% stage I). Family history, female sex and age >60 predicted risk; cooking and passive smoke did not. No control group and no mortality data; overdiagnosis of in situ/indolent adenocarcinoma is a concern. Chang GC, Chiu CH, Yu CJ et al.. Lancet Respir Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38042167/
- **Benzene exposure and risk of lung cancer in the Norwegian Offshore Petroleum Worker cohort: a prospective case-cohort study.** Norwegian Offshore Petroleum Worker case-cohort (25,347 men, 399 lung cancers, 1999-2021): low-level benzene exposure was not clearly associated with lung cancer overall (top-quartile cumulative exposure HR 1.15, 0.61-2.35), with a positive trend by exposure duration for adenocarcinoma (P-trend 0.044). Exposure from job-exposure matrices. Babigumira R, Veierød MB, Hosgood HD et al.. Occup Environ Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38154914/

### lung-local-therapy

- **Neoadjuvant durvalumab plus radiation versus durvalumab alone in stages I-III non-small cell lung cancer: survival outcomes and molecular correlates of a randomized phase II trial.** Randomised phase II (Altorki): 3-year DFS after neoadjuvant durvalumab with or without stereotactic RT was 63% vs 67% (exploratory, per arm); the trial's earlier MPR benefit for durvalumab-RT did not translate into a clear DFS difference. Small and not powered for DFS. Altorki NK, Walsh ZH, Melms JC et al.. Nat Commun 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38114518/
- **Standard-of-care systemic therapy with or without stereotactic body radiotherapy in patients with oligoprogressive breast cancer or non-small-cell lung cancer (Consolidative Use of Radiotherapy to Block [CURB] oligoprogression): an open-label, randomised, controlled, phase 2 study.** CURB randomised phase 2 (n=106, Lancet): SBRT to oligoprogressive sites plus standard therapy vs standard therapy improved PFS overall (7.2 vs 3.2 months, HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.35-0.81), driven by NSCLC (10.0 vs 2.2 months, HR 0.41) with no benefit in breast cancer. Single-institution network, stopped early at interim, subgroup by disease site. Tsai CJ, Yang JT, Shaverdian N et al.. Lancet 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38104577/

### car-t-solid

- **Immune determinants of CAR-T cell expansion in solid tumor patients receiving GD2 CAR-T cell therapy.** Phase I trial of GD2-CAR.OX40.28.z.iC9 CAR-T in children and young adults with osteosarcoma and neuroblastoma (Cancer Cell): feasible and safe. Naive T cells in apheresis and CXCR3+ monocytes associated with good CAR-T expansion, exhausted T cells in products with poor expansion. Correlative analysis; response data not in abstract. Kaczanowska S, Murty T, Alimadadi A et al.. Cancer Cell 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38134936/
- **HER2 and HLA-A*02 dual CAR-T cells utilize LOH in a NOT logic gate to address on-target off-tumor toxicity.** Logic-gated HER2 CAR-T with an HLA-A*02 inhibitory CAR (NOT gate exploiting HLA loss of heterozygosity) killed A2-negative HER2+ lung cancer cells and spared A2-positive cells in vitro and in vivo. Preclinical; designed to avoid on-target off-tumour toxicity. Bassan D, Weinberger L, Yi J et al.. J Immunother Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38097342/
- **EpCAM-targeting CAR-T cell immunotherapy is safe and efficacious for epithelial tumors.** EpCAM CAR-T (Dectin-1 costimulated) in epithelial tumours (phase 1, n=12, Sci Adv): grade 1-2 toxicity in 50%, one reversible grade 3 leukopenia, 2 partial responses and 3 patients with PFS >23 months, with CAR-T persistence to 200 days. Very small, mixed tumour types. Li D, Guo X, Yang K et al.. Sci Adv 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38039357/
