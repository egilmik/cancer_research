## High

### Local therapy / key evidence

- **Stereotactic Ablative Radiotherapy Versus Surgical Resection for Early-Stage Non-Small Lung Cancer: An Emulated Target Trial.** Emulated target trial from English registry data (T1-2N0M0 NSCLC, 2015–2020; 2,299 SABR vs 10,247 surgery), instrumental-variable analysis. Adjusted 3-year all-cause mortality 30.3% vs 21.6% (difference 8.8 pp, 95% CI 1.4–16.2); lung-cancer-specific difference 6.1 pp (−0.6 to 12.9); no significant difference at age ≥80 (4.3 pp, −3.7 to 12.3). Observational with residual confounding risk; authors conclude surgery should remain preferred while SABR is a curative alternative, especially for older patients. (Kagenaar E, Lugo-Palacios DG, Aggarwal A et al.; J Clin Oncol, 2026) — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42970669/
- **Radical local treatment for patients with NSCLC following oligoprogression after chemoradiation and durvalumab.** Seven-centre retrospective series: of 198 patients progressing after CRT and durvalumab, 41 had exclusive local ablative therapy for oligoprogression (71% RT). Median PFS 11.0 mo (95% CI 7.9–36.7), median OS 18.7 mo; 2-year PFS 32.6%, OS 46.1%. PFS ≥12 months on durvalumab and no brain metastases were favourable. Small, selected, retrospective; prospective validation needed. (Pravisano F, Cortiula F, Nassar AH et al.; Eur J Cancer, 2026) — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42967453/

### Vaccines / cell therapy

- **A CCL21 gene-modified dendritic cell vaccine combined with pembrolizumab in advanced non-small cell lung cancer: a phase I trial.** Phase I (NCT03546361), n=23 (11 escalation, 12 expansion at 3×10^7 cells/injection) of intratumoral CCL21-DC vaccine plus pembrolizumab. Primary ORR endpoint not met (no objective responses); 36.8% best response stable disease; well tolerated. Post-hoc biopsies showed increased CD4 T-cell infiltration and new clones that did not persist. Small, single-arm, non-randomised. (Oh MS, Lisberg AE, Dumitras C et al.; Nat Commun, 2026) — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42967950/
