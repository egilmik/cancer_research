### Guidelines and consensus statements

- **Chinese clinical practice guideline on ALK-TKIs in NSCLC (2025 edition).** Two Chinese oncology associations issued a guideline on using the eight ALK-TKIs approved by the NMPA as of December 2024, including iruplinalkib and the newer agents. The abstract does not say which recommendations changed or how first-line agents are ranked, so it stays high, not practice-changing. Its relevance outside China is limited. Chinese Association for Clinical Oncologists, Medical Oncology Branch of China International Exchange and Promotive Association for Medical and Health Care. Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40268545/
- **Chinese guideline on perioperative multimodality treatment of NSCLC.** It gives 24 recommendations on neoadjuvant therapy, perioperative management, adjuvant therapy, psychological rehabilitation, prognosis and follow-up. The abstract does not list the recommendations or what changed. Jiao W, Zhao L, Mei J et al. Chin Med J (Engl) 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40246578/
- **American Radium Society appropriate-use criteria and guideline on thoracic reirradiation for NSCLC.** It combines a PRISMA systematic review with consensus on patient selection, technique and composite dose constraints, aimed at avoiding high-grade or fatal toxicity. It is the first dedicated guideline in this area, but the evidence underneath is mostly retrospective. Simone CB, Amini A, Chetty IJ et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40185207/
- **ESTRO clinical practice guideline on diagnosis and treatment of radiation pneumonitis.** It gives flowcharts for diagnosis and management, dosimetric constraints and risk factors such as idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, and covers combining radiotherapy with systemic drugs. - **Chinese expert consensus on checkpoint-inhibitor pneumonitis (2025).** Chinese thoracic and oncology societies set out how to diagnose, grade and treat CIP and when rechallenge is reasonable. It is consensus-level, and the abstract gives no specific recommendations. Chinese Thoracic Society, Chinese Medical Association, Oncology Respiratory Disease Committee of China Anti-Cancer Association, Beijing Cancer Prevention and Treatment Research Association. Zhonghua Jie He He Hu Xi Za Zhi 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40159051/

### lung-key-evidence (trials and pooled analyses)

- **MEDIASTrial 2-year follow-up (randomised non-inferiority, n=360).** After negative endosonography, going straight to resection rather than confirmatory mediastinoscopy first gave similar OS (time to 80% OS 25 vs 20 months, adjusted HR 0.8, 95% CI 0.5-1.3) and DFS (HR 0.9, 95% CI 0.6-1.4), and similar quality of life. It supports omitting routine confirmatory mediastinoscopy, but the 2-year survival data are a secondary short communication with wide confidence intervals. Bousema JE, Spaans LN, Dijkgraaf MGW et al. Lung Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40147400/
- **APPLE phase 3 biomarker analysis (serum VEGF-A).** A preplanned analysis of the APPLE trial (atezolizumab + platinum chemotherapy with or without bevacizumab in non-squamous NSCLC) found that PFS improved with bevacizumab mainly in patients with low baseline serum VEGF-A. It is a candidate predictive marker that needs validation. The abstract gives no effect sizes. Tanaka K, Sugisaka J, Shiraishi Y et al. Nat Commun 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40121197/
- **Telpegfilgrastim for chemotherapy-induced neutropenia in NSCLC (randomised phase 3, n=133, China).** Both telpegfilgrastim doses were non-inferior to the control G-CSF on duration of grade 4 neutropenia in cycle 1 (differences −0.14 and −0.06 days, margin 1 day). It is another long-acting G-CSF, not a new standard, and the trial is small. Shi Y, Wang X, Pei Z et al. BMC Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40098083/
- **ctDNA clearance across eight TKI trials in advanced NSCLC (aggregate analysis).** Patients whose detectable baseline ctDNA cleared on treatment had better OS than those with persistent ctDNA (adjusted HR 2.12 for persistence, P<0.001). This supports ctDNA clearance as an early endpoint, but it is a retrospective pooled analysis with heterogeneous assays and timepoints. Andrews HS, Zariffa N, Nishimura KK et al. Clin Cancer Res 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40261185/

### nsclc-drivers

- **National Lung Matrix Trial arm B2: vistusertib in STK11-deficient NSCLC (phase 2, n=54).** The mTORC1/2 inhibitor gave few responses: estimated true ORR 9.8% in the KRAS-co-mutant cohort and durable clinical benefit 24% and 15%. The authors conclude it cannot be recommended. Longitudinal ctDNA showed SMARCA4 enrichment, and AKT reactivation was an adaptive resistance mechanism. This is a negative result for a common co-mutation with no targeted option. Middleton G, Robbins HL, Fletcher P et al. NPJ Precis Oncol 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40069402/
- **EMBRACE: ensartinib in MET exon 14 skipping NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, n=31, China).** ORR was 53.3% (95% CI 35.5-71.2), median PFS 6.0 months and median DoR 7.9 months, with grade 3 AEs in 23%. Activity is modest next to approved MET TKIs, the trial is small and follow-up is short (9.2 months). Xia Y, Tian P, Zhou M et al. EClinicalMedicine 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40034576/

### sclc

- **DeLLphi-301 patient-reported outcomes with tarlatamab 10 mg (phase 2, n=100 PRO-evaluable).** Global health status trended towards improvement, physical functioning was stable, and dyspnoea improved. Median time to deterioration exceeded 6 months for cough and dyspnoea. It is single-arm, with no comparator for the PRO trends. Hummel HD, Ahn MJ, Blackhall F et al. Adv Ther 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40025391/
- **Tarlatamab cycle 1 with outpatient monitoring (DeLLphi-300 phase 1 substudy).** With 6-8 h outpatient monitoring (n=30) vs 48 h inpatient monitoring (n=58), cycle 1 treatment-related AEs (93% vs 100%), hospitalisation including ER visits (27% vs 34%) and CRS (60% vs 62%; serious 17% vs 22%) were similar. This supports outpatient delivery, but the groups were small and not randomised. Chiang AC, Olmedo Garcia ME, Carlisle JW et al. ESMO Open 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40187110/

### nsclc-immunotherapy

- **Outcomes after stopping ICIs for irAEs (n=2,794, of whom 271 stopped for irAE).** Longer treatment before discontinuation went with better outcomes: median post-discontinuation PFS 6.2, 13.9 and 25.8 months, and OS 21.7, 42.7 and 86.9 months, for <3, 3-6 and >6 months on ICI. It informs counselling on whether to resume, but it is observational and subject to lead-time bias. Pecci F, Thummalapalli R, Alden SL et al. Clin Cancer Res 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40247431/

### lung-nonpharm / lung-repurposed

- **Esketamine for smoking cessation in lung cancer with major depression (multicentre placebo-controlled RCT, n=236).** Eight weekly intranasal esketamine sessions gave 6-month continuous abstinence of 44.1% self-reported and 28.8% biochemically verified, and also eased depression and anxiety, with no serious AEs. The abstract does not give placebo rates, and the trial is single-country and needs replication. Hong X, Xu S, Sun G et al. J Affect Disord 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40274117/
- **PEP: personalised perioperative exercise around lung cancer surgery (single-centre phase 3 RCT, n=182, JAMA Surg).** A home-based programme with remote physiotherapist monitoring gave a 22.7 m between-group 6MWT difference at 2 months (P=0.08), with significant gains in women and stable fatigue. The primary comparison was not significant, and the benefit by sex is a subgroup finding. Ulrich CM, Himbert C, Barnes CA et al. JAMA Surg 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40072448/
- **Protocol: faecal microbiota transplantation + platinum doublet + tislelizumab first line in driver-negative NSCLC (multicentre single-arm exploratory trial).** It tests whether FMT improves chemo-immunotherapy outcomes. There are no results yet, and the single-arm design limits what it can show. Wei Y, Qin L, Wu X et al. BMJ Open 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40037667/

### lung-screening

- **Screen Assist: tobacco treatment inside lung screening (factorial RCT, n=642, JAMA Intern Med).** Offering 8 rather than 4 counselling sessions raised 6-month 7-day abstinence (17.3% vs 11.7%, risk difference 5.6, 95% CI 0.1-11.0). Longer NRT and screening for social needs did not help. The effect is modest and self-reported. Park ER, Haas JS, Rigotti NA et al. JAMA Intern Med 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40029643/
- **Manchester Lung Health Check: 6-year performance of PLCOm2012 risk-based selection (n=2,541).** 7.3% of the screened high-risk group vs 0.9% of the unscreened low-risk group developed lung cancer, and most models underestimated risk (E/O 0.68). It supports risk-model selection, but it is a single programme and reports no mortality. Goodley P, Balata H, Robbins HA et al. BMJ Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40046247/

### lung-ctdna-mrd

- **LEMA: tumour-informed ctDNA after curative treatment of stage 0-III NSCLC (retrospective validation).** Patient-specific ctDNA assays on serial post-treatment plasma predicted recurrence, and were compared with the independent LUCID cohort. It adds to MRD validation but does not test ctDNA-guided treatment, and the abstract gives no performance numbers. Schuurbiers MMF, Smith CG, Hartemink KJ et al. PLoS Med 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40233104/
- **LUNGCA: longitudinal postoperative ctDNA in NSCLC (n=233, 2,336 samples, median follow-up 51 months).** MRD status after all treatment predicted relapse (PPV 100%, NPV 90.3%), better than postoperative MRD alone. Patients with positive pre-adjuvant ctDNA and targetable mutations did better with adjuvant TKI. It is observational and not randomised by ctDNA. Xia L, Pu Q, Kang R et al. J Natl Cancer Inst 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40131728/

### lung-local-therapy

- **Expanded HILUS: SBRT for central T1-T3N0 inoperable NSCLC (Nordic cohort).** 3-year local control was 97%, but 3-year OS was 38%, and 17% of patients died of toxicity, mostly bronchopulmonary bleeding. It is a strong safety warning for SBRT to tumours near the main bronchi. The subgroup is small and comorbid. Lindberg S, Grozman V, Karlsson K et al. Lung Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40184836/
- **CRES3T: S-1 + cisplatin with radical-dose radiotherapy then surgery for superior sulcus NSCLC (single-arm confirmatory trial, n=61, Japan).** ORR was 42% and pCR 33%, and 3-year OS and PFS were 73.2% and 53.3%. These are good results for trimodality therapy, but there is no comparator and it predates perioperative immunotherapy. Takamochi K, Suzuki K, Okada M et al. Lung Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40120334/

### lung-nordic

- **Timing of adjuvant chemotherapy after NSCLC resection (Swedish nationwide target-trial emulation, n=510).** 5-year DFS and OS were similar when chemotherapy began within 8 weeks (50.3%/58.1%) or at 8-16 weeks (49.1%/57.1%). Starting up to 16 weeks after surgery appears acceptable. The design is observational emulation with modest numbers. Lampa E, Boros M, Berglund A et al. Ann Surg Oncol 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40106109/
- **First-line pembrolizumab alone vs with chemotherapy in PD-L1 ≥50% adenocarcinoma (Norwegian nationwide, n=410).** The combination (n=93) had longer median OS than monotherapy (n=317), 22.6 vs 14.2 months, but this was not significant after adjustment. It is direct Norwegian evidence on a common decision, but retrospective, with confounding by fitness. Hektoen HH, Tsuruda KM, Brustugun OT et al. ESMO Open 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40305908/
- **Sex and survival with first-line pembrolizumab (Norwegian population-based cohort, 1,586 women and 1,978 men).** Lung-cancer-specific survival was compared by sex among pembrolizumab users (2017-2021) and against historical chemotherapy users (2012-2016), adjusted for clinical and socioeconomic factors. The abstract does not give the effect sizes. The historical comparator limits causal reading. Tsuruda KM, Hektoen HH, Aamelfot C et al. Int J Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40298324/
- **Long-term real-world outcomes of first-line immunotherapy (Swedish population-based cohort, n=1,153).** Median OS was 15.2/12.9 months with PD-(L)1 monotherapy and 17.0/18.0 months with combinations (non-squamous/squamous), and only about 5 months for PS 2. It is a useful Nordic benchmark against the trials, but the treatment groups are not comparable. Wagenius G, Vikström A, Berglund A et al. Acta Oncol 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40094477/

### lung-vaccines-cell

- **ARTEMIA trial design: OSE2101 (Tedopi) vs docetaxel in HLA-A2+ NSCLC with secondary resistance to ICIs (randomised phase 3).** This is the confirmatory trial after ATALANTE-1 for the neoepitope peptide vaccine. Only the design is published (no abstract), and there are no results. Liu SV, Dziadziuszko R, Viteri S et al. Lung Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40174385/

### thoracic-other

- **Bintrafusp alfa in previously treated pleural mesothelioma (single-arm phase 2, n=46).** Median PFS was 1.9 months and ORR 6.5%, a clearly negative result for the PD-L1/TGF-β trap in this setting. Nadal E, Martín-Martorell P, Benítez JC et al. Lung Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40253943/
- **Ad-SGE-DKK3 gene therapy + nivolumab in chemotherapy-refractory epithelioid pleural mesothelioma (single-arm phase 2, n=12).** ORR was 16.6% and durable clinical response 58.3%, with median OS 14.5 and PFS 4.5 months. Grade 3 AEs occurred in 41.7%. Responders had more effector-memory CD8 T cells in the tumour. The trial is very small and exploratory. Jang HJ, Patel M, Wang DY et al. Clin Cancer Res 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40208070/
- **MARBLE: atezolizumab + carboplatin + paclitaxel first line in advanced thymic carcinoma (single-arm phase 2, n=48, Japan, Lancet Oncol).** ORR was 56% (95% CI 41-71), above the prespecified threshold. Grade 3+ neutropenia occurred in 56% and febrile neutropenia in 23%, with no treatment-related deaths. It is the first chemo-immunotherapy data in this rare tumour, but single-arm with median follow-up 15.3 months and immature survival. Shukuya T, Asao T, Goto Y et al. Lancet Oncol 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40049197/
