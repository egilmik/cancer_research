### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **Efficacy and Toxicity of Moderately Hypofractionated Radiation Therapy with Helical TomoTherapy Versus Conventional Radiation Therapy in Patients with Unresectable Stage III Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer Receiving Concurrent Chemotherapy: A Multicenter, Randomized Phase 3 Trial.** Multicentre randomised phase 3 in China (n=146, median follow-up 46 months): moderately hypofractionated RT (60 Gy/20 fractions, helical TomoTherapy) vs 60 Gy/30 with concurrent chemotherapy in unresectable stage III NSCLC improved the primary endpoint 3-year OS (58.4% vs 38.4%; median 41 vs 30 months, HR 0.61, 95% CI 0.40-0.94), with no difference in grade >=2 toxicity. Small and without consolidation durvalumab, so applicability to the current standard is uncertain; it needs confirmation. Zhang Q, Fan S, Xu X et al.. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38631536/
- **Programmed Death Ligand-1 and Tumor Mutation Burden Testing of Patients With Lung Cancer for Selection of Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Therapies: Guideline From the College of American Pathologists, Association for Molecular Pathology, International Association for the Study of Lung Cancer, Pulmonary Pathology Society, and LUNGevity Foundation.** Joint CAP/AMP/IASLC/PPS/LUNGevity evidence-based guideline (GRADE) with six recommendations on PD-L1 and TMB testing to select checkpoint-inhibitor therapy in advanced NSCLC. A testing guideline for pathologists; the abstract does not list which recommendations change practice. Sholl LM, Awad M, Basu Roy U et al.. Arch Pathol Lab Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38625026/

Early dose-finding data for the PD-1xVEGF class now in phase 3 in NSCLC; not NSCLC-specific and no efficacy comparison. Frentzas S, Austria Mislang AR, Lemech C et al.. ### sclc

- **Long-Term Outcomes After Concurrent Once- or Twice-Daily Chemoradiation in Limited-Stage Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Brief Report From the CONVERT Trial.** CONVERT long-term update (randomised phase 3, n=547, median follow-up 81.2 months): once-daily 66 Gy/33 vs twice-daily 45 Gy/30 concurrent chemoradiation in LS-SCLC gave median OS 25.4 vs 30.0 months (HR 1.13, 95% CI 0.92-1.38), still not significant. Late grade 3 oesophagitis occurred only with once-daily RT (7 vs 0). Supports twice-daily 45 Gy as a standard; the original trial was designed for superiority of once-daily. Walls GM, Mistry H, Barlesi F et al.. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38521132/

### lung-screening / lung-nonpharm / lung-nordic

- **Early Incorporation to Palliative Care (EPC) in Patients With Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: The PACO Randomized Clinical Trial.** PACO single-centre randomised trial in Mexico (n=146): early palliative care added to standard oncology care in treatment-naive metastatic NSCLC improved the primary endpoint OS (median 18.1 vs 10.5 months, P=.029; control-arm HR 1.5, 95% CI 1.03-2.3), without significant differences in QoL, anxiety or depression. Supports early palliative care in a resource-limited setting; single centre, open label, enrolled 2012-2015 before immunotherapy. Allende S, Turcott JG, Verástegui E et al.. Oncologist 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38558247/
- **Improving Lung Cancer Screening Selection: The HUNT Lung Cancer Risk Model for Ever-Smokers Versus the NELSON and 2021 United States Preventive Services Task Force Criteria in the Cohort of Norway: A Population-Based Prospective Study.** Cohort of Norway (10 linked prospective cohorts, 44,831 ever-smokers, median follow-up 11.6 years): the HUNT lung cancer risk model selected screening candidates more efficiently than NELSON and 2021 USPSTF criteria. At the same number screened it predicted 41.0% and 12.1% more cancers, and a 6-year risk cutoff of 0.98% (14% of ever-smokers) captured 70.7% of cancers. Directly relevant to selection criteria for a Norwegian screening programme; prediction of diagnosis only, not a screening trial. Nguyen OTD, Fotopoulos I, Markaki M et al.. JTO Clin Res Rep 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38586302/
- **Overall Survival after Radiotherapy for Brain Metastases According to ECOG Status-A Prospective Study of 294 NSCLC Patients.** Norwegian prospective observational study (Oslo; 294 consecutive NSCLC patients with first brain metastases): median OS after radiotherapy was 4.6 months overall, 2.5 months after WBRT and 7.5 months after stereotactic RT. After WBRT, median OS was 1.9 months for ECOG 2 and 1.2 months for ECOG 3-4. The authors conclude WBRT should be questioned for ECOG >=2 patients not eligible for surgery or SRT; observational, single-region. Karlsson AT, Hjermstad MJ, Aass N et al.. Cancers (Basel) 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38672568/
- **First-line Treatment Patterns and Outcomes in Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer in Sweden: A Population-based Real-world Study with Focus on Immunotherapy.** Swedish National Lung Cancer Registry (2,204 patients starting first-line treatment for advanced NSCLC, 2017-2020): PD-L1 testing reached about 85%, and PD-(L)1-based therapy displaced platinum doublets over time. In PS 0-1, median OS was 18.6 (non-squamous) and 13.3 months (squamous) with PD-(L)1 monotherapy and 24.0 months with chemo-immunotherapy (non-squamous), similar to trial results. A Nordic population benchmark for first-line immunotherapy; descriptive, not comparative. Wagenius G, Vikström A, Berglund A et al.. Acta Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38643377/

### lung-local-therapy / lung-toxicity

- **KN046, a bispecific antibody against PD-L1 and CTLA-4, plus chemotherapy as first-line treatment for metastatic NSCLC: A multicenter phase 2 trial.** Multicentre single-arm phase 2 (n not given in the abstract): KN046 (PD-L1/CTLA-4 bispecific) plus platinum chemotherapy first line in metastatic NSCLC gave ORR 46.0%, median duration of response 8.1 months, median PFS 5.8 and OS 26.6 months; anaemia (87.4%) and loss of appetite were common, pruritus the most frequent irAE. Single-arm; a phase 3 against PD-1 plus chemotherapy is needed. Zhao Y, Chen G, Li X et al.. Cell Rep Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38508135/

### lung-ctdna-mrd / lung-repurposed / lung-vaccines-cell

- **Neoantigen-targeted dendritic cell vaccination in lung cancer patients induces long-lived T cells exhibiting the full differentiation spectrum.** Phase I trial (Belgium, n=10 enrolled, 6 vaccinated) of a personalised neoantigen dendritic-cell vaccine after resection of NSCLC: manufacturing feasible in 6/10, only grade 1-2 toxicity, systemic T-cell responses in 5/6 lasting up to 19 months; 3/6 recurred within 2 years. Feasibility and immunogenicity only; very small. Ingels J, De Cock L, Stevens D et al.. Cell Rep Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38626769/

### thoracic-other

- **UV1 telomerase vaccine with ipilimumab and nivolumab as second line treatment for pleural mesothelioma - A phase II randomised trial.** NIPU randomised phase II (Norway-led, n=118): adding the telomerase vaccine UV1 to second-line ipilimumab-nivolumab in pleural mesothelioma did not improve the primary endpoint BICR-assessed PFS (4.2 vs 4.7 months, HR 1.01). Investigator-assessed PFS (HR 0.60) and BICR ORR (31% vs 16%) favoured the vaccine, and OS was 15.4 vs 11.1 months (HR 0.73, 80% CI 0.53-1.0, not significant). A negative trial; the secondary signals need confirmation. Haakensen VD, Öjlert ÅK, Thunold S et al.. Eur J Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38447379/
- **Long-term follow-up and exploratory analysis of lenvatinib in patients with metastatic or recurrent thymic carcinoma: Results from the multicenter, phase 2 REMORA trial.** REMORA long-term follow-up (single-arm phase 2, 8 Japanese centres, n=42): lenvatinib in metastatic or recurrent thymic carcinoma gave updated median OS 28.3 months (95% CI 17.1-34.0) and 3-year OS 35.7%. Patients who kept relative dose intensity >=75% at 8 weeks had longer OS (38.5 vs 17.3 months, HR 0.46), an exploratory finding. Supports lenvatinib as a second-line option; single-arm and small. Niho S, Sato J, Satouchi M et al.. Lung Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38626709/
- **Recommendations for Post-Operative RadioTherapy After Complete Resection of Thymoma-a French DELPHI Consensus Initiative.** French RYTHMIC Delphi consensus (24 experts) on how to deliver postoperative radiotherapy after complete resection of thymoma: 4D-CT or breath-hold planning, delineation of coronary arteries, rotational IMRT/VMAT, 50 Gy in 1.8-2 Gy fractions, and cardiac follow-up. It standardises technique while the randomised RADIORYTHMIC trial tests whether PORT is needed at all; it does not change the indication. Basse C, Khalifa J, Thillays F et al.. J Thorac Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38608932/
- **Guidelines for Pathologic Diagnosis of Mesothelioma: 2023 Update of the Consensus Statement From the International Mesothelioma Interest Group.** 2023 update of the International Mesothelioma Interest Group consensus guideline for pathologic diagnosis of mesothelioma: histologic subtyping and variants, immunohistochemistry for mesothelial lineage, ancillary tests for benign vs malignant proliferations (BAP1 and MTAP immunostains, CDKN2A FISH, newer markers such as Merlin and p53). A diagnostic standard for pathologists, not a treatment recommendation. Husain AN, Chapel DB, Attanoos R et al.. Arch Pathol Lab Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38586983/
- **NCCN Guidelines® Insights: Mesothelioma: Pleural, Version 1.2024.** NCCN Guidelines Insights for pleural mesothelioma, version 1.2024: summarises the significant updates, namely revised guidance on disease classification and on systemic therapy options. The abstract does not say which recommendations changed, so it stays below the practice-changing tier; the full text should be checked against current practice. Stevenson J, Ettinger DS, Wood DE et al.. J Natl Compr Canc Netw 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38503043/
