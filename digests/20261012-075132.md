### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **ARTEMIS-CTONG1509: erlotinib plus bevacizumab vs erlotinib in untreated EGFR-mutant advanced NSCLC (phase 3, n=311, China, Cancer Cell).** Median PFS was 17.9 vs 11.2 months (HR 0.55, 95% CI 0.41–0.73), and the PFS benefit held in patients with brain metastases (HR 0.48, 0.27–0.84). Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 54.8% vs 26.1%. OS is not reported in the abstract, and erlotinib is no longer the first-line standard where osimertinib is available. Zhou Q, Xu CR, Cheng Y et al. Cancer Cell 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34388377/
- **NEJ026 OS: erlotinib plus bevacizumab vs erlotinib (open-label phase 3, n=228 enrolled, 224 analysed, Japan, Lancet Respir Med).** At a median follow-up of 39.2 months, median OS was 50.7 vs 46.2 months (HR 1.007, 95% CI 0.681–1.490), so the earlier PFS gain did not translate into OS. Time to progression on second-line treatment or death was 28.6 vs 24.3 months (HR 0.773, exploratory). OS was a secondary endpoint, and the authors suggest post-progression therapy may have diluted any effect. Kawashima Y, Fukuhara T, Saito H et al. Lancet Respir Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34454653/
- **CHRYSALIS: amivantamab in EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC after platinum (phase I expansion, n=81 efficacy, J Clin Oncol).** ORR was 40% (95% CI 29–51) with 3 CRs, median DoR 11.1 months and median PFS 8.3 months. Rash (86%), infusion-related reactions (66%) and paronychia (45%) were the most common AEs. The cohort is single-arm. Park K, Haura EB, Leighl NB et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34339292/
- **OCEAN T790M cohort: osimertinib for radiotherapy-naive CNS metastases (phase II, n=40, Japan, JTO).** Brain metastasis response rate (PAREXEL) was 66.7% (90% CI 54.3–79.1), brain-metastasis PFS 25.2 months, median PFS 7.1 months and OS 19.8 months. Brain PFS was longer with exon 19 deletion than L858R (31.8 vs 8.3 months). Pneumonitis occurred in 10%, and the CSF penetration rate was 0.79%. The study is small and single-arm. Yamaguchi H, Wakuda K, Fukuda M et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34419684/
- **NVALT-18: docetaxel plus intercalated erlotinib vs docetaxel in relapsed EGFR-wild-type non-squamous NSCLC (randomised phase III, stopped for slow accrual at n=45).** The combination was worse: median PFS 1.9 vs 4.0 months (adjusted HR 2.51, 95% CI 1.16–5.43) and OS 4.7 vs 10.6 months (HR 3.67, 1.46–9.27). Grade ≥3 toxicity was 77% vs 26%. The trial is small and closed early, but the signal is clearly negative. Steendam CMJ, Peric R, van Walree NC et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34403911/
- **ORCHARD: design of a biomarker-directed phase II platform after progression on first-line osimertinib (Clin Lung Cancer).** Patients are allocated by post-progression biopsy to osimertinib combinations for defined resistance biomarkers (group A), non-biomarker chemotherapy- or TKI-based therapy (group B), or observation (group C, including transformation). It is a design paper with no results. Yu HA, Goldberg SB, Le X et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34389237/
- **Telisotuzumab vedotin (c-Met ADC) monotherapy in NSCLC (phase I/Ib, n=52, Clin Cancer Res).** Among 40 c-Met-positive patients (H-score ≥150), ORR was 23% with a median DoR of 8.7 months and median PFS 5.2 months. Fatigue (54%), peripheral neuropathy (42%) and nausea (38%) were most common, and RP2Ds were 1.9 mg/kg every 2 weeks and 2.7 mg/kg every 3 weeks. The study is early phase and not restricted to EGFR-mutant disease. Camidge DR, Morgensztern D, Heist RS et al. Clin Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34426443/

### nsclc-drivers
- **Dabrafenib plus trametinib in BRAF V600E-mutant metastatic NSCLC, 5-year update (phase 2, n=93, JTO).** ORR was 68.4% pretreated and 63.9% treatment-naive. Median PFS was 10.2 and 10.8 months and median OS 18.2 and 17.3 months, with 5-year OS of 19% and 22%. Pyrexia (56%) was the most frequent AE. It is single-arm, and co-alterations may influence outcomes (exploratory). Planchard D, Besse B, Groen HJM et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34455067/
- **FDA approval summary: capmatinib and tepotinib for METex14 NSCLC (Clin Cancer Res).** The FDA approved capmatinib on 6 May 2020 (GEOMETRY mono-1: ORR 68% treatment-naive, 41% pretreated) and tepotinib on 3 February 2021 (VISION: ORR 43% in both groups), the first two drugs approved specifically for METex14 NSCLC. This summarises earlier decisions. Mathieu LN, Larkins E, Akinboro O et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34344795/
- **Sotorasib: First Approval (Drugs profile).** Sotorasib received FDA accelerated approval in May 2021 for KRAS G12C-mutated locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC after at least one prior systemic therapy, with an FDA-approved test. This is a profile, not the decision itself. Blair HA Drugs 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34357500/
- **Savolitinib: First Approval (Drugs profile).** Savolitinib was conditionally approved in China for metastatic NSCLC with METex14 skipping after or intolerant of platinum chemotherapy, based on a pivotal phase II, pending a phase III. This is a profile, not the decision itself. Markham A Drugs 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34455538/
- **ALTA-3: design of a phase III of brigatinib vs alectinib after crizotinib in ALK+ NSCLC (Future Oncol).** The primary endpoint is blinded IRC-assessed PFS and the key secondary OS. ### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy
- **ORIENT-11 updated OS: sintilimab plus pemetrexed-platinum vs placebo plus chemotherapy in first-line non-squamous NSCLC (phase 3, n=397, JTO).** At a median follow-up of 22.9 months, median OS was not reached vs 16.8 months (HR 0.60, 95% CI 0.45–0.79). In exploratory biomarker work, high MHC class II pathway expression was associated with longer PFS and OS on the combination, including in PD-L1-low tumours. The population is Chinese. Yang Y, Sun J, Wang Z et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34358724/
- **Veliparib plus carboplatin-paclitaxel in first-line advanced squamous NSCLC (phase III, n=970, J Clin Oncol).** There was no OS benefit in current smokers, the primary population (11.9 vs 11.1 months, HR 0.905, 95% CI 0.744–1.101). OS favoured veliparib in the overall population (HR 0.853, 0.747–0.974, not the primary endpoint), and PFS did not differ. The trial is negative, and the comparator lacks immunotherapy. Ramalingam SS, Novello S, Guclu SZ et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34436928/
- **Hypofractionated IGRT (60 Gy/15) vs conventional RT (60 Gy/30) in stage II/III NSCLC ineligible for concurrent CRT (phase 3, n=103 randomised, 96 analysed, JAMA Oncol).** The trial closed when an interim analysis suggested futility. 1-year OS was 37.7% vs 44.6% (p=0.29), and PFS, local and distant failure and grade ≥3 toxicity did not differ. Hypofractionation was not superior; equivalence was not tested. Iyengar P, Zhang-Velten E, Court L et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34383006/
- **SAFRON II: single-fraction (28 Gy) vs multifraction (4×12 Gy) SABR for pulmonary oligometastases (randomised phase 2, n=90, Australia/NZ, JAMA Oncol).** Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs at 1 year were 5% vs 3%, and local control, OS, DFS and PROs did not differ (median follow-up 36.5 months). The primaries were mixed and the trial was not powered for equivalence; the authors favour single-fraction for efficiency. Siva S, Bressel M, Mai T et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34455431/
- **CLEAR: cisplatin-pemetrexed-bevacizumab vs carboplatin-paclitaxel-bevacizumab in advanced non-squamous NSCLC (randomised phase 2, Japan).** The PFS HR was 0.825 (95% CI 0.600–1.134; 7.6 vs 7.0 months), which met the prespecified threshold of <0.830. OS was 23.4 vs 21.6 months, and grade ≥3 AEs 67% vs 82%. The regimens are pre-immunotherapy and the confidence interval crosses 1. Udagawa H, Sugiyama E, Harada T et al. Transl Lung Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34430347/
- **Lung-MAP S1400F: durvalumab plus tremelimumab after anti-PD-(L)1 in squamous NSCLC (phase II, n=58, JITC).** ORR was 7% in primary resistance and 0% in acquired resistance. Median PFS was 2.0 and 2.1 months and OS 7.7 and 7.6 months, with grade ≥3 related AEs in 34%. Adding CTLA-4 blockade did not overcome PD-(L)1 resistance. Leighl NB, Redman MW, Rizvi N et al. J Immunother Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34429332/
- **Induction immuno(chemo)therapy in stage III–IVA NSCLC without a primary curative option (prospective single-arm, n=35, Germany, Eur J Cancer).** 91% then received curative treatment (11 resections, 21 CRT). Among those resected, 73% had a major and 64% a complete pathological response. At a median follow-up of 17 months, recurrence was 18% after resection and 43% after CRT, with no treatment-related deaths. The study is small and follow-up short. Faehling M, Fallscheer S, Kramberg S et al. Eur J Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34461420/
- **SIR multidisciplinary position statement on percutaneous ablation of NSCLC and lung metastases (J Vasc Interv Radiol, endorsed by the Canadian Association of Radiologists).** From 63 studies (systematic reviews, retrospective cohorts and single-arm trials), the panel made 7 recommendations. It considers thermal ablation acceptable for inoperable stage I NSCLC, recurrent NSCLC and lung metastases. The evidence base has no randomised trials. Genshaft SJ, Suh RD, Abtin F et al. J Vasc Interv Radiol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34332724/

### sclc
- **PREMER: PCI with vs without hippocampal avoidance in SCLC (phase III, J Clin Oncol).** At a median follow-up of 40.4 months, decline in delayed free recall at 3 months was 5.8% vs 23.5% (OR 5, 95% CI 1.57–15.86), and the benefit persisted at 6 and 24 months on other recall measures. Brain metastasis incidence, OS and QoL did not differ. The primary endpoint is a cognitive test score. Rodríguez de Dios N, Couñago F, Murcia-Mejía M et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34379442/
- **Trilaciclib myeloprotection in ES-SCLC by age and risk group (pooled analysis of three randomised phase 2 trials, n=242).** Trilaciclib before chemotherapy improved neutrophil and red-cell endpoints vs placebo across age and febrile-neutropenia risk categories, with larger effects in patients ≥65 years and those at higher risk. This is a secondary pooled analysis without survival data. Hussein M, Maglakelidze M, Richards DA et al. Cancer Manag Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34408488/
- **Quavonlimab (anti-CTLA-4) plus pembrolizumab in previously treated ES-SCLC (phase I, n=40, Lung Cancer).** Confirmed ORR was 18% (95% CI 7–33), 7% in PD-L1-positive and 19% in PD-L1-negative tumours. Median PFS was 2.0 months, DLTs occurred in 10% and grade 3 TRAEs in 33%. Activity is modest and the study single-arm. Cho BC, Yoh K, Perets R et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34371366/

### lung-screening / lung-ctdna-mrd
- **DELFI cfDNA fragmentome for lung cancer detection (prospective study of 365 at-risk individuals plus validation, Nat Commun).** Combining fragmentation features, clinical risk factors and CEA, followed by CT, detected 94% of cancers (91% stage I/II) at 80% specificity in a validation cohort of 385 non-cancer individuals and 46 cancers. Fragmentation at ASCL1 binding sites separated SCLC from NSCLC (AUC 0.98), and a higher score was prognostic. The validation set has only 46 cancers. Mathios D, Johansen JS, Cristiano S et al. Nat Commun 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34417454/
- **IASLC Early Detection and Screening Committee report on screening never-smokers (JTO).** It notes that current eligibility criteria miss more than half of lung cancers, many in never-smokers in East Asia. It reviews risk factors (secondhand smoke, radon, air pollution, occupational exposures, COPD/ILD) and the open questions on risk models and cost-effectiveness. The abstract makes no new screening recommendation. Kerpel-Fronius A, Tammemägi M, Cavic M et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34455065/

### lung-repurposed
- **Antibiotic exposure and first-line chemo-immunotherapy outcomes in stage IV NSCLC (multicentre cohort, n=302, Ann Oncol).** Peri-treatment antibiotic exposure was not associated with OS (HR 1.42, 95% CI 0.91–2.22) or PFS (HR 1.12), and concurrent exposure (time-varying) was not either. This contrasts with reports on single-agent ICI. The study is observational and modest in size, and the OS CI is wide. Cortellini A, Ricciuti B, Facchinetti F et al. Ann Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34400292/

### lung-vaccines-cell / car-t-solid
- **TIL plus nivolumab after progression on nivolumab monotherapy in advanced NSCLC (phase 1, n=20, Nat Med).** The safety endpoint was met. Of 13 evaluable patients, 3 had confirmed responses and 11 had tumour shrinkage; 2 CRs were ongoing at 1.5 years. Neoantigen-reactive clones expanded and persisted. The study is small and single-arm, with lymphodepletion and IL-2. Creelan BC, Wang C, Teer JK et al. Nat Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34385708/
- **CRISPR PD-1/TCR-disrupted mesothelin CAR-T in solid tumours (phase I, n=15, Cell Mol Immunol).** Without lymphodepletion there were no DLTs. The best response was stable disease in 2/15, and CAR-T cells peaked at days 7–14 and were undetectable after 1 month. TCR-deficient cells persisted less, in patients and in mice. Activity is minimal. Wang Z, Li N, Feng K et al. Cell Mol Immunol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34381179/

### thoracic-other
- **Neoadjuvant pemetrexed-cisplatin then pleurectomy/decortication for pleural mesothelioma (prospective multi-institutional feasibility trial, n=24, Japan, JTCVS).** Of 20 who received chemotherapy, 18 had surgery, all with macroscopic complete resection (90%, the primary endpoint). There was no 30- or 90-day mortality. 2-year OS was 70% and median OS 3.45 years from registration; the median time to progression was 1.71 years. The study is small and single-arm. Hasegawa S, Yokoi K, Okada M et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34419248/
- **PECATI: design of a single-arm phase II of pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib in pretreated B3 thymoma and thymic carcinoma (n=43, Clin Lung Cancer).** There is no standard after platinum. It is a design paper with no results. Remon J, Girard N, Novello S et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34393061/
