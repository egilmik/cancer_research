### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers
- **Neoadjuvant gefitinib in resectable stage II–IIIA EGFR-mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase II, Shanghai, n=33 ITT).** Gefitinib was given for 42 days before surgery. ORR was 54.5% (95% CI 37.7–70.7) and major pathological response 24.2% (95% CI 11.9–40.4). Median DFS was 33.5 months, and patients with MPR had longer DFS. There were no grade 3–4 adverse events, but 4 of 33 had postoperative chylothorax. It is an early signal for neoadjuvant TKI, a question now addressed by NeoADAURA (below). It is small, single-centre and uncontrolled, with a first-generation TKI. Zhang Y, Fu F, Hu H et al. *J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg*, 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32340810/
- **Capmatinib in MET-dysregulated NSCLC: biomarker analysis of the phase I expansion (n=55).** Overall ORR was 20% (95% CI 10.4–33.0). In patients with MET gene copy number ≥ 6 (n=15), ORR was 47% and median PFS 9.3 months (95% CI 3.8–11.9). All four patients with METex14 responded. MET GCN ≥ 6 and METex14 emerged as the selection markers later used in GEOMETRY mono-1. It is a small phase I with heterogeneous biomarker definitions. Schuler M, Berardi R, Lim WT et al. *Ann Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32240796/
- **LOGiK1401 update: alectinib in ALK-positive NSCLC with poor performance status (phase II, n=18, PS 2–4).** At 27.3 months' median follow-up, median PFS was 16.2 months (95% CI 7.1–30.8), median OS 30.3 months and 3-year OS 43.8%. PFS was similar with and without baseline CNS metastases. It supports giving alectinib to patients considered too frail for chemotherapy. The sample is very small and single-arm. Iwama E, Goto Y, Murakami H et al. *Oncologist*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32297438/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals
- **MYSTIC: first-line durvalumab ± tremelimumab vs platinum chemotherapy (phase 3, n=1118).** In PD-L1 ≥ 25% (n=488), none of the primary endpoints was met:
  - durvalumab vs chemotherapy: OS 16.3 vs 12.9 months (HR 0.76, 97.54% CI 0.56–1.02; not significant)
  - durvalumab + tremelimumab vs chemotherapy: OS 11.9 vs 12.9 months (HR 0.85), PFS 3.9 vs 5.4 months (HR 1.05)

  In an exploratory analysis, blood TMB ≥ 20 mut/Mb was associated with OS 21.9 vs 10.0 months for the combination (HR 0.49). Grade ≥ 3 treatment-related AEs were 14.9%, 22.9% and 33.8%. It is a negative trial; the bTMB finding is hypothesis-generating and was not confirmed prospectively. Rizvi NA, Cho BC, Reinmuth N et al. *JAMA Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32271377/
- **IMpower131: atezolizumab + carboplatin/nab-paclitaxel in first-line squamous NSCLC (phase 3, n=1021).** PFS improved (6.3 vs 5.6 months, HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.60–0.85), but final OS did not (14.2 vs 13.5 months, HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.73–1.05; p=0.16). An OS signal in PD-L1-high tumours (HR 0.48) was not formally tested. Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs were 68.0% vs 57.5%. Without an OS benefit, this regimen did not displace pembrolizumab-chemotherapy (KEYNOTE-407) in squamous disease. Jotte R, Cappuzzo F, Vynnychenko I et al. *J Thorac Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32302702/
- **IFCT-1103 ULTIMATE: weekly paclitaxel + bevacizumab vs docetaxel in second/third-line non-squamous NSCLC (phase 3, n=166).** PFS was 5.4 vs 3.9 months (adjusted HR 0.61, 95% CI 0.44–0.86) and ORR 22.5% vs 5.5%, but OS was similar (adjusted HR 1.17). Grade 3–4 AEs were 45.9% vs 54.5%, with less neutropenia and more neuropathy and hypertension. It gives an alternative to docetaxel after platinum. Patients were largely immunotherapy-naive, the trial was open-label with 2:1 randomisation, and there was no OS gain. Cortot AB, Audigier-Valette C, Molinier O et al. *Eur J Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32276179/
- **PROLUNG: pembrolizumab + docetaxel vs docetaxel in immunotherapy-naive pretreated NSCLC (randomised phase 2, Mexico, n=78).** ORR was 42.5% vs 15.8% (OR 3.94, 95% CI 1.34–11.54) and PFS 9.5 vs 3.9 months (HR 0.24, 95% CI 0.13–0.46), including in EGFR-mutant patients (PFS 6.8 vs 3.5 months). Pneumonitis was more frequent with the combination (23% vs 5%). It is a small trial with an ORR primary endpoint, in a setting where most patients now get immunotherapy first-line. Arrieta O, Barrón F, Ramírez-Tirado LA et al. *JAMA Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32271354/
- **Pembrolizumab for untreated NSCLC brain metastases (phase 2, Yale, n=42).** In PD-L1 ≥ 1% (n=37), the brain metastasis response rate was 29.7% (95% CI 15.9–47.0). There were no responses in PD-L1 < 1%. Treatment-related serious AEs occurred in 14%, with no deaths. It supports systemic immunotherapy without upfront brain radiotherapy in selected asymptomatic, PD-L1-positive patients. It is single-centre, uses a non-standard dose (10 mg/kg) and has short follow-up (8.3 months). Goldberg SB, Schalper KA, Gettinger SN et al. *Lancet Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32251621/
- **CRISPR–Cas9 PD-1-edited T cells in refractory NSCLC (first-in-human phase I, n=12 treated of 22 enrolled).** All treatment-related AEs were grade 1–2, and edited cells were detectable in blood. Median PFS was 7.7 weeks and median OS 42.6 weeks. The median off-target mutation frequency was 0.05%. It shows feasibility of gene-edited T cells, but efficacy was minimal and manufacturing failed in 10 of 22 patients. Lu Y, Xue J, Deng T et al. *Nat Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32341578/
- **ESTRO-ASTRO consensus: lung cancer radiotherapy during the COVID-19 pandemic.** Thirty-two experts used a modified Delphi process covering six common cases (stage I NSCLC, locally advanced NSCLC, PORT after pN2, LS-SCLC thoracic RT and PCI, palliative RT) in two pandemic scenarios. In risk mitigation, guideline radiotherapy should not be compromised, and treatment of COVID-positive patients is generally postponed. In resource shortage, triage relies on curability, benefit of RT, life expectancy and performance status, with hypofractionation where possible. These are temporary pandemic recommendations, not a permanent change. Guckenberger M, Belka C, Bezjak A et al. *Radiother Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32342863/

### sclc
- **Lurbinectedin second-line in SCLC (single-arm phase 2 basket, n=105).** ORR by investigator was 35.2% (95% CI 26.2–45.2) at 17.1 months' median follow-up. Grade 3–4 neutropenia occurred in 46%, febrile neutropenia in 5%, and there were no treatment-related deaths. This trial was the basis for FDA accelerated approval in June 2020 (the approval itself is not in this item). It is single-arm, and patients with brain metastases were excluded. Trigo J, Subbiah V, Besse B et al. *Lancet Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32224306/
- **NABSTER: nab-paclitaxel in relapsed SCLC (phase 2, n=68).** Partial response was 8% in refractory and 14% in sensitive disease. Median PFS was 1.8–1.9 months and median OS 3.6 vs 6.6 months. The primary endpoint was not met. It is a negative trial with a tolerable safety profile. Gelsomino F, Tiseo M, Barbieri F et al. *Br J Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32346071/

### lung-toxicity
- **Immune-related ILD on anti-PD-1 in NSCLC (prospective multicentre cohort, Japan, n=138).** Ir-ILD occurred in 14.5%, about three times the rate in trials, at a median of 51.5 days. All high-grade cases occurred within 60 days. Impaired spirometry (low FVC and FEV1) and baseline dyspnoea (mMRC) predicted risk. It argues for baseline spirometry and close early monitoring. It is a Japanese cohort, where ILD rates are known to be higher. Suzuki Y, Karayama M, Uto T et al. *J Thorac Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32289515/

### lung-screening / lung-ctdna-mrd
- **Lung-CLiP: cfDNA-based early lung cancer detection (Nature).** The authors improved CAPP-Seq and showed that ctDNA is present before treatment in most early-stage lung cancers and is strongly prognostic. Most cfDNA mutations in patients and risk-matched controls came from clonal haematopoiesis, and these can be told apart by fragment length and smoking signatures. A machine-learning classifier (Lung-CLiP) was validated prospectively against risk-matched controls, with performance close to tumour-informed detection. It is a strong methodological step for blood-based screening. Cohorts are modest, and it is not yet tested as a screening test in a screening population. Chabon JJ, Hamilton EG, Kurtz DM et al. *Nature*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32269342/
- **K-LUCAS: Korean national LDCT screening project (prospective multicentre cohort, n=11,394).** This secondary analysis found 16% positive screens by Lung-RADS and 0.6% lung cancers. Tuberculosis sequelae were seen in 13% and were associated with more positive results. It is relevant to screening performance where TB is endemic. The participants were almost all men, and there are no mortality or stage-shift outcomes. Kim H, Kim HY, Goo JM et al. *Radiology*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32286195/
- **COPD and lung cancer in never smokers (Korean national cohort, n=338,548).** Over 2.36 million person-years, COPD in never smokers carried an adjusted HR for lung cancer of 2.67 (95% CI 2.09–3.40), higher than smoking without COPD (HR 1.97). It supports considering COPD as a risk factor in screening eligibility regardless of smoking history. It relies on claims-based COPD diagnosis, with possible residual confounding (passive smoking, exposures). Park HY, Kang D, Shin SH et al. *Thorax*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32241883/

### car-t-solid
- **Intrathecal CAR-T for metastatic medulloblastoma and ependymoma (preclinical, Nat Med).** EPHA2, HER2 and IL13Rα2 were identified as targets expressed on these tumours but not on normal developing brain. CAR-T cells given into the CSF were effective against primary, metastatic and recurrent xenografts, also combined with azacytidine. It provides the rationale for locoregional CAR-T trials in paediatric CNS tumours. These are mouse models only. Donovan LK, Delaidelli A, Joseph SK et al. *Nat Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32341580/
- **Locoregional B7-H3 CAR-T for atypical teratoid/rhabdoid tumours (preclinical, Nat Med).** ATRTs strongly express B7-H3. Intracerebroventricular or intratumoural B7-H3 CAR-T cells worked faster and more potently than IV delivery, with lower systemic cytokines, and gave protection against rechallenge. It supports locoregional B7-H3 CAR-T trials. These are mouse models only. Theruvath J, Sotillo E, Mount CW et al. *Nat Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32341579/
