## Practice-changing

- **IMpower010: adjuvant atezolizumab after adjuvant chemotherapy vs best supportive care in resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC (randomised open-label phase 3, 227 sites in 22 countries, Lancet).** Of 1,280 patients enrolled after complete resection, 1,005 were randomised after one to four cycles of platinum chemotherapy to atezolizumab 1200 mg every 21 days for 16 cycles or 1 year (n=507) or best supportive care, meaning observation with scans (n=498). The primary endpoint, investigator-assessed DFS, was tested hierarchically. At a median follow-up of 32.2 months, DFS improved in stage II–IIIA with PD-L1 ≥1% (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.50–0.88; p=0.0039), in all stage II–IIIA (HR 0.79, 0.64–0.96; p=0.020), and in the ITT stage IB–IIIA population (HR 0.81, 0.67–0.99; p=0.040). Atezolizumab-related grade 3–4 AEs occurred in 11% and grade 5 in 1% (4 patients). OS is not reported in the abstract, and the benefit is largest in PD-L1-positive tumours. The abstract says nothing on regulatory status or reimbursement in Norway. Felip E, Altorki N, Zhou C et al. Lancet 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34555333/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **HER3-DXd (patritumab deruxtecan) in EGFR-TKI-resistant EGFR-mutant NSCLC (phase I dose escalation/expansion, n=57 at 5.6 mg/kg, Cancer Discov).** Confirmed ORR by blinded central review was 39% (95% CI 26.0–52.4), and median PFS was 8.2 months (4.4–8.3). Responses occurred with known and unknown TKI resistance mechanisms and across a broad range of HER3 expression. Haematological toxicity was the most common grade ≥3 event. The study is early phase and single-arm. Jänne PA, Baik C, Su WC et al. Cancer Discov 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34548309/
- **TREM brain metastasis subgroup: osimertinib in relapsed EGFR-mutant NSCLC with and without T790M (Nordic phase II, 48 of 199 with brain metastases, Acta Oncol).** Intracranial PFS was 39.7 months with T790M vs 3.5 months without (p<0.001), and intracranial DCR was 89% vs 55%. 73% had prior whole-brain RT. It is a small subgroup analysis; the authors conclude other options should be considered for T790M-negative patients with brain metastases. Eide IJZ, Grut H, Helland Å et al. Acta Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34486915/
- **FLAURA2 safety run-in: osimertinib plus platinum-pemetrexed first line (n=30, ESMO Open).** AEs occurred in 90%, and serious AEs in 20% in each group. One patient stopped all treatment for pneumonitis, and haematological toxicity was manageable. This supported moving to the randomised phase; there are no efficacy data. Planchard D, Feng PH, Karaseva N et al. ESMO Open 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34543864/
- **TORG1632: low-dose afatinib (20 mg) first line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=53, median age 70, Lung Cancer).** Median PFS was 12.6 months (90% CI 9.7–14.3), meeting the primary endpoint, with ORR 66.0% and grade ≥3 AEs 22.6%. The study is single-arm and its comparison with full-dose afatinib is indirect. Noro R, Igawa S, Bessho A et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34536731/
- **Structure-based classification of EGFR mutations (Nature).** In 16,715 patients with EGFR-mutant NSCLC, mutations fell into four structure/sensitivity groups. These retrospectively predicted outcomes on EGFR inhibitors better than exon-based groups, which may guide drug choice for atypical mutations. The clinical validation is retrospective. Robichaux JP, Le X, Vijayan RSK et al. Nature 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34526717/
- **Furmonertinib: First Approval (Drugs profile).** Furmonertinib was approved in China in March 2021 for locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC with EGFR T790M after progression on an EGFR TKI. This is a profile, not the decision itself. Deeks ED Drugs 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34528187/

### nsclc-drivers
- **DESTINY-Lung01: trastuzumab deruxtecan in previously treated HER2-mutant NSCLC (phase 2, n=91, NEJM).** Centrally confirmed ORR was 55% (95% CI 44–65), median DoR 9.3 months, PFS 8.2 months and OS 17.8 months. Grade ≥3 drug-related AEs occurred in 46%, and adjudicated ILD in 26% with 2 deaths. Responses occurred across HER2 mutation subtypes. It is single-arm, and ILD is a major toxicity. Li BT, Smit EF, Goto Y et al. N Engl J Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34534430/
- **ALTA-1L final analysis: brigatinib vs crizotinib in ALK-inhibitor-naive ALK+ NSCLC (phase 3, n=275, JTO).** At a median follow-up of 40.4 months, BIRC PFS was 24.0 vs 11.1 months (HR 0.48, 95% CI 0.35–0.66), and 3-year PFS 43% vs 19%. OS was not reached in either arm (HR 0.81, 0.53–1.22). A post-hoc OS signal in baseline brain metastases (HR 0.43) is exploratory. Crizotinib is no longer the standard comparator. Camidge DR, Kim HR, Ahn MJ et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34537440/
- **eXalt3: ensartinib vs crizotinib first line in ALK+ NSCLC (randomised phase 3, n=290, JAMA Oncol).** ITT median PFS was 25.8 vs 12.7 months (HR 0.51, 95% CI 0.35–0.72). The intracranial response rate was 63.6% vs 21.1%, in only 11 and 19 patients with target lesions. OS is not reported in the abstract, and the comparator is no longer standard. Horn L, Wang Z, Wu G et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34473194/
- **Poziotinib in HER2 exon 20 mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=30, JCO).** Confirmed ORR was 27% (95% CI 12–46), median DoR 5.0 months, PFS 5.5 months and OS 15 months. Grade 3 rash occurred in 47% and diarrhoea in 20%, with one possible treatment-related pneumonitis death. Responses are modest and short and toxicity is high. Elamin YY, Robichaux JP, Carter BW et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34550757/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy
- **Revised STARS: SABR for operable stage IA NSCLC (single-arm prospective, n=80, with a prespecified propensity-matched VATS lobectomy cohort, Lancet Oncol).** At a median follow-up of 5.1 years, OS was 91% at 3 years and 87% at 5 years, vs 91% and 84% after VATS lobectomy with mediastinal dissection (HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.45–1.65). There was no grade 4–5 toxicity. Non-inferiority was claimed against a non-randomised comparison. Chang JY, Mehran RJ, Feng L et al. Lancet Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34529930/
- **WJTOG0101: adjuvant gemcitabine vs uracil-tegafur after complete resection of stage IB–IIIA NSCLC (phase III, n=608, Japan).** After a median follow-up of 6.8 years, 5-year OS was 70.0% vs 68.8% (HR 0.948, 95% CI 0.73–1.23). The trial is negative, and the uracil-tegafur comparator is used mainly in Japan. Yamaguchi M, Tada H, Mitsudomi T et al. Int J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34463869/
- **JASPER: first-line niraparib plus pembrolizumab in advanced NSCLC (phase 2, n=38, Cancer).** ORR was 56.3% with PD-L1 ≥50% (n=16) and 20.0% with PD-L1 1–49% (n=20). Median PFS was 8.4 and 4.2 months. Grade ≥3 TEAEs occurred in 88.2% and 85.7%. It is small and single-arm, with no comparator. Ramalingam SS, Thara E, Awad MM et al. Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34478166/
- **Concurrent or sequential ipilimumab-nivolumab with multisite SBRT in stage IV NSCLC (phase 1, n=37, JTO).** There were no DLTs with concurrent treatment. The sequential central-lung group needed a dose reduction after 2 grade 4 pneumonitis events. ORR was 46% (2 CR, 15 PR) and median PFS 5.8 months at a median follow-up of 17 months. It is early phase and uncontrolled. Bestvina CM, Pointer KB, Karrison T et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34500113/
- **CheckMate 73L: design of a phase 3 of nivolumab plus concurrent CRT, then nivolumab with or without ipilimumab, vs CRT then durvalumab in stage III NSCLC (Clin Lung Cancer).** The primary endpoints are PFS and OS (arm A vs C). It is a design paper with no results. De Ruysscher D, Ramalingam S, Urbanic J et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34489161/
- **ESMO Clinical Practice Guidelines update: early and locally advanced NSCLC, diagnosis, staging, systemic and local therapy (Ann Oncol).** Published in letter format with no abstract on PubMed, so the changed recommendations cannot be reported here. Remon J, Soria JC, Peters S et al. Ann Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34481037/
- **American Radium Society appropriate use criteria for RT in oligometastatic or oligoprogressive NSCLC (IJROBP).** The panel defines oligometastasis as ≤3 deposits and strongly recommends trial enrolment. It prefers first-line systemic therapy followed by consolidative RT to all sites, including the primary, over up-front RT. Consolidative RT for 4–5 sites, driver-positive disease and oligoprogression is case by case. The recommendations rest on few randomised trials. Amini A, Verma V, Simone CB et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34571054/

### sclc
- **STIMULI: consolidation nivolumab-ipilimumab vs observation after CRT in limited-stage SCLC (randomised phase II, n=153 randomised, Ann Oncol).** PFS did not improve (median 10.7 vs 14.5 months, HR 1.02, 95% CI 0.66–1.58), nor did OS at 35 months (HR 0.95, 0.59–1.52). Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 62% vs 25%, with 4 vs 1 fatal. The median time to treatment discontinuation was only 1.7 months. The trial is negative. Peters S, Pujol JL, Dafni U et al. Ann Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34562610/

### lung-repurposed
- **Acid suppression with EGFR-TKIs (systematic review and meta-analysis, 14 studies, 4,010 patients, Transl Lung Cancer Res).** Acid suppression was used by 30.7%. TKI alone was associated with better OS (HR 1.46, 95% CI 1.27–1.72) and PFS (HR 1.63, 1.35–1.98) than TKI plus acid suppression, driven by PPIs; H2 antagonists showed no significant difference. The pooled studies are observational and largely on first-generation TKIs, so confounding by indication is likely. Sim W, Jain SR, Lim WH et al. Transl Lung Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34584857/
- **Denosumab plus first-line chemotherapy in advanced NSCLC (pooled patient-level analysis of SPLENDOUR and AMGEN-249, n=740).** There was no OS benefit (9.2 vs 9.9 months, HR 0.98, 95% CI 0.82–1.18) and no PFS benefit. An OS benefit in squamous histology (HR 0.70, 0.49–0.98) is a subgroup finding. The pooled trials are negative. Peters S, Danson S, Ejedepang D et al. Lung Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34543941/

### lung-screening
- **Genomic and evolutionary classification of lung cancer in never smokers (WGS of 232 tumours, Nat Genet).** Three subtypes emerged: piano (dominant, low TMB, UBA1 mutations, germline AR variants, slow growth), mezzo-forte (amplifications and EGFR mutations) and forte (whole-genome doubling). No strong tobacco signature was seen, even with secondhand smoke exposure. Five alterations independently doubled mortality. It is descriptive, and the clinical implications are not yet tested. Zhang T, Joubert P, Ansari-Pour N et al. Nat Genet 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34493867/

### lung-vaccines-cell
- **Racotumomab (anti-idiotype NeuGcGM3 vaccine) in routine practice (n=71, Cuba, MEDICC Rev).** 84.5% had no AEs. Median OS from first immunisation was 24.5 months, 17.2 months with switch maintenance and 6.8 months second line. It is an uncontrolled observational series, so the survival figures cannot be attributed to the vaccine. Cáceres-Lavernia HH, Nenínger-Vinageras E, Varona-Rodríguez LM et al. MEDICC Rev 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34516533/

### thoracic-other
- **RAMES: gemcitabine with or without ramucirumab second line in pleural mesothelioma (randomised double-blind phase 2, n=161, Italy, Lancet Oncol).** Median OS was 13.8 vs 7.5 months (HR 0.71, 70% CI 0.59–0.85; p=0.028). Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 44% vs 30%, mainly neutropenia and hypertension. It is phase 2, with a 70% CI, and a confirmatory trial is not reported. Pinto C, Zucali PA, Pagano M et al. Lancet Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34499874/
- **FDA approval summary: nivolumab plus ipilimumab first line for unresectable pleural mesothelioma (CCR).** The FDA approved it on 2 October 2020 based on CheckMate 743 (OS 18.1 vs 14.1 months, HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.61–0.89). The benefit was larger in non-epithelioid histology, and it was the first FDA-approved MPM regimen since 2004. This summarises an earlier decision, already indexed as practice-changing via the CheckMate 743 publication. Nakajima EC, Vellanki PJ, Larkins E et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34462287/
- **GOECP/SEOR clinical guideline on radiotherapy for pleural mesothelioma (Spanish Society of Radiation Oncology, World J Clin Oncol).** It reviews the evidence on RT with surgery and chemotherapy, volumes, techniques and reirradiation. The abstract does not state specific recommendations. Luna J, Bobo A, Cabrera-Rodriguez JJ et al. World J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34513595/
