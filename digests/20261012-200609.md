### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy

- **CALGB 30610/RTOG 0538 (phase 3, LS-SCLC).** 638 patients were randomised to 70 Gy once daily or 45 Gy twice daily with chemotherapy. At 4.7 years median follow-up, OS was not improved: HR 0.94 (95% CI 0.76–1.17), median 30.1 vs 28.5 months, 5-year OS 32% vs 29%. Severe toxicity was similar. It was a superiority trial, so it does not formally establish equivalence, although once-daily dosing remains a reasonable option. Bogart J, Wang X, Masters G et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36623230/
- **ESMO Clinical Practice Guideline: non-oncogene-addicted metastatic NSCLC.** This is the updated ESMO guideline for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up. There is no abstract, so the changed recommendations cannot be stated here. Hendriks LE, Kerr KM, Menis J et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36669645/
- **Chinese clinical practice guideline for stage IV primary lung cancer (2023 edition).** It comes from two Chinese oncology associations. The abstract describes scope only. Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36709116/
- **CameL (phase 3) extended follow-up.** 412 patients with non-squamous NSCLC were randomised to camrelizumab plus carboplatin/pemetrexed or chemotherapy alone. Median OS was 27.1 vs 19.8 months (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.57–0.92), despite 45.9% crossover (crossover-adjusted HR 0.55). The comparator is no longer standard, and the drug is China-only. Zhou C, Chen G, Huang Y et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36646210/
- **KEYNOTE-042 biomarker analysis (exploratory).** Among 793 patients with WES data, tTMB ≥175 mutations/exome favoured pembrolizumab over chemotherapy for OS (HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.48–0.80), while tTMB <175 did not (HR 1.09). STK11, KEAP1 and KRAS mutations did not remove the benefit. The analysis is retrospective and exploratory. Mok TSK, Lopes G, Cho BC et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36709038/
- **LungMAP S1400I quality of life (randomised phase 3).** Nivolumab plus ipilimumab vs nivolumab in squamous NSCLC (n=158 evaluable) showed no difference in symptom severity at 7 or 13 weeks. High appetite loss plus dyspnoea predicted progression or death (HR 3.06). This is a QoL secondary report of a negative trial. Unger JM, Qian L, Redman MW et al. J Natl Cancer Inst 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36625510/
- **PET-Boost (randomised phase 2, n=107).** Dose escalation to the whole tumour or to a PET-defined subvolume both met the 1-year local control objective (97% and 91%). However, grade 5 toxicity occurred in 18% overall. Neither approach is ready for practice. Cooke SA, de Ruysscher D, Reymen B et al. Radiother Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36706958/
- **RVlob RCT (single centre, n=320).** Robotic and video-assisted lobectomy were compared. Pain at week 4 was slightly lower after robotic surgery, but QLQ-C30/LC13 and EQ-5D did not differ through 48 weeks. Jin R, Zhang Z, Zheng Y et al. Chest 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36621757/
- **BRACHY RCT (n=134, closed early).** Adding endobronchial HDR brachytherapy to palliative EBRT did not improve symptom response at 6 weeks (29.9% vs 28.4%). The trial was underpowered (planned n=250). Sur R, Pond G, Falkson C et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36610615/
- **Induction chemo-immunotherapy before CRT for bulky stage III NSCLC (retrospective, n=75).** 2-year OS was 75.1% and median PFS 30.6 months. Grade ≥3 pneumonitis occurred in 9.3% and was associated with worse OS. The study is a single-centre retrospective. Wang Y, Zhang T, Wang J et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36623605/
- **French Delphi consensus on non-bulky stage IIIA N2 NSCLC.** It favours surgery with (neo)adjuvant chemotherapy for single-station N2, and CRT followed by durvalumab for multi-station disease or when surgery is less suitable. It is expert consensus, not a society guideline. Scherpereel A, Martin E, Brouchet L et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36682142/
- **ctDNA dynamics in a phase 2 trial of sintilimab plus docetaxel, second line (n=33).** ctDNA clearance at 6 weeks tracked tumour shrinkage and survival. The trial is small and single-arm. Han X, Tang X, Zhu H et al. J Immunother Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36600554/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers

- **Rezivertinib phase 2a, first line (n=43).** ORR by BICR was 83.7% and median PFS 20.7 months. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 9.3%. It is a new third-generation EGFR-TKI, the trial is single-arm, and OS is immature. Shi Y, Zhou J, Zhao Y et al. BMC Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36617560/
- **Oleclumab plus osimertinib (phase 1b/2) in T790M-negative disease after first/second-generation TKI (n=26).** ORR was 11.8–25% and median PFS 7.4 months at the RP2D, so activity is modest. Kim DW, Kim SW, Camidge DR et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36641093/
- **Afatinib plus osimertinib after osimertinib resistance (phase 1, n=13).** RR was 7.7%, with diarrhoea and rash, and the combination showed limited activity. Miura S, Koh Y, Azuma K et al. BMC Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36597021/
- **Chinese expert consensus on adjuvant icotinib.** It is consensus guidance on postoperative icotinib use. Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36709117/
- **Ensartinib phase 1 (n=48, China).** RP2D is 225 mg. In TKI-naive ALK+ patients, ORR was 81.3% and median PFS 25.7 months. Rash occurred in 87.5%. Ma Y, Pan H, Liu Y et al. J Thorac Dis 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36647478/
- **Selpercatinib and CNS disease (LIBRETTO-001/201, n=61).** No CNS progression occurred in patients without baseline CNS metastases up to 36 months. With baseline CNS disease, cumulative CNS progression was 20% at 36 months. This is a secondary analysis. Murciano-Goroff YR, Falcon CJ, Lin ST et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36657661/
- **BFAST cohort B: high-dose alectinib in RET fusion-positive NSCLC (n=8).** Activity was limited and the cohort closed once selective RET inhibitors arrived. Dziadziuszko R, Peled N, Mok T et al. Contemp Oncol (Pozn) 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38405208/
- **Adagrasib (Krazati) for NSCLC.** This is a Medical Letter drug profile after the FDA accelerated approval for KRAS G12C NSCLC. There is no abstract. Med Lett Drugs Ther 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36651795/

### sclc / lung-nordic

- **Tarlatamab phase 1 (n=107, relapsed SCLC).** ORR was 23.4% (2 CR), median DoR 12.3 months, PFS 3.7 months and OS 13.2 months. CRS occurred in 52%, nearly all grade 1–2, and there was one grade 5 pneumonitis. This is the first-in-class DLL3 BiTE, and the data are uncontrolled early-phase. Paz-Ares L, Champiat S, Lai WV et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36689692/
- **Older patients in the Norwegian randomised phase 2 trial of 45 vs 60 Gy twice-daily TRT in LS-SCLC (n=170, 53 aged ≥70).** Older patients had similar treatment completion, toxicity, response and PFS but shorter OS. The authors conclude that age alone should not preclude standard twice-daily chemoradiotherapy. This is a secondary analysis. Killingberg KT, Grønberg BH, Slaaen M et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36716960/

### lung-screening

- **Sybil (deep learning, single LDCT).** The model predicted 1-year lung cancer risk with AUC 0.92 (NLST hold-out), 0.86 (MGH) and 0.94 (Chang Gung, including never-smokers), with 6-year C-indices of 0.75–0.81. Validation was retrospective, and its clinical use is untested. Mikhael PG, Wohlwend J, Yala A et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36634294/
- **Anhui LDCT screening RCT (baseline round).** 600 participants were screened. Cancer incidence was 4.1% in female never/passive smokers vs 1.0% in smokers, and 91% of cancers were stage 0–I. The report is baseline-only, without mortality data. Rong F, Shi R, Hu L et al. Front Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36591449/
- **Chinese expert consensus on diagnosis of early lung cancer (2023).** It comes from the Lung Cancer Group of the Chinese Thoracic Society. Zhonghua Jie He He Hu Xi Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36617923/
- **Slovak national lung cancer screening implementation guideline.** It sets out the methodology for a national LDCT programme. Juskanic D, Sandor F, Denkova L et al. Bratisl Lek Listy 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36598297/

### car-t-solid / lung-vaccines-cell / thoracic-other

- **GD2-specific 4SCAR-T in glioblastoma (n=8).** 4 PRs lasted 3–24 months, median OS was 10 months and there were no severe AEs. GD2 antigen loss was seen after infusion. The study is very small. Liu Z, Zhou J, Yang X et al. Mol Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36617554/
- **Locoregional intracranial CAR-T in children (Seattle).** This reports the procedure and catheter safety across 307 intracranial doses. It has no efficacy data. Vitanza NA, Ronsley R, Choe M et al. Neoplasia 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36599192/
- **Intravenous oncolytic Coxsackievirus A21 (V937) ± pembrolizumab (phase 1).** ORR in the NSCLC expansion cohort (n=43) was 9%, and there were few grade ≥3 AEs. Activity is low. Rudin CM, Pandha HS, Zibelman M et al. J Immunother Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36669791/
- **Avelumab plus SBRT in pretreated pleural mesothelioma (phase 1, n=13).** The combination was tolerable, with 4 grade 3 avelumab-related AEs and no grade 4–5. Efficacy is not established. Rimner A, Adusumilli PS, Offin MD et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36590015/
