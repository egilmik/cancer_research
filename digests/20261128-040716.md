### lung-key-evidence

- [Optimizing early and locally advanced NSCLC: A Portuguese expert consensus on treatment pathways](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43100223/): A 12-member multidisciplinary Portuguese panel (4 pulmonologists/oncologists, 4 thoracic surgeons, 4 radiation oncologists) produced consensus statements by iterative discussion and voting. Consensus covered mandatory molecular testing at diagnosis and re-evaluation, standardised resectability criteria and integration of immunotherapy and targeted therapy, with MDT discussion throughout. Limitations: expert opinion, no outcome data, and the abstract does not state which recommendation changed from prior practice. Dias M, Simões S, Estevinho F et al., Pulmonology, 2026.

### car-t-solid — new trial registration

- [NCT07960745](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07960745) (registered, posted 2026-11-27, not yet recruiting): Phase 1/2, n=185, sponsor NCI. Autologous PBL engineered with drug-regulatable membrane-bound IL-12 (DRIM-IL-12) plus TCRs against neoantigens (e.g. KRAS, p53) in metastatic solid tumours including NSCLC, colorectal, breast, ovarian, GI and GU cancers. Arms/interventions: cyclophosphamide, fludarabine, DRIM-IL12-TCR PBL, lenalidomide. Early-phase safety/activity study; no results.
