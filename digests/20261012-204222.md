## Practice-changing

- **IPSOS: first-line atezolizumab vs single-agent chemotherapy in platinum-ineligible advanced NSCLC (open-label phase 3, Lancet 2023).**
  - **Design:** 453 patients with stage IIIB–IV NSCLC were randomised 2:1 across 91 sites in 23 countries. All were unsuitable for platinum doublets: ECOG PS 2–3, or age ≥70 with PS 0–1 and substantial comorbidity. Arms were atezolizumab 1,200 mg every 3 weeks vs vinorelbine or gemcitabine.
  - **Primary endpoint (OS):** It improved, 10.3 vs 9.2 months (stratified HR 0.78, 95% CI 0.63–0.97, P = .028). 2-year OS doubled, 24% vs 12%.
  - **Toxicity and QoL:** Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 16% vs 33% and treatment-related deaths in 1% vs 3%. HRQoL was stabilised or improved.
  - **Limitations and still open:**
    - The absolute median OS gain is about one month.
    - It is open-label, and PD-L1 was not a selection criterion.
    - The comparator is single-agent chemotherapy, the usual standard for this frail group.
    - EU label status and Norwegian use in PS 2–3 patients are not covered by this item.
  - Lee SM, Schulz C, Prabhash K et al. Lancet 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37423228/
- **ASCO Guideline Rapid Recommendation Update: management of stage III NSCLC (J Clin Oncol 2023).**
  - **Lookup note:** The abstract only describes the rapid-update process. These changed recommendations come from a lookup (ASCO Post, 25 August 2023, and the ASCO summary table), as for the 2022 rapid update already in the index.
  - **Recommendation 3.2:** Patients with stage III NSCLC planned for resection should receive neoadjuvant chemoimmunotherapy, neoadjuvant chemotherapy or neoadjuvant concurrent chemoradiation (high-quality evidence, strong recommendation). It is based on CheckMate 816 and KEYNOTE-671.
  - **Recommendation 4.2:** Patients with resected stage III NSCLC with EGFR ex19del or L858R should be offered adjuvant osimertinib after platinum-based chemotherapy. It is based on ADAURA, whose OS paper appeared in the previous run.
  - **Still open:** Neoadjuvant-only vs perioperative regimens, and consolidation osimertinib after chemoradiation, which came in the 2024 update.
  - Singh N, Daly ME, Ismaila N et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37471673/

### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **IMpower010 first interim OS (open-label phase III, n=1,005).** At 45.3 months median follow-up, OS showed no difference in the stage IB–IIIA ITT population (HR 0.995, 95% CI 0.78–1.28). HRs were 0.95 in stage II–IIIA, 0.71 (0.49–1.03) in stage II–IIIA PD-L1 ≥1%, and 0.43 (0.24–0.78) in PD-L1 ≥50%. OS is immature, and the PD-L1 subgroups are exploratory. Felip E, Altorki N, Zhou C et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37467930/
- **I-SABR (open-label randomised phase 2, n=156, three Texas hospitals, Lancet).** SABR plus 4 cycles of nivolumab vs SABR alone in stage I–II node-negative or isolated parenchymal recurrent NSCLC gave 4-year EFS 77% vs 53% (per-protocol HR 0.38; ITT HR 0.42, 95% CI 0.22–0.80). Grade 3 immune AEs occurred in 15% and there was no grade 3 pneumonitis. It is phase 2 and needs phase 3 confirmation. Chang JY, Lin SH, Dong W et al. Lancet 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37478883/
- **ASCO living guideline v2023.2, stage IV NSCLC with driver alterations.** The abstract states no changes. A lookup (ASCO podcast description) suggests new second-line recommendations for EGFR exon 20 insertions and for KRAS G12C (sotorasib, after CodeBreaK 200), but the exact wording was not confirmed, so it is rated high. Owen DH, Singh N, Ismaila N et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37433095/
- **ORION (double-blind randomised phase 2, n=269).** Maintenance durvalumab plus olaparib vs durvalumab plus placebo after durvalumab-platinum induction gave PFS 7.2 vs 5.3 months (HR 0.76, P = .074, not significant). Grade 3–4 AEs occurred in 34% vs 18%. It is negative. Ahn MJ, Bondarenko I, Kalinka E et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37390980/
- **KEYNOTE-495/KeyImPaCT (biomarker-directed, adaptively randomised phase 2).** First-line pembrolizumab with lenvatinib, quavonlimab (CTLA-4) or favezelimab (LAG-3) was allocated by T-cell-inflamed GEP and TMB. ORR was 50–60% in GEP-non-low/TMB-high tumours, and only pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib met its threshold in group III. It is an interim, non-comparative analysis. Gutierrez M, Lam WS, Hellmann MD et al. Nat Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37429923/
- **CHEERS (randomised phase 2, n=99, 5 Belgian hospitals, mixed tumours incl. NSCLC).** Adding SBRT 3×8 Gy to ≤3 lesions to ICI did not improve PFS (2.8 vs 4.4 months, HR 0.95) or OS (HR 0.82). It is a negative trial and not lung-specific. Spaas M, Sundahl N, Kruse V et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37410476/
- **KN046, PD-L1/CTLA-4 bispecific, after platinum (phase II, n=64).** ORR was 13.3–14.7% and PFS 3.7 months, with OS 19.7 months at 3 mg/kg. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 42%. It is single-arm. Xiong A, Li W, Li X et al. Eur J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37393762/
- **PPI use and first-line ICI in PD-L1 ≥50% NSCLC (Japanese multicentre retrospective study, n=425).** PPI history was associated with shorter PFS on pembrolizumab monotherapy (HR 1.38). Among PPI users, ICI plus chemotherapy gave longer PFS (HR 0.38) and OS (HR 0.43) than monotherapy. It is observational, with confounding by indication likely. Kawachi H, Yamada T, Tamiya M et al. JAMA Netw Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37432682/

### nsclc-drivers / lung-ctdna-mrd

- **First-line pyrotinib in HER2-mutant NSCLC (patient-centric phase 2, criteria-fulfilled cohort n=28).** ORR was 35.7%, PFS 7.3 months and OS 14.3 months. Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 10.7%. It is single-arm and small. Liu SM, Tu HY, Wei XW et al. Nat Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37488286/
- **ACCELERATE: ctDNA genotyping before tissue diagnosis (single-group nonrandomised trial, n=150, Toronto).** In advanced non-squamous NSCLC, time from referral to treatment was 39 vs 62 days compared with a reference cohort. Plasma results came in 7 days vs 23 days for tissue NGS. It is non-randomised with a historical comparison. García-Pardo M, Czarnecka-Kujawa K, Law JH et al. JAMA Netw Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37490292/
- **Canadian consensus on KRAS G12C NSCLC.** KRAS G12C testing should be part of comprehensive panels. Sotorasib is recommended after first-line ICI with or without chemotherapy, with liver function monitoring. Evidence is insufficient for specific CNS recommendations. Cheema PK, Banerji SO, Blais N et al. Curr Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37504336/
- **Consensus recommendations for MET-altered advanced NSCLC (diagnosis, testing, management).** Mahrous M, Omar Jebriel A, Allehebi A et al. Cureus 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37492039/
- **Chinese expert consensus on targeted therapy for METex14 NSCLC.** Editorial Office of Chinese Journal of Lung Cancer. Zhongguo Fei Ai Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37488079/

### lung-local-therapy / guidelines

- **CALGB 140503 post hoc by procedure (n=697).** 5-year DFS was 64.7% with lobectomy, 63.8% with segmentectomy and 62.5% with wedge resection, with OS 78.7%, 81.9% and 79.7%. Locoregional recurrence was 12% after segmentectomy and 14% after wedge. It is post hoc, and the sublobar type was not randomised. Altorki N, Wang X, Damman B et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37473998/
- **ISRS practice guideline and meta-analysis: SBRT for ultra-central tumours (27 studies, 1,183 targets).** 2-year local control was 89%, grade 3–4 toxicity 6% and treatment-related death 4%, mostly haemoptysis. Risk factors were anticoagulation, ILD, endobronchial tumour and concurrent targeted therapy. The evidence is mostly retrospective. Yan M, Louie AV, Kotecha R et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37393758/
- **Segmentectomy consensus documents.** These are the Asia expert consensus (modified Delphi; Liu L, Aokage K, Chen C et al. JTCVS Open 2023, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37425437/) and the German Society for Thoracic Surgery Delphi consensus (Koryllos A, Veit S, Schega O et al. Zentralbl Chir 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37402390/).
- **Chinese Medical Association lung cancer guideline, 2023 edition (published in two journals).** Stated updates: screening criteria based on Chinese data, sublobar resection options citing CALGB 140503, JCOG0802 and JCOG1211, perioperative immunotherapy and consolidation options. It is rated high, as earlier editions were. Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2023, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37460438/ and Zhonghua Yi Xue Za Zhi 2023, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37455124/
- **SEOM-GECP clinical guideline for SCLC (2022).** García-Campelo R, Sullivan I, Arriola E et al. Clin Transl Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37418123/
- **Canadian consensus on extensive-stage SCLC.** Melosky BL, Leighl NB, Dawe D et al. Curr Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37504325/

### lung-screening

- **IASLC CT-screening roadmap 2023–2027.** It sets 9 priorities, including quality indicators, evidence-based criteria for high-risk never-smokers, AI integration and risk-based screening intervals. Lam S, Bai C, Baldwin DR et al. J Thorac Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37487906/
- **Asian expert consensus on lung cancer screening (19 advisors, 11 countries).** For smokers it recommends age 50–75 and ≥20 pack-years. Family history is the main risk factor in non-smokers, and screening stops at age >80. Lam DC, Liam CK, Andarini S et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37390982/
- **LDCT screening of relatives with a family history of lung cancer (Taiwan, prospective multicentre, n=1,102, 70% never-smokers).** The detection rate was 4.5% overall and 9.4% in never-smokers from multiplex families, and 68% of cancers were stage I. Maternal history increased risk (rate ratio about 4.5–5.4). There was no control arm or mortality endpoint. Wang CL, Hsu KH, Chang YH et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37414358/
