### thoracic-other
- First-line toripalimab in combination with platinum-doublet chemotherapy for advanced B3 thymoma and thymic carcinoma: an open-label prospective phase Ⅱ trial. — Zhu K, Zhu H, Shi H et al., ESMO Open 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42854640/ 
  First-line toripalimab with carboplatin/paclitaxel was tested in 24 patients with advanced B3 thymoma or thymic carcinoma in a single-arm, open-label phase II trial (enrolled Nov 2020–Jan 2024; primary endpoint PFS). Median PFS was 19.3 months (95% CI 12.8–25.8), ORR 37.5% (95% CI 18.8–59.4%), DCR 100%; grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 50% and irAEs in 45.8% (grade ≥3: 4.2%). Higher PD-L1 and certain CD8+/NK infiltration patterns and NF-κB pathway mutations were associated with better outcomes. Limitations: small n, single arm, no comparator, exploratory biomarkers, OS not reported in the abstract.
