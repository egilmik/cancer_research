## High
### nsclc-drivers / lung-key-evidence
- **DIAMOND-2: dirozalkib vs crizotinib in ALK-inhibitor-naïve ALK+ advanced NSCLC (randomised phase 3).** This open-label multicentre trial randomised 275 patients (untreated or after one chemotherapy line) 1:1 to dirozalkib 500 mg daily or crizotinib. Investigator-assessed median PFS was 29.5 mo (95% CI 20.3–NR) vs 12.9 mo (11.1–17.6), HR 0.47 (95% CI 0.34–0.66); BICR PFS 27.5 vs 12.9 mo, HR 0.54 (0.39–0.74), at a median follow-up of about 32 months. Grade ≥3 TEAEs occurred in 60.6% vs 72.5%, and discontinuation in 2.2% vs 7.2%. Crizotinib is no longer the standard first-line comparator (alectinib/lorlatinib/brigatinib are), OS is not reported and the trial is China-only, so it adds a new ALK option but does not change practice in Norway. Liu Y, Meng X, Yu Y et al., Cancer Commun (Lond), 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43011476/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43011476/) (NCT05204628)

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / sclc
- **Osimertinib + platinum-etoposide for EGFR/TP53/RB1-mutant LUAD at risk of SCLC transformation.** A small prospective trial treated 11 patients with first-line osimertinib plus platinum and etoposide; despite SCLC-directed chemotherapy, 5 of 11 still transformed to SCLC, with median PFS 11 months and OS 33 months. Genomic and single-cell profiling showed plasticity toward SCLC, mesenchymal and squamous states tied to progressive RB-pathway disruption. It matters because it suggests that adding etoposide upfront does not prevent transformation in this high-risk group. Main limitations: single-arm, n=11, no control. Gavarró LM, Chen MF, Villalonga ÁQ et al., Sci Transl Med, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43013682/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43013682/)
