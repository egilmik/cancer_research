### nsclc-immunotherapy
- **Adjuvant nivolumab fails to improve disease-free survival in resected NSCLC (Cancer news).** This journal news item reports a negative result: adjuvant nivolumab did not improve DFS after resection of NSCLC. This matters because adjuvant and perioperative checkpoint inhibition is current practice, and a negative adjuvant-only nivolumab trial adds to the evidence that sequencing (neoadjuvant/perioperative versus adjuvant-only) matters. Limitations: this is a news piece with no abstract. The PubMed record does not name the trial or give n, HR, CI, OS or subgroup data, so it cannot be judged further. Go to the primary publication or presentation before drawing conclusions. Lawrence L, Cancer, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43032902/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43032902/) (doi:10.1002/cncr.70638)

## Medium
- [Delaying thoracic radiotherapy does not compromise overall survival in extensive-stage small cell lung cancer after first-line systemic treatment: a multicenter real-world cohort study.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43032292/): Retrospective multicentre cohort (n=307). No OS difference between consolidative and delayed TRT. PFS-DR was longer with consolidative TRT (HR 0.30), with more grade 2 pneumonitis (11.1% vs 4.0%).
- [Immune checkpoint inhibitor-based vs. non-immunotherapy subsequent treatment after progression on first-line chemoimmunotherapy in ES-SCLC: A meta-analysis.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43031952/): Meta-analysis of 10 non-randomised studies. ICI rechallenge was associated with OS HR 0.62 (0.51–0.74); prone to selection bias.
- [Second-Line Treatment of ES-SCLC in the Immunotherapy Era: An Exploratory Real-World Study.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43034147/): Retrospective study (n=94). Median PFS 3.8 mo and OS 9.8 mo after progression on chemoimmunotherapy. Anti-angiogenic-containing therapy was associated with worse OS.
- [Cytoreductive Surgery with or without Hyperthermic Intrathoracic Chemotherapy for Thymic Epithelial Malignancies with Pleural Dissemination.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43034220/): Retrospective PSM study (44 matched pairs). 3-yr PFS 87.3% vs 48.6% (HR 0.28, 0.11–0.66) with HITCH; needs a prospective study.
- [Prognostic Factors for Survival in ICI-Treated NSCLC: An Umbrella Review of Evidence Certainty and Clinical Relevance.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43033040/): Umbrella review covering 113 associations. Only 7 reached Class I credibility (ALI, tissue TMB, probiotics, thyroid dysfunction, among others).
- [Fragment-end sequence architecture of cell-free DNA and its association with tumour DNA in non-small cell lung cancer.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43034279/): Exploratory study. cfDNA end-motif features correlated with ctDNA abundance in 50 EGFR-mutant patients (rho 0.73).
- [Clinical impact of updated cardiopulmonary exercise testing guidelines in lung cancer surgery.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43034056/): Swedish (Linköping) study on how updated CPET guidelines affect assessment for lung cancer surgery. No abstract available.
