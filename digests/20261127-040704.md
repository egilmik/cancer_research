## High

### Mucosal, Ocular, and Pulmonary Adverse Events Reported with Datopotamab Deruxtecan: An Indication-Restricted Disproportionality Analysis of the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System.
Pharmacovigilance analysis of FAERS reports (2025Q1–2026Q1; 416 reports, 118 NSCLC) for datopotamab deruxtecan. Stomatitis was strongly disproportionate (ROR 21.5, 95% CI 15.0–30.7) with ocular events and candidiasis also signalling; interstitial lung disease did not pass the Bayesian/multiplicity thresholds but 18/19 ILD reports were serious and 5/19 fatal. Relevant to toxicity management after EGFR-TKI. Limitation: spontaneous reporting, no incidence or causality, no denominators.
Kaya OB, Karabuğa B, Aydemir E et al., Pharmaceuticals (Basel), 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43093254/

### Increased risk of Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia associated with immune checkpoint inhibitors in lung cancer: a nationwide retrospective cohort study.
Korean nationwide claims cohort of newly treated lung cancer, 13,426 propensity-matched pairs. Pneumocystis pneumonia occurred at 7.77 vs 3.35 per 1,000 person-years with ICI vs cytotoxic chemotherapy (adjusted HR 2.83, 95% CI 1.81–4.44), consistent across subgroups. Raises the question of PJP prophylaxis awareness in ICI-treated patients. Limitations: retrospective claims data, few events (93), residual confounding (steroid use), no prophylaxis recommendation can be drawn.
Cheon S, Kim KR, Lee H et al., Respir Res, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43086889/
