## Practice-changing

- **ASCO Guideline Rapid Recommendation Update: adjuvant systemic therapy and adjuvant radiation therapy for completely resected stage I–IIIA NSCLC (J Clin Oncol 2022).** It is a rapid update of the ASCO adjuvant guideline, issued in response to new practice-changing trial data. The item's abstract describes only the rapid-update process. A lookup (ASCO Post summary, April 2022) gives the changed recommendation: adjuvant atezolizumab after adjuvant cisplatin-based chemotherapy for resected stage II–IIIA NSCLC with PD-L1 ≥1%, except in patients with sensitising EGFR mutations. It rests on IMpower010 (DFS HR 0.66, moderate certainty). Adjuvant chemotherapy and atezolizumab are not recommended routinely in stage IB, and osimertinib is the adjuvant option for sensitising EGFR mutations. Open: IMpower010 OS was immature at the time, the benefit is concentrated in PD-L1-positive disease, and in Norway the decision rests on the Nye Metoder assessment of adjuvant atezolizumab. Pisters K, Kris MG, Gaspar LE et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35167335/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **ESMO expert consensus statements on EGFR-mutant NSCLC (Ann Oncol).** A 34-member panel voted recommendations on tissue and biomarker analysis, early and locally advanced disease, metastatic disease and trial design, on topics not covered in detail by the ESMO CPG. The abstract does not list the recommendations. Passaro A, Leighl N, Blackhall F et al. Ann Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35176458/
- **Rezivertinib (BPI-7711), a third-generation EGFR TKI, in T790M-positive NSCLC (phase 1 escalation/expansion, n=172, China, JTO).** BICR ORR was 59.3% (95% CI 51.6–66.7) and median PFS 9.7 months. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 17.4%, with no DLTs. It is single-arm, in a setting osimertinib already covers. Shi Y, Zhao Y, Yang S et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35181498/
- **TATTON: osimertinib plus durvalumab in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (phase 1b, n=34, JTO).** ILD-related AEs occurred in 35%, which stopped first-line enrolment. ORR was 43% after a prior TKI and 82% first line, with first-line PFS 9.0 months. It supports caution when combining EGFR TKIs with PD-(L)1 blockade. Ahn MJ, Cho BC, Ou X et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35181499/
- **Osimertinib for asymptomatic brain metastases in EGFR-mutant adenocarcinoma (3-arm phase II pilot, n=48, Neuro-Oncol Adv).** Intracranial ORR was 84.2% in treatment-naive patients, 66.7% if T790M-positive and 50% if T790M-negative after prior TKI. Median iPFS was 11.8, 7.6 and 6.3 months. It is small and non-randomised. Peled N, Kian W, Inbar E et al. Neurooncol Adv 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35156036/

### nsclc-drivers
- **KRYSTAL-1: adagrasib first-in-human dose finding (phase I/IB, JCO).** The RP2D is 600 mg twice daily. In KRAS G12C NSCLC at that dose, 8 of 15 (53.3%, 95% CI 26.6–78.7) had a confirmed PR, with median DoR 16.4 months and PFS 11.1 months. GI toxicity was common, and grade 3–4 fatigue occurred in 15%. The NSCLC cohort is small. Ou SI, Jänne PA, Leal TA et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35167329/
- **Pyrotinib in HER2-mutant advanced NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=78, China, BMC Med).** 6-month PFS was 49.5%, ORR 19.2%, PFS 5.6 months and OS 10.5 months. Diarrhoea occurred in 85.9% (grade 3 16.7%). Activity is modest; HER2 ADCs perform better. Song Z, Li Y, Chen S et al. BMC Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35101045/
- **Zenocutuzumab (HER2×HER3 bispecific) in NRG1 fusion-positive cancers (preclinical models plus 3 patients, Cancer Discov).** Two ATP1B1-NRG1 pancreatic cancers responded for over 12 months. A CD74-NRG1 NSCLC patient progressing after six lines, including afatinib, had a rapid partial response. The clinical evidence is three patients. Schram AM, Odintsov I, Espinosa-Cotton M et al. Cancer Discov 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35135829/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy / lung-repurposed
- **PACIFIC 5-year update: durvalumab vs placebo after concurrent CRT in unresectable stage III NSCLC (phase III, n=713, exploratory, JCO).** OS HR was 0.72 (95% CI 0.59–0.89; median 47.5 vs 29.1 months) and PFS HR 0.55. 5-year OS was 42.9% vs 33.4% and 5-year PFS 33.1% vs 19.0%. It confirms an established standard. Spigel DR, Faivre-Finn C, Gray JE et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35108059/
- **PROACTIVE: oesophagus-sparing IMRT vs standard palliative thoracic RT (phase 3, n=90, Canada, JAMA Oncol).** The primary 2-week oesophageal QoL score was 54.3 vs 50.5 (p=0.06, not significant). Symptomatic oesophagitis was 2% vs 24% (p=0.002), mostly with 30 Gy, and OS was similar (8.7 vs 8.6 months). It is negative on its primary endpoint but supports ES-IMRT where available. Louie AV, Granton PV, Fairchild A et al. JAMA Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35201290/
- **Continuing pembrolizumab with chemotherapy after progression on PD-(L)1 monotherapy (randomised placebo-controlled phase II, n=98, Korea, CCR).** PFS was 4.1 vs 5.9 months (HR 1.06, 95% CI 0.69–1.62) and OS 11.5 vs 12.0 months (HR 1.09). The trial is negative and argues against continuing PD-1 blockade beyond progression in this setting. Jung HA, Park S, Choi YL et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35101883/
- **IMpower150 in KRAS-mutant NSCLC (exploratory ctDNA-defined subgroup, JITC).** In KRAS-mutant disease, atezolizumab-bevacizumab-carboplatin-paclitaxel vs bevacizumab-chemotherapy gave OS of 19.8 vs 9.9 months (HR 0.50, 95% CI 0.34–0.72) and PFS HR 0.42. Effects with STK11, KEAP1 and TP53 co-mutations were also reported. It is a post hoc subgroup analysis. West HJ, McCleland M, Cappuzzo F et al. J Immunother Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35190375/
- **Antibiotics, PPIs and atezolizumab efficacy (pooled analysis of 5 RCTs, n=4,458, JTO).** Antibiotic use did not change atezolizumab's OS benefit. PPI use did reduce it (HR 1.00 in users vs 0.76 in non-users, p-interaction 0.003) and was associated with lower lymphocyte counts. It is a post hoc analysis; it supports reviewing non-essential PPI use with ICIs. Hopkins AM, Badaoui S, Kichenadasse G et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35183773/
- **Faecal Akkermansia muciniphila and response to PD-1 blockade in advanced NSCLC (prospective validation, n=338, Nat Med).** Baseline Akk was associated with higher ORR and OS, independent of PD-L1, antibiotics and PS. Very high Akk relative abundance, often after antibiotics, was linked to resistance. It is a biomarker, not an intervention. Derosa L, Routy B, Thomas AM et al. Nat Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35115705/
- **FDA approval summary: single-agent pembrolizumab, atezolizumab and cemiplimab first line in PD-L1-high NSCLC (CCR).** It covers the cemiplimab approval of 22 February 2021 alongside the earlier approvals. OS HRs vs platinum doublet were 0.60 (KEYNOTE-024), 0.59 (IMpower110) and 0.68 (Study 1624). It is a journal profile of approvals. Akinboro O, Larkins E, Pai-Scherf LH et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35101885/
- **ACCURE antiracism intervention and time to lung cancer surgery (pragmatic trial secondary analysis, n=2,363, JCO).** The intervention combined real-time milestone alerts, race-specific feedback and navigation. Timely surgery was 87.1% for Black and 85.4% for White patients vs 58.7% and 75.0% in retrospective controls (RR 1.43 for Black patients). The comparison is non-randomised and US-specific. Charlot M, Stein JN, Damone E et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35157498/

### sclc / lung-nordic
- **Thoracic radiotherapy patterns of care in LS-SCLC in Norway (Cancer Registry of Norway, n=2,222, 2000–2018, JTO CRR).** Twice-daily (BID) 45 Gy use rose from 1.8% to 83.2% after a Norwegian randomised trial. Median OS with curative thoracic RT improved from 17.9 to 25.0 months, and was 26.2 months with 45 Gy BID vs 19.6 months with 42 Gy once daily. It is observational, but shows a national practice change and its effect. Graabak G, Grønberg BH, Sandvei MS et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35146461/
- **HRQoL in the Norwegian randomised phase II of 60 Gy vs 45 Gy BID thoracic RT in LS-SCLC (n=150 evaluable, Lung Cancer).** Peak dysphagia (week 8) did not differ significantly. The 60 Gy arm had more dysphagia in early convalescence, which returned to baseline by week 16, and dyspnoea did not differ. It supports the higher dose as tolerable. Killingberg KT, Halvorsen TO, Fløtten Ø et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35183991/
- **RESILIENT part 1: liposomal irinotecan after platinum in SCLC (phase 2 dose finding/expansion, n=25, Cancer).** At 70 mg/m², ORR was 44.0%, PFS 3.98 months and OS 8.08 months. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 10 of 25, with 2 treatment-related deaths. It is single-arm; part 2 is a phase 3 vs topotecan. Paz-Ares L, Spigel DR, Chen Y et al. Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35195913/

- **Serum RNAs predicting lung cancer up to 10 years before diagnosis (Norwegian Janus Serum Bank, 925 individuals, eLife).** Machine-learning models on serum RNA separated smokers later diagnosed with lung cancer from controls (AUC 0.76). The AUC was 0.82 for NSCLC 6–8 years before diagnosis and 0.89 for SCLC 2–5 years before. It is discovery work needing external validation. Umu SU, Langseth H, Zuber V et al. eLife 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35147498/
- **Recommendations for practical ctDNA mutation testing in metastatic NSCLC (international experts, ESMO Open).** Scenarios include complementary tissue and plasma testing, identifying resistance mechanisms, and suspected heterogeneity. It is an expert position paper, not a society guideline. Heitzer E, van den Broek D, Denis MG et al. ESMO Open 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35202954/

### car-t-solid
- **GD2-CAR T cells in H3K27M diffuse midline glioma (first-in-human phase I, Nature).** It reports the first 4 patients at dose level 1 (intravenous), with later intracerebroventricular doses for those benefiting. Three of four had clinical and radiographic improvement. Toxicity was tumour-location related and reversible with intensive care, with no on-target off-tumour toxicity. It is very small. Majzner RG, Ramakrishna S, Yeom KW et al. Nature 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35130560/
- **Off-the-shelf, glucocorticoid-resistant allogeneic IL13Rα2 CAR-T in recurrent glioblastoma (phase I, n=6, Neuro-Oncology).** Intracranial cells with IL-2 were tolerated on dexamethasone. Four of 6 had transient tumour reduction or necrosis at the infusion site. It is a feasibility study. Brown CE, Rodriguez A, Palmer J et al. Neuro Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35100373/

- **CheckMate 743 3-year update: first-line nivolumab-ipilimumab vs chemotherapy in unresectable pleural mesothelioma (phase III, Ann Oncol).** At a median follow-up of 43.1 months, OS was 18.1 vs 14.1 months (HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.61–0.87) and 3-year OS 23% vs 15%. 3-year PFS was 14% vs 1%, and ORR 40% vs 44%. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 27%. It is single-arm and small, with short follow-up (median 18.4 weeks). Fennell DA, King A, Mohammed S et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35157829/
- **SEOM-GECP-GETTHI clinical guideline for thymic epithelial tumours, 2021 (Spain, Clin Transl Oncol).** It recommends surgery when resectable and platinum chemotherapy for advanced disease, and cautions about ICIs given autoimmunity. It is a national guideline, but the abstract does not name a changed recommendation. Remon J, Bernabé R, Diz P et al. Clin Transl Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35122634/
