### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-top-journals

- **POSEIDON (phase 3).** 1,013 patients with EGFR/ALK wild-type metastatic NSCLC were randomised 1:1:1 to durvalumab+chemotherapy, tremelimumab+durvalumab+chemotherapy, or chemotherapy alone. Durvalumab+chemotherapy improved PFS (HR 0.74), but OS missed significance (HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.72–1.02). Adding a limited course of tremelimumab improved OS (median 14.0 vs 11.7 months, HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.65–0.92; 24-month OS 32.9% vs 22.1%), a key secondary endpoint. Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 51.8% vs 44.4% for chemotherapy. The comparator (chemotherapy alone) is no longer standard, and there was no comparison with pembrolizumab-chemotherapy. Johnson ML, Cho BC, Luft A et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36327426/
- **Pooled long-term nivolumab+ipilimumab (first line, N=1,332).** Median OS was 18.6 months and 3-year OS 35%. By PD-L1, 3-year OS was 30% (<1%), 38% (≥1%) and 48% (≥50%), and patients aged ≥75 did as well as the rest (3-year OS 34%). Response at 6 months and depth of response predicted long-term survival. It is a pooled analysis of trial cohorts without a control arm. Borghaei H, Ciuleanu TE, Lee JS et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36414192/
- **ORIENT-3 (phase 3).** 290 patients with squamous NSCLC were randomised second line to sintilimab or docetaxel. Median OS was 11.8 vs 8.3 months (HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.56–0.96), ORR 25.5% vs 2.2%, and grade ≥3 TRAEs 18.1% vs 36.2%. The trial was China-only and confirms a PD-1 vs docetaxel benefit already established by other agents. Shi Y, Wu L, Yu X et al. Cancer Commun (Lond) 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36336841/
- **DETERRED (phase 2, final).** Atezolizumab was given with and/or after chemoradiation in unresectable NSCLC. At 39 months of follow-up, median PFS was 18.9 months (part 1) and 15.1 months (part 2), and 3-year OS was 53.8%. Driver-positive tumours had worse PFS (9.4 vs 16.6 months). The trial is small and single-arm, so the authors' comparison with PACIFIC is indirect. Liu Y, Yao L, Kalhor N et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36371941/
- **European Respiratory Society guideline on quality in lung cancer care.** This is a GRADE-based guideline built on eight PICO questions. It recommends avoiding diagnostic and therapeutic delay, multidisciplinary teams, adherence to guidelines, high-volume specialised surgery, pathological and molecular confirmation, early palliative care integration, quality-improvement measures and patient decision tools. These are care-organisation recommendations, not treatment changes. Blum TG, Morgan RL, Durieux V et al. Eur Respir J 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36396145/
- **Asian expert consensus on biomarker testing (metastatic and non-metastatic NSCLC).** The consensus stresses testing for uncommon EGFR mutations as well as common ones, given the high EGFR prevalence in Asia, and discusses liquid biopsy alongside tissue. It is region-specific. Mitsudomi T, Tan D, Yang JC et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36379356/
- **Chinese expert consensus on immunotherapy in driver-mutant advanced NSCLC (2022).** This is guidance on when immunotherapy may be considered in oncogene-driven NSCLC. The abstract gives no specific recommendations. Society of Cancer Precision Medicine, Chinese Anti-Cancer Association. Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36319450/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-drivers / lung-ctdna-mrd

- **ctDNA-guided therapy and OS (prospective, 1,127 patients, Nat Med).** Detectable ctDNA was associated with shorter survival (HR 2.05, 95% CI 1.74–2.42). Among patients with detectable ctDNA, those matched to targeted therapy by ctDNA (23%) lived longer (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.52–0.76). 25% had alterations found only in ctDNA, often subclonal resistance drivers. This supports plasma genotyping in practice. The matched-therapy comparison is non-randomised. Jee J, Lebow ES, Yeh R et al. Nat Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36357680/
- **Nivolumab vs nivolumab+ipilimumab in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (randomised phase 2, closed for futility).** 31 of 184 planned patients were treated after TKI resistance. ORR was 3.2%, median PFS 1.2 months, and no crossover patient responded. ICI is ineffective after EGFR-TKI resistance. Lai GGY, Yeo JC, Jain A et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36426287/
- **SPIRAL II (single-arm phase 2).** 31 Japanese patients with EGFR-mutant NSCLC and malignant effusion received osimertinib+bevacizumab. Median PFS was 8.5 months and ORR 74.2%, and drainage-free survival was 18.4 months. The combination did not improve PFS over historical expectations. Hibino M, Hiranuma O, Takemura Y et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36438852/
- **ICAPE (single-arm phase 2, adjuvant icotinib).** 79 patients with resected stage IIA–IIIA EGFR-mutant adenocarcinoma received icotinib for 1.5 years. Median DFS was 41.4 months and 5-year OS 61.7%. There was no control arm. Qian K, Chen QR, He M et al. Invest New Drugs 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36355317/
- **WJOG5108LFS OS analysis (phase 3).** Gefitinib vs erlotinib in previously treated adenocarcinoma: OS in EGFR-mutant patients was 28.0 vs 32.0 months (HR 1.116, NS), and brain-metastasis outcomes were similar. Both drugs are now superseded by osimertinib. Katakami N, Yokoyama T, Morita S et al. Int J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36414827/
- **Conteltinib (CT-707) first-in-human phase 1.** 64 patients with ALK+ NSCLC received this new second-generation ALK TKI. ORR was 64.1% with median PFS 15.9 months in TKI-naive patients (n=39), and ORR 33.3% after crizotinib. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 14%, and diarrhoea in 72%. The evidence is early-phase. Xing P, Zhao Q, Zhang L et al. BMC Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36424628/
- **CROWN patient-reported outcomes (phase 3).** Most functioning and symptom scores favoured lorlatinib over crizotinib, while cognitive functioning and peripheral neuropathy favoured crizotinib. This is a secondary analysis against a comparator that is no longer preferred. Mazieres J, Iadeluca L, Shaw AT et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36410210/
- **Swedish nationwide ALK-TKI use (registry, 579 patients).** 37% received chemotherapy first. Median OS was 1.83 years overall: 2.02 years with frontline ALK TKI vs 1.44 years with frontline chemotherapy. Most first-line TKI was crizotinib. It is a Nordic real-world benchmark. Lauppe R, Nilsson FOL, Fues Wahl H et al. Acta Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36368902/
- **Chinese expert consensus on HER2-altered NSCLC.** It covers testing methods (PCR, NGS, FISH, IHC) and treatment, including TKIs and ADCs such as trastuzumab deruxtecan. It is consensus-based. Zhang S, Wang W, Xu C et al. Thorac Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36444143/
- **Selpercatinib regular FDA approval for RET fusion-positive NSCLC (JAMA news).** This is a news note with no abstract. It is rated high rather than practice-changing because it is not the agency's own record. Larkin HD. JAMA 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36318150/

### lung-screening / lung-nordic / car-t-solid / thoracic-other

- **Eleven lung cancer risk models in the German ESTHER cohort (4,812 ever-smokers, 17 years).** The Bach, LCRAT and LCDRAT models discriminated best (AUC about 0.77–0.79). At the same positivity rate, they would have captured 12–18 percentage points more 6-year cancers than LDCT trial criteria. This supports risk-model-based selection for screening. Bhardwaj M, Schöttker B, Holleczek B et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36356492/
- **ComIT-1 (Norwegian phase 2): atezolizumab plus SBRT in second line or later.** In 21 patients, grade 3 toxicity occurred in 3. There were 4 PRs and 8 SDs, and median OS was not reached at 26.5 months. Ten of 15 PD-L1-negative patients were alive beyond 18 months. Early ctDNA decline tracked benefit in 3 patients. The trial is small and single-arm. Horndalsveen H, Alver TN, Dalsgaard AM et al. Mol Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36330681/
- **T-RAD trial design (Norwegian phase 1/2).** The trial tests mRNA-electroporated autologous T cells carrying the Radium-4 TCR against hTERT (HLA-DP04) in metastatic NSCLC with no other treatment options. Expression is transient for safety, with escalating doses twice weekly for 6 weeks. Only the protocol is reported, with no results. Maggadóttir SM, Kvalheim G, Wernhoff P et al. Front Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36439452/
- **MU-MA402C protocol (phase 1).** This is a TCR-like CAR-T against HLA-A*02:01/MAGE-A4 with GITR signalling, in MAGE-A4+ solid cancers. Only the protocol is reported. Okumura S, Ishihara M, Kiyota N et al. BMJ Open 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36375974/
- **Results posted: letetresgene autoleucel (NY-ESO-1 TCR-T) in myxoid/round cell liposarcoma (NCT02992743, phase 2, GSK, n=23).** The record has no efficacy summary. https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02992743
- **Buparlisib in relapsed thymoma (phase 2).** 14 patients with B2/B3 thymoma received the drug. There was 1 PR (7%), DCR was 50% and median PFS 11.1 months. Pulmonary toxicity and rash led to discontinuations. Activity is modest and n is very small. Abu Zaid MI, Radovich M, Althouse S et al. Front Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36330484/
