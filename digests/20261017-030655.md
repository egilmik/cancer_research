## High

### egfr-nsclc / lung-local-therapy
- **Durvalumab plus high-dose radiation in stage III NSCLC: ALLSTAR registry long-term results.** Prospective nationwide Austrian registry (observational), n=228 (156 CRT plus durvalumab, 72 CRT alone), median follow-up 41.0 vs 37.8 months. Durvalumab was associated with median PFS 22.8 vs 16.5 months (HR 0.61, 95% CI 0.42–0.88) and median OS 52.6 vs 27.4 months (HR 0.52, 0.34–0.78). Dose escalation >66 Gy plus durvalumab versus neither gave HR 0.61 (0.37–1.006, p=0.051) for the time-to-event endpoint ITC. Limitations: non-randomised, treatment chosen by tumour board, so confounded; it confirms the established PACIFIC standard rather than changing it, and the dose-escalation signal is not significant. Hörmandinger K, et al. Cancers (Basel) 2026. [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42882867/)
