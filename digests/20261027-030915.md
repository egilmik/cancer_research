## Practice-changing

### Osimertinib-Savolitinib in Advanced NSCLC after Progression during EGFR-TKI Therapy.
**SAFFRON (osimertinib-savolitinib vs platinum-pemetrexed)** is a randomised, open-label phase 3 (n=338; 171 vs 167) in EGFR-mutated NSCLC progressing on osimertinib with MET overexpression (IHC 3+ in ≥90% of cells) and/or MET amplification (FISH ≥10 copies); 1,746 patients were screened. Primary endpoint was BICR-assessed PFS: HR 0.55 (95% CI 0.42–0.72; P<0.001), with 12-month PFS 36% vs 10%. The final OS analysis also favoured the combination (HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.50–0.91; P=0.01; 12-month OS 70% vs 53%). Grade ≥3 adverse events were similar (64% vs 63%); peripheral oedema (49%), anaemia (26%) and diarrhoea (26%) were the most frequent events with the combination. Median follow-up and absolute median PFS/OS are not in the abstract. Open questions: a chemotherapy-only comparator (not an ADC or amivantamab-based option), the need for central MET testing with stringent cut-offs, approval status and Norwegian reimbursement (not stated in the abstract).

Lu S, Girard N, Yang JC et al. *N Engl J Med* 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929693/

### Tarlatamab-Durvalumab Maintenance for Extensive-Stage Small-Cell Lung Cancer.
**DeLLphi-305** is a randomised, open-label phase 3 (n=563; 282 vs 281) of tarlatamab-durvalumab vs durvalumab as first-line maintenance in ES-SCLC without progression after chemoimmunotherapy. At the prespecified interim analysis (data cut-off 7 July 2026; median follow-up ~16 months) median OS was 21.1 vs 12.7 months (HR 0.42, 95% CI 0.32–0.54; P<0.001), and median PFS 7.4 vs 1.9 months (HR 0.34, 95% CI 0.28–0.42). Grade ≥3 adverse events were 46.8% vs 40.6%; cytokine release syndrome, mostly grade 1–2, occurred in nearly half of tarlatamab-treated patients. Open questions: upper OS bound not estimable (immature), comparator is durvalumab alone rather than lurbinectedin-atezolizumab maintenance, CRS/neurotoxicity monitoring burden, regulatory status and Norwegian reimbursement (not stated in the abstract).

Pérol M, Yoshida T, Ahn MJ et al. *N Engl J Med* 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929702/

### Divarasib as Compared with Sotorasib or Adagrasib in KRAS G12C-Mutated NSCLC.
**Krascendo 1** is a randomised, open-label phase 3 (n=338; divarasib 167 vs sotorasib 156 / adagrasib 15) in previously treated KRAS G12C-mutated NSCLC. Median follow-up was 14.3 months. Median PFS (BICR, primary) was 9.8 vs 5.7 months (HR 0.58, 95% CI 0.44–0.76; P<0.001) and median OS 17.7 vs 12.0 months (HR 0.56, 95% CI 0.41–0.78). Confirmed response was 58.4% vs 32.3% (DoR 11.9 vs 6.9 months). Grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events were fewer with divarasib (24.6% vs 34.5%). Open questions: control arm is almost entirely sotorasib (91%), open-label design, follow-up still moderate, and approval/reimbursement of divarasib is not yet established (not stated in the abstract).

Skoulidis F, Ahn MJ, Gadgeel S et al. *N Engl J Med* 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929675/

## High

### egfr-nsclc
- **Dalmelitinib Plus Aumolertinib in Advanced NSCLC Patients With EGFR-TKI Resistance and MET Amplification: A Randomized Phase 3 Trial.** Randomised open-label phase 3 (n=314) of dalmelitinib plus aumolertinib vs platinum chemotherapy after EGFR-TKI progression in MET-amplified (FISH) NSCLC. Median PFS 6.2 vs 4.4 months (HR 0.60, 95% CI 0.45–0.81); ORR 59.6% vs 38.6%; grade ≥3 events ~50% in both arms, with more hepatic laboratory abnormalities on the combination. Median follow-up only 11.2 months and OS immature; a second positive MET-directed phase 3 after SAFFRON, though with a modest PFS gain.  
Li Z, Dong X, Yu Y et al. *J Clin Oncol* 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42928710/

### nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **Sacituzumab Tirumotecan plus Pembrolizumab in PD-L1-Negative Advanced NSCLC.** **OptiTROP-Lung06** (phase 3, open-label, n=455): sacituzumab tirumotecan plus pembrolizumab vs chemotherapy plus pembrolizumab in first-line PD-L1-negative (<1%) nonsquamous NSCLC without targetable alterations. Interim analysis (median follow-up 9.7 months): PFS 15.0 vs 5.7 months (HR 0.49, 95% CI 0.36–0.65), ORR 50.2% vs 47.3%, grade ≥3 AEs 47.6% vs 60.6%, stomatitis common. OS immature (HR 0.58, 95% CI 0.38–0.88). Rated high rather than practice-changing: interim, short follow-up, PFS-only, sponsor-funded Chinese population, no approval yet.  
Zhou F, Meng X, Ding Q et al. *N Engl J Med* 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42929694/
- **ctDNA clearance and outcomes for resectable lung cancer from NeoCOAST-2.** **NeoCOAST-2** (phase 2 randomised platform, stage IIA–IIIB resectable NSCLC): chemotherapy plus durvalumab with oleclumab, monalizumab or Dato-DXd. 24-month EFS 70.5%, 75.7%, 77.9%; ctDNA clearance at C3D1 54.5–72.5% (highest with Dato-DXd). ctDNA clearance associated with EFS (pooled HR 0.22, 95% CI 0.11–0.45); clearance plus pCR HR 0.10. Arms were not powered for comparison and ctDNA associations are exploratory.  
McGrath L, Kar G, Bonanno L et al. *Nature* 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42927884/
