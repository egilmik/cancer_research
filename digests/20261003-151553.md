### Studies and papers
- **Reduced Risk of Lung Cancer Associated With Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter-2 Inhibitors in Patients With COPD and Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus.** Nationwide Taiwanese new-user cohort (14,927 COPD+T2DM patients) with IPTW: SGLT2i vs sulfonylureas gave lung cancer HR 0.73 (0.60-0.90), 4-yr incidence 1.6% vs 2.7%; observational with median follow-up ~3 years, risk of residual confounding and no lung cancer survival/response data.
  Kang D, Yoo JW, Lee M et al. — Chest 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508725/
- **Use of PCSK9 Inhibitors Among Patients Receiving Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors for Cancer.** TriNetX comparative effectiveness study with 239 propensity-matched pairs on ICIs (66% lung cancer, mixed with melanoma/RCC): PCSK9 inhibitor vs high-intensity statin had better OS (HR 0.69, 0.53-0.90) but no MACE difference; retrospective, not NSCLC-specific, residual confounding and exposure-window/immortal-time concerns.
  Lu CA, Jiang C, Wang AZ et al. — JAMA Netw Open 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42213438/
- **Association between Statin Use and the Risk of Colorectal, Liver, and Lung Cancers: A Population-Based Retrospective Cohort Study.** Hong Kong EHR cohort of 1.5M patients with Cox adjustment: statin use associated with lower lung cancer incidence (aHR 0.73, 0.71-0.76); very large but observational, incidence rather than survival, confounding by indication/healthy-user effects possible and not lung-specific.
  Yau STY, Hung CT, Leung EYM et al. — Cancer Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42030927/
- **Protective effect of H1 antihistamine against lung cancer in patients with allergic diseases: A global federated health network analysis.** TriNetX retrospective cohort in asthma/allergic rhinitis comparing H1 antihistamines with LTRAs and glucocorticoids on lung cancer incidence with Cox adjustment; H1AH alone had higher risk than LTRA (HR 1.88) but lower than GC (HR 0.34), so no protective effect vs untreated and strong confounding by indication (asthma severity, GC use).
  Nguyen VT, Le NH, Phan NQ et al. — Am J Med 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41946436/
- **Impact of NSAID type, initiation timing, duration and dose on clinical outcomes of immunotherapy in NSCLC: a multicenter two-cohort study.** Multicentre two-cohort study (1,748 advanced NSCLC patients on first ICI, 232 prospective) with multivariable adjustment: NSAID use associated with better PFS (HR 0.72) and OS (HR 0.76), low-dose aspirin PFS HR 0.69, with PGE2/neutrophil biomarker data; mostly retrospective, timing/immortal-time and indication confounding remain possible.
  Li Y, Jia X, Liu M et al. — J Immunother Cancer 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41922087/
