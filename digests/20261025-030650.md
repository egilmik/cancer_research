### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy
- **Nivolumab plus relatlimab with platinum-doublet chemotherapy versus nivolumab with platinum-doublet chemotherapy in stage IV or recurrent non-small cell lung cancer: clinical, translational, and patient-reported outcomes results from the phase II RELATIVITY-104 study.** Randomized phase 2 RELATIVITY-104 part 2, first-line stage IV/recurrent NSCLC: nivolumab+relatlimab+platinum doublet vs nivolumab+platinum doublet. The ORR primary endpoint was not met (51.3% vs 45.0%; PFS HR 0.89, 90% CI 0.72–1.09). Exploratory PD-L1 ≥1% nonsquamous subgroup showed ORR 58.0% vs 39.6%, PFS HR 0.68 (0.46–1.01) and OS HR 0.60 (0.38–0.94), which is hypothesis-generating only; it motivates phase 3 RELATIVITY-1093. Limitations: phase 2, subgroup analysis, 90% CIs. Girard N, Burotto M, Paz-Ares LG et al., Ann Oncol 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42918419/

### lung-local-therapy
- **PET-Guided Dose Escalation for Stage III Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Randomized Clinical Trial.** Phase 2 randomized, 7 Canadian centres, n=78 inoperable FDG-avid stage III NSCLC; 60 Gy/30 vs PET-adaptive boost up to 85 Gy/30 (median boost 75 Gy); 76% had consolidation durvalumab. 2-year locoregional failure 18.4% vs 27.5% (P=.13); 2-year OS 73.7% vs 72.5% and PFS 57.9% vs 52.5% did not differ; grade ≥3 toxicity 37% vs 25% (P=.26), one grade 5 pulmonary hemorrhage. Median follow-up 5.0 years. Underpowered for the primary endpoint. Raman S, Bissonnette JP, Palma DA et al., Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42918583/
- **Durvalumab completion and re-administration after grade 2 radiation pneumonitis following concurrent chemoradiotherapy for unresectable stage III NSCLC: a single-center real-world study.** Single-centre retrospective study (n=101) of durvalumab after concurrent CRT in unresectable stage III NSCLC. Grade ≥2 radiation pneumonitis in 47.5%; of those with interruption, 25 (59.5%) restarted durvalumab with one recurrence. Durvalumab completion strongly associated with OS (HR 0.14), 2-/3-year OS 75.1%/60.9%. Retrospective, confounding by immortal-time/selection likely. Aoki S, Ariyasu R, Kamima T et al., BMC Cancer 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42919171/

### sclc
- **Lung neuroendocrine cancer induces immunosuppressive macrophages via serotonin to drive resistance to the T cell engager tarlatamab.** Preclinical: DLL3-high neuroendocrine cancers produce serotonin via TPH1, driving HTR7+ immunosuppressive macrophages and resistance to tarlatamab; genetic ablation of HTR7 or TPH1 restores T-cell function and TCE/PD-1 efficacy in models.
