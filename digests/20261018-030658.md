### egfr-nsclc
- **Firmonertinib in EGFR PACC-mutant NSCLC** (phase 1b FURTHER, NCT05364073). Patients with first-line metastatic PACC-mutant NSCLC were randomised to 160 mg (n=25) or 240 mg (n=22). Confirmed ORR was 43% and 68%, median PFS 11.1 and 16.0 months, and intracranial ORR 47% (8/17); dose reductions 19%/24%, discontinuation 3%/0%. It suggests a possible option for a structurally distinct, poorly served subgroup. Limitations: small, non-comparative, early-phase, DoR not reached in the 160 mg arm. Le X, Wang J, Yu Y et al., Cancer Cell 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42887497/
- **Lazertinib + pemetrexed/carboplatin after progression on lazertinib** (single-arm phase II LUCAS, NCT05786430, n=86). Median PFS 9.5 months (one-sided p=0.017 vs a 6.0-month null) in 81 evaluable patients; median OS 15.9 months, ORR 36.0%; 46.5% had brain metastases. Baseline ctDNA EGFR amplification was associated with inferior survival. It informs post-third-generation-TKI strategy, but there is no comparator and the benchmark is a historical assumption. Hong MH, Shim BY, Kim CG et al., Eur J Cancer 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42887461/
