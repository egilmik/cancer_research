## Practice-changing

- **AEGEAN: perioperative durvalumab with neoadjuvant chemotherapy in resectable NSCLC (randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3, NEJM 2023).**
  - **Design:** 802 patients with resectable stage II–IIIB (N2) NSCLC received four cycles of platinum chemotherapy plus durvalumab or placebo before surgery, then 12 cycles of adjuvant durvalumab or placebo. Randomisation was stratified by stage and PD-L1. Patients with EGFR/ALK alterations (n=62) were excluded from the efficacy population.
  - **Primary endpoints:** EFS improved at the first interim analysis (stratified HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.53–0.88, P = .004; 12-month EFS 73.4% vs 64.5%). pCR was 17.2% vs 4.3% (difference 13.0 percentage points, 95% CI 8.7–17.6). Benefit was seen regardless of stage and PD-L1.
  - **Toxicity:** Grade 3–4 AEs occurred in 42.4% vs 43.2%.
  - **Still open:**
    - OS is immature.
    - The trial does not show how much the adjuvant phase adds beyond neoadjuvant chemo-IO (cf. CheckMate 816).
    - EU approval and Norwegian reimbursement status are not covered by this item.
    - It joins KEYNOTE-671 and CheckMate 816 (already in the index) as a perioperative/neoadjuvant chemo-IO option.
  - Heymach JV, Harpole D, Mitsudomi T et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37870974/
- **PAPILLON: first-line amivantamab plus chemotherapy in EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC (international randomised phase 3, NEJM 2023).**
  - **Design:** 308 untreated patients were randomised 1:1 to amivantamab plus carboplatin-pemetrexed or chemotherapy alone, with crossover to amivantamab monotherapy at progression.
  - **Primary endpoint (BICR PFS):** It was 11.4 vs 6.7 months (HR 0.40, 95% CI 0.30–0.53, P < .001). 18-month PFS was 31% vs 3%, and ORR 73% vs 47%.
  - **Overall survival:** Interim OS (33% maturity) gave HR 0.67 (95% CI 0.42–1.09, not significant), and crossover will confound later OS.
  - **Toxicity:** It was mainly haematological and EGFR-related (rash, paronychia), and 7% discontinued amivantamab for adverse reactions. IV infusion burden is a practical cost.
  - **Still open:** OS maturity, and EU approval and Norwegian reimbursement status, which this item does not cover.
  - Zhou C, Tang KJ, Cho BC et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37870976/

LIBRETTO-431 (first-line selpercatinib vs chemo ± pembrolizumab in RET fusion NSCLC) and MARIPOSA-2 (amivantamab-chemotherapy after osimertinib) are rated high. Selpercatinib was already a first-line option, and MARIPOSA-2's OS is immature.

## High

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers

- **LIBRETTO-431 (randomised phase 3, n=261, NEJM).** First-line selpercatinib vs platinum-pemetrexed with or without pembrolizumab gave BICR PFS 24.8 vs 11.2 months (HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.31–0.70) in the pembrolizumab-ITT population (n=212). ORR was 84% vs 65%, and the CNS progression cause-specific HR was 0.28. Results in the overall ITT were similar. Crossover was allowed and OS is immature. It confirms targeted therapy over chemo-IO for RET fusions. Zhou C, Solomon B, Loong HH et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37870973/
- **MARIPOSA-2 (phase III, n=657, randomised 2:2:1, Ann Oncol).** After progression on osimertinib, amivantamab-chemotherapy and amivantamab-lazertinib-chemotherapy vs chemotherapy gave BICR PFS 6.3 and 8.3 vs 4.2 months (HR 0.48 and 0.44). ORR was 64% and 63% vs 36%, and intracranial PFS 12.5 and 12.8 vs 8.3 months. Toxicity was more haematological, EGFR- and MET-related, and the lazertinib arm had to be modified for haematological toxicity. OS is immature. Passaro A, Wang J, Wang Y et al. Ann Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37879444/
- **ATTLAS (KCSG-LU19-04, open-label phase III, n=228, 16 Korean sites, J Clin Oncol).** After EGFR/ALK-TKI failure, atezolizumab-bevacizumab-carboplatin-paclitaxel (ABCP) vs pemetrexed-platinum gave ORR 69.5% vs 41.9% and PFS 8.48 vs 5.62 months (HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.45–0.86), with larger benefit at higher PD-L1. OS did not differ (20.6 vs 20.3 months, HR 1.01). Toxicity was higher with ABCP. Park S, Kim TM, Han JY et al. J Clin Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37861993/
- **DESTINY-PanTumor02 (phase II, n=267, J Clin Oncol).** T-DXd in HER2-expressing non-breast, non-gastric, non-lung solid tumours gave ORR 37.1% overall and 61.3% in central IHC 3+, with median OS 21.1 months in IHC 3+. Adjudicated ILD occurred in 10.5%. There is no lung cohort; it is relevant as the basis for tumour-agnostic HER2 use. Meric-Bernstam F, Makker V, Oaknin A et al. J Clin Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37870536/

### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **SAPPHIRE (phase III, n=577, Ann Oncol).** Sitravatinib plus nivolumab vs docetaxel after chemo-ICI did not improve OS (12.2 vs 10.6 months, HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.70–1.05) or PFS (HR 1.08). It is negative. Borghaei H, de Marinis F, Dumoulin D et al. Ann Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37866811/
- **CANOPY-A (double-blind phase 3, n=1,382, J Clin Oncol).** Adjuvant canakinumab (anti-IL-1β) after resection and cisplatin chemotherapy in stage II–III NSCLC did not improve DFS (35.0 vs 29.7 months, HR 0.94, 95% CI 0.78–1.14). It is negative. Garon EB, Lu S, Goto Y et al. J Clin Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37788412/
- **Bintrafusp alfa with chemoradiotherapy vs CCRT then durvalumab, unresectable stage III (randomised phase 2, n=153, J Thorac Oncol).** PFS was 12.8 vs 14.6 months (HR 1.48, 95% CI 0.69–3.17), with no efficacy benefit. It is negative. Vokes EE, Mornex F, Sezer A et al. J Thorac Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37797733/
- **Pathologic response and survival in CheckMate 816 (prespecified exploratory analysis, Nat Med).** Residual viable tumour (%RVT) in the primary tumour and lymph nodes after neoadjuvant nivolumab-chemotherapy was associated with EFS across thresholds beyond pCR and MPR. It supports %RVT as a continuous surrogate. It is exploratory and from a single trial. Deutsch JS, Cimino-Mathews A, Thompson E et al. Nat Med 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37903504/
- **German S3 interdisciplinary lung cancer guideline, abridged (Pneumologie 2023).** New recommendations include:
  - mandatory tumour-board presentation before treatment;
  - CT screening of asymptomatic at-risk people (after federal approval);
  - incidental nodule management;
  - molecular testing of all NSCLC, including resectable stages;
  - adjuvant TKI for common EGFR mutations;
  - adjuvant ICI for resected PD-L1 ≥50%;
  - mandatory PD-L1 testing;
  - consolidation ICI after chemoradiotherapy.

  It is a national guideline from another country. Schütte W, Gütz S, Nehls W et al. Pneumologie 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37884003/

### sclc

- **DeLLphi-301 tarlatamab (phase 2, n=220, N Engl J Med).** In pretreated SCLC (median two prior lines), ORR was 40% at 10 mg and 32% at 100 mg, median PFS 4.9 and 3.9 months, and 9-month OS 68% and 66%. CRS occurred in 51% and 61%, mostly in cycle 1. It is non-comparative and supports the 10 mg dose. Ahn MJ, Cho BC, Felip E et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37861218/
- **Berzosertib plus topotecan vs topotecan in relapsed SCLC (randomised phase 2, n=60, JAMA Oncol).** The primary endpoint PFS was not improved (HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.46–1.41). OS was longer with the combination (8.9 vs 5.4 months, HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.29–0.96). The trial is small and the OS finding is secondary. Takahashi N, Hao Z, Villaruz LC et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37824137/
- **ASCO–Ontario Health guideline: systemic therapy for SCLC (J Clin Oncol 2023).** Based on 95 studies, it covers systemic options, timing, older and poor-PS patients, biomarkers and myeloid support. The abstract does not list the specific recommendations, so it is rated high rather than practice-changing. Khurshid H, Ismaila N, Bian J et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37820295/

### lung-ctdna-mrd

- **BR.36 stage 1: ctDNA molecular response on pembrolizumab (randomised adaptive phase 2, n=50, Nat Med).** Maximal mutant allele fraction clearance by cycle 3 had 82% sensitivity and 75% specificity for RECIST response. Molecular responders had longer PFS (5.03 vs 2.6 months) and OS (not reached vs 7.23 months). It is small, and stage 2 randomises treatment intensification by ctDNA. Anagnostou V, Ho C, Nicholas G et al. Nat Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37814061/

### lung-screening / lung-nonpharm

- **Immediate smoking cessation support at lung screening, QuLIT1 and QuLIT2 (two RCTs, n=426 analysed, Thorax).** At 12 months, CO-verified quit rates were 12.1% vs 4.7% (aOR 2.97, 95% CI 1.38–6.90) and self-reported quits 20.0% vs 12.8%. It is a UK targeted lung health check setting. Williams PJ, Philip KEJ, Buttery SC et al. Thorax 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37875371/
- **European statement on incidental findings in LDCT lung screening (ERS/ESTS/ESTRO/ESR/ESTI/EFOMP, Eur J Cardiothorac Surg and Eur Respir J).** It gives management guidance for interstitial lung abnormalities, emphysema, bronchiectasis, coronary calcification, aortic valve disease, mediastinal and thyroid findings. It is relevant to any Nordic screening programme. O'Dowd EL, Tietzova I, Bartlett E et al. 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37804174/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37802631/
- **ACR Lung-RADS v2022 (J Am Coll Radiol).** It adds classification for atypical cysts and juxtapleural, airway-centred and potentially infectious findings, clarifies nodule growth, and introduces stepped management for stable or shrinking nodules. Christensen J, Prosper AE, Wu CC et al. J Am Coll Radiol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37820837/
- **German statement paper on a national organised LDCT screening programme (three parallel publications: Zentralbl Chir, Pneumologie, Rofo).** The professional societies set cornerstones for a uniform, quality-assured programme ahead of the federal ordinance and G-BA programme. Hoffmann H, Kaaks R, Andreas S et al. 2023–2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37816386/

### lung-local-therapy / staging

- **IASLC 9th edition N descriptors (45,032 cN and 35,009 pN cases, J Thorac Oncol).** N0–N3 are kept, and N2 is split into N2a (single station) and N2b (multistation), with consistently distinct prognosis. The data are retrospective and registry-based. Huang J, Osarogiagbon RU, Giroux DJ et al. J Thorac Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37866624/
- **SBRT vs surgery for cT1a-bN0 NSCLC (I-ELCAP/IELCART prospective cohort, n=1,115, J Thorac Oncol).** 10-year lung cancer-specific survival was 90% after surgery vs 88% after SBRT (P = .55). All-cause survival differed crudely (75% vs 45%) but not after propensity matching. It is non-randomised, with only 112 SBRT patients. Henschke CI, Yip R, Sun Q et al. J Thorac Oncol 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37806384/

### lung-nordic

- **Norwegian registries as an external comparator (Cancer Registry of Norway, NPR and prescription registry, Acta Oncol).** Mimicking KEYNOTE-407 eligibility, the real-world cohorts had median OS 7.1 months (individual-level data) and 8.0 months (aggregated) vs 11.3 months in the trial control arm. The registries capture key criteria, but real-world survival is shorter. Børø S, Thoresen S, Boge Brant S et al. Acta Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37801361/
- **ALK+ NSCLC in Denmark (national cohort, n=209, Acta Oncol).** Median OS was 44.0 months overall and 31.8 months in stage IIIB–IVB. First-line second-generation ALK-TKIs had median duration of treatment 25.1 vs 9.1 months for crizotinib. It is retrospective. Hansen KH, Johansen JS, Urbanska EM et al. Acta Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37815923/

### car-t-solid

- **BNT211-01: CLDN6 CAR-T with or without CAR-T-amplifying RNA vaccine (phase 1 dose escalation, n=22, Nat Med).** Unconfirmed ORR was 33% (7/21), 57% in germ cell tumours at the higher dose level, and disease control 67%. CRS occurred in 46% (one grade 3). RP2D is not set and manufacturing is being changed. No lung responses are highlighted. Mackensen A, Haanen JBAG, Koenecke C et al. Nat Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37872225/

### thoracic-other

- **PEMMELA: pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib in second/third-line pleural mesothelioma (single-arm phase 2, n=38, Lancet Oncol).** ORR was 58% by investigator (45% by independent review) at 17.7 months' follow-up. Serious TRAEs occurred in 26%, including one treatment-related death, and 76% needed lenvatinib dose reduction or discontinuation. It is single-arm. Douma LH, Lalezari F, van der Noort V et al. Lancet Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37844598/
- **Subxiphoid vs lateral intercostal thoracoscopic thymectomy (multicentre RCT, J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg).** The subxiphoid approach lowered the 0–7 day pain AUC (difference −4.82) with similar length of stay, opioid use and complications (7.84% vs 8.00%). Follow-up was only 6 months. Wang S, Yang X, Jiang J et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37890660/
