## High
### egfr-nsclc
- **High PD-L1 Expression and Clinical Outcomes of First-Line Osimertinib in EGFR-Mutant NSCLC: A Multicenter Real-World Validation Study (WJOG 20724L).** Retrospective REAL-WIND cohort from 16 Japanese centres (n=385; non-overlapping validation cohort n=200). PD-L1 TPS ≥50% was associated with shorter rwPFS (validation HR 1.95, 95% CI 1.30–2.92; full cohort HR 1.76, 1.30–2.37) and OS (HR 1.72, 1.02–3.09; HR 1.92, 1.28–2.87), robust to propensity-score analysis. It suggests a subgroup that may need intensification, but it is retrospective and the authors state it is an association, not a validated predictive biomarker. Sakata Y, Murotani K, Fujimoto D et al., JTO Clin Res Rep, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42964815/

### lung-key-evidence
- **Modified CARG Score-Guided First-Line Treatment in Older or Vulnerable Patients With Advanced NSCLC: DURATION (AIO-YMO/TRK-0416).** Randomised open-label phase 2 (n=201; age ≥70 and/or CCI>1 and/or ECOG ≥2), mCARG-stratified to doublet vs single-agent chemotherapy, then 4 cycles of chemotherapy vs 2 cycles then durvalumab. Grade 3/4 TRAEs (primary) did not differ (P=.81). mCARG ≤3 had longer PFS (6.1 vs 3.2 mo, HR 0.54) and OS (10.2 vs 5.3 mo, HR 0.48), which the authors regard as prognostic. Among responders, early durvalumab switch gave OS 25.3 vs 11.3 months (P=.057, numeric only). Phase 2, no formal comparison against the standard of care. Kuon J, Blasi M, Bozorgmehr F et al., Clin Lung Cancer, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42966192/

### nsclc-immunotherapy
- **PD-(L)1×VEGF bispecific antibodies in solid tumors: clinical trial updates from ASCO 2026.** Review of ASCO 2026 data: phase 3 HARMONi-6 showed an OS benefit for ivonescimab + chemotherapy over tislelizumab + chemotherapy in first-line squamous NSCLC; pumitamig, SSGJ-707 and HB0025 phase 2 data in first-line NSCLC were encouraging. The abstract gives no effect sizes, and the non-squamous and non-NSCLC data are hypothesis-generating. A secondary summary, so check primary HARMONi-6 publication for HR/CI. Wang Z, Guo C, Yuan X et al., J Hematol Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42966842/

### lung-local-therapy
- **Local ablative treatment for oligoprogression during immunotherapy in metastatic NSCLC: systematic review and meta-analysis.** 22 studies in the review; the primary pooled analyses each rest on 4 studies (one randomised subgroup, three retrospective cohorts). LAT (mostly SBRT) was associated with PFS HR 0.45 (95% CI 0.33–0.62) and OS HR 0.41 (0.27–0.63), I²=0%. The evidence is predominantly observational and low certainty (selection bias likely), with no NSCLC-specific RCT; the authors say randomised trials are pending. Massalha I, Hijab A, Zabit R et al., Oncoimmunology, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42965526/

### lung-repurposed
- **Effect of Risedronate on Rib Cortical Thickness After SBRT to Peripheral Lung Tumors: randomized phase 2.** Double-blind placebo-controlled trial, 73 patients (78 tumours), risedronate 150 mg vs placebo before SBRT. The primary endpoint, change in rib cortical thickness at 3 months within the >30 Gy region, did not differ (3.1% vs 6.4%; difference −3.3%, p=0.44). Later differences (−11.8% at 12 months) were descriptive only. Surrogate radiological endpoint, no fracture or pain outcome, so clinical benefit is unproven. Hughes RT, Black PJ, Wood IR et al., JTO Clin Res Rep, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42964859/
