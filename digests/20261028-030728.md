## Practice-changing

### Izalontamab brengitecan vs platinum chemotherapy in EGFR-mutated NSCLC after third-generation TKI (PANKU-Lung01)
PANKU-Lung01 is a multicentre, randomised, open-label phase 3 at 73 Chinese sites. It assigned 432 patients with EGFR-mutated non-squamous NSCLC, progressing on a third-generation EGFR TKI and without prior chemotherapy for advanced disease, 1:1 to iza-bren (EGFRxHER3 bispecific ADC) or pemetrexed plus platinum (modified ITT 422). The primary endpoint, BICR-assessed PFS, was 11.1 vs 5.7 months (HR 0.40, 95% CI 0.31-0.51) at a 15 July 2026 cutoff, with median follow-up of about 15 months. Grade >=3 treatment-related AEs were more frequent with iza-bren (79% vs 62%, mainly haematological); treatment-related ILD occurred in 2 patients (1%) and there was one treatment-related death per arm. OS is an interim analysis only and no OS result is in the abstract, so survival benefit is open. It is open-label, China-only and sponsor-funded (Baili-Bio); no approval or Norwegian reimbursement exists yet, and it joins sac-TMT data in the same setting.
Hong S, Zhou T, Hou X et al., Lancet, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42933461/
