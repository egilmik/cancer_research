### New trial registrations / results
- **RGL-270 + ICI in Advanced NSCLC** (NCT07652125, registered). Investigator-initiated trial (n=40) of RGL-270 (mRNA neoantigen vaccine) plus PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor in advanced NSCLC, assessing safety and immunogenicity. Phase N/A, sponsor Guangdong Association of Clinical Trials, n=40, recruiting. Arms: RGL-270 (mRNA neoantigen vaccine) plus PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor vs physician's choice standard of care (real-world observational cohort). Single-centre, open-label, investigator-initiated; endpoints are safety, immunogenicity, preliminary efficacy and exploratory biomarkers.
  https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07652125
- **Universal Immunization to Fortify Immunotherapy Efficacy and Response** (NCT07597070, registered). Phase 2 (n=500) of Pfizer-BioNTech COVID mRNA vaccine added to ICI in stage IV NSCLC, testing the retrospective-cohort signal prospectively. Phase 2, sponsor University of Florida, planned n=500, not yet recruiting. Arm: Pfizer-BioNTech mRNA COVID-19 vaccine added to immune checkpoint inhibitors in stage IV NSCLC. Rationale cited: retrospective cohorts linking COVID mRNA vaccination with ICIs to better 3-year OS and higher tumour PD-L1. No results yet; the retrospective association is observational and may be confounded.
  https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07597070
