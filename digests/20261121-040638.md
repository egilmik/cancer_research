### EGFR / resistance / drivers

- **New registration (phase 3, not yet recruiting, posted 2026-11-20, sponsor Shanghai JMT-Bio):** A Study of Glumetinib in Combination With Osimertinib as 1st Line Treatment in Patients With Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NCT07951463). n=375, glumetinib + osimertinib vs placebo + osimertinib, with a chemotherapy arm (pemetrexed/carboplatin or cisplatin) in the intervention list, for classic EGFR mutations with MET overexpression/amplification. https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07951463
- **Nivolumab Plus Ipilimumab as First-Line Treatment for Patients With Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: Final, 7.5-Year Outcomes From CheckMate 227.** Final 7.5-year analysis of CheckMate 227 Part 1 (phase 3, stage IV/recurrent NSCLC without EGFR/ALK, nivolumab+ipilimumab vs chemotherapy; PD-L1 ≥1% n=1189, <1% n=550). At minimum follow-up 91.9 months, 7-year OS was 19% vs 10% (HR 0.78, 95% CI 0.67-0.91) for PD-L1 ≥1% and 14% vs 4% (HR 0.64, 0.51-0.79) for PD-L1 <1%; ongoing response at 7 years 25% vs 3% and 20% vs 3%. No new safety signals. Confirms durable benefit of dual checkpoint blockade; limitations are an older chemotherapy comparator and subgroup analyses not powered for conclusions. Reck M, Ciuleanu TE, Pluzanski A et al. J Thorac Oncol 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43057628/
- **Alectinib as Neoadjuvant Treatment in Potentially Resectable Stage III Anaplastic Lymphoma Kinase-Positive Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: ALNEO Phase II Trial (GOIRC-01-2020-ML42316).** ALNEO, single-arm multicentre phase II (n=33, stage III ALK+ NSCLC): 8 weeks neoadjuvant alectinib, surgery, then up to 96 weeks adjuvant. Primary endpoint major pathologic response 42% (95% CI 26-61), pCR 12%, ORR 67%; 85% underwent surgery, R0 86%. At median 26.8 months follow-up, EFS/DFS/OS not reached, 18% recurred; grade ≥3 AEs 9% neoadjuvant, 27% adjuvant. Shows feasibility and activity; no comparator, small n, immature survival, and MPR is a surrogate for TKIs. Leonetti A, Boni L, Gnetti L et al. J Clin Oncol 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43063368/
- **CHMP summary of positive opinion: Aumseqa (aumolertinib), NSCLC** Positive CHMP opinion for Aumseqa (aumolertinib, SFL Pharmaceuticals) in advanced NSCLC with EGFR ex19del/L858R first line (PFS benefit vs gefitinib) and T790M-positive disease (response rate/duration). Opinion date is inconsistent between EMA pages (11 Dec 2025 summary vs 12 Nov 2026), so verify before relying on it. An EC decision follows about 67 days after an opinion; Norwegian reimbursement status not known.  . https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/smop-initial/chmp-summary-positive-opinion-aumseqa_en.pdf

### Immunotherapy and guidelines

All conditional, based on low-certainty evidence. Tzouvelekis A, Hellemons ME, Billiet C et al. Eur Respir J 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43057869/
