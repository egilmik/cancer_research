### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-top-journals
- **KEYNOTE-189 protocol-specified final analysis: pembrolizumab or placebo plus pemetrexed-platinum in untreated metastatic nonsquamous NSCLC (phase III, n=616, 2:1).** At a median follow-up of 31.0 months, OS HR was 0.56 (95% CI 0.46–0.69) and PFS HR 0.49 (0.41–0.59), regardless of PD-L1. Grade 3–5 AEs were 72.1% vs 66.8%, and 40.8% of the control arm crossed over on-study. This confirms an existing standard rather than changing one. Rodríguez-Abreu D, Powell SF, Hochmair MJ et al. Ann Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33894335/
- **KEYNOTE-024 at 5 years: pembrolizumab vs platinum chemotherapy for PD-L1 TPS ≥50% NSCLC (phase III, n=305).** Median OS was 26.3 vs 13.4 months (HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.48–0.81), and 5-year OS was 31.9% vs 16.3%, despite a 66% effective crossover. 82% of the 39 patients who completed 2 years of pembrolizumab were alive at about 5 years. This is the first 5-year data from a first-line IO phase III; OS was a secondary endpoint. Reck M, Rodríguez-Abreu D, Robinson AG et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33872070/
- **J-AXEL: nab-paclitaxel vs docetaxel in previously treated advanced NSCLC (open-label non-inferiority phase 3, n=503, Japan).** Median OS was 16.2 vs 13.6 months (HR 0.85, 95.2% CI 0.68–1.07), meeting non-inferiority. PFS (HR 0.76) and ORR (29.9% vs 15.4%) favoured nab-paclitaxel. Febrile neutropenia was 2% vs 22%, but sensory neuropathy was 10% vs 1%. Patients had had cytotoxic chemotherapy, and most had not had immunotherapy, which limits applicability to the post-IO setting. Yoneshima Y, Morita S, Ando M et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33915251/
- **RATIONALE 307: tislelizumab plus paclitaxel or nab-paclitaxel and carboplatin vs chemotherapy alone in first-line squamous NSCLC (open-label phase 3, n=360, China).** IRC-assessed PFS was 7.6 vs 5.5 months (HR 0.52 and 0.48), with ORR about 73–75% vs 50%, and the benefit did not depend on PD-L1. At a median follow-up of 8.6 months OS is immature, and chemotherapy alone is no longer the standard comparator. Wang J, Lu S, Yu X et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33792623/
- **JAVELIN Lung 200 at 2 years: avelumab vs docetaxel after platinum in PD-L1+ NSCLC (phase 3, n=792; 529 PD-L1+).** The primary OS endpoint had already failed. Post-hoc 2-year OS was 29.9% vs 20.5% in PD-L1+, and 36.4% vs 17.7% in ≥50%. Median DoR was 19.1 vs 5.7 months. These are post-hoc analyses of a negative trial. Park K, Özgüroğlu M, Vansteenkiste J et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33845211/
- **Chinese Experts Consensus on ICIs for NSCLC (2020 version, CSCO NSCLC expert committee).** It is an update of the 2019 consensus covering ICIs in advanced and locally advanced disease and in the adjuvant/neoadjuvant setting. The abstract does not say which recommendations changed. Zhou C, Wang J, Wang B et al. Zhongguo Fei Ai Za Zhi 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33896153/
- **Chinese guideline on clinical practice of molecular testing in NSCLC (Chinese Society of Pathology and partners).** There is no abstract, so the content cannot be summarised from this item. Chinese Society of Pathology, Pathology Quality Control Center, Chinese Medical Association Chinese Society of Oncology et al. Zhonghua Bing Li Xue Za Zhi 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33831989/
- **Osimertinib vs osimertinib plus carboplatin-pemetrexed at T790M-positive progression after first-line EGFR TKI (open-label randomised phase 2, n=62, Japan).** Accrual stopped early once osimertinib moved to first line. Median PFS was 15.8 vs 14.6 months (HR 1.09, 95% CI 0.51–2.32), and ORR 71.4% vs 53.6%. Adding chemotherapy gave no benefit, though the trial is small, underpowered, and in a setting that has largely disappeared. Tanaka K, Asahina H, Kishimoto J et al. Eur J Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33813328/

### sclc
- **MERU: rovalpituzumab tesirine (DLL3 ADC) vs placebo as maintenance after first-line platinum in ES-SCLC (double-blind phase 3, n=748).** In DLL3-high tumours, OS HR was 1.07 (95% CI 0.84–1.36; median 8.5 vs 9.8 months), which met the futility criteria, and the trial was stopped. Investigator-assessed PFS improved (4.0 vs 1.4 months, HR 0.48), but toxicity (effusions, oedema, photosensitivity) was higher. This is a negative trial and the end of Rova-T development. DLL3 remains a target, now pursued with bispecifics. Johnson ML, Zvirbule Z, Laktionov K et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33823285/
- **ZL-2306-005: niraparib vs placebo as maintenance in platinum-responsive ES-SCLC (double-blind phase 3, n=185 of a planned 591, China).** The trial stopped early because the treatment landscape changed. BICR PFS was 1.54 vs 1.36 months (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.46–0.95), and OS did not differ (HR 1.03). Grade ≥3 AEs were 34.4% vs 25.0%. The PFS gain has no clinical meaning, so the trial is negative. Ai X, Pan Y, Shi J et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33915252/
- **Berzosertib (ATR inhibitor) plus topotecan in relapsed small cell neuroendocrine cancers (Cancer Cell; chemical-genetic screens plus a proof-of-concept clinical study).** ORR was 36% (9/25) in SCLC, which met the primary endpoint, with durable regressions in platinum-resistant disease. Tumours with high neuroendocrine differentiation and replication stress were more likely to respond. This is a small single-arm study. Thomas A, Takahashi N, Rajapakse VN et al. Cancer Cell 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33848478/
- **Trilaciclib: First Approval (Drugs profile).** The FDA approved trilaciclib in February 2021 to reduce chemotherapy-induced myelosuppression when given before platinum/etoposide or topotecan regimens in ES-SCLC. This is a journal profile of the decision, not the decision itself, and the agent is supportive care rather than antitumour therapy. Dhillon S Drugs 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33861388/
- **ESMO Clinical Practice Guidelines for SCLC: diagnosis, treatment and follow-up (2021).** There is no abstract, so this item does not show which recommendations changed. It is relevant as the first ESMO SCLC guideline of the chemo-immunotherapy era. Dingemans AC, Früh M, Ardizzoni A et al. Ann Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33864941/

### thoracic-other
- **KEYNOTE-158 mesothelioma cohort: pembrolizumab in previously treated MPM (single-arm phase 2, n=118, 14 countries).** ORR was 8% (95% CI 4–15), with median DoR 14.3 months, and responses occurred regardless of PD-L1 (8% PD-L1+, 13% PD-L1−). Median OS was 10.0 months and median PFS 2.1 months. Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs were 16%, with one treatment-related death. Activity is modest, and the cohort has no comparator. Yap TA, Nakagawa K, Fujimoto N et al. Lancet Respir Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33836153/
- **NIBIT-MESO-1 4-year follow-up and retreatment: tremelimumab plus durvalumab in unresectable mesothelioma (non-randomised phase 2, n=40).** Median OS was 16.5 months, with 36-month OS 20% and 48-month OS 15%. Of the 17 retreated patients none responded, though 41% had stable disease. Higher TMB was associated with longer OS only in a small post-hoc analysis. Calabrò L, Rossi G, Morra A et al. Lancet Respir Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33844995/

### nsclc-drivers / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **Clinical acquired resistance to adagrasib in KRAS G12C NSCLC (Cancer Discov, case-based mechanistic study).** One patient developed 10 resistance alterations in serial cfDNA across KRAS, NRAS, BRAF and MAP2K1, all reactivating RAS-MAPK. A novel KRAS Y96D switch-II pocket mutation blocked inactive-state inhibitors, but the active-state tricomplex inhibitor RM-018 overcame it in models. The clinical data come from a single patient. Tanaka N, Lin JJ, Li C et al. Cancer Discov 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33824136/

### car-t-solid
- **SynNotch-CAR T cells for glioblastoma (Sci Transl Med).** A synNotch receptor primes CAR expression on a tumour-specific or brain-specific signal (EGFRvIII or MOG). In mouse models this overcame antigen heterogeneity, improved specificity and gave more persistent T cells. The data are preclinical. Choe JH, Watchmaker PB, Simic MS et al. Sci Transl Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33910979/
- **SynNotch-CAR circuits for solid tumours (Sci Transl Med).** These are logic-gated circuits (for example ALPPL2-primed CARs against mesothelin or HER2) that improved tumour recognition and persistence over constitutive CARs in mesothelioma and ovarian cancer mouse models. The data are preclinical. Hyrenius-Wittsten A, Su Y, Park M et al. Sci Transl Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33910981/
