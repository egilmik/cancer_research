## High

### nsclc-drivers / egfr-nsclc / lung-key-evidence
- **Third- vs first-generation EGFR-TKIs for CNS metastases (systematic review and meta-analysis).** Five phase 3 RCTs (AENEAS, FLAURA, FURLONG, IBIO-103, LASER301) in first-line EGFR-mutant NSCLC with CNS metastases. Third-generation TKIs gave CNS-PFS HR 0.44 (0.33–0.58) and PFS HR 0.44 (0.35–0.54), with higher CNS ORR (RR 1.32). It supports third-generation TKIs as first-line choice in CNS disease; limitations are trial-level pooling of CNS subgroups and no new patient-level data. Gan J, Zhu S, Fang Y et al. — J Thorac Dis, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42866146/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42866146/)
- **First-line T-DXd in HER2-altered advanced NSCLC (real-world, six centres, n=69).** In HER2-only disease, T-DXd gave mPFS 15.7 mo (95% CI 12.5–18.9) and ORR 85.7%, versus 7.6 mo (chemo-based) and 4.4 mo (pan-HER TKI); HER2/EGFR co-altered patients analysed separately. Relevant to first-line HER2 options; limited by retrospective design, small groups, selection and unadjusted comparisons. Liu C, Li T, Qin BY et al. — BMC Cancer, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42864628/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42864628/)
- **Zidesamtinib (Jideytro) for previously treated ROS1+ NSCLC.** Journal profile of the FDA approval of 22 Jul 2026 (adults, locally advanced/metastatic ROS1+ NSCLC after a ROS1 TKI), resting on the single-arm ARROS-1 trial; brain-penetrant, active against G2032R. Commentary, no new data; no head-to-head comparison with repotrectinib/taletrectinib. Norwegian status not stated. Zahra Hujjat SF, Islam MT — Ann Med Surg (Lond), 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42866738/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42866738/)

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence
- **ICI in resectable NSCLC: neoadjuvant, perioperative and adjuvant (meta-analysis of 12 RCTs, n=8,197).** Treatment started before surgery reduced EFS events (HR 0.59, 0.53–0.66; 7 trials) and death (HR 0.68, 0.58–0.80; 5 trials). About 70% of patients in perioperative arms began postoperative study treatment. Trial-level pooling with heterogeneous designs; does not show that postoperative ICI can be omitted. Dai J, Liu M, Li H et al. — Front Pharmacol, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42865638/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42865638/)
