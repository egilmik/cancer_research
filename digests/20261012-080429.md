## Practice-changing

- **CONFIRM: nivolumab vs placebo in relapsed mesothelioma after first-line platinum (multicentre double-blind placebo-controlled phase 3, 24 UK hospitals, Lancet Oncol).** 332 patients with pleural or peritoneal mesothelioma, ECOG PS 0–1, were randomised 2:1 to nivolumab 240 mg every 2 weeks for up to 12 months (n=221) or placebo (n=111). Both co-primary endpoints were met at a median follow-up of 11.6 months: OS 10.2 vs 6.9 months (adjusted HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.52–0.91; p=0.0090) and investigator-assessed PFS 3.0 vs 1.8 months (HR 0.67, 0.53–0.85). Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs were few (diarrhoea 3%, infusion reactions 3%), serious AEs were 41% vs 44%, and there were no treatment-related deaths. The authors state no earlier phase 3 had shown a survival gain after platinum. The absolute OS gain is about 3 months and follow-up is short. Patients were enrolled after first-line platinum chemotherapy; the abstract does not describe prior immunotherapy, regulatory status or reimbursement in Norway. Fennell DA, Ewings S, Ottensmeier C et al. Lancet Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34656227/

## High

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **Mobocertinib in platinum-pretreated EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC (phase 1/2, PPP n=114, EXCLAIM n=96, JAMA Oncol).** In the PPP cohort, IRC-confirmed ORR was 28% (95% CI 20–37), median DoR 17.5 months, PFS 7.3 months and OS 24.0 months. EXCLAIM ORR was 25%. Diarrhoea and rash were the most common TRAEs. It is non-randomised. Zhou C, Ramalingam SS, Kim TM et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34647988/
- **Mobocertinib: First Approval (Drugs profile).** Mobocertinib received US accelerated approval for EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC progressing on or after platinum, based on a phase I/II trial. EU and Chinese applications are under assessment. This is a profile, not the decision itself. Markham A Drugs 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34716908/
- **Toripalimab plus carboplatin-pemetrexed after first-line EGFR-TKI in T790M-negative EGFR-mutant NSCLC (multicentre phase II, n=40, STTT).** ORR was 50.0%, DCR 87.5%, median PFS 7.0 months and OS 23.5 months. PD-L1, TMB and CD8+ TIL density did not predict benefit. It is single-arm, with no chemotherapy-alone comparator. Jiang T, Wang P, Zhang J et al. Signal Transduct Target Ther 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34650034/
- **IMpower150 final exploratory subgroup analyses: EGFR mutations and liver/brain metastases (JTO).** At a median follow-up of 39.3 months, atezolizumab-bevacizumab-carboplatin-paclitaxel vs bevacizumab-chemotherapy gave OS HR 0.60 (95% CI 0.31–1.14) in sensitising EGFR mutations and 0.74 (0.38–1.46) after prior TKI. ACP showed no benefit in these groups. These are exploratory subgroups with CIs crossing 1. Nogami N, Barlesi F, Socinski MA et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34626838/
- **TIGER-3: rociletinib vs chemotherapy after first/second-generation EGFR-TKI (phase 3, halted at n=149, JTO Clin Res Rep).** Median PFS was 4.1 months (500 mg) and 5.5 months (625 mg) vs 2.5 months on chemotherapy. In T790M-positive patients the PFS HR was 0.55 (95% CI 0.28–1.07). Grade ≥3 hyperglycaemia occurred in 24% and QT prolongation in 6.7%. Enrolment stopped when development was discontinued, so the trial is underpowered and of historical interest. Yang JC, Reckamp KL, Kim YC et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34589984/
- **NEJ032B: osimertinib in PS 2–3 patients with T790M-positive NSCLC after first/second-generation TKI (phase II, n=33).** ORR was 53.1%, PFS 5.1 months and OS 10.0 months. ILD occurred in 15.2% (grade 3–5 in 6.1%), with one treatment-related death. It is small and single-arm. Tsubata Y, Watanabe K, Saito R et al. Int J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34643820/
- **Japanese Lung Cancer Society guideline update for stage IV EGFR-mutant NSCLC (GRADE, JTO Clin Res Rep).** Osimertinib has the strongest first-line recommendation. EGFR-TKI combinations with platinum chemotherapy or antiangiogenics are also recommended, with a toxicity caveat. EGFR-TKIs are recommended for uncommon mutations except exon 20 insertions. The abstract does not say which recommendations differ from 2018. Ninomiya K, Teraoka S, Zenke Y et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34589972/
- **JIPANG-TR: EGFR mutation and benefit from adjuvant platinum doublet (exploratory biomarker study of the JIPANG phase III, n=389, Cancer Sci).** EGFR mutation was associated with shorter RFS (24 months vs not reached; multivariable HR 1.57, 95% CI 1.15–2.16). The authors read this as poor prognosis or poor response to adjuvant chemotherapy. It is post-hoc and cannot separate prognostic from predictive effects. Takahashi T, Sakai K, Kenmotsu H et al. Cancer Sci 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34689382/

### nsclc-drivers
- **Taletrectinib in ROS1+ NSCLC (pooled US and Japan phase 1, n=22 ROS1+, JTO Clin Res Rep).** ORR was 66.7% in TKI-naive patients (n=9) and 33.3% after crizotinib (n=6), and median PFS 29.1 and 14.2 months. ALT elevation occurred in 72.7%. The numbers are very small and the CIs wide. Ou SI, Fujiwara Y, Shaw AT et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34589973/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy
- **CheckMate 227 Part 1: 4-year outcomes of first-line nivolumab-ipilimumab vs chemotherapy (phase 3, JTO).** At a median follow-up of 54.8 months, OS HR was 0.76 (95% CI 0.65–0.90) for PD-L1 ≥1% and 0.64 (0.51–0.81) for PD-L1 <1%. 4-year OS was 29% vs 18% and 24% vs 10%. Patients who stopped for TRAEs still had long-term benefit. This updates an established option. Paz-Ares LG, Ramalingam SS, Ciuleanu TE et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34648948/
- **CheckMate 9LA: 2-year update of nivolumab-ipilimumab plus 2 cycles of chemotherapy vs chemotherapy (phase III, ESMO Open).** At a median follow-up of 30.7 months, median OS was 15.8 vs 11.0 months (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.61–0.86), and 2-year OS 38% vs 26%. Grade 3/4 TRAEs clustered in the first two cycles. This updates an established option. Reck M, Ciuleanu TE, Cobo M et al. ESMO Open 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34607285/
- **ITACA: pharmacogenomic-tailored vs standard adjuvant chemotherapy in resected stage II–IIIA NSCLC (phase III, n=690, Ann Oncol).** 5-year OS was 72.9% vs 65.4% (HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.56–1.06; p=0.109), and RFS HR 0.89. Grade 3–5 toxicity was lower in the tailored arm. The trial is negative for its primary endpoint. Novello S, Torri V, Grohe C et al. Ann Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34624497/
- **Neoadjuvant toripalimab plus carboplatin with pemetrexed or nab-paclitaxel in resectable stage III NSCLC (phase 2, n=33, Oncoimmunology).** 90.9% had resection and 96.7% of those were R0. ITT MPR was 60.6% and pCR 45.5%. It is single-arm with no survival data. Zhao ZR, Yang CP, Chen S et al. Oncoimmunology 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34712513/
- **AFT-09: two sequences of pembrolizumab and platinum chemotherapy in stage IV NSCLC (randomised phase 2, n=90, JTO Clin Res Rep).** ORR at 24 weeks was 39.5% vs 40.4%, PFS HR 1.06 and OS HR 1.04. The authors conclude neither sequence merits phase 3. Hensing TA, Wang X, Stinchcombe TE et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34590049/
- **KCSG LU20-11: outcomes after 2 years of ICI or early discontinuation without progression (multicentre retrospective, Korea, Cancer).** Among 96 patients completing 2 years, 12-month PFS and OS after completion were 81.1% and 96.4%. Among 43 who stopped early without progression (60.5% for AEs), they were 71.0% and 90.0%. It is retrospective and selected for responders, but bears on treatment-duration questions. Kim H, Kim DW, Kim M et al. Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34705268/
- **AATS expert panel consensus: defining high risk for lobectomy in stage I NSCLC (JTCVS).** The panel ranked supplemental oxygen need for severe lung disease, low DLCO, frailty and functional status as the most important risk factors, and age alone as less important. It is survey-based consensus without outcome data. Pennathur A, Brunelli A, Criner GJ et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34716030/

### lung-repurposed
- **Proton pump inhibitors and atezolizumab efficacy (post-hoc analysis of IMpower150, n=1,202, Br J Cancer).** 37% used a PPI. PPI use was associated with worse OS on the atezolizumab arms (HR 1.53, 95% CI 1.21–1.95) but not on bevacizumab-chemotherapy (HR 1.01). The atezolizumab OS benefit was HR 1.03 in PPI users vs 0.68 in non-users (interaction p=0.028). It is observational within a trial, so confounding by indication is possible. Hopkins AM, Kichenadasse G, McKinnon RA et al. Br J Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34711947/

### sclc
- **Pembrolizumab plus amrubicin in relapsed SCLC (single-arm phase 2, n=25, Japan).** 88% relapsed within 90 days of platinum. ORR was 52.0% (95% CI 31.3–72.2), PFS 4.0 months and OS 10.6 months. Grade ≥3 neutropenia occurred in 64% and febrile neutropenia in 16%. It is small, single-arm and ICI-naive; amrubicin is a Japanese standard. Akamatsu H, Teraoka S, Hayashi H et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34590034/

### lung-screening / lung-nonpharm / lung-nordic
- **Integrated smoking cessation counselling in a screening programme, 2-year follow-up (RCT, n=337 smokers, Canada).** 30-day abstinence at 24 months was 21.4% with telephone counselling vs 18.3% with written information (difference 3.1%, 95% CI −5.4 to 11.6; p=0.48). Quit rates rose in both arms over screening participation. The trial is negative for the intervention. Tremblay A, Taghizadeh N, MacEachern P et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34589978/
- **SCAN-LEAF: NSCLC epidemiology and survival in Sweden (n=30,067) and Denmark (n=31,939), 2005–2015 (JTO Clin Res Rep).** 1-year survival improved for non-squamous NSCLC at all stages, but 5-year survival only for stage I–II. Squamous histology improved little. This is a pre-immunotherapy Nordic baseline. Ekman S, Horvat P, Rosenlund M et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34590017/
- **Nationwide survival before and after first-line ICI in advanced NSCLC without EGFR/ALK (Danish Lung Cancer Registry, n=2,055 post vs 1,658 pre-approval, Cancers).** 3-year OS was 18% vs 6%. Among ICI-treated patients, PS ≥1 and bone or liver metastases predicted poorer OS. It is a before-after comparison and other changes over time may contribute. Mouritzen MT, Carus A, Ladekarl M et al. Cancers (Basel) 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34638329/

### lung-vaccines-cell
- **JNJ-757, a live attenuated Listeria-based mesothelin immunotherapy, in previously treated NSCLC (two phase 1 studies, n=18 monotherapy, n=12 with nivolumab).** It induced transient mesothelin-specific T-cell responses (10/13) but produced no objective responses; the best outcome was stable disease. Two treatment-related fatal pneumonitis cases occurred with nivolumab. It is negative. Brahmer JR, Johnson ML, Cobo M et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34589981/

### thoracic-other
- **MERIT 3-year update: nivolumab in previously treated pleural mesothelioma (phase II, n=34, Japan).** Median OS was 17.3 months, and 2- and 3-year OS 35.3% and 23.5%. Median PFS was 5.9 months, and 8 patients were alive at 3 years. It is single-arm and small. Fujimoto N, Okada M, Kijima T et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34589998/
- **JME-001: first-line cisplatin-pemetrexed-nivolumab in unresectable pleural mesothelioma (phase II, n=18, Japan, JITC).** ORR was 77.8% (95% CI 52.4–93.6) and DCR 94.4%. Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 55.6%, with no treatment-related deaths. It is small, single-arm and without survival data. Miyamoto Y, Kozuki T, Aoe K et al. J Immunother Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34711664/
- **Olaparib in previously treated mesothelioma (single-centre phase 2, n=23, JTO Clin Res Rep).** There was 1 PR (4%). Median PFS was 3.6 months and OS 8.7 months. Germline BAP1 mutation was associated with shorter PFS (2.3 vs 4.1 months) and OS (4.6 vs 9.6 months). Activity is limited, including in BAP1-mutant disease. Ghafoor A, Mian I, Wagner C et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34661178/
- **YS110 (anti-CD26 antibody) in advanced pleural mesothelioma (phase 2, n=31, Japan).** The 6-month DCR of 3.2% did not exceed the predefined threshold. Median PFS was 2.8 months and OS 9.7 months, and ILD occurred in 9.7%. It is negative. Nakagawa K, Kijima T, Okada M et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34590026/
- **RESOUND: regorafenib in pretreated B2–B3 thymoma and thymic carcinoma (phase 2, n=19, Cancer).** The primary endpoint (8-week PFS 78.9%) was met. The RECIST PR rate was 5.3% (68.4% by Choi criteria), median PFS 9.6 months and OS 33.8 months. Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 52.6%. It is small and single-arm. Perrino M, De Pas T, Bozzarelli S et al. Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34706060/
