### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / lung-local-therapy
- **SINDAS: first-generation EGFR TKI with or without RT to all sites in synchronous oligometastatic EGFR-mutant NSCLC (randomised trial, n=133, prespecified interim analysis at 68% accrual, China, JNCI).** Patients had ≤5 metastases and no brain metastases, and RT was 25–40 Gy in 5 fractions to all metastases and the primary/nodes. At a median follow-up of 23.6 months, median PFS was 20.2 vs 12.5 months and OS 25.5 vs 17.4 months (both p<0.001; HRs not given in the abstract). Symptomatic grade 3–4 pneumonitis occurred in 6% with RT, with no grade 5 events. The ethics committee stopped the trial early. The comparator (gefitinib, erlotinib or icotinib) is no longer the first-line standard, and early stopping may overstate the effect. Wang XS, Bai YF, Verma V et al. J Natl Cancer Inst 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35094066/
- **ADAURA HRQoL: adjuvant osimertinib vs placebo (phase 3, secondary endpoint, n=682, CCR).** SF-36 physical and mental component scores were maintained to week 96, with no clinically meaningful difference between arms. Time to deterioration did not differ (PCS HR 1.17, 95% CI 0.82–1.67; MCS HR 0.98). It shows the DFS gain does not come at a quality-of-life cost. Majem M, Goldman JW, John T et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35012927/
- **MATCH-R: single-cell DNA sequencing of osimertinib resistance (Ann Oncol).** Of 45 patients progressing on osimertinib, 9 developed a second targetable driver (e.g. ALK, RET, FGFR3 fusions or BRAF, KRAS mutations). Single-cell sequencing addressed whether these co-occur with EGFR in the same cells, and combination treatments were tested in patient-derived models. It is translational, with small numbers. Chen J, Facchinetti F, Braye F et al. Ann Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35066105/
- **Dacomitinib-based EGFR PROTAC degrader (preclinical, Bioorg Chem).** Compound 13 selectively degraded EGFR del19 (DC50 3.57 nM) but not other EGFR mutants, wild-type EGFR, HER2 or HER4. It gave 90% tumour growth inhibition in vivo at 30 mg/kg without observable toxicity. The authors call it the first EGFR-PROTAC tested in vivo. It is preclinical only. Shi S, Du Y, Huang L et al. Bioorg Chem 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35081479/

### nsclc-drivers
- **IFCT-1703 R2D2: trastuzumab, pertuzumab and docetaxel in pretreated HER2-mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=45, JCO).** ORR was 29%, with 58% stable disease. Median PFS was 6.8 months (95% CI 4.0–8.5) and DoR 11 months. Grade 3/4 TRAEs occurred in 64%, mainly neutropenia (33%), but none discontinued for toxicity. It is single-arm, and HER2 ADCs (trastuzumab deruxtecan) have since shown higher activity. Mazieres J, Lafitte C, Ricordel C et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35073148/
- **ALUR final analysis: alectinib vs chemotherapy after crizotinib and platinum in ALK NSCLC (phase III, n=119, ESMO Open).** Investigator PFS was 10.9 vs 1.4 months (HR 0.20, 95% CI 0.12–0.33) and ORR 50.6% vs 2.5%. Exploratory plasma NGS correlated molecular factors with outcome. It confirms an established option; crizotinib is no longer the usual first line. Wolf J, Helland Å, Oh IJ et al. ESMO Open 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35042152/
- **ASCEND-7: ceritinib in ALK NSCLC with active brain or leptomeningeal metastases (phase II, CCR).** Whole-body ORR was 35.7% (prior RT and ALKi), 30.0% (ALKi, no RT), 50.0% (RT, ALKi-naive) and 59.1% (ALKi-naive, no RT). It was designed specifically for CNS disease, but ceritinib is no longer a preferred ALK TKI. Chow LQM, Barlesi F, Bertino EM et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35091443/
- **Iruplinalkib (WX-0593), a new ALK/ROS1 TKI (phase 1 dose escalation and expansion, n=153, China, STTT).** Two DLTs occurred at 300 mg (QT prolongation, heart failure) and the MTD was not reached. TRAEs occurred in 92%. Expansion-phase ORR was the primary endpoint; the figures are beyond the truncated abstract. It is early phase. Shi Y, Fang J, Hao X et al. Signal Transduct Target Ther 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35087031/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy
- **GEMSTONE-302: sugemalimab plus platinum chemotherapy vs placebo plus chemotherapy first line in metastatic squamous and non-squamous NSCLC (double-blind phase 3, n=479, China, Lancet Oncol).** PFS was 7.8 vs 4.9 months at interim (HR 0.50). At the final PFS analysis (median follow-up 17.8 months) it was 9.0 vs 4.9 months (HR 0.48, 95% CI 0.39–0.60). Grade 3–4 neutropenia was similar (33% both). The comparator, chemotherapy alone, is no longer standard, and OS is not mature. Zhou C, Wang Z, Sun Y et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35038432/
- **GEMSTONE-301: sugemalimab vs placebo after concurrent or sequential CRT in unresectable stage III NSCLC (double-blind phase 3 interim, n=381, China, Lancet Oncol).** BICR PFS was 9.0 vs 5.8 months (HR 0.64, 95% CI 0.48–0.85). Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 9% vs 6%, with 4 treatment-related deaths with sugemalimab. It covers patients after sequential CRT, whom PACIFIC did not include, but placebo is not the standard after concurrent CRT and OS is immature. Zhou Q, Chen M, Jiang O et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35038429/
- **IFCT-GFPC-1101: response-adapted maintenance vs cisplatin-pemetrexed then pemetrexed in advanced non-squamous NSCLC (phase III, n=932, Lung Cancer).** OS was 10.9 vs 10.4 months (HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.84–1.13) and PFS 4.8 vs 4.5 months. Tailoring maintenance to induction response gave no benefit. It is pre-immunotherapy. Souquet PJ, Audigier-Valette C, Molinier O et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35051725/
- **Durvalumab-tremelimumab alone or with low-dose or hypofractionated RT after PD-(L)1 progression (randomised phase 2, n=90, NCI ETCTN, Lancet Oncol).** ORR was 11.5%, 7.7% and 11.5% (n=26 each treated). The trial was stopped for futility; serious TRAEs were more frequent with RT. Adding RT does not overcome PD-(L)1 resistance in this design. Schoenfeld JD, Giobbie-Hurder A, Ranasinghe S et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35033226/
- **CheckMate 078 3-year update: nivolumab vs docetaxel after platinum in a predominantly Chinese population (phase 3, Lung Cancer).** At ≥37.3 months, 3-year OS was 19% vs 12%, and 30% vs 0% of responders remained in response at 3 years. Patient-reported outcomes were also reported. It is long-term follow-up of an established standard. Chang J, Wu YL, Lu S et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35093625/

### lung-screening / lung-nonpharm / lung-nordic
- **BioMILD: baseline LDCT plus a blood microRNA classifier to set screening intervals (prospective, n=4,119, Italy, Ann Oncol).** At a median follow-up of 5.3 years, CT-positive participants had a 15.8-fold higher 4-year lung cancer incidence (95% CI 10.3–24.1), and classifier-positive participants about twofold higher. Participants negative on both (64.7%) were rescreened at 3 years. It supports risk-tailored intervals; it is a single-centre programme. Pastorino U, Boeri M, Sestini S et al. Ann Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35091076/
- **LDCT screening promotion and overdiagnosis among Asian women (Taiwan Cancer Registry ecological cohort, 57,898 cases, JAMA Intern Med).** Screening was promoted in a population with <5% female smoking. Early-stage incidence rose without a matching fall in late-stage incidence, which suggests overdiagnosis. It is an ecological design; it is relevant to proposals to screen never-smokers. Gao W, Wen CP, Wu A et al. JAMA Intern Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35040922/
- **Blood four-protein panel plus PLCOm2012 for screening risk (blinded validation in PLCO, 1,299 cases and 8,709 controls, JCO).** The panel alone had an AUC of 0.79 for cases within 1 year of diagnosis, and combined with PLCOm2012 0.85 (95% CI 0.82–0.88). It improved selection vs USPSTF criteria. It is a retrospective validation in archived sera. Fahrmann JF, Marsh T, Irajizad E et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34995129/
- **AHRR (cg05575921) methylation to refine screening eligibility (Copenhagen City Heart Study, n=9,206 and 5,370, CEBP).** Adding blood AHRR methylation raised the specificity of all tested criteria (NLST, NELSON, PLCOm2012 and others). The model was validated in the later examination cohort. It is a Danish population cohort with 5-year outcomes. Jacobsen KK, Schnohr P, Jensen GB et al. Cancer Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35064064/
- **ePRO-based symptom management vs usual care after lung cancer surgery (multicentre RCT, n=166, China, JCO).** Surgeons responded to alerts for any of five symptoms scoring ≥4. The intervention reduced symptom threshold events at discharge (median 0 vs 2) and at 4 weeks, and complications (21.5% vs 40.6%, p=0.019). The analysis was per-protocol, and the trial is modest in size. Dai W, Feng W, Zhang Y et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34995100/
- **Second-line or later ICIs in an unselected Danish nationwide NSCLC population (n=840, Acta Oncol).** 19% were ≥75 years, 19% PS ≥2 and 36% had moderate to severe comorbidity. Median OS was 12.2 months (15.1 in women, 10.0 in men). It gives real-world Nordic outcomes for groups under-represented in RCTs; it is retrospective. Mouritzen MT, Junker KF, Carus A et al. Acta Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35012430/

### lung-ctdna-mrd
- **Perioperative ctDNA in resectable stage I–IIIA NSCLC (prospective, n=119, Cancer).** Presurgical ctDNA (24.8% detectable) was associated with shorter RFS (HR 2.42, 95% CI 1.11–5.27) and OS. Postsurgical detection (10.3%) was also associated with shorter RFS. It is small, with wide CIs. Li N, Wang BX, Li J et al. Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35076939/

### car-t-solid
- **Image-guided intrapleural delivery of mesothelin CAR-T for pleural malignancies (phase I/II, n=31, MSKCC, Lung Cancer).** 33 procedures, intracavitary (42%) or intratumoral (58%), delivered the planned dose within 2 hours of thawing. No procedure-related AE exceeded grade 1. It is a technique report; efficacy is not reported here. Ghosn M, Cheema W, Zhu A et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35045358/
