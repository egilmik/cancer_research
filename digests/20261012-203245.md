## High

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals

- **Befotertinib vs icotinib, first line (open-label phase 3, n=362, 39 Chinese centres).** IRC-assessed PFS was 22.1 vs 13.8 months (HR 0.49, 95% CI 0.36–0.68) at about 20 months follow-up. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 30% vs 8%, and treatment-related SAEs in 20% vs 3%. OS is immature. The comparator is a first-generation TKI, so this does not test befotertinib against osimertinib. Lu S, Zhou J, Jian H et al. Lancet Respir Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37244266/
- **ORIENT-31, second interim analysis (double-blind phase 3, n=476).** The trial treats EGFR-mutant non-squamous NSCLC after progression on EGFR-TKI. Sintilimab plus chemotherapy vs chemotherapy improved PFS (5.5 vs 4.3 months, HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.55–0.94), and adding the bevacizumab biosimilar IBI305 gave PFS 7.2 months (HR 0.51). Preliminary OS showed no difference: 20.5 vs 19.2 months (HR 0.97), and 21.1 months with IBI305 (HR 0.98). It is China-only, OS is preliminary, and other phase 3 trials of PD-1 after TKI have been negative. Lu S, Wu L, Jian H et al. Lancet Respir Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37156249/
- **ADAURA 3-year safety and HRQoL (phase 3, n=337 vs 343).** Median exposure was 35.8 months. AEs led to dose reduction in 12%, interruption in 27% and discontinuation in 13% (placebo 1%, 13% and 3%). ILD was the most common reason for discontinuation, and SF-36 HRQoL was maintained. It is a secondary analysis that supports tolerability of the adjuvant standard. John T, Grohé C, Goldman JW et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37236398/
- **Rezivertinib CNS efficacy (pooled phase 1/2b, n=150 with CNS metastases).** In T790M-positive NSCLC with measurable CNS lesions (n=45), CNS ORR was 68.9% and CNS DCR 100%. CNS PFS was 16.5 months. It is post-hoc and in a now largely historical second-line T790M setting. Yang S, Wu S, Zhao Y et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37163774/
- **BBT-176, fourth-generation EGFR TKI (preclinical plus early clinical).** It potently inhibits C797S-containing EGFR (IC50 1.8–5.4 nM vs 125–574 nM for osimertinib) and is active in PDX models. Two phase 1 patients with 19del/T790M/C797S had tumour shrinkage. Clinical data are anecdotal so far. Lim SM, Fujino T, Kim C et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37249619/

### nsclc-drivers / lung-toxicity

- **Severe sotorasib toxicity after recent anti-PD-(L)1 (French multicentre retrospective study, n=102).** Severe sotorasib-related AEs occurred in 50% when sotorasib directly followed anti-PD-(L)1 vs 13% otherwise, and severe hepatotoxicity in 33% vs 11%. Risk was concentrated when the last IO dose was within 30 days. It is retrospective but has a clear sequencing implication. Chour A, Denis J, Mascaux C et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37217096/
- **SWOG S1507: docetaxel plus trametinib in KRAS-mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, n=53).** ORR was 34% overall but only 28% in G12C (n=18), with PFS 4.1 months and OS 10.9 months. TP53 co-mutation was associated with worse OS (HR 2.85). It is small and superseded by direct G12C inhibitors. Gadgeel SM, Miao J, Riess JW et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37233987/
- **Binimetinib plus hydroxychloroquine in KRAS-mutant NSCLC (phase 2, n=9).** There were no responses, PFS was 1.9 months, and grade 3 AEs occurred in 56%. The trial stopped for futility, so this is a negative repurposing signal. Aggarwal C, Maity AP, Bauml JM et al. Oncologist 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37186063/
- **CSCO consensus on ALK-TKI adverse reactions (ESMO Open).** It covers incidence, grading and management for crizotinib through lorlatinib. It is a Chinese expert consensus. Zhou F, Yang Y, Zhang L et al. ESMO Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37230029/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence

- **FDA Approval Summary: neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy (approved 4 March 2022).** It is based on CheckMate 816 (n=358, stage IB ≥4 cm to IIIA): EFS 31.6 vs 20.8 months (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.45–0.87). The interim OS HR was 0.57 but did not cross the boundary. Definitive surgery was done in 83% vs 75%. It is a journal summary of an earlier decision. Akinboro O, Drezner N, Amatya A et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37141544/
- **CheckMate 227 Part 1, brain metastases (post-hoc, 5-year follow-up).** Among 202 of 1,739 patients with baseline brain metastases, nivolumab plus ipilimumab vs chemotherapy gave OS HR 0.63 (95% CI 0.43–0.92). 5-year intracranial PFS was 16% vs 6%, and new brain lesions occurred in 4% vs 20%. The analysis is exploratory, and the comparator was chemotherapy alone. Reck M, Ciuleanu TE, Lee JS et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37146754/
- **KEYNOTE-033 (phase 3, n=425, mostly mainland China).** Pembrolizumab vs docetaxel in pretreated PD-L1-positive NSCLC did not meet the OS significance threshold in TPS ≥50% (12.3 vs 10.9 months, HR 0.83, P = .13). The OS HR was 0.75 in TPS ≥1%. Grade 3–5 TRAEs occurred in 11.3% vs 47.5%. Formally negative, it is consistent with KEYNOTE-010. Ren S, Feng J, Ma S et al. Int J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37141294/
- **QL1706, PD-1/CTLA-4 MabPair (phase I/Ib, n=518).** At the RP2D, ORR was 14.0% in NSCLC overall and 24.2% in IO-naive NSCLC. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 16%. It is single-arm and early-phase. Zhao Y, Ma Y, Zang A et al. J Hematol Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37158938/
- **Rivaroxaban vs nadroparin after lung cancer surgery (randomised non-inferiority trial, n=403).** VTE occurred in 12.5% vs 17.7% (ARR −5.2%), so rivaroxaban was non-inferior. Any bleeding was 12.2% vs 7.0% (RR 1.9, P = .08), and prophylaxis ran only until discharge. It is a single-country trial and the bleeding signal needs confirmation. Zhao M, Bao Y, Jiang C et al. Am J Hematol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37139837/

### sclc / lung-local-therapy

- **Carboplatin-irinotecan vs carboplatin-etoposide in elderly ES-SCLC (randomised phase II/III, n=258, Japan).** OS was 13.2 vs 12.0 months (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.65–1.11, one-sided P = .11), so the trial was negative. There was more GI toxicity with irinotecan and more myelosuppression with etoposide. The authors conclude carboplatin-etoposide stays standard, and it predates chemo-immunotherapy. Shimokawa T, Okamoto H, Machida R et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37156212/
- **MEDIOLA SCLC cohort: olaparib plus durvalumab (phase 1/2, n=40).** 12-week DCR was 28.9%, missing the 60% target, with ORR 10.5%, PFS 2.4 months and OS 7.6 months. Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 80%. The result is negative. Krebs MG, Delord JP, Jeffry Evans TR et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37146473/
- **CROSS-FIRE: first-line SRS for SCLC vs NSCLC brain metastases (multicentre retrospective, n=892 SCLC, 4,785 NSCLC, plus JLGK0901 prospective data).** OS was shorter in SCLC (8.6 vs 10.5 months). In matched cohorts CNS progression did not differ, and neurological mortality and leptomeningeal progression were not higher with SCLC. It is retrospective, but supports SRS rather than WBRT as an option in SCLC. Rusthoven CG, Staley AW, Gao D et al. J Natl Cancer Inst 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37142267/

### lung-screening / guidelines

- **ERS technical standard for a pan-European LDCT screening programme.** It follows the November 2022 European Council recommendation and gives 56 statements on programme components (nodule actions excluded). It is relevant to Norwegian programme planning. Baldwin DR, O'Dowd EL, Tietzova I et al. Eur Respir J 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37202154/
- **ACR Appropriateness Criteria: lung cancer screening, 2022 update.** It reflects the USPSTF 2021 criteria (age 50–80, ≥20 pack-years). The abstract does not state a specific new appropriateness rating. Expert Panel on Thoracic Imaging, Sandler KL, Henry TS et al. J Am Coll Radiol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37236754/
- **EACTS/ESTS guideline: surgical management of pure GGO and part-solid nodules.** It covers risk-tool-guided referral, multidisciplinary decisions and sublobar vs lobar resection in light of JCOG0802 and CALGB 140503. The abstract gives no concrete changed recommendation. Cardillo G, Petersen RH, Ricciardi S et al. Eur J Cardiothorac Surg 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37243746/
- **eNose breath analysis in COPD (prospective multicentre study, BreathCloud, n=682 COPD and 211 lung cancer).** 5.4% of COPD patients developed lung cancer within 2 years. Baseline breath profiles separated them, with AUC 0.90 on cross-validation. It needs external validation. de Vries R, Farzan N, Fabius T et al. Chest 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37209772/

### lung-nordic / thoracic-other

- **Lung cancer survival in the Nordic countries, 1971–2020 (NORDCAN).** 5-year relative survival rose steeply after 2000. Norway had the best figures in 2016–2020 (men 26.6%, women 33.2%), and the sex difference was significant in every country. It is descriptive registry data. Tichanek F, Försti A, Hemminki O et al. Clin Epidemiol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37153073/
- **Nordic 2023 guidelines for lung neuroendocrine neoplasms (Nordic NET group, excluding SCLC).** It updates the group's diagnostic and treatment recommendations. The abstract does not list changes. Dam G, Grønbæk H, Sundlöv A et al. Acta Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37194281/
- **American Radium Society appropriate use criteria for thymic carcinoma (JAMA Oncol).** These are Delphi-based consensus recommendations: minimally invasive thymectomy is usually inappropriate, RT should be conventionally fractionated (45–60 Gy adjuvant, 60–66 Gy definitive) without elective nodal irradiation, and IMRT or protons are preferred over 3D-CRT. It is expert consensus with little prospective data. Chun SG, Rimner A, Amini A et al. JAMA Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37186595/

### car-t-solid

- **GD2-CAR.15 NKT cells in relapsed/refractory neuroblastoma (phase 1 interim, n=12, Nat Med).** There were no DLTs and one grade 2 CRS. ORR was 25% (1 CR, 2 PR), and the CD62L+ NKT fraction correlated with expansion and response. It is very early-phase. Heczey A, Xu X, Courtney AN et al. Nat Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37188782/
- **Intracranial HER2 CAR-NK cells (NK-92/5.28.z) in recurrent glioblastoma (phase 1, n=9).** Cells were injected into the resection cavity margin. There were no DLTs, CRS or ICANS. 5 patients had stable disease for 7–37 weeks, with median PFS 7 weeks and OS 31 weeks. The trial is single-dose and small. Burger MC, Forster MT, Romanski A et al. Neuro Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37148198/
