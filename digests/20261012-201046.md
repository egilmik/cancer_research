## Practice-changing

- **CALGB 140503 (Alliance): lobar vs sublobar resection for peripheral stage IA NSCLC (phase 3, NEJM).**
  - **Design:** 697 patients with peripheral NSCLC ≤2 cm, with hilar and mediastinal nodes negative on intraoperative frozen section, were randomised to sublobar resection (segmentectomy or wedge, n=340) or lobectomy (n=357) between 2007 and 2017.
  - **Primary endpoint (DFS, non-inferiority):** after a median follow-up of 7 years, sublobar resection was non-inferior (HR for recurrence or death 1.01, 90% CI 0.83–1.24), with 5-year DFS 63.6% vs 64.1%.
  - **OS:** similar (HR 0.95, 95% CI 0.72–1.26), with 5-year OS 80.3% vs 78.9%.
  - **Recurrence:** locoregional and distant recurrence did not differ substantially.
  - **What changes:** together with JCOG0802, this makes sublobar resection a standard option for carefully staged, small, peripheral, node-negative tumours.
  - **Open questions:**
    - Wedge resection and segmentectomy were pooled.
    - Benefit depends on intraoperative nodal staging and adequate margins.
    - Pulmonary-function gains appear modest.
    - Applicability to central, larger or GGO-dominant tumours, and to EGFR-mutant disease in the adjuvant-TKI era, is not addressed.

  Altorki N, Wang X, Kozono D et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36780674/

## High

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers / lung-key-evidence

- **CodeBreaK 200 (phase 3, Lancet).** 345 patients with previously treated KRAS G12C NSCLC were randomised to sotorasib or docetaxel. PFS was 5.6 vs 4.5 months (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.51–0.86). Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 33% vs 40%, and serious TRAEs in 11% vs 23%. The trial was not powered for OS, and the absolute PFS gain is about 1 month. It confirms sotorasib as a better-tolerated second-line option rather than a large efficacy step. de Langen AJ, Johnson ML, Mazieres J et al. Lancet 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36764316/
- **ADAURA updated DFS (phase 3).** In stage II–IIIA disease, DFS HR was 0.23 (95% CI 0.18–0.30), with 4-year DFS 70% vs 29%. Overall DFS HR was 0.27, and CNS DFS HR in stage II–IIIA was 0.24. No new safety signals emerged. OS is reported separately, and placebo arm follow-up was shorter. Herbst RS, Wu YL, John T et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36720083/
- **GAP BRAIN (phase 3, n=161).** Gefitinib plus pemetrexed-platinum vs gefitinib in EGFR-mutant NSCLC with brain metastases improved intracranial PFS (15.6 vs 9.1 months, HR 0.36) and overall PFS (16.3 vs 9.5 months). Intracranial ORR was 85% vs 63%. The comparator is a first-generation TKI, not osimertinib. Hou X, Li M, Wu G et al. JAMA Netw Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36753281/
- **CORIN/GASTO1003 (randomised phase 2, n=128).** Adjuvant icotinib vs observation in resected stage IB EGFR-mutant NSCLC gave 3-year DFS of 96.1% vs 84.0% (HR 0.23, 95% CI 0.07–0.81). OS is immature. The trial is small and single-country. Ou W, Li N, Wang BX et al. EClinicalMedicine 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36816343/
- **EMERGING-CTONG 1103 final OS (randomised phase 2, n=72).** Neoadjuvant erlotinib vs gemcitabine-cisplatin in IIIA-N2 EGFR-mutant disease gave OS of 42.2 vs 36.9 months (HR 0.83, 95% CI 0.47–1.47). Five-year OS was 40.8% vs 27.6%, not significant. The trial is underpowered for OS. Zhong WZ, Yan HH, Chen KN et al. Signal Transduct Target Ther 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36823150/
- **Osimertinib resistance in AURA3 (n=78) and FLAURA (plasma NGS), two Nat Commun papers.** After second-line osimertinib, T790M was lost in 50%, and MET amplification and C797X each occurred in 18%. After first-line osimertinib, no T790M-mediated resistance was found, and MET amplification (16%) and C797S (6%) were most common. Both are plasma-only analyses and miss histological transformation. Chmielecki J, Mok T, Wu YL et al. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36849516/ and Chmielecki J, Gray JE, Cheng Y et al. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36849494/ Nat Commun 2023.
- **PACIFIC EGFR-mutant subgroup (post hoc, n=35).** Durvalumab after CRT showed no PFS (HR 0.91) or OS (HR 1.02) benefit vs placebo, but the CIs are very wide. Naidoo J, Antonia S, Wu YL et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36841540/
- **Dutch nationwide registry, EGFR-TKIs (n=1,109).** OS did not differ between gefitinib, erlotinib, afatinib and osimertinib after adjustment, while del19 had longer OS than L858R (28.4 vs 17.7 months). The study is observational, and osimertinib use was mostly recent. Gijtenbeek RGP, Damhuis RAM, van der Wekken AJ et al. Lancet Reg Health Eur 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36817181/
- **Atezolizumab + platinum-pemetrexed ± bevacizumab after TKI in EGFR/ALK/ROS1 NSCLC (French phase 2, n=149).** ORR was 58% with bevacizumab vs 47% without, and PFS about 7 months in both cohorts. Grade 3–4 AEs occurred in 69% vs 51%. The cohorts were not randomised against each other. Bylicki O, Tomasini P, Radj G et al. Eur J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36801605/
- **WJOG12819L (phase 2, n=55).** Osimertinib for T790M-negative progression after first- or second-generation TKI gave ORR 29.1% and PFS 4.1 months, which is modest. Takeda M, Shimokawa M, Nakamura A et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36731290/
- **Osimertinib plus dacomitinib (CCR brief report).** First-line ORR was 73%, with no secondary EGFR alterations at progression, but skin toxicity occurred in 93%. ORR in acquired resistance was 14%. Elkrief A, Makhnin A, Moses KA et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36729110/
- **Small randomised exploratory study: EGFR-TKI ± pemetrexed.** There was no overall PFS difference, and the subgroup benefit was in co-mutated tumours. Gu W, Zhang H, Lu Y et al. Cancer Res Treat 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36791768/
- **SH-1028 phase 1 (n=20, T790M+).** MTD was 240 mg and ORR 40%. It is a new third-generation EGFR-TKI. He J, Ma P, Zhao D et al. Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36813747/
- **INTELLECT (single-arm phase 2, n=146).** Iruplinalkib after crizotinib in ALK+ NSCLC gave IRC ORR 69.9% and PFS 14.5 months. Shi Y, Chen J, Zhang H et al. BMC Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36829154/
- **HS-10241 phase 1 (selective MET inhibitor, n=27).** MTD was 400 mg once daily, with responses in MET-altered NSCLC. Dong X, Li X, Chen J et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36846572/
- **Adagrasib: First Approval (drug profile).** It covers the FDA accelerated approval in December 2022 for KRAS G12C NSCLC. It is a journal profile, not the agency record. Dhillon S. Drugs 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36763320/
- **Atezolizumab adjuvant approval note (Bull Cancer).** It covers PD-L1 ≥50% resected NSCLC after platinum chemotherapy. There is no abstract. Boudier B, Aldea M. Bull Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36803981/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals

- **KEYNOTE-189 5-year update.** Pembrolizumab plus pemetrexed-platinum gave OS HR 0.60 (95% CI 0.50–0.72) and 5-year OS 19.4% vs 11.3%. Among 57 patients who completed 35 cycles, 3-year OS after completion was 71.9%. This confirms an established standard. Garassino MC, Gadgeel S, Speranza G et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36809080/
- **KEYNOTE-407 5-year update.** In squamous NSCLC, OS HR was 0.71 (95% CI 0.59–0.85), with 5-year OS 18.4% vs 9.7%. This confirms an established standard. Novello S, Kowalski DM, Luft A et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36735893/
- **NEPTUNE China cohort (phase 3 extension, n=160).** Durvalumab plus tremelimumab vs chemotherapy gave ITT OS HR 0.70 (0.48–1.02) and, in PD-L1 <1%, HR 0.60. This is an exploratory regional cohort of a globally negative trial. Cheng Y, Zhou Q, Han B et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36806898/
- **CheckMate 817 (phase 3b/4).** Flat-dose nivolumab plus ipilimumab, including ECOG PS 2 and other special populations, gave grade 3–4 IMAEs of ≤5% each. Three-year OS was 33.7% (cohort A) and 20.5% (cohort A1, special populations). It is single-arm. Ready NE, Audigier-Valette C, Goldman JW et al. J Immunother Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36725084/
- **MRTX-500 (phase 2, n=156).** Sitravatinib plus nivolumab after checkpoint inhibitors gave ORR 11–17% with grade 3/4 TRAEs in 58%, so activity is modest. He K, Berz D, Gadgeel SM et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36842467/
- **CAP-BRAIN (phase 2, n=45).** First-line camrelizumab plus carboplatin/pemetrexed with untreated brain metastases gave intracranial ORR 52.5% and intracranial PFS 7.6 months. It is single-arm. Hou X, Zhou C, Wu G et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36738928/
- **Five-year outcomes of neoadjuvant nivolumab (pilot, n=21).** 5-year RFS was 60% and OS 80%, and all patients with MPR were alive and disease-free. The trial is tiny and single-arm. Rosner S, Reuss JE, Zahurak M et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36794455/
- **IMpower010 after pneumonectomy or bilobectomy (safety analysis).** Grade 3/4 AEs (21% vs 23%) and discontinuation rates were similar to lobectomy. Lee JM, Vallières E, Ding B et al. J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36841745/
- **tTMB in KEYNOTE-189/407 (exploratory).** Continuous tTMB was not associated with the benefit of pembrolizumab-combination. Garassino MC, Gadgeel S, Novello S et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36793385/
- **Stage III consensus statements.** Two national statements were published:
  - an Indian expert Delphi consensus: Batra U, Prabhash K, Agarwal JP et al. Asia Pac J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36815621/
  - a joint Lebanese oncology and pulmonology statement: Aoun-Bacha Z, Bitar N, Saleh WA et al. Oncol Lett 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36844621/

  Both are consensus documents aligning with international standards.

### lung-local-therapy / lung-ctdna-mrd

- **Omitting CTV under PET-CT guidance in stage III CRT (randomised phase 2, n=90).** Grade ≥3 respiratory or oesophageal toxicity was 11.1% vs 28.9%, with similar PFS (9 vs 10 months) and OS. The trial is single-centre and small. Cui T, Zhang A, Cui J et al. Radiother Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36764460/
- **Selective mediastinal node dissection for cT1N0 invasive NSCLC (prospective multicentre, n=720).** The lobe-specific strategy correctly predicted all node-negative zones. It supports less extensive dissection, but survival outcomes are pending. Zhang Y, Deng C, Zheng Q et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36841542/
- **SICI (phase 1, n=15).** SBRT to the primary tumour with durvalumab ± tremelimumab was safe, with one DLT (colitis). Kievit H, Muntinghe-Wagenaar MB, Hijmering-Kappelle LBM et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36806899/
- **Postoperative ctDNA MRD (prospective, four centres, n=177).** ctDNA positivity after surgery independently predicted recurrence and preceded radiological relapse. It is not interventional. Fu R, Huang J, Tian X et al. Mol Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36732646/

### lung-screening

- **China national LDCT screening guideline (2023 version).** This revision of the 2018 guideline is by the expert group designated by China's National Health Commission. The abstract does not state the changes. Zhongguo Fei Ai Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36792074/
