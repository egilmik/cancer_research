## Practice-changing

- **ASCO and Ontario Health (CCO) joint guideline update: therapy for stage IV NSCLC with driver alterations (JCO 2021; replaces the 2017 ASCO guideline).** It is based on a systematic review of 27 RCTs, 26 observational studies and one meta-analysis (December 2015 to January 2020, plus ASCO 2020 abstracts). It recommends that all patients with non-squamous NSCLC have targetable-alteration results before starting therapy, regardless of smoking status. The new or revised recommendations are:
  - osimertinib is the optimal first-line treatment for activating EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletion, L858R, T790M);
  - alectinib or brigatinib is the optimal first-line treatment for ALK fusions;
  - for the first time, there are recommendations for RET, MET exon 14 and NTRK alterations;
  - targeted therapy for ROS1, BRAF V600E, RET, MET exon 14 and NTRK should be offered first or second line.

  It is the companion to the driver-negative update already indexed as practice-changing (run 20261012-051214). The evidence cut-off (January 2020) predates later first-line data such as lorlatinib (CROWN), so it is already partly superseded. Norwegian practice follows its own handlingsprogram and Nye Metoder decisions. Hanna NH, Robinson AG, Temin S et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33591844/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals
- **EMPOWER-Lung 1: cemiplimab vs platinum doublet first line in advanced NSCLC with PD-L1 ≥50% (open-label phase 3, n=710; PD-L1 ≥50% by 22C3 in 563).** In the PD-L1 ≥50% population, OS improved (median not reached vs 14.2 months; HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.42–0.77) and so did PFS (8.2 vs 5.7 months; HR 0.54, 95% CI 0.43–0.68), despite 74% crossover. Grade 3–4 AEs were 28% vs 39%. Never-smokers were excluded. The comparator, chemotherapy, is not the current standard, so there is no head-to-head comparison with pembrolizumab. Sezer A, Kilickap S, Gümüş M et al. Lancet 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33581821/
- **FDA approval summary: nivolumab + ipilimumab + 2 cycles of chemotherapy first line (26 May 2020; Project Orbis).** The approval was based on CheckMate 9LA (n=719): OS 14.1 vs 10.7 months (HR 0.69, 96.71% CI 0.55–0.87). It was the first NSCLC application reviewed under Project Orbis, with Singapore, Australia and Canada. This is a journal summary of an earlier decision. Vellanki PJ, Mulkey F, Jaigirdar AA et al. Clin Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33632925/
- **NEOSTAR: neoadjuvant nivolumab vs nivolumab + ipilimumab in operable NSCLC (randomised phase 2, n=44).** Major pathologic response was 38% (8/21) with the combination, which met its primary endpoint against historical chemotherapy controls, and 22% with nivolumab alone. Among resected patients, pCR was 38% vs 10%. Gut Ruminococcus and Akkermansia were associated with response. The trial is small, the comparison is against historical controls, and MPR is a surrogate endpoint. Cascone T, William WN, Weissferdt A et al. Nat Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33603241/
- **JAMA news item: FDA approval of adjuvant therapy for NSCLC.** There is no abstract. Per the title, it reports the FDA approval of an adjuvant therapy for NSCLC (the ADAURA trial was indexed as practice-changing in run 20261012-063351). It is a secondary report, not the agency notice. Voelker R. JAMA 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33528522/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-drivers / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **FLAURA China: osimertinib vs gefitinib first line in Chinese EGFR-mutant NSCLC (double-blind phase III cohort, n=136).** PFS was 17.8 vs 9.8 months (HR 0.56, 95% CI 0.37–0.85), and OS 33.1 vs 25.7 months (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.56–1.29; not significant). Grade ≥3 AEs were 54% vs 28%, attributed to more local reporting of laboratory and disease-related events. These are consistent regional data from a small cohort not powered for OS, against a first-generation comparator that is no longer standard. Cheng Y, He Y, Li W et al. Target Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33544337/
- **Mobocertinib in platinum-pretreated EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC (phase I/II, 28 patients at 160 mg/day).** Confirmed ORR was 43% (95% CI 24–63), median duration of response 14 months and median PFS 7.3 months. Diarrhoea occurred in 83% (21% grade ≥3). These were early data for an oral exon 20 TKI. The cohort is small and single-arm. Riely GJ, Neal JW, Camidge DR et al. Cancer Discov 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33632775/
- **Lorlatinib after second-generation ALK TKIs (phase II expansion cohorts, n=139).** ORR was 39.6%, intracranial ORR 56.1% and extracranial ORR 36.7%. Median PFS was 6.6 months and median OS 20.7 months. Activity was higher after only one prior second-generation TKI (intracranial ORR 66.7%). These single-arm data support lorlatinib after alectinib or brigatinib. Felip E, Shaw AT, Bearz A et al. Ann Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33639216/
- **Capmatinib after a prior MET inhibitor in MET-altered NSCLC (phase 2, n=20, all crizotinib-pretreated).** ORR was 10%, DCR 80%, median PFS 5.5 months and median OS 11.3 months. Plasma showed MET D1228/Y1230 mutations and MAPK alterations. Activity is modest, consistent with overlapping resistance, so sequencing type I MET TKIs has limited value. The trial is small. Dagogo-Jack I, Moonsamy P, Gainor JF et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33545388/

### sclc
- **CheckMate 331: nivolumab vs topotecan or amrubicin in relapsed SCLC (phase III, n=569).** OS did not improve (median 7.5 vs 8.4 months; HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.72–1.04). PFS was worse (1.4 vs 3.8 months), but grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs were much lower (13.8% vs 73.2%). The trial is negative, and second-line chemotherapy remains standard. Spigel DR, Vicente D, Ciuleanu TE et al. Ann Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33539946/
- **TAHOE: rovalpituzumab tesirine vs topotecan second line in DLL3-high SCLC (phase 3, n=444, 2:1).** OS was shorter with Rova-T (6.3 vs 8.6 months; HR 1.46, 95% CI 1.17–1.82), and enrolment was stopped. Rova-T also caused more serosal effusions, photosensitivity and oedema. Results were also posted on ClinicalTrials.gov this run (NCT03061812). The trial is negative. Blackhall F, Jao K, Greillier L et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33607312/
- **PCI with or without hippocampal avoidance in SCLC (randomised phase 3, n=168, Netherlands/Belgium).** The 4-month HVLT-R recall decline was 29% vs 28% (p=1.000). Other cognitive domains and OS did not differ, and 2-year brain metastasis incidence was 20% vs 16%. The trial is negative: hippocampal avoidance did not preserve cognition. It is modest in size. Belderbos JSA, De Ruysscher DKM, De Jaeger K et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33545387/

### lung-nonpharm / lung-repurposed / lung-screening
- **ASCO guideline: management of dyspnoea in advanced cancer.** It is based on an AHRQ review of 48 RCTs, most in lung cancer and mesothelioma. It recommends a hierarchical approach: assessment, treating reversible causes and referral to interdisciplinary palliative care. Fans, oxygen for hypoxaemia and psychoeducational or self-management measures can be offered, and systemic opioids should be offered if non-drug measures are inadequate. The evidence for pharmacological agents is limited. It is a first ASCO guideline on this topic, but it consolidates existing practice. Hui D, Bohlke K, Bao T et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33617290/
- **Itraconazole added to first-line platinum chemotherapy in metastatic NSCLC (open-label RCT, n=60, Egypt).** ORR was 90% vs 66.7% and mean 1-year PFS 6.6 vs 5.4 months (both significant). 1-year OS did not differ, and one patient had grade 2 cardiotoxicity. The trial is very small and single-centre, its PFS metric is unconventional, and the comparator excludes immunotherapy. Mohamed AW, Elbassiouny M, Elkhodary DA et al. Med Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33559053/
- **Recommendations of French specialists on lung cancer screening.** There is no abstract, so the recommended eligibility criteria cannot be summarised from this item. Couraud S, Ferretti G, Milleron B et al. Rev Mal Respir 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33637394/

### car-t-solid / thoracic-other
- **E7-targeted TCR-T cells in metastatic HPV-associated epithelial cancers (first-in-human phase 1, n=12).** No dose-limiting toxicity occurred up to 1×10^11 cells. Objective responses were seen in 6 of 12 patients, including 4 of 8 with anti-PD-1-refractory disease, with some near-complete regressions. Resistance arose through defects in antigen presentation and interferon response. It is early evidence that engineered T cells can regress common carcinomas. The study is small and single-centre, and durability is not given in the abstract. Nagarsheth NB, Norberg SM, Sinkoe AL et al. Nat Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33558725/
- **SEOM clinical guideline for pleural mesothelioma (2020).** Platinum-pemetrexed remains standard for unresectable disease, and maximal cytoreduction is considered in selected early-stage patients after tumour board review. Dual immunotherapy is flagged as likely to be incorporated. It changes no recommendation. Nadal E, Bosch-Barrera J, Cedrés S et al. Clin Transl Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33538989/
