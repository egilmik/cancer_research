## High

### lung-key-evidence
- **Envonalkib vs crizotinib in treatment-naive ALK+ NSCLC: final efficacy and biomarker analyses (phase III, NCT04009317).** 264 patients randomised 1:1; median follow-up ~46.5 months. Median investigator-assessed PFS was 30.4 vs 12.0 months (HR 0.48, 95% CI 0.35-0.65). Post-hoc ctDNA analysis linked p53-pathway co-mutations or other concurrent drivers to shorter PFS (15.7 and 8.3 months); putative resistance mechanisms were found in 14/52 patients with post-progression ctDNA (secondary ALK mutations n=9, bypass activation n=9). Limitations: open-label, crizotinib is an older comparator, OS immature, biomarker work is exploratory. Huang J, Wang Y, Sun H et al. MedComm (2020) 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42830869/
- **Type II ROS1 inhibition with cabozantinib after type I TKI resistance: phase II (NCT01639508).** Single-centre, first stage of a Simon two-stage design, 7 pretreated ROS1+ patients (prior crizotinib, entrectinib, repotrectinib, zidesamtinib). ORR 43% (95% CI 10-81.5%), including responses in ROS1 D2033N and G2032R/L2086F tumours; median PFS 4.5 months, OS 11 months; grade 3 TRAEs 57% and all patients needed dose reduction. Resistance to cabozantinib involved MET overexpression/MET D1228N. Limitations: tiny n with very wide CIs, single arm, poor tolerability, interim stage only. Rubio-Perez J, Repetto M, Ye LF et al. Clin Cancer Res 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42832369/
- **Smoking cessation among underserved patients referred for lung cancer screening (RCT, NCT04798664; also lung-top-journals).** 3220 analysed of 3259 randomised in 4 arms. Six-month biochemically confirmed abstinence: 4.3% usual care, 5.1% plus free pharmacotherapy, 8.8% plus financial incentives, 7.2% plus mHealth tool. Incentives beat usual care (+4.6%, 95% CI 2.1-7.0) and pharmacotherapy alone (+4.1%); free pharmacotherapy alone did not (+0.5%, P=.66). Limitations: absolute abstinence is low in every arm, with abstinence as the only endpoint through 6 months (no lung cancer outcomes). Hart JL, Vachani A, Neslund-Dudas CM et al. JAMA 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42832220/
