## Practice-changing

**Zybrivo (sintilimab): CHMP positive opinion** (regulatory). On 12 Nov 2026 the EMA CHMP adopted a positive opinion recommending marketing authorisation of Zybrivo (sintilimab; applicant Fortvita Biologics, EMEA/H/C/006743) in combination with pemetrexed and platinum-based chemotherapy for first-line non-squamous NSCLC. The indication is adults without EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations, with locally advanced disease not suited to curative surgery or chemoradiation, or with metastatic disease. A CHMP opinion is a recommendation; the European Commission decision is still pending and the SmPC wording is not yet published. The pivotal trial is not named in the item recorded for this run (the record is a web-search snippet), so the effect sizes, toxicity trade-off and OS maturity are not stated here and should be checked in the EPAR. Norwegian reimbursement has not been assessed (Nye Metoder), and sintilimab is not listed in the Norwegian handlingsprogram as far as this run shows. Source: EMA, 2026-11-12, https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/node/289118. Caveat: rated from the web snippet; verify against the EMA page.

### lung-screening / sclc

- **Computed Tomography Screening and Small Cell Lung Cancer Stage Shift and Survival.** Retrospective Medicare-SEER cohort of 17,099 patients aged 65–78 with SCLC (2015–2021); 949 (5.5%) had LDCT screening in the prior 12 months. Screened patients less often had distant disease (44.2% vs 70.4%) and had better 3-year OS (28.0%, 95% CI 24.9–31.2 vs 10.8%, 10.3–11.3), consistent after propensity matching. Observational, small screened group, and lead-time and selection bias are possible, so it does not show screening reduces SCLC mortality. Potter AL, Kothagundla S, Ananthanarayanan A et al.. JAMA Oncol 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43056109/
