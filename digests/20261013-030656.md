### Screening / risk (lung-screening, lung-top-journals, lung-nordic)

- **Copenhagen Lung Cancer Risk Model (CPH-Lung).** Blood-test-informed 6-year risk model trained on 689 cases/1,924 controls (CGPS) and validated in 3,195 ever-smokers aged ≥50 (CCHS; 68 events). AUC 0.783 (95% CI 0.731–0.835) vs 0.720 (0.669–0.770) for PLCOm2012 (P=.0059). At 77.7% specificity, sensitivity was 57.6% vs 39.2% in women and 61.4% vs 54.7% in men. It could improve who is selected for CT screening, especially women and younger people; limitations are a single (Danish) population, few validation events and no test of screening outcomes.
  Skov-Jeppesen SM, Kobylecki CJ, Egstrand S et al. J Clin Oncol 2026. doi:10.1200/JCO-25-02326 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42863103/

### SCLC / local therapy (sclc, nsclc-immunotherapy, lung-local-therapy, lung-toxicity)

- **Consolidative thoracic RT after chemo-immunotherapy in ES-SCLC.** Retrospective real-world cohort, n=199 (cTRT 97 vs none 102). Median PFS and OS were longer with cTRT (both P<0.001; abstract gives no medians/HRs); cTRT and longer maintenance immunotherapy were independently associated with PFS. Grade 1–2 dermatitis and GI toxicity were more frequent with cTRT; pneumonitis, esophagitis, irAEs and grade 3–4 toxicity were comparable. Supports prospective testing of cTRT in the immunotherapy era; groups differed (cTRT arm had fewer brain metastases and better response), so selection bias is likely.
  Bai B, Jiang Y, Meng H et al. Strahlenther Onkol 2026. doi:10.1007/s00066-026-02583-4 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42862283/
