## High
### nsclc-resistance-nextgen / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals
- **OptiTROP-Lung06: sacituzumab tirumotecan + pembrolizumab, first line, PD-L1-negative nonsquamous NSCLC (Cancer Discovery news).** This is a journal news report on the phase III OptiTROP-Lung06 trial. Patients had newly diagnosed, advanced, PD-L1-negative nonsquamous NSCLC with no targetable alterations. They got either the TROP-2 ADC sacituzumab tirumotecan plus pembrolizumab, or platinum chemotherapy plus pembrolizumab. Median PFS was 15 vs 5.7 months. If this holds up, it could replace chemo-immunotherapy in PD-L1-negative disease, a group that gets little from immunotherapy alone. Limitations: this is a news summary, not the trial paper. It gives no HR, CI, OS, toxicity or sample size, so it does not meet the practice-changing bar yet, and the study population may have been largely Chinese. Wait for the peer-reviewed publication. Cancer Discov, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43031331/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43031331/)

### thoracic-other
- **PECATI final analysis: lenvatinib + pembrolizumab in pretreated B3 thymoma and thymic carcinoma.** Single-arm phase 2 trial (n=43; 84% thymic carcinoma; 44% had at least 2 prior lines) in patients whose disease progressed after platinum chemotherapy. At a median follow-up of 24.6 months: median PFS 22.1 months (95% CI 10.9–25.1), median OS 40.2 months (19.1–NE), 36-month OS 53%, confirmed ORR 25.6%. Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 44.2% and led to discontinuation in 27.9%. Grade 3 immune-related AEs occurred in 11.6%. There is no standard second-line option here, so this supports the combination as a candidate. Limitations: small, single arm, no comparator, and patients with autoimmune disorders were excluded, which matters in thymoma. Remon J, Bironzo P, Girard N et al., J Thorac Oncol, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43025701/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43025701/)
