## High

- **Less Versus More Intensive Strategies for Follow-up of Small Pulmonary Nodules : A Cluster Randomized Pragmatic Clinical Trial.** (lung-screening / key-evidence) Cluster-randomised pragmatic trial (n=34,686, 24 clusters) of less vs more intensive nodule follow-up missed the 5-point non-inferiority margin for cancers >20 mm at diagnosis (36.3% vs 27.7%; adjusted +7 points, 95% CI -2.0 to 15.9), relevant to screening/early-detection criteria. Gould MK, Kelly K, Qi L et al., *Ann Intern Med*, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43071962/

- **Chinese Thoracic Surgeons Expert Consensus on perioperative treatment of locally advanced non-small cell lung cancer based on technical resectability.** (lung-key-evidence) Chinese Thoracic Surgeons expert consensus on perioperative treatment of locally advanced NSCLC by technical resectability; no abstract available, so the recommendations could not be assessed and it is not rated practice-changing. Zhao J, Duan H, Wang J et al., *Chin Med J (Engl)*, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43073091/

- **Conversion Surgery After EGFR-TKI Induction for Initially Unresectable EGFR-Mutant Stage III NSCLC.** (egfr-nsclc) Retrospective cohort (n=162; 42 conversion surgery) after EGFR-TKI induction in unresectable stage III EGFR-mutant NSCLC: R0 97.6%, MPR 31%; longer EFS/OS after weighting but selected and confounded. Jiang Y, Lin Y, Chen P et al., *Clin Lung Cancer*, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072177/

- **Efficacy and safety of third-generation EGFR-TKIs combined with local consolidative therapy as first-line treatment for EGFR-mutated stage IIIB-IV NSCLC: a retrospective study.** (egfr-nsclc / lung-local-therapy) Retrospective study (n=49) of third-gen EGFR-TKI plus local consolidative therapy: median PFS 32.0 vs 21.0 months (HR 0.39); small, retrospective, high bias risk. Zhang Z, Qiao D, Sun Z et al., *J Cardiothorac Surg*, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43074063/

- **Transcription factor subtype governs response and resistance to DLL3-directed T cell engagement in small cell lung cancer.** (sclc) Cancer Cell study linking SCLC transcription-factor subtype (ASCL1/NEUROD1/POU2F3) to tarlatamab response and resistance, using plasma chromatin and a mouse model; mechanistic, not a clinical trial. Vasseur D, Saito S, Gulati GS et al., *Cancer Cell*, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072366/

- **Robot-assisted versus video-assisted thoracoscopic thymectomy for early-stage thymic epithelial tumours: multicentre randomized clinical trial.** (thoracic-other) Randomised multicentre trial (n=122) of robotic vs VATS thymectomy for stage I-II thymic epithelial tumours: lower drainage volume (170 vs 266 ml), more nodes retrieved, longer operating time; surrogate primary endpoint, no oncologic outcomes. Wang S, Zhu J, Yue W et al., *BJS Open*, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43076448/

- **Locoregional administration of bispecific CAR-T cells targeting CD44 and CD133 in recurrent high-grade glioma: a first-in-human investigator-initiated trial.** (car-t-solid) First-in-human single-arm trial (n=10) of locoregional CD44/CD133 CAR-T in recurrent glioma: no DLT, 3 partial responses, median OS 465 days; solid-tumour CAR-T feasibility only, tiny and uncontrolled. Zhai Y, Li G, Wang A et al., *Signal Transduct Target Ther*, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43073246/
