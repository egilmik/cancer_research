### Studies and papers
- **Statin use after cancer diagnosis and survival among patients with cancer.** SEER-Medicare cohort (lung n=20,133, median follow-up 1.7 y) with time-dependent Cox models (addresses immortal-time bias): post-diagnosis statin use linked to lower cancer-specific mortality (HR 0.81, 0.74-0.88) with duration dose-response; observational, confounding by indication/healthy-user effects remain, and not lung-specific by histology or stage.
  Guo H, Malone KE, Heckbert SR et al. — Cancer Causes Control 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39719543/
- **Hydroxychloroquine in combination with platinum doublet chemotherapy as first-line treatment for extensive-stage small cell lung cancer (Study 15): A randomised phase II multicentre trial.** Randomised phase II (n=72, ES-SCLC) adding hydroxychloroquine to platinum doublet: no PFS benefit (HR 1.12, 0.69-1.84) or OS benefit (HR 0.83), with more grade >=3 AEs (83% vs 28%, partly due to higher initial gemcitabine dose); small, underpowered, negative result for a repurposed antimalarial.
  Lee SM, Hewish M, Ahmed S et al. — Eur J Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39693892/
- **Preclinical studies on the antitumor and non-toxic effect of combining pirfenidone with vinorelbine and carboplatin in non-small cell lung cancer.** Preclinical study of repurposed antifibrotic pirfenidone with vinorelbine/carboplatin in three NSCLC lines plus A549 xenograft: triplet reduced tumour growth with a better toxicity profile than doublet, authors propose perioperative use; single xenograft model, no clinical data.
  Branco H, Rodrigues CA, Oliveira J et al. — Int J Cancer 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39630019/
- **Obesity-specific improvement of lung cancer outcomes and immunotherapy efficacy with metformin.** Two retrospective NSCLC cohorts plus mouse models: metformin associated with improved recurrence-free survival after lobectomy in overweight patients (HR 0.47, 0.24-0.94) and PFS on ICIs in overweight patients (HR 0.60, 0.39-0.93), with obese-mouse support; retrospective, subgroup finding, residual confounding/indication bias likely.
  Smith RJ, Zollo R, Kalvapudi S et al. — J Natl Cancer Inst 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39560490/
- **Association of concomitant H1 antihistamine and immune checkpoint inhibitor therapy on survival outcome and safety in patients with advanced primary lung cancer: a cohort study.** Retrospective single-centre cohort (n=211 advanced lung cancer on PD-1/PD-L1 ICIs, IPTW-balanced): concomitant H1 antihistamine linked to longer mPFS (12.7 vs 4.3 mo) and mOS (32.8 vs 18.1 mo); retrospective, single centre, short-term use so immortal-time bias and confounding by indication are possible despite IPTW.
  Zhang WH, Li BX, Ma CX et al. — Transl Lung Cancer Res 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39507043/
- **Treatment with trimetazidine dihydrochloride and lung cancer survival: Implications on metabolic re-programming.** Territory-wide Hong Kong cohort (279,894 IHD patients; 6,561 with lung cancer, 547 on trimetazidine): trimetazidine use associated with lower all-cause mortality (adjusted HR 0.65; pre-existing lung cancer HR 0.49), mostly non-cardiovascular deaths; retrospective with comparator of nitrates, residual confounding and immortal-time bias possible, authors call for RCTs.
  Chan YH, Yuen-Ting C, Sin CF et al. — Lung Cancer 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39490205/
- **The association of azole antifungals with overall survival in patients with non-small cell lung cancer receiving immune checkpoint inhibitors.** VA cohort of 3,413 NSCLC patients on ICIs (324 on azoles) with Cox and propensity matching and exclusion of late azole starts to limit immortal-time bias: azoles overall not associated with OS (HR 0.96), clotrimazole showed HR 0.71 (0.56-0.91) but PS-matched result was not significant (HR 0.74, p=0.058, n=101 per arm); retrospective, mostly male veterans, small subgroup.
  Sebastian NT, Stokes WA, Behera M et al. — Oncologist 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39321212/
- **Statin therapy enhances survival in unresectable stage III lung squamous cell carcinoma with concurrent chemoradiotherapy.** Taiwan registry propensity-matched cohort of unresectable stage III squamous NSCLC receiving CCRT: statin use during CCRT associated with better OS (adjusted HR 0.60, 0.53-0.68) and lung cancer-specific survival (HR 0.61) with dose-response; retrospective, one histology, confounding by indication and immortal-time bias possible given exposure defined during treatment.
  Yu CH, Lin KC, Chang CL et al. — Am J Cancer Res 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39005681/
- **Loratidine is associated with improved prognosis and exerts antineoplastic effects via apoptotic and pyroptotic crosstalk in lung cancer.** Retrospective analysis finding dose-dependent association of loratadine use with improved lung cancer survival, backed by in vitro and in vivo work implicating apoptosis/pyroptosis via PPAR-gamma/caspase-8; antihistamine repurposing with mechanistic support, but retrospective design with possible confounding and cohort size/effect sizes not clear from abstract.
  Liu X, Zhong R, Huang J et al. — J Exp Clin Cancer Res 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38163866/
- **Metformin in Conjunction With Stereotactic Radiotherapy for Early-stage Non-small Cell Lung Cancer: Long-term Results of a Prospective Phase II Clinical Trial.** Randomised phase II of metformin vs placebo with SBRT for early-stage NSCLC, but only 15 patients randomised (14 vs 1) so effectively single-arm; 5-y OS 46%, no grade >=3 AEs, outcomes similar to historic controls and authors state results do not support therapeutic metformin; very small, underpowered long-term follow-up.
  Tate MK, Hernandez M, Chang JY et al. — Anticancer Res 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38159979/
