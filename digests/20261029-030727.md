### lung-key-evidence / lung-local-therapy

- **KEYNOTE-867: A Phase III, Randomized, Double-Blind Study of Stereotactic Body Radiotherapy With Pembrolizumab for Unresected Stage I or II Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer.** (pmid:42950173)  
  KEYNOTE-867 was a double-blind phase 3 trial adding pembrolizumab 200 mg q3w (≤17 cycles) to SBRT in inoperable or surgery-refusing stage I–II NSCLC (n=448; 227 vs 221). At the first prespecified interim analysis (median follow-up 20.6 months) median EFS by BICR was 31.2 vs 28.3 months (HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.69–1.24; P=0.29). Grade 3–5 treatment-related AEs were 20.4% vs 3.7%, with five treatment-related deaths (2.2%), all in the pembrolizumab arm; the trial was stopped early and treatment discontinued. It argues against adding PD-1 blockade to SBRT outside trials; limitations are the interim analysis, immature OS and short follow-up. Rated high rather than practice-changing because the result confirms existing SBRT-alone practice rather than changing it.  
  Jabbour SK, Sótér S, Eaton M et al., J Clin Oncol 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42950173/


### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence

- **Neoadjuvant and perioperative chemoimmunotherapy versus chemotherapy for resectable non-small-cell lung cancer: A systematic review and meta-analysis.** (pmid:42945719)  
  Systematic review and meta-analysis of eight randomised trials (n=3387) of neoadjuvant or perioperative chemoimmunotherapy vs chemotherapy in resectable NSCLC. Pooled EFS HR was 0.59 (95% CI 0.52–0.66) and OS HR 0.68 (0.57–0.80); the indirect perioperative vs neoadjuvant ratio of HRs was 0.86 (0.63–1.18), too imprecise to establish equivalence. In PD-L1 <1% tumours the pooled EFS HR was 0.77 (0.65–0.91), though all six trial-specific CIs included 1, and grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs rose with perioperative regimens (RR 1.12, 1.02–1.24). Limitations: trial-level data, exploratory subgroup/indirect comparisons, and no validated pathological-response surrogacy.  
  Zhu T, Zhou J, Liu D et al., Cancer Treat Res Commun 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42945719/
