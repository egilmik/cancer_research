## High

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-drivers / nsclc-resistance-nextgen
- **SWOG S1403: afatinib + cetuximab vs afatinib alone first-line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (randomised phase 2, n=168 eligible).** PFS was 11.9 vs 13.4 months (HR 1.01, 0.72–1.43), ORR 67% vs 74%, and OS HR 0.82 (0.50–1.36). Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs were 72% vs 40%, and 30% stopped cetuximab for toxicity. The trial closed at interim analysis for lack of benefit. It is a clear negative result for upfront EGFR antibody combination. Goldberg SB, Redman MW, Lilenbaum R et al. *J Clin Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33021871/
- **GOAL: gefitinib + olaparib vs gefitinib first-line in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (randomised phase IB/II, Spain/Mexico, n=182).** PFS was 12.8 vs 10.9 months (HR 1.38, 1.00–1.92; p=0.124), so the trial is negative. Anaemia was 78% vs 38% with the combination. The comparator, gefitinib, is no longer standard. Garcia-Campelo R, Arrieta O, Massuti B et al. *Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33070053/
- **Savolitinib + gefitinib after EGFR-TKI progression in MET-amplified NSCLC (phase Ib, China, n=64).** There were no DLTs, and grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 37%. ORR was 52% (12/23) in T790M-negative, 9% in T790M-positive and 40% in unknown T790M status. It is an early signal for MET-directed combinations after EGFR-TKI, later tested with osimertinib. It is single-arm, and the subgroups are small. Yang JJ, Fang J, Shu YQ et al. *Invest New Drugs*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33052556/
- **Tetracyclines and survival on EGFR TKIs: Finnish nationwide registry (n=1,271, 2011–2016).** Tetracycline purchase was associated with longer survival (HR 0.72, 0.64–0.82), most with prophylactic use (HR 0.74), and only with erlotinib (HR 0.68). It is a Nordic registry study with outcome data. It is observational and confounded by rash, which tracks TKI benefit, so causality is not shown. The relevance to osimertinib-era practice is unclear. Alanen V, Iivanainen S, Arffman M et al. *ESMO Open*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33087401/
- **Lung-MAP (SWOG S1400): biomarker-driven master protocol in pretreated squamous NSCLC.** Of 1,864 enrolled, 90.9% had biomarker results and 655 joined a substudy. Responses were 7.0% with targeted therapies, 16.8% with anti-PD-(L)1 and 5.4% with docetaxel. Median OS was 5.9, 10.8 and 7.7 months. It shows that a national NGS-screening platform works but that matched targeted therapy had little activity in squamous NSCLC. Redman MW, Papadimitrakopoulou VA, Minichiello K et al. *Lancet Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33125909/
- **News: FDA accelerated approval of pralsetinib for metastatic RET fusion-positive NSCLC.** This is a journal news item reporting the approval, not the agency notice, so it is rated high. Wright KM. *Oncology (Williston Park)*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33058106/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals
- **IMpower110: atezolizumab vs platinum chemotherapy first-line in PD-L1-positive NSCLC (phase 3, NEJM, n=572).** In EGFR/ALK wild-type patients with the highest PD-L1 expression (n=205), OS was 20.2 vs 13.1 months (HR 0.59; P=0.01). Grade 3–4 AEs were 30.1% vs 52.5%. It supports atezolizumab monotherapy as an option for PD-L1-high disease. The comparator is chemotherapy, which was no longer the standard for PD-L1-high disease once pembrolizumab was established. PD-L1 was measured by SP142. Herbst RS, Giaccone G, de Marinis F et al. *N Engl J Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32997907/
- **KEYNOTE-021 cohort G long-term: pemetrexed-carboplatin ± pembrolizumab, nonsquamous NSCLC (randomised phase 2, n=123).** With 49.4 months of follow-up, PFS was 24.5 vs 9.9 months (HR 0.54, 0.35–0.83) and OS 34.5 vs 21.1 months (HR 0.71, 0.45–1.12). 70% of the control arm crossed over. Grade 3–5 TRAEs were 39% vs 31%. This is long-term follow-up of the trial that led to the first chemo-immunotherapy approval. It is small and OS is not significant. Awad MM, Gadgeel SM, Borghaei H et al. *J Thorac Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33069888/
- **ABOUND.sqm: maintenance nab-paclitaxel after nab-paclitaxel-carboplatin induction in squamous NSCLC (phase 3, n=202 randomised).** The trial missed its PFS endpoint (3.12 vs 2.60 months, HR 0.85, 0.61–1.19) and stopped for futility. An updated OS showed 17.6 vs 12.2 months (nominal HR 0.68), which is exploratory. The comparator, best supportive care, does not include immunotherapy. Spigel DR, Jotte RM, Aix SP et al. *Clin Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33097414/
- **Pembrolizumab ± radiotherapy in metastatic NSCLC: pooled PEMBRO-RT and MDACC randomised trials (n=148).** Best abscopal response was 41.7% vs 19.7% (OR 2.96). PFS was 9.0 vs 4.4 months (HR 0.67, 0.45–0.99) and OS 19.2 vs 8.7 months (HR 0.67, 0.54–0.84). There were no new safety concerns. It is a pooled analysis of two small trials with different RT schedules and settings. The authors call for a phase 3 trial. Theelen WSME, Chen D, Verma V et al. *Lancet Respir Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33096027/

### sclc
- **Trilaciclib before topotecan in previously treated ES-SCLC (randomised double-blind phase II, n=61).** Severe neutropenia in cycle 1 was 40.6% vs 75.9%, and mean duration 2 vs 7 days. Grade ≥3 anaemia was 28.1% vs 60.7%, with comparable antitumour efficacy and better fatigue-related PROs. It is one of the trials behind trilaciclib's US use as myeloprotection. It is small, and topotecan is used less as second-line in Norway. Hart LL, Ferrarotto R, Andric ZG et al. *Adv Ther*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33123968/

### car-t-solid / lung-vaccines-cell / thoracic-other
- **FAPME: intrapleural FAP-targeted CAR-T in pleural mesothelioma (phase I, first report).** The letter (no abstract) reports that local delivery was safe. Details and patient numbers were not available in the record, so the efficacy signal is unknown. Hiltbrunner S, Britschgi C, Schuberth P et al. *Ann Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33098996/
- **PD-L1-targeted CAR-T in NSCLC (phase I).** Only 1 of 4 recruited patients was treated. Stable disease was followed on day +43 by fever, bilateral pulmonary infiltrates, a >100-fold IL-6 rise and respiratory failure, which reversed with tocilizumab and methylprednisolone. It is a warning of on-target, off-tumour lung toxicity with PD-L1 CAR-T. It is a single case. Liu H, Ma Y, Yang C et al. *Clin Transl Immunology*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33072320/
- **GD2 CAR-NKT cells with IL-15 in relapsed or refractory neuroblastoma (phase 1 interim, Nat Med, n=3).** There were no DLTs, and grade 3–4 haematological AEs were attributed to lymphodepletion. The cells expanded, homed to tumour, and one patient had an objective response with regression of bone metastases. This is the first clinical report of CAR-NKT cells. It covers three patients at the first dose level. Heczey A, Courtney AN, Montalbano A et al. *Nat Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33046868/
- **NEO-PV-01 personalised neoantigen vaccine + nivolumab in melanoma, NSCLC and bladder cancer (phase Ib, Cell, n=82).** There were no treatment-related serious AEs. De novo neoantigen-specific CD4+ and CD8+ responses occurred in all patients, with epitope spreading. It shows that personalised neoantigen vaccination is feasible and immunogenic with PD-1 blockade. It is single-arm with no efficacy comparison. Ott PA, Hu-Lieskovan S, Chmielowski B et al. *Cell*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33064988/
