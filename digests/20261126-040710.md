### egfr-nsclc

- **Pneumonitis Following Thoracic Radiotherapy in EGFR-Mutated Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer Receiving Osimertinib.** Retrospective single-centre Canadian study (2016–2020) of 41 patients receiving osimertinib and thoracic RT to 68 lesions. Grade ≥2 pneumonitis occurred in 5 (12.2%), including one grade 4 and one grade 5; median time to pneumonitis 56 days, and cases clustered in re-irradiation and centrally located lesions. Relevant to combining local therapy with osimertinib. Limitations: very small, retrospective, osimertinib timing undocumented in 11 patients, and the multivariable analysis (5 events) is highly uncertain. M et al., Curr Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43082463/

### lung-key-evidence

- **Radiotherapy combined with first-line chemo-immunotherapy for extensive-stage small cell lung cancer: a systematic review and meta-analysis.** Systematic review and meta-analysis of 23 studies (n=4601) of RT added to first-line chemo-immunotherapy in ES-SCLC. Pooled HR was 0.63 (95% CI 0.53–0.74) for PFS and 0.64 (0.58–0.71) for OS, with no significant difference in grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs (RR 1.22, 0.96–1.55) and pooled grade ≥3 pneumonitis 5%. Informs the role of consolidative thoracic RT. Limitations: mostly non-randomised studies, so selection bias and confounding likely inflate the effect; heterogeneity of RT approaches. T et al., Strahlenther Onkol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43083910/

- **Proton pump inhibitors use and the efficacy and safety of immune checkpoint inhibitors in non-small cell lung cancer: A meta-analysis and FAERS pharmacovigilance study.** Meta-analysis of 44 studies plus FAERS pharmacovigilance (~18 million reports) on PPI use with ICI in NSCLC. PPI exposure was associated with shorter OS (HR 1.32, 95% CI 1.23–1.42) and PFS (HR 1.63, 1.42–1.87), but not with response or overall irAEs; GRADE certainty was very low. Relevant to repurposed/co-medication effects on immunotherapy. Limitations: observational data, confounding by indication, and FAERS signals are hypothesis-generating only. X et al., J Oncol Pharm Pract, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43081763/
