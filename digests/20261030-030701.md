## High

- **Local ablative therapy plus systemic treatment versus systemic therapy alone in oligometastatic non-small cell lung cancer: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials.** Systematic review and meta-analysis of 16 RCTs (1,343 patients) adding local ablative therapy (LAT) to systemic therapy in oligometastatic NSCLC. LAT improved PFS (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.55-0.72) and OS (HR 0.68, 0.58-0.81); the benefit was largest with EGFR-TKIs (OS HR 0.52, 0.40-0.67) and absent for OS with chemo/immunotherapy (HR 0.83, 0.64-1.07) or immunotherapy alone. Grade >=3 AEs (RR 1.62) and pneumonitis (RR 6.28) rose. Limitations: heterogeneous small RCTs and subgroup analyses; hypothesis-generating for patient selection. Xu Y, Liang Y, Gao W et al., Int J Clin Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955614/

- **A randomized phase III study optimizing adjuvant osimertinib for resected EGFR-mutant stage II-III non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer: OASIS trial (JACTOP-01).** Protocol of OASIS (JACTOP-01), a Japanese multicentre open-label phase 3 non-inferiority trial of adjuvant osimertinib 40 mg vs 80 mg/day for 3 years in resected EGFR-mutant stage II-III non-squamous NSCLC (planned n=420, primary endpoint DFS). No results yet; relevant to toxicity and cost of adjuvant osimertinib. Onodera K, Okada Y, Kudo Y et al., Jpn J Clin Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955056/

- **RET fusion-positive non-small cell lung cancer in Sweden: a nationwide registry study.** Swedish nationwide registry study (2018-2022) of 68 RET fusion-positive NSCLC patients, mostly young never-smoker adenocarcinoma. Adjusted median OS was 28 months (13-46) vs 39 (ALK) and 17-26 (others), with overlapping CIs, before wide use of selective RET inhibitors. Nordic real-world baseline; small, retrospective. Äng Eklund E, Brunnström H, Lampa E et al., Acta Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42954455/

- **Efficacy and safety of antibody-drug conjugates in EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer after EGFR-TKI resistance: a systematic review and meta-analysis.** Meta-analysis of 8 reports of ADCs after EGFR-TKI resistance in EGFR-mutant NSCLC. Pooled ORR 39.2% (31.1-48.0), DCR 78.9%, grade >=3 TEAEs 65.2% and ILD 6.0%; two RCTs support sac-TMT. Mostly non-randomised pooled data with platform heterogeneity and overlapping trials. Chen Y, Chen S, Zhang H et al., Front Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42952490/

- **The evolving landscape of DLL3-directed Therapeutics in small cell lung cancer.** Review of DLL3-directed therapeutics in SCLC. No new patient data; background to the tarlatamab landscape. Tieu VT, Arter ZL, Nagasaka M, Cancer Treat Rev, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42951714/

- **Therapy for Stage IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer With Driver Alterations: ASCO Living Guideline, Version 2026.3.4.** ASCO living guideline update (version 2026.3.4) on stage IV NSCLC with driver alterations. The abstract is only the standard living-guideline boilerplate and does not state which recommendations changed, so it is rated high rather than practice-changing; the full text should be checked. Reuss J, Ismaila N, Doroshow DB et al., J Clin Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42956324/

- **Benmelstobart With or Without Anlotinib After Chemoradiotherapy for Unresectable Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer: The R-ALPS Randomized Clinical Trial.** R-ALPS, double-blind phase 3 in China (42 centres, n=553) of consolidation after chemoradiotherapy for unresectable stage III NSCLC. Stage 1 median IRC-PFS was 15.1 mo (benmelstobart + anlotinib), 9.7 mo (benmelstobart) and 4.2 mo (placebo); HR 0.53 (0.39-0.72) for benmelstobart vs placebo. Adding anlotinib was not significant in the pooled analysis (HR 0.82, 0.63-1.08) and raised grade 3-5 TRAEs (48.8% vs 29.4%) and grade 5 events (7.2% vs 1.4%). Placebo comparator rather than durvalumab, Chinese population (89.5% male), OS immature; not rated practice-changing. Ji Y, Li Q, Chen L et al., JAMA Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955021/

- **Routes to diagnosis and survival in Danish patients with non-small cell lung cancer: the impact of emergency admission.** Danish registry study of routes to diagnosis and survival in NSCLC, examining emergency presentation. Nordic health-system relevance; observational, so confounded by stage and performance status. Christensen NL, Kristensen K, Rasmussen TR et al., Eur Clin Respir J, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42953701/
