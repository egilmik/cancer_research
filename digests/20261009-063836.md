- **Afamitresgene autoleucel (Tecelra): FDA traditional approval, 22 Jun 2026 (secondary report).** A third-party report says FDA converted the 2024 accelerated approval of afami-cel to traditional approval for MAGE-A4-positive, HLA-restricted synovial sarcoma and extended it to patients aged 12 and older. The original approval rested on the single-arm phase 2 SPEARHEAD-1 (ORR ~43%, CR ~4.5%). The confirmatory evidence behind the conversion is not given. This is relevant as the only engineered TCR-T approved for a solid tumour, but it is not confirmed by FDA or the company. Morningglory Sciences, 2026 · https://www.morningglorysciences.com/en/afamitresgene-autoleucel-synovial-sarcoma-fda-2026-en/

### guidelines-meetings

- **NCCN NSCLC v1.2026: Dato-DXd becomes a preferred option in EGFR-mutant disease (secondary report).** OncLive (Dec 2025) reports that datopotamab deruxtecan is now a preferred second-line regimen after progression on first-line osimertinib plus chemotherapy, and a preferred third-line regimen after other first-line regimens. Staging is aligned with AJCC 9th edition. The change is named, but the source is trade press rather than the NCCN document, so it stays high. Dato-DXd's EGFR-mutant data are single-arm (TROPION-Lung05) plus subgroup data. OncLive, 2025 · https://www.onclive.com/view/experts-unpack-the-most-notable-nccn-guideline-changes-heading-into-2026
- **SAFFRON phase 3 (savolitinib + osimertinib) selected for the ESMO 2026 Presidential Symposium.** HUTCHMED announces that SAFFRON will be presented at ESMO 2026 (Madrid, 23–27 Oct). SAFFRON compares savolitinib plus osimertinib with platinum doublet chemotherapy in EGFR-mutant NSCLC with MET overexpression or amplification after osimertinib. The topline release (17 Aug 2026) reported improved PFS and OS, but gave no hazard ratios. This could matter for post-osimertinib MET-driven resistance. This is a company announcement only; the full data come at ESMO. SANOVO China (first-line) also has a proffered paper. HUTCHMED via Investegate, 2026 · https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/hutchmed-china-limited--hcm/data-to-be-presented-at-esmo-2026/9788005
