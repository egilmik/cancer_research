## High

### egfr-nsclc / lung-key-evidence

- **Cemiplimab plus chemotherapy vs chemotherapy in NSCLC with PD-L1 ≥1%: 5-year update of EMPOWER-Lung 3 Part 2.** Phase 3, double-blind; 327 of 466 patients had PD-L1 ≥1% (217 cemiplimab plus chemo, 110 placebo plus chemo, 2:1). At median follow-up of 60.5 months, median OS was 24.0 months (95% CI 20.9-28.4) vs 12.1 months (10.1-15.7), HR 0.54 (0.41-0.70); PFS 8.3 vs 5.5 months, HR 0.50 (0.39-0.65); ORR 48.4% vs 22.7%. Grade ≥3 TEAEs 48.8% vs 27.5%, no new toxicities. This confirms durable benefit of an established regimen. It is a subgroup analysis, so it is not rated practice-changing. Gogishvili M, Makharadze T, Melkadze T et al. ESMO Open 2026. [PMID 43035066](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43035066/)
- **Adding chemotherapy to first-line EGFR-TKI in advanced EGFR-mutated NSCLC: GRADE-assessed meta-analysis with trial sequential analysis.** Seven RCTs, 2454 patients. TKI plus chemotherapy improved OS (HR 0.64, 95% CI 0.53-0.77), PFS (HR 0.54, 0.45-0.64) and ORR (RR 1.14, 1.07-1.20), with consistent benefit across TKI generations but substantially more grade ≥3 and haematological toxicity. Limitations: pooled trials differ in design, and this is a meta-analysis, which cannot be practice-changing under the rubric. Khalid M, Nawaz A, Khan MFI et al. Am J Clin Oncol 2026. [PMID 43038156](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43038156/)
- **Multidisciplinary management of stage III NSCLC in the immunotherapy era: Italian Delphi consensus (AIOM, SICT, SIAPEC, AIRO).** 65 statements, two Delphi rounds, consensus on 62. Experts endorsed comprehensive staging (CT, PET, brain MRI, endosonographic mediastinal staging), neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy as preferred for most resectable stage III without actionable drivers, lung-sparing surgery, and concurrent chemoradiotherapy plus consolidation immunotherapy for unresectable disease. Mostly restates existing standards; expert opinion rather than trial evidence. Gallina FT, Ambrosi F, Marinelli D et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2026. [PMID 43036783](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43036783/)
