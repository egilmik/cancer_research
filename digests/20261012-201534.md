### lung-local-therapy / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals

- **JCOG1211 (single-arm confirmatory phase 3, Lancet Respir Med).** 396 patients with GGO-dominant NSCLC ≤3 cm were registered and 357 underwent segmentectomy. At 5.4 years median follow-up, 5-year RFS was 98.0% (95% CI 95.9–99.1), exceeding the pre-set 87% threshold. Grade 3–4 complications occurred in 2%, with no treatment-related deaths. It supports segmentectomy for this subgroup, but there was no randomised comparator. Aokage K, Suzuki K, Saji H et al. Lancet Respir Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36893780/
- **ESMO Clinical Practice Guideline: oncogene-addicted metastatic NSCLC.** This is the updated ESMO guideline. There is no abstract, so the changed recommendations cannot be stated here. Hendriks LE, Kerr KM, Menis J et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36872130/
- **FDA Approval Summary: adjuvant atezolizumab.** Atezolizumab was approved on 15 October 2021 for stage II–IIIA NSCLC with PD-L1 ≥1% after resection and platinum chemotherapy, based on IMpower010 DFS. This is a journal summary of a decision already captured via IMpower010. Mathieu LN, Larkins E, Sinha AK et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36951523/
- **Chinese expert consensus on pathological diagnosis of small lung cancer biopsies.** It comes from the Chinese Anti-Cancer Association. There is no abstract. Zhonghua Bing Li Xue Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36973192/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers / lung-ctdna-mrd

- **APPLE (EORTC 1613, randomised phase 2, n=103).** Gefitinib with plasma T790M monitoring and an early switch to osimertinib met its primary endpoint (PFS on osimertinib at 18 months 67.2% vs 53.5%). 17% switched on molecular progression before radiological progression. Upfront osimertinib is now standard, so the strategy is mostly of historical and methodological interest. Remon J, Besse B, Aix SP et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36863484/
- **INSIGHT final analysis (randomised phase 2, n=55).** Tepotinib plus gefitinib vs chemotherapy after EGFR-TKI resistance gave overall PFS of 4.9 vs 4.4 months. In the 19 patients with MET amplification, PFS HR was 0.13 and OS HR 0.10 (90% CIs), and ORR 66.7% vs 42.9%. The subgroup is very small, and the trial used a first-generation TKI backbone. Liam CK, Ahmad AR, Hsia TC et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36971777/
- **OPAL (phase 2, n=67).** First-line osimertinib plus platinum-pemetrexed gave ORR 90.9% and median PFS 31.0 months. 14.9% discontinued for AEs. It is single-arm and was a precursor to FLAURA2. Saito R, Sugawara S, Ko R et al. Eur J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36966696/
- **Osimertinib in uncommon exon 19 deletions (n=200, multi-institutional).** Among 72 distinct variants, L747_A750>P had shorter first-line osimertinib PFS than E746_A750del (11.7 vs 21.3 months, adjusted HR 0.52). The cohort is retrospective. Grant MJ, Aredo JV, Starrett JH et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36913537/
- **Osimertinib in G719X, S768I and L861Q (single-arm phase 2, n=17).** ORR was 47% and PFS 10.5 months. The trial is small. Villaruz LC, Wang X, Bertino EM et al. ESMO Open 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36905787/
- **NEOS (phase 2b, n=40).** Six weeks of neoadjuvant osimertinib in resectable stage II–IIIB EGFR-mutant adenocarcinoma gave ORR 71.1% and R0 resection in 93.8% of those operated. Survival is not yet reported. Lv C, Fang W, Wu N et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36863124/
- **Neoadjuvant osimertinib then definitive RT and/or surgery in stage III (phase 2, n=24).** Induction ORR was 95.2%. The cohort was small and elderly (median age 73). Peled N, Roisman LC, Levison E et al. Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36925073/
- **Osimertinib plus selpercatinib for acquired RET fusions (prospective multicentre, n=14).** ORR was 50% and median treatment duration 7.9 months, with complex resistance. Rotow J, Patel JD, Hanley MP et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36996322/
- **ZENITH20-4 (phase 2, n=80).** First-line poziotinib in HER2 exon 20 NSCLC gave ORR 39% and PFS 5.6 months, with grade 3 rash in about 40% and stomatitis in 15–21%. Toxicity limits it. Cornelissen R, Prelaj A, Sun S et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36958688/
- **Garsorasib (D-1553) phase 1 (n=79, KRAS G12C NSCLC).** ORR was 40.5%, DCR 91.9%, and grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 38%. Li Z, Song Z, Zhao Y et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36948246/
- **Longitudinal ctDNA survival model (IMpower150, n=466).** A machine-learning model on ctDNA at five time points was associated with OS, with external validation. It is a retrospective trial-embedded analysis. Assaf ZJF, Zou W, Fine AD et al. Nat Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36928816/

### nsclc-immunotherapy

- **NEOSTAR (phase 2 platform arms, Nat Med).** MPR was 32.1% with neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy and 50% with ipilimumab plus nivolumab plus chemotherapy (n=22 each). The arms are small and not randomised against each other. Cascone T, Leung CH, Weissferdt A et al. Nat Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36928818/
- **Neoadjuvant camrelizumab plus apatinib (phase 2, n=78).** 83% had surgery, MPR was 57% and pCR 23%, with better responses in squamous histology. It is single-arm. Zhao J, Zhao L, Guo W et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36870519/
- **CheckMate 9LA 2-year patient-reported outcomes.** Nivolumab plus ipilimumab with 2 cycles of chemotherapy maintained QoL with no deterioration vs chemotherapy. Differences did not reach minimally important thresholds. Reck M, Ciuleanu TE, Cobo M et al. Eur J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36871487/
- **SITC consensus definitions for resistance to ICI-chemotherapy combinations.** These are standard definitions for trials and practice. Rizvi N, Ademuyiwa FO, Cao ZA et al. J Immunother Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36918220/

### lung-nonpharm / lung-repurposed / lung-screening

- **Olanzapine for chemotherapy-related anorexia (double-blind RCT, JCO, n=124, 35% lung).** Weight gain >5% occurred in 60% vs 9%, with better appetite and QoL. The population was mixed, gastric-predominant and single-centre in India. Sandhya L, Devi Sreenivasan N, Goenka L et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36977285/
- **Simvastatin with irinotecan-cisplatin in ES-SCLC (randomised phase 2, n=125).** 1-year survival was 53.2% vs 58.7%, with no PFS or OS benefit. This is a negative repurposing trial. Lee Y, Lee SH, Lee GK et al. Cancer Res Treat 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36960628/
- **OaSiS (randomised implementation trial, Chest).** A tobacco cessation intervention at lung screening sites did not improve quit rates at 14 days, 3 months or 6 months. Foley KL, Dressler EV, Weaver KE et al. Chest 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36931460/
