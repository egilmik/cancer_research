## Practice-changing

### JCOG0802/WJOG4607L: segmentectomy vs lobectomy for small peripheral NSCLC (Lancet)
This was a multicentre, open-label, randomised phase 3 non-inferiority trial at 70 Japanese institutions. It enrolled 1,106 patients with clinical stage IA NSCLC (tumour ≤2 cm, consolidation-to-tumour ratio >0.5), assigned to lobectomy (n=554) or segmentectomy (n=552). The primary endpoint was overall survival. At a median follow-up of 7.3 years, 5-year OS was 94.3% with segmentectomy vs 91.1% with lobectomy (HR 0.663, 95% CI 0.474–0.927), which met both non-inferiority and superiority (p=0.0082). Five-year relapse-free survival was identical (88.0% vs 87.9%, HR 0.998). Local relapse was twice as common after segmentectomy (10.5% vs 5.4%, p=0.0018). The FEV1 advantage at 1 year (3.5%) did not reach the predefined 10% threshold for clinical significance. Deaths from other diseases made up 63% of deaths after lobectomy vs 47% after segmentectomy, which suggests the OS gain is not from better cancer control. Open questions: the trial is from Japan only, tumours with a more ground-glass appearance were excluded, and the higher local relapse rate needs surveillance. Saji H, Okada M, Tsuboi M et al. Lancet 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35461558/

### CheckMate 816: neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy in resectable NSCLC (NEJM, primary report)
This was an open-label randomised phase 3 trial in stage IB–IIIA resectable NSCLC (n=358): nivolumab plus platinum doublet vs platinum doublet alone, followed by surgery. The co-primary endpoints were event-free survival and pathological complete response, both by blinded independent review. Median EFS was 31.6 vs 20.8 months (HR 0.63, 97.38% CI 0.43–0.91, p=0.005), and pCR was 24.0% vs 2.2% (OR 13.94). More patients reached surgery with nivolumab (83.2% vs 75.4%), and grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs were similar (33.5% vs 36.9%). OS was immature at this interim (HR 0.57, 99.67% CI 0.30–1.07, not significant). The later final OS analysis (HR 0.72) is already in the index from another run. Open questions at this point: OS, the role of adjuvant continuation (perioperative designs), and benefit in PD-L1 <1%. For Norway, the regimen's funding goes through Nye Metoder. Forde PM, Spicer J, Lu S et al. N Engl J Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35403841/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers
- **WJOG10818L: alternating osimertinib and afatinib first line (single-arm phase 2, n=46, Lung Cancer).** Eight-week alternating cycles gave a 12-month PFS of 70.2% (95% CI 54.2–81.5), so the primary endpoint was not met. Median PFS was 21.3 months and ORR 69.6%. Five pneumonitis cases occurred, all during osimertinib. It is single-arm and small, and the strategy is not supported. Hayashi H, Yonesaka K, Nakamura A et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35477147/
- **Sunvozertinib in pretreated EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC (discovery and two phase I studies, Cancer Discov).** It was tolerated up to 400 mg daily, with diarrhoea and rash the main AEs. Responses were seen from 100 mg across insertion subtypes, including after amivantamab and with brain metastases. The paper gives no pooled ORR, and the data are early phase I. Wang M, Yang JC, Mitchell PL et al. Cancer Discov 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35404393/
- **JBJ-09-063, an allosteric mutant-selective EGFR inhibitor (preclinical, Nat Cancer).** It is active against TKI-sensitive and resistant models, including T790M and C797S, alone or with ATP-competitive TKIs. EGFR homo- or heterodimerisation and the L747S mutation confer resistance. The work is preclinical, a fourth-generation concept. To C, Beyett TS, Jang J et al. Nat Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35422503/
- **Emibetuzumab ± erlotinib in MET IHC-positive NSCLC after erlotinib resistance (randomised phase 2, n=111, Clin Lung Cancer).** ORR was 3.0% with the combination and 4.3% with monotherapy. PFS was 3.3 vs 1.6 months. Antibody blockade of MET selected by IHC does not reverse EGFR-TKI resistance, which supports selection by MET amplification or TKI-based approaches instead. Camidge DR, Moran T, Demedts I et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35400584/
- **RELAY+: ramucirumab plus gefitinib first line in East Asian EGFR-mutant NSCLC (single-arm exploratory, n=82, JTO Clin Res Rep).** One-year PFS was 62.9% (95% CI 50.3–73.1). Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 73.2%, with two deaths from cardiac failure. It is single-arm and uses an older TKI backbone. Nishio M, Nishio K, Reck M et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35369607/
- **Adagrasib in KRAS G12C NSCLC brain metastases (preclinical models plus 2 KRYSTAL-1 patients, Clin Cancer Res).** Adagrasib reached CSF concentrations above the cellular IC50, regressed intracranial xenografts and extended survival. Both patients with untreated brain metastases had intracranial regression. A database query found ≥40% of KRAS-mutant NSCLC develops brain metastases. The clinical evidence is two patients. Sabari JK, Velcheti V, Shimizu K et al. Clin Cancer Res 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35404402/
- **Proton pump inhibitors with gefitinib/erlotinib (Taiwanese nationwide cohort, n=5,975, Sci Rep).** Concurrent PPI use (≥20% overlap) was associated with shorter OS: aHR 1.58 (95% CI 1.42–1.76) on gefitinib and 1.54 (1.30–1.82) on erlotinib. H2 blockers gave an intermediate effect. It is observational with likely confounding by frailty, but consistent with reduced TKI absorption. Lee CH, Shen MC, Tsai MJ et al. Sci Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35488047/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-local-therapy / lung-ctdna-mrd
- **COAST: durvalumab ± oleclumab (anti-CD73) or monalizumab (anti-NKG2A) after chemoradiotherapy in stage III NSCLC (randomised phase 2, n=189, JCO).** Confirmed ORR was 30.0% and 35.5% with the combinations vs 17.9% with durvalumab. PFS improved with both (HR 0.44, 95% CI 0.26–0.75, and HR 0.42, 0.24–0.72). Grade ≥3 AEs were similar. This was an interim analysis at 11.5 months' median follow-up, and the authors call for phase 3 testing. Herbst RS, Majem M, Barlesi F et al. J Clin Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35452273/
- **J-SONIC: nintedanib plus carboplatin/nab-paclitaxel in advanced NSCLC with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (randomised phase 3, n=243, Eur Respir J).** The primary endpoint, exacerbation-free survival, was not met (14.6 vs 11.8 months, HR 0.89, 90% CI 0.67–1.17). PFS was longer (HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.50–0.92), and OS improved in non-squamous disease (HR 0.61), an exploratory subgroup. Acute IPF exacerbation was rare (2.9%), which supports carboplatin/nab-paclitaxel as a tolerable backbone in IPF. Otsubo K, Kishimoto J, Ando M et al. Eur Respir J 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35361630/
- **B-F1RST: blood TMB as a biomarker for first-line atezolizumab (phase 2, n=152, Nat Med).** PFS did not differ significantly between bTMB ≥16 and <16 (co-primary endpoint not met). ORR was higher with high bTMB and rose with higher cut-offs. In exploratory analysis OS was longer with bTMB ≥16. The assay is not ready for standalone use. Kim ES, Velcheti V, Mekhail T et al. Nat Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35422531/
- **Bevacizumab added after progression on atezolizumab in pretreated NSCLC (two-stage phase 2, 42/24 patients, JTO).** After progression on atezolizumab monotherapy, adding bevacizumab gave a DCR of 87.5% (95% CI 67.6–97.3) but only 12.5% partial responses. PFS was 5.6 and OS 14.0 months, with no grade ≥3 related AEs. It is small, single-arm, and DCR is a weak endpoint. Lee J, Koh J, Kim HK et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35427805/
- **Enoblituzumab (anti-B7-H3) plus pembrolizumab in advanced solid tumours (phase I/II, n=133, JITC).** The MTD was not reached, and the RP2D is 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks. Grade ≥3 related AEs occurred in 28.6%, with one fatal pneumonitis. Responses were seen in HNSCC (6/18) and in NSCLC cohorts. It is an interim, non-randomised analysis. Aggarwal C, Prawira A, Antonia S et al. J Immunother Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35414591/

### sclc / lung-repurposed
- **Statins overcome chemoresistance in SCLC (PDX models plus 3 patients, Nat Cancer).** Chemoresistant SCLC relies on the mevalonate–GGPP pathway. Statins induce oxidative stress and apoptosis through the GGPS1–RAB7A–autophagy axis and overcame intrinsic and acquired resistance in GGPS1-high PDX models. Three patients who relapsed after first-line chemotherapy had durable responses to statin plus chemotherapy. It needs a prospective trial. Guo C, Wan R, He Y et al. Nat Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35449308/

### lung-screening
- **CT screening and stage shift and survival in the US (quasi-experimental, NCDB and SEER 2010–2018, BMJ).** In screening-eligible ages (55–80), the share of stage I NSCLC rose 3.9% per year after 2014 (30.2% to 35.5%), against no change before. Median all-cause survival rose 11.9% per year (19.7 to 28.2 months). The gains were concentrated in white and higher-income groups. It is ecological, and lead-time and treatment advances also contribute. Potter AL, Rosenstein AL, Kiang MV et al. BMJ 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35354556/

### thoracic-other
- **ARCS-M: anetumab ravtansine vs vinorelbine in relapsed mesothelin-positive pleural mesothelioma (randomised phase 2, n=248, Lancet Oncol).** The mesothelin ADC did not improve PFS (4.3 vs 4.5 months, HR 1.22, 95% CI 0.85–1.74). The trial is negative, and vinorelbine remains a reasonable second-line comparator. Kindler HL, Novello S, Bearz A et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35358455/
- **CheckMate 743 patient-reported outcomes: first-line nivolumab plus ipilimumab vs chemotherapy in unresectable pleural mesothelioma (phase 3, n=605, Lung Cancer).** Nivolumab plus ipilimumab delayed definitive HRQoL deterioration (HR 0.52, 95% CI 0.36–0.74). Symptom burden trended better but did not cross the clinically important thresholds. It is open-label and descriptive. Scherpereel A, Antonia S, Bautista Y et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35367910/
