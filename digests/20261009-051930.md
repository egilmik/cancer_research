### nsclc-drivers: real-world comparative effectiveness
- **Real-world comparative effectiveness of sotorasib vs docetaxel monotherapy in second line and beyond (England).** National Cancer Analysis System study comparing sotorasib-treated KRAS G12C NSCLC (n=394) with docetaxel-treated NSCLC (n=1,271) from second line on, with propensity-score weighting. Adjusted median OS 10.2 vs 6.9 months (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.54-0.75); TTNTD 8.5 vs 5.6 months (HR 0.57, 0.49-0.66); TTDD 5.1 vs 2.3 months. This contrasts with the randomised CodeBreaK 200, which showed a PFS but not an OS benefit. The docetaxel group was not restricted to KRAS G12C and spans earlier calendar years (from 2017), so residual confounding and era effects are likely. Karim N, Arnold K, Summers Y et al., Lung Cancer, 2026. [PMID 42229337](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42229337/)
