### lung-key-evidence / lung-local-therapy

- **NRG-LU002: maintenance systemic therapy vs local consolidative therapy (LCT) plus maintenance in limited metastatic NSCLC.** Randomised phase II (1:2), 215 patients (81 MST, 134 LCT+MST) with ≤3 residual extracranial sites after four cycles of first-line therapy (90% IO-based); median follow-up 21.9 months. PFS did not differ (HR 0.93, P=.66; 2-year PFS 35.9% vs 40.1%), OS HR 1.05, and grade ≥3 pneumonitis was higher with LCT (10% vs 1%, P=.02), with early pneumonitis linked to worse outcomes. It argues against routine LCT on top of IO-based first-line therapy, but it is phase II, primary endpoint PFS, and follow-up is short. Authors: Iyengar P et al., J Clin Oncol 2026. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43071915/)

### lung-local-therapy
- **Adaptive vs non-adaptive radiation dose escalation in LA-NSCLC (RTOG 0617 vs 1106 IPD).** Non-randomised cross-trial comparison of 84 PET-adapted vs 184 non-adaptive dose-escalated patients, all with chemoradiation without immunotherapy. Unweighted 3-year OS was 39% vs 27% (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.52–1.01); weighted analyses showed similar outcomes, while grade ≥3 esophagitis was lower with adaptive RT (6% vs 22%, OR 0.24, 95% CI 0.09–0.63). Hypothesis-generating only: confounded by cohort differences (performance status, IMRT use) and pre-dates consolidation immunotherapy. Authors: Mic et al., Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys 2026. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43068714/)
