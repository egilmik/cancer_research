## High
### nsclc-resistance-nextgen / lung-top-journals
- **TROP2 normalized membrane ratio (QCS-NMR) as a predictive biomarker for datopotamab deruxtecan (TROPION-Lung01 biomarker analysis).** A computational-pathology score of membrane vs cytoplasmic TROP2 was developed in TROPION-PanTumor01 (n=115) and validated in the biomarker-evaluable population of the phase 3 TROPION-Lung01 trial (n=352; primary intended-use group 221 nonsquamous patients without actionable alterations). NMR-positive patients had longer PFS on Dato-DXd (median 7.2 vs 4.0 mo, HR 0.40, 95% CI 0.25–0.64) but not on docetaxel (4.1 vs 4.4 mo, HR 0.94, 0.59–1.49), interaction P=0.0098. This could select the patients who benefit from TROP2 ADCs, where visual IHC scoring has failed. Limitations: retrospective post hoc analysis of a subset, cut-point optimised in-study, no OS data in the abstract; prospective validation (e.g. TROPION-Lung10, AVANZAR) is pending. Garassino MC, Ahn MJ, Schick M et al., Nat Med, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43015400/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43015400/)

### egfr-nsclc / lung-top-journals
- **Clinicogenomic determinants of early progression on first-line osimertinib.** A 13-centre retrospective cohort of 1488 patients with ex19del/L858R NSCLC on osimertinib monotherapy reported median rwPFS 16.3 mo (95% CI 15.3–17.4) and OS 36.7 mo (34.8–39.4) at 43.3 mo follow-up. TP53 mutation was independently associated with shorter rwPFS in ex19del (aHR 1.68) but not clearly in L858R (aHR 1.18, p=0.2); co-mutations in tumour-suppressor genes (RBM10, CREBBP, ATM, RB1, KMT2D, TSC2, PTEN) shortened rwPFS (aHR 1.5), mainly in L858R, and baseline PD-L1 was the strongest predictor of very early progression. This helps define who may need intensified first-line therapy (FLAURA2, MARIPOSA). Limitations: retrospective, real-world PFS, genomic data only in a subset, no prospective validation. Pecci F, Jeng MY, Di Federico A et al., J Thorac Oncol, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43014696/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43014696/)
