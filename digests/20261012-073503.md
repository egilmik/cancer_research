## High

### nsclc-drivers / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / egfr-nsclc
- **CodeBreaK 100: sotorasib in previously treated KRAS G12C NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, n=126, NEJM).** 81% had had both platinum and PD-(L)1 inhibitors. ORR was 37.1% (95% CI 28.6–46.2) and median DoR 11.1 months. Median PFS was 6.8 months and OS 12.5 months. Grade 3 TRAEs were 19.8%, and responses occurred across STK11, KEAP1 and TP53 co-mutations. The trial has no comparator. Skoulidis F, Li BT, Dy GK et al. N Engl J Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34096690/
- **FDA approves sotorasib (Cancer Discov news).** It is the first KRAS-targeted therapy, approved for previously treated KRAS G12C NSCLC on CodeBreaK 100 (median PFS 6.8 months). This is a journal news report of the decision, not the agency's report.  Cancer Discov 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34158284/
- **Amivantamab approved for EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC (Cancer Discov news).** The FDA gave accelerated approval to amivantamab, an EGFR×MET bispecific and the first treatment for exon 20 insertion NSCLC. This is a news report of the decision, and the item gives no trial data.  Cancer Discov 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34083225/
- **Acquired resistance to adagrasib in KRAS G12C cancers (NEJM, n=38: 27 NSCLC, 10 CRC, 1 appendiceal).** Resistance mechanisms were found in 45%, multiple in 18%. They included secondary KRAS mutations (G12D/R/V/W, G13D, Q61H, R68S, H95, Y96C), KRAS G12C amplification, bypass alterations (MET amplification, NRAS, BRAF, MAP2K1, RET, ALK/RET/BRAF/RAF1/FGFR3 fusions, NF1/PTEN loss) and squamous transformation. The cohort is small, and the findings argue for combination strategies. Awad MM, Liu S, Rybkin II et al. N Engl J Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34161704/
- **ARROW: pralsetinib in RET fusion-positive NSCLC (phase 1/2, Lancet Oncol; 233 enrolled).** ORR was 61% (95% CI 50–71) after platinum (n=87) and 70% (50–86) in treatment-naive patients (n=27). Grade ≥3 TRAEs included neutropenia (18%), hypertension (11%) and anaemia (10%). The trial is single-arm, and the treatment-naive cohort is small. Gainor JF, Curigliano G, Kim DW et al. Lancet Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34118197/
- **Selpercatinib intracranial efficacy in LIBRETTO-001 (CCR; 80 RET fusion-positive NSCLC patients with brain metastases).** Intracranial ORR was 82% (95% CI 60–95) in 22 with measurable disease, including 23% CR. Median intracranial PFS was 13.7 months. This is a pre-planned subset of a single-arm trial. Subbiah V, Gainor JF, Oxnard GR et al. Clin Cancer Res 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34088726/
- **Savolitinib in METex14 NSCLC including pulmonary sarcomatoid carcinoma (single-arm phase 2, n=70, China, Lancet Respir Med).** IRC-assessed ORR was 49.2% (evaluable set) and 42.9% (full set). Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 46%, mainly transaminase rises and oedema, with one possibly related death from tumour lysis. The trial is single-arm and Chinese. Lu S, Fang J, Li X et al. Lancet Respir Med 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34166627/
- **eNRGy1 global registry of NRG1 fusion-positive lung cancer (n=110, 22 centres, JCO).** Tumours were more heterogeneous than expected: 43% were in smokers and 43% were non-mucinous. Responses were poor to platinum (ORR 13%), chemo-immunotherapy (0%), single-agent IO (20%) and afatinib (25%, median PFS 2.8 months). These are retrospective registry data, and they underline the unmet need. Drilon A, Duruisseaux M, Han JY et al. J Clin Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34077268/
- **Multiomic analysis of neuroendocrine transformation in lung adenocarcinoma (Cancer Discov).** In combined LUAD/SCLC tumours and pre/post-transformation samples, transformation was driven by transcriptional reprogramming, with PRC2, PI3K/AKT and NOTCH activation and frequent 3p loss. PI3K/AKT inhibition delayed transformation in an EGFR-mutant PDX. These are translational data with a potential treatment angle for transformed SCLC. Quintanal-Villalonga A, Taniguchi H, Zhan YA et al. Cancer Discov 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34155000/
- **TASUKI-52: nivolumab or placebo plus carboplatin-paclitaxel-bevacizumab in first-line nonsquamous NSCLC (double-blind phase III, n=550, Japan/Korea/Taiwan).** At a preplanned interim, IRRC-assessed PFS was 12.1 vs 8.1 months (HR 0.56, 96.4% CI 0.43–0.71) across PD-L1 levels. ORR was 61.5% vs 50.5%, and grade 3–4 TRAEs were similar. OS is not reported, and the comparator has no immunotherapy. Sugawara S, Lee JS, Kang JH et al. Ann Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34139272/
- **2 years vs 3 months of adjuvant afatinib after resection of stage I–III EGFR-mutant NSCLC (randomised phase II, n=46 of a planned 92).** 2-year RFS was 81% vs 70% (p=0.55), and only 41% completed 2 years. The trial underaccrued and used an agent since superseded by osimertinib, but it bears on the question of adjuvant TKI duration. Neal JW, Costa DB, Muzikansky A et al. JCO Precis Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34151132/
- **LOGIK0902/OLCSG0905: gefitinib induction then cisplatin-docetaxel CRT in unresectable stage III EGFR-mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=20).** 2-year OS was 90%, meeting the primary endpoint, with ORR 85%. 2-year PFS was 36.9%, and there was no grade ≥3 pneumonitis. The trial is very small. Hotta K, Saeki S, Yamaguchi M et al. ESMO Open 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34153652/
- **WJOG6911L: gefitinib for 2 years with concurrent thoracic RT (64 Gy) in unresectable stage III EGFR-mutant NSCLC (single-arm phase II, n=27).** 2-year PFS was 29.6%, so the primary endpoint was not clearly met. Median PFS was 18.6 months and median OS 61.1 months. About half relapsed first in the brain. Toxicity was low. Akamatsu H, Murakami H, Harada H et al. J Thorac Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34116229/

### lung-key-evidence / lung-local-therapy / sclc
- **PORT-C: postoperative RT (50 Gy) vs observation after resection and 4 cycles of chemotherapy in pIIIA-N2 NSCLC (phase 3, n=394; 364 eligible, China, JAMA Oncol).** 3-year DFS was 40.5% vs 32.7% (HR 0.84, 95% CI 0.65–1.09, p=0.20) and 3-year OS 78.3% vs 82.8% (HR 1.02). Locoregional recurrence-free survival improved (HR 0.71). There was no grade 4–5 RT toxicity. The trial is negative on its primary endpoint, consistent with omitting routine PORT. Hui Z, Men Y, Hu C et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34165501/
- **KEYNOTE-799: pembrolizumab plus concurrent CRT in unresectable stage III NSCLC (nonrandomised phase 2, n=216, JAMA Oncol).** ORR was 70.5% in cohort A (carboplatin-paclitaxel) and 70.6% in cohort B (cisplatin-pemetrexed). Grade ≥3 pneumonitis was 8.0% and 6.9%, and grade 3–5 TRAEs 64.3% and 50.0%. The trial has no comparator against the durvalumab-consolidation standard. Jabbour SK, Lee KH, Frost N et al. JAMA Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34086039/
- **HOT1401/NJLCG1401: irinotecan vs amrubicin maintenance after cisplatin-irinotecan in ED-SCLC (randomised phase 2, terminated).** Only 14 of 34 were randomised, and there was no PFS difference. The trial is too small to inform practice. Tanaka H, Hasegawa Y, Fujita Y et al. Thorac Cancer 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34076966/
- **Serial ctDNA molecular response on pembrolizumab-based therapy in metastatic NSCLC (n=67; 51 evaluable).** A >50% fall in mean VAF at 9 weeks was associated with PFS HR 0.25 (95% CI 0.13–0.50) and OS HR 0.27 (0.12–0.64). It is a single-institution study, and the authors call for prospective validation. Thompson JC, Carpenter EL, Silva BA et al. JCO Precis Oncol 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34095713/
