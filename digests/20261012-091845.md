## Practice-changing

### PEARLS/KEYNOTE-091: adjuvant pembrolizumab after resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC (Lancet Oncol, 2022)
PEARLS is a randomised, triple-blind phase 3 trial (196 centres, 29 countries). 1,177 patients with completely resected stage IB (≥4 cm)–IIIA NSCLC (AJCC 7th edition), of any histology or PD-L1 level, were randomised to pembrolizumab 200 mg every 3 weeks for up to 18 cycles or placebo, after adjuvant chemotherapy where recommended. The dual primary endpoints were DFS in the overall population and DFS in PD-L1 TPS ≥50%. This second interim analysis had a median follow-up of 35.6 months. Overall-population DFS was improved: median 53.6 vs 42.0 months, HR 0.76 (95% CI 0.63–0.91, p=0.0014). The PD-L1 ≥50% co-primary was not met (HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.57–1.18), so the benefit is not concentrated in high PD-L1 tumours. The cost is more toxicity: grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 34% vs 26% and serious AEs in 24% vs 15%. There were four treatment-related deaths with pembrolizumab (two with myocarditis) and none with placebo. Still open:
- OS is immature.
- Benefit by PD-L1 subgroup is unexplained.
- The item says nothing about regulatory approval or a Nye Metoder decision.
- It overlaps with adjuvant atezolizumab (PD-L1 ≥1%) and adjuvant osimertinib (EGFR-mutant), so how patients are selected in Norway is still to be settled.

O'Brien M, Paz-Ares L, Marreaud S et al. Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36108662/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers

- **Norwegian nationwide EGFR testing and TKI use (registry cohort).** The cohort covers 10,717 patients with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC in Norway, 2010–2017. EGFR testing in metastatic disease rose from 41% to >64% and EGFRm+ incidence was about 9%. More than 85% of EGFRm+ patients received a TKI. Patients over 80, those with comorbidity and those diagnosed by cytology or imaging only were tested less, and testing varied by region. This is directly relevant to Norwegian practice. The data end in 2017. Helland Å, Andersen KK, Myklebust TÅ et al. Cancer Treat Res Commun 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36155129/
- **FDA approval summary: mobocertinib (EGFR exon 20 insertions).** The FDA granted accelerated approval on 15 September 2021, after platinum, based on a non-randomised trial. ORR was 28% (95% CI 20–37%) in 114 platinum-pretreated patients and median DoR 17.5 months. The label carries a boxed warning for QTc prolongation and torsades. This is a journal summary of a decision made a year earlier, so it is rated high. Duke ES, Stapleford L, Drezner N et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36112541/
- **Rezivertinib (BPI-7711) phase 2b.** 226 patients with T790M-positive NSCLC received this new third-generation EGFR TKI. ORR was 64.6%, median PFS 12.2 months and OS 23.9 months. CNS ORR was 69% in 29 patients with target brain lesions. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 19.9% and no ILD was reported. The trial is single-arm in a second-line T790M setting that osimertinib first line has largely replaced. Shi Y, Wu S, Wang K et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36049654/
- **Resistance to first-line osimertinib, French multicentre study.** Among 104 patients, ORR was 76%, median PFS 12.6 months and OS 52 months. In 27 informative rebiopsies, the commonest findings were MET amplification (15%) and C797S (11%), with one histological transformation and one CCDC6-RET fusion. The study is retrospective with few rebiopsies. Akli A, Girard N, Fallet V et al. Target Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36129569/
- **APOBEC mutagenesis in osimertinib resistance.** APOBEC-dominant signatures were more frequent in osimertinib-resistant than naive EGFR-mutant samples (28% vs 14%). They were also enriched in mutations acquired at resistance (44% vs 23%) and associated with large-scale rearrangements and kataegis. This points to APOBEC as an engine of resistance. It is a genomic association study with no treatment data. Selenica P, Marra A, Choudhury NJ et al. Ann Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36089134/
- **Fusion genes as resistance to EGFR inhibitors.** RNA-seq in 504 EGFR-mutant patients showed that only a minority of DNA-detected fusions are functional resistance drivers. The authors identify which patients benefit from adding a fusion-targeted drug, and they model further resistance in CRISPR-edited lines. The practical implication is to confirm fusions functionally (RNA) before adding a second TKI. Kobayashi Y, Oxnard GR, Cohen EF et al. Nat Commun 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36153311/
- **ATORG consensus on MET alterations.** Asia-Pacific experts recommend MET inhibitors first or later line for METex14, with METex14 testing within multigene panels. For MET-amplified EGFR-TKI resistance they recommend enrolment in EGFR+MET combination trials. The statement is consensus-based and written around Asian access constraints. Ahn MJ, Mendoza MJL, Pavlakis N et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36151006/
- **CSCO consensus on third-generation EGFR-TKIs (2022 version).** This item has no abstract, so the recommendations cannot be summarised from it. Zhongguo Fei Ai Za Zhi 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36172727/
- **Esomeprazole and afatinib (randomised PK crossover).** In 18 patients, concomitant esomeprazole changed afatinib AUC by −10.2% (95% CI −29.2 to +14.0) and dosing three hours apart by −0.6%, with no toxicity difference. PPIs can be co-prescribed with afatinib, unlike with erlotinib or gefitinib. The study is small and measured pharmacokinetics only. Veerman GDM, Hurkmans DP, Paats MS et al. Biomed Pharmacother 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36126454/
- **Induction ICI in resectable oncogene-mutant NSCLC (pooled multicentre).** 40 patients with oncogene-mutant NSCLC received induction immunotherapy. ORR was 62.5%, MPR 37.5% and pCR 12.5%, and median DFS was 28.5 months. No MPR occurred with uncommon EGFR insertions or point mutations. The sample is small and heterogeneous, and the comparison with CTONG1103 is indirect. Zhang C, Chen HF, Yan S et al. NPJ Precis Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36123526/
- **ALTA-2 (phase 2).** 103 patients received brigatinib after alectinib or ceritinib. IRC ORR was 26.2% and median PFS only 3.8 months, falling to 1.9 months when ALK fusion was detectable in ctDNA. G1202R made up 54% of baseline resistance mutations. Brigatinib has limited activity after second-generation ALK TKIs. Ou SI, Nishio M, Ahn MJ et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36096442/
- **ALTA final results (phase 1/2 and randomised phase 2).** In crizotinib-refractory ALK+ NSCLC, brigatinib 180 mg (arm B) gave a median IRC PFS of 16.7 months and median OS of 40.6 months. The 90 mg arm (A) gave 9.9 and 25.9 months. This is mature follow-up of the post-crizotinib dosing trial, in a setting that is now uncommon. Gettinger SN, Huber RM, Kim DW et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36065449/
- **LIBRETTO-001 NSCLC update (registrational phase 1/2).** In RET fusion-positive NSCLC, ORR to selpercatinib was 84% treatment-naive (median PFS 22.0 months) and 61% after platinum (median PFS 24.9 months). Intracranial ORR was 85% in 26 patients with measurable CNS disease. Treatment duration in the safety population (n=796) was 36 months. The trial is single-arm. Drilon A, Subbiah V, Gautschi O et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36122315/

### lung-key-evidence / nsclc-immunotherapy / sclc / lung-local-therapy

- **ASTRUM-005 (phase 3, interim).** 585 patients with ES-SCLC were randomised 2:1 to serplulimab or placebo with carboplatin-etoposide. Median OS was 15.4 vs 10.9 months (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.49–0.82) and PFS 5.7 vs 4.3 months (HR 0.48). Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 33.2% vs 27.6%. The comparator (chemotherapy alone) is no longer the ES-SCLC standard, and the trial was mostly Asian and Eastern European, so it is rated high. Cheng Y, Han L, Wu L et al. JAMA 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36166026/
- **LCMC3 (single-arm phase 2).** 181 patients with resectable stage IB–IIIB NSCLC received two doses of neoadjuvant atezolizumab. MPR was 20% (95% CI 14–28%) in the 143 primary-analysis patients without EGFR/ALK, and 3-year survival was 80%. A peripheral-blood immunophenotype predicted MPR. Chaft JE, Oezkan F, Kris MG et al. Nat Med 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36097216/
- **CAPITAL (phase 3, Japan).** 196 patients aged ≥70 with squamous NSCLC received first-line carboplatin+nab-paclitaxel or docetaxel. Median OS was 16.9 vs 10.9 months (HR 0.52, 90% CI 0.38–0.70). Febrile neutropenia was 10% vs 20% and anaemia 39% vs 2%. Neither arm contains immunotherapy, so the result applies to patients unsuitable for ICI. Kogure Y, Iwasawa S, Saka H et al. Lancet Healthy Longev 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36098037/
- **IMpower132 China cohort.** 163 patients with non-squamous NSCLC received atezolizumab+carboplatin/cisplatin-pemetrexed or chemotherapy. PFS was 8.3 vs 5.8 months (HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.50–1.08) and OS was immature. This is a regional subgroup that confirms consistency only. Lu S, Fang J, Wang Z et al. Cancer Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36052772/
- **IMpower010 Japanese subgroup.** 117 Japanese patients were in the ITT population. DFS HRs for adjuvant atezolizumab vs BSC were 0.52 (PD-L1 ≥1% stage II–IIIA), 0.62 (all stage II–IIIA) and 0.61 (ITT), all with CIs crossing 1. Grade 3/4 related AEs occurred in 16%. The subgroup is small and consistent with the global result. Kenmotsu H, Sugawara S, Watanabe Y et al. Cancer Sci 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36062851/
- **Pooled safety of first-line nivolumab+ipilimumab (CheckMate 227/568/817).** Across 1,255 patients, grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 34% and 21% discontinued for TRAEs. Pneumonitis was the commonest cause of treatment-related death (5/16). Safety in patients ≥75 was similar, apart from more discontinuations (29%). 3-year OS was 50% among those who stopped for toxicity. Paz-Ares LG, Ciuleanu TE, Pluzanski A et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36049658/
- **German segmentectomy vs lobectomy (randomised phase 3, small).** 108 patients with NSCLC ≤2 cm were randomised. 5-year OS was 78.2% after segmentectomy vs 86.5% after lobectomy (HR 0.61, 95% CI 0.23–1.66), so OS non-inferiority was not shown. DFS (78% vs 77%) and recurrence were similar. The trial is small and underpowered for its non-inferiority margin. Stamatis G, Leschber G, Schwarz B et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36058174/
- **SABR-5 (single-arm phase 2).** 381 patients with up to 5 oligometastases (9% lung primaries; lung was the commonest treated site) received SABR. Grade 3 toxicity occurred in 4.2% and grade 5 in 0.3%, with 2-year cumulative grade ≥3 of 4%. The trial shows population-based SABR is safe but reports no efficacy here. Olson R, Jiang W, Liu M et al. JAMA Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36173619/
- **UK expert consensus on tissue acquisition and molecular testing.** The consensus gives recommendations on adequacy, turnaround and multidisciplinary communication for NSCLC genomic testing in the NHS Genomic Laboratory Hub era. It is expert consensus specific to the UK. Navani N, Butler R, Ibrahimo S et al. Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36099709/
- **FDA analysis of new lurbinectedin adverse events.** The FDA identified extravasation, rhabdomyolysis and tumour lysis syndrome as new adverse events with lurbinectedin. There is no abstract, so whether a label change followed cannot be confirmed from the item. Kaland DA, Dores GM, Nayernama A et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36151005/

### lung-screening

- **Stage migration after LDCT screening (four US health systems).** Among 3,678 incident lung cancers, the share diagnosed after screening started rose to 20%. Overall incidence did not change (AAPC −0.8), stage I incidence rose (AAPC +8.0) and stage IV fell (AAPC −6.0), suggesting limited overdiagnosis. The design is observational. Vachani A, Carroll NM, Simoff MJ et al. J Thorac Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36087860/
- **China Kadoorie Biobank absolute risk models (ALARM).** These are separate lung cancer mortality models for never-smokers and ever-smokers. The never-smoker model uses passive smoking, indoor air pollution and lung function, among others. 5-year AUCs were 0.77 (never) and 0.81 (ever). The models identify Asian never-smokers whose risk exceeds usual screening thresholds. External validation outside China is not reported. Warkentin MT, Tammemägi MC, Espin-Garcia O et al. J Natl Cancer Inst 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36083018/

### lung-vaccines-cell / lung-mrna / car-t-solid

- **UCPVax (phase 1b/2a).** 59 heavily pretreated NSCLC patients (95% after three prior lines) received this telomerase-derived peptide vaccine. Th1 responses occurred in 87% after six doses and disease control in 39%, with 1-year OS 34.1% and median OS 9.7 months. Immune responders lived longer (median OS 11.6 vs 5.6 months), but this comparison is not randomised. The trial is single-arm. Adotévi O, Vernerey D, Jacoulet P et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36070539/
- **CV301 poxviral vaccine with nivolumab or pembrolizumab (phase 1b).** In advanced non-squamous NSCLC, 1 CR and 1 PR were seen among 11 evaluable patients, with no apparent increase in irAEs. There was one DLT (pneumonitis) and one on-trial death. The cohort is very small. Rajan A, Gray JE, Devarakonda S et al. Int J Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36054490/
- **Results posted: mRNA vaccine BI 1361849 (CV9202) with durvalumab ± tremelimumab (NCT03164772, phase 1/2, Ludwig, n=61).** The trial ran in metastatic NSCLC. Results were posted to ClinicalTrials.gov, and the record has no efficacy summary. https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03164772
- **NY-ESO-1 TCR-T (lete-cel) biomarkers in synovial sarcoma.** This is a post-hoc analysis of 45 patients. Response was associated with pre-infusion IL-15, the dose of effector-memory CD8+ cells, post-infusion IFNγ/IL-6 and peak expansion. It is a hypothesis-generating analysis. Gyurdieva A, Zajic S, Chang YF et al. Nat Commun 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36075914/

### thoracic-other

Lancet Oncol 2022. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36096156/
