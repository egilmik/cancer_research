## High
### lung-key-evidence
- **Broad genomic sequencing vs focused biomarker testing and survival in advanced NSCLC (Flatiron, 2016–2022).** A retrospective US cohort of 35,060 patients with stage IIIB–IV NSCLC compared broad-based sequencing (BGS) with focused testing done <3 months before first- or second-line therapy; 20,016 were propensity-matched for the first-line analysis. BGS was linked to slightly longer median PFS (6.4 vs 6.0 mo, aHR 0.96, 95% CI 0.92–1.00) and OS (16 vs 14 mo, aHR 0.91, 0.86–0.95), with more biomarker positivity (60% vs 22%) and targeted-therapy use (19% vs 16%). There was no benefit when testing was done before second-line therapy. This supports broad NGS up front, which Norwegian guidelines already recommend. Limitations: observational EHR data with residual confounding (access, practice type), small absolute PFS gain, and the comparison may partly reflect which patients get tested rather than the test itself. Santos PMG, Boe LA, Al Ksir K et al., J Natl Compr Canc Netw, 2026. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019765/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019765/)

### guidelines-meetings / nsclc-resistance-nextgen (web)
- **ESMO 2026: ADCs and new combinations advance lung cancer care (Targeted Oncology).** A conference report listing phase 3 late-breakers in EGFR-mutant NSCLC after TKI progression: OptiTROP-Lung04 (sacituzumab tirumotecan, LBA67) and PANKU-Lung01 (izalontamab brengitecan, LBA71), plus PERSEE (pembrolizumab ± chemotherapy in PD-L1 ≥50% NSCLC, 2614O). These ADC trials bear directly on what comes after osimertinib. Limitations: secondary trade-press source; the snippet gives no effect sizes, and the results are conference abstracts, not peer reviewed. Wait for the full publications before drawing conclusions. Targeted Oncology, October 2026. [https://www.targetedonc.com/view/esmo-2026-adcs-and-new-combinations-advance-lung-cancer-care](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/esmo-2026-adcs-and-new-combinations-advance-lung-cancer-care)

## Medium
- [Repurposing of abemaciclib to overcome C797S-mediated osimertinib resistance in EGFR-mutant lung adenocarcinoma](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43021317/) — Preclinical study (Ba/F3, isogenic xenografts, PDX) showing abemaciclib directly inhibits C797S-mutant EGFR and overcomes osimertinib resistance; no clinical data.
- [Recent advances in treating small-cell lung cancer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43021706/) — Narrative review of recent SCLC advances (lurbinectedin+atezolizumab maintenance, intensified maintenance, novel agents).
- [Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists and lung cancer risk in patients with type 2 diabetes and pulmonary nodules](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43024059/) — Target-trial emulation (TriNetX, 12,622 matched pairs, diabetes + pulmonary nodule): GLP-1RA vs metformin lung cancer HR 0.77 (0.64-0.92), but null vs SGLT2i and null Mendelian randomisation argue against true chemoprevention.
- [First-line immune checkpoint inhibitors versus chemotherapy in delaying quality of life deterioration in advanced NSCLC: a systematic review and meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43021226/) — Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (n=7,127): first-line ICI-based therapy delayed global QoL deterioration vs chemotherapy (HR 0.75, 0.69-0.81), driven by chemo-immunotherapy; trial-level, open-label PRO bias.
- [Clinical Study of Ivonescimab Combined With Concurrent Chemoradiotherapy in the Treatment of Advanced Non-small Cell Lung Cancer](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07936578) — New registration: single-arm exploratory study of ivonescimab + chemo after concurrent chemoradiotherapy in stage IV NSCLC, compared with historical controls.
- [Perioperative ctDNA dynamics predict outcomes in resectable NSCLC using an ultrasensitive and cost-effective assay](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43020876/) — Prospective cohort (n=119, EVOLUTION) of perioperative ctDNA (PEAC assay): baseline ctDNA-negative DFS HR 0.18; MRD detected in only 48% of relapses, lead time 6.5 mo; single centre, not interventional.
- [Medicare beneficiaries have less follow-up imaging and more invasive procedures than NLST participants following lung screening](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43020361/) — Medicare vs NLST comparison: community screening had less follow-up imaging and more invasive procedures at 6 months, no mortality difference; observational, imputed NLST data.
- [Exponential growth and slow-growing tumours in screen-detected lung cancers: findings from the NELCIN-B3 LDCT screening study in China](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019606/) — NELCIN-B3 Chinese LDCT cohort (n=57 cancers): 65% of screen-detected cancers slow-growing (VDT >400 d), especially non-solid and never-smokers; small exploratory sample.
- [Particle therapy for T3N0M0 non-small cell lung cancer: a Japanese nationwide prospective registry study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43020138/) — Japanese prospective registry (n=96) of particle therapy for cT3N0M0 NSCLC: 3-y OS 48%, local failure 19%, grade>=3 AEs 5%; uncontrolled, selected inoperable patients.
- [Recent advances in the treatment of pleural mesothelioma](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43021707/) — Narrative review of pleural mesothelioma advances (CheckMate 743 5-y HR 0.74, BEAT-meso missed OS primary).
