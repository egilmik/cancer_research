## High

- **Clinical and molecular prognostic factors associated with outcomes of first-line osimertinib in EGFR-Mutated advanced NSCLC: a systematic review and meta-analysis.** Meta-analysis of 18 studies (4,070 pts) on first-line osimertinib: TP53 mutation (PFS HR 1.65, 95% CI 1.15-2.37; OS HR 1.55, 1.14-2.12) and L858R vs exon 19del (PFS HR 1.62; OS HR 1.63) predict worse outcomes; ECOG ≥2 and CNS metastases also adverse. Limitation: observational inputs, heterogeneous estimates, lower certainty for liver/bone/PD-L1; authors state it does not justify intensifying first-line therapy. Ruan Y, Xue H, Long C et al., BMC Cancer, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42926199/
- **Dual Checkpoint Inhibition With or Without Chemotherapy in Metastatic NSCLC: A Reconstructed Individual Patient Data Meta-Analysis.** Reconstructed-IPD meta-analysis of 4 trials (1,654 pts) comparing dual checkpoint inhibition ± chemotherapy in metastatic NSCLC: median OS 15.4 vs 15.0 mo (HR 0.93, 95% CI 0.81-1.08); 5-yr OS 19.4% vs 17.1% favouring DICI alone. STK11 early benefit not sustained. Limitation: data reconstructed from Kaplan-Meier curves, non-proportional hazards, cross-trial comparison. Taylor S, Mamede I, Gazzoni G et al., JTO Clin Res Rep, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42927193/
- **Garsorasib (D-1553) in Combination With Pembrolizumab and Chemotherapy for First-Line Metastatic NSCLC With KRAS G12C Mutation: Results of a Phase 1b Study.** Phase 1b (n=17) garsorasib (KRAS G12C) + pembrolizumab + platinum chemo first-line: ORR 59%, median PFS 7.4 mo, but 94% grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs and 88% dose modifications; one DLT. Study did not proceed to phase 2. Limitation: very small single-arm study. Yan D, Liu C, Li X et al., JTO Clin Res Rep, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42924167/
- **ASCO 2026 plenary: HARMONi-6 ivonescimab + chemo vs tislelizumab + chemo, squamous NSCLC (LBA4)** Web listing: ASCO 2026 plenary LBA4, HARMONi-6, phase 3 ivonescimab + chemo vs tislelizumab + chemo in untreated advanced squamous NSCLC, presented 31 May 2026 by Shun Lu. No results in the item, so not rated practice-changing; check the publication when available. Web announcement. https://www.1stoncology.com/blog/?p=111313
