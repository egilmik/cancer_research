## High

### lung-key-evidence / sclc / nsclc-immunotherapy
- **Vibostolimab Coformulated with Pembrolizumab Plus Chemotherapy as First-Line Therapy for Extensive-Stage Small-Cell Lung Cancer.** KEYVIBE-008, a randomised phase 3 (n=460), compared vibostolimab/pembrolizumab plus etoposide-platinum with atezolizumab plus EP as first-line therapy for ES-SCLC. The prespecified non-binding OS futility criterion was met (HR 1.26, 95% CI 1.00-1.59) with no PFS difference (HR 1.01, 0.82-1.23) and ORR 71.7% vs 74.8%. Grade 3 or higher treatment-related AEs were 66.8% vs 57.2%. Limitation: interim analysis (median follow-up 16.6 months); this is a negative result for the TIGIT strategy in SCLC.
  Sands J, Lu S, Aerts JG et al., Target Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42900351/

### egfr-nsclc
- **Firmonertinib 160mg in EGFR-Mutated NSCLC with Brain Metastases After EGFR-TKI Resistance (iFORCE): Clinical Outcomes and Mechanistic Insights.** iFORCE, a single-centre single-arm study (n=23), gave firmonertinib 160 mg to EGFR-mutant NSCLC with brain metastases after prior EGFR-TKI. Median intracranial PFS was 14.6 months (95% CI 4.2-NE), intracranial ORR 34.8% and DCR 91.3%, with one grade 3 event (stomatitis). Exploratory spatial profiling suggested L858R versus exon 19 differences. Limitations: very small, heterogeneous, wide CIs, no comparator.
  You M, Xie T, Wu L et al., Neuro Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42899412/
