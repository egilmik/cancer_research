### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers
- **Osimertinib 160 mg for T790M-positive brain or leptomeningeal metastases (phase II, Korea, two cohorts of 40).** In the brain metastasis cohort, intracranial ORR was 55.0%, median PFS 7.6 months and median OS 16.9 months. In the leptomeningeal cohort, intracranial disease control was 92.5% and median OS 13.3 months (primary endpoint, against a 5-month hypothesis). Prior T790M-targeted therapy did not change PFS, and AEs were mostly grade 1–2. It supports dose-escalated osimertinib for CNS progression. It is single-arm, and T790M-positive patients after first-generation TKI are now uncommon. Park S, Lee MH, Seong M et al. *Ann Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32634610/
- **Pyrotinib in HER2-mutant lung adenocarcinoma after platinum (single-arm phase II, China, n=60).** ORR by independent review was 30.0% (95% CI 18.8–43.2), similar with and without brain metastases (25% vs 31%). Median PFS was 6.9 months and median OS 14.4 months. Grade 3 diarrhoea occurred in 20%, with no treatment-related deaths. It supports pyrotinib as an option for HER2 exon 20 NSCLC (a phase 3 against docetaxel was registered in the June chunk). It is single-arm. Zhou C, Li X, Wang Q et al. *J Clin Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32614698/
- **First-iGAP: intercalated gefitinib with pemetrexed–cisplatin in never-smokers with advanced adenocarcinoma (randomised placebo-controlled phase II, Korea, n=76, 45% EGFR-mutant).** ORR was 79.5% vs 51.4% and PFS 12.4 vs 6.7 months (HR 0.49), mainly in EGFR-mutant tumours (13.3 vs 7.8 months). OS did not differ significantly (31.8 vs 22.9 months, HR 0.78). It adds to evidence for combining EGFR TKIs with chemotherapy. It is small, uses a first-generation TKI, and enrolled unselected never-smokers. Lee Y, Kim HY, Nam BH et al. *Clin Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32605893/
- **ATLANTIC: durvalumab third-line or later, final OS (phase II, n=444).** With PD-L1 TC ≥ 25%, median OS was 13.3 months in EGFR+/ALK+ and 10.9 months in EGFR−/ALK− patients, and 13.2 months with TC ≥ 90%. With TC < 25%, it was 9.9 and 9.3 months. There were no new safety signals. It documents modest late-line single-agent activity, including in EGFR/ALK-positive disease. It is single-arm and of limited relevance now that immunotherapy is used first-line. Garassino MC, Cho BC, Kim JH et al. *Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32702570/
- **Spanish SEAP/SEOM national consensus on predictive biomarker testing in advanced NSCLC (2019 update).** It makes testing for EGFR and BRAF mutations, ALK and ROS1 rearrangements, and PD-L1 mandatory for all patients with advanced NSCLC, with growing routine use of NGS. The abstract does not state which recommendations changed from 2015, so it is rated high. López-Ríos F, Paz-Ares L, Sanz J et al. *Rev Esp Patol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32650968/
- **Proteogenomics of never-smoker lung adenocarcinoma in East Asia (*Cell*, prospective Taiwanese cohort).** Mostly early-stage, female never-smoker tumours showed an APOBEC mutational signature in younger women and environmental carcinogen-like signatures in older women. A proteomics-informed classification separated clinical subgroups of EGFR-mutant early-stage disease. It is relevant to never-smoker aetiology and screening debates. It is a single-population molecular study without intervention. Chen YJ, Roumeliotis TI, Chang YH et al. *Cell*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32649875/

### nsclc-immunotherapy / lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals
- **KEYNOTE-407 protocol-specified final analysis: pembrolizumab + carboplatin/(nab-)paclitaxel in squamous NSCLC (phase 3, n=559).** At 14.3 months' median follow-up, OS was 17.1 vs 11.6 months (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.58–0.88) and PFS 8.0 vs 5.1 months (HR 0.57). PFS2 also favoured pembrolizumab (HR 0.59), despite crossover. Grade 3–5 AEs were 74.1% vs 69.6%. It confirms the established first-line standard in squamous NSCLC. Paz-Ares L, Vicente D, Tafreshi A et al. *J Thorac Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32599071/
- **HCRN LUN14-179: consolidation pembrolizumab after chemoradiation in stage III NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, n=92).** Median time to metastatic disease or death was 30.7 months (vs a 12-month historical control), median PFS 18.7 months and median OS 35.8 months, with 3-year OS 48.5%. Grade ≥ 2 pneumonitis occurred in 17.2% (grade 3–5 in 6.5%, one death). It is supportive evidence for PD-1 consolidation alongside PACIFIC. It is single-arm, with a historical comparator. Durm GA, Jabbour SK, Althouse SK et al. *Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32697352/
- **National Lung Matrix Trial: umbrella trial of genotype-matched therapy in NSCLC (*Nature*, UK).** Of 5,467 patients screened, 2,007 were molecularly eligible and 302 were treated across 19 drug–biomarker cohorts (Bayesian design). Few combinations showed clinically relevant benefit, and these were concentrated in cancers with low tobacco exposure. It is a sobering result for genotype-matching in smoking-associated NSCLC. Cohorts are small, and several agents are no longer in development. Middleton G, Fletcher P, Popat S et al. *Nature*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32669708/
- **ASCO antiemetics guideline update.** Adult recommendations are unchanged except for: olanzapine as an option in stem cell transplantation, a 5-mg olanzapine dose option, and IV formulations of aprepitant and netupitant–palonosetron. It adds that dexamethasone should not be omitted from prophylaxis when checkpoint inhibitors are combined with chemotherapy. That rests on the pembrolizumab–chemotherapy NSCLC trials, which included dexamethasone. It is relevant to chemo-immunotherapy practice in lung cancer but does not change lung-specific treatment. Hesketh PJ, Kris MG, Basch E et al. *J Clin Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32658626/
- **ESTRO ACROP guideline on target volume definition for thoracic radiotherapy in SCLC.** It is a consensus on imaging, GTV/CTV, ITV/PTV and OAR delineation for definitive, adjuvant and consolidative thoracic radiotherapy in SCLC, aimed at standardising practice and trials. It is a technical standardisation, not a change in indications. Le Pechoux C, Faivre-Finn C, Ramella S et al. *Radiother Oncol*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32673777/

### lung-nonpharm / lung-repurposed
- **PROLUCA: early (2 weeks) vs late (14 weeks) rehabilitation after lung cancer surgery, secondary HRQoL outcomes (Danish RCT, n=235).** Early rehabilitation (high-intensity interval and strength training twice weekly for 12 weeks) improved FACT-L/FACT-G up to 26 weeks and avoided the temporary decline seen with late start. The late group improved after its programme but did not reach the early group's level. It supports starting structured rehabilitation 2 weeks after surgery. These are secondary outcomes, and HRQoL fell again after the programme ended. Sommer MS, Vibe-Petersen J, Stærkind MB et al. *Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32593918/
- **Probiotic *Clostridium butyricum* (CBM588) and checkpoint inhibitor outcomes (retrospective, Japan, n=118).** In propensity-score-adjusted analysis, CBM588 use before or after ICB was associated with longer PFS and OS, including in antibiotic-treated patients. It is single-centre and retrospective, with likely confounding by indication and possible immortal-time bias; the abstract gives no effect sizes. Tomita Y, Ikeda T, Sakata S et al. *Cancer Immunol Res*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32665261/

### lung-screening / lung-nordic
- **AIR: circulating tumour cells for lung cancer screening (prospective multicentre cohort, France, n=614 with COPD).** Over three annual LDCT rounds, 38 lung cancers were diagnosed. Baseline sensitivity of CTC detection (ISET) was 26.3% (95% CI 11.8–48.8), and CTCs did not predict later cancers. It is a clear negative: CTC detection by ISET is not suitable for screening. Marquette CH, Boutros J, Benzaquen J et al. *Lancet Respir Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32649919/
- **Fleischner Society position paper on interstitial lung abnormalities (ILA).** ILA are found in 4–9% of smokers and 2–7% of non-smokers, and more often as CT screening expands. They are associated with progression, mortality and complications of chemotherapy and surgery. The paper standardises definitions (notably the subpleural fibrotic subtype), evaluation and monitoring. It is relevant to screening workflows and to toxicity risk assessment before lung cancer treatment. It is expert consensus. Hatabu H, Hunninghake GM, Richeldi L et al. *Lancet Respir Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32649920/
- **Helsedirektoratet: assessment of lung cancer screening in Norway (news, February 2025; web result logged in this run).** A working group appointed by the directorate has delivered a report on how a national screening programme could be organised, and recommends offering screening to high-risk smokers aged 50–79. The directorate awaits health-economic analyses before giving its final recommendation to the ministry. It is directly relevant to Norwegian practice, but it is not yet a decision, so it is rated high. This item is from 2025, outside the 2020 backfill window; the web query has no date window. https://www.helsedirektoratet.no/nyheter/utredning-av-lungekreftscreening-i-norge

### car-t-solid
- **Mesothelin CAR-T plus CD19 CAR-T in metastatic pancreatic cancer (pilot, n=3).** After cyclophosphamide lymphodepletion, both products were given with no dose-limiting toxicity. Best response was stable disease in one patient. CART-19 depleted B cells for at least 28 days but did not improve CART-meso persistence. It is a negative pilot of B-cell depletion to enhance solid-tumour CAR-T. With three patients, no efficacy conclusion is possible. Ko AH, Jordan AC, Tooker E et al. *Mol Ther*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32730744/

### thoracic-other
- **CALGB 30901: maintenance pemetrexed vs observation after first-line pemetrexed–platinum in pleural mesothelioma (randomised phase II, n=49 eligible, closed early).** PFS was 3.4 vs 3.0 months (HR 0.99, 95% CI 0.51–1.90) and OS 16.3 vs 11.8 months (HR 0.86, not significant). Toxicity was mild. Higher soluble mesothelin-related peptide predicted worse PFS. It argues against routine pemetrexed maintenance in MPM. It is underpowered. Dudek AZ, Wang X, Gu L et al. *Clin Lung Cancer*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32727707/
- **Anti-PD-1 after mesothelin immunotoxin LMB-100 in mesothelioma (*Sci Transl Med*, n=10 off-protocol plus mouse models).** After progression on LMB-100, PD-1 blockade gave ORR 40% (one CR, three PR among seven evaluable), with OS 13.8–39+ months in responders. PD-L1-positive tumours had longer PFS (11.3 vs 2.1 months). Synergy was reproduced in two mouse models, and a prospective trial was started. It is a hypothesis-generating, non-randomised observation. Jiang Q, Ghafoor A, Mian I et al. *Sci Transl Med*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32611684/
- **Alisertib (Aurora A kinase inhibitor) in relapsed mesothelioma (single-arm phase II, n=26).** Four-month disease control was 32%, with no objective responses. Median PFS was 2.8 months and median OS 6.3 months, and MYC copy number did not predict benefit. The sponsor closed the trial at interim. Activity was modest. Gay CM, Zhou Y, Lee JJ et al. *Oncologist*, 2020. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32608142/
