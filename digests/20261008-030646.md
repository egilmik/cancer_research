### nsclc-resistance-nextgen / egfr-nsclc
- **MET Testing and Treatment Sequencing After Disease Progression on First-Line Osimertinib in EGFR-Mutated Advanced NSCLC With Acquired MET Overexpression and/or Amplification: A Global Real-World Study.** A global survey-based chart review of 280 physicians and 367 patients (94% MET amplification) who progressed on first-line osimertinib and received second-line treatment in 2018–2023. Second-line choices were MET-TKI 29%, EGFR-TKI 24% and chemotherapy 20%; among 193 patients with high MET levels, MET-TKI was 35% and EGFR-TKI 23%. NGS was the main test (78% of physicians), 35–47% of physicians did not know the appropriate MET cutoffs, and IHC/FISH were underused. This bears on the criteria for MET-directed strategies after EGFR TKIs. Limitations: physician-reported survey data, descriptive only, no efficacy endpoints, and probable selection and recall bias. Rotow J, Malapelle U, Cuadras D et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42837955/ (doi 10.1016/j.cllc.2026.08.011)
