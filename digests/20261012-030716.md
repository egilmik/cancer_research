### egfr-nsclc

- **Cost-effectiveness of first-line amivantamab+lazertinib and osimertinib+chemotherapy vs osimertinib.** A US-payer partitioned survival model using MARIPOSA and FLAURA2 data found amivantamab+lazertinib added 0.54 QALYs at +$411,500 (ICER $756,638/QALY) and osimertinib+chemo added 0.36 QALYs at +$300,513 (ICER $829,930/QALY), far above the $150,000/QALY threshold. Relevant to reimbursement debate; limitation: a model on published trial data from a US perspective, not Norwegian. Gokhale P, Caballero J, Li N et al., Expert Rev Pharmacoecon Outcomes Res, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42858230/

- **Post-resistance retesting in EGFR-mutant NSCLC.** Retrospective real-world study (n=229): ctDNA retesting shortened time to second-line treatment vs tissue NGS (11.0 vs 20.5 days) with similar matched 2L-PFS (9.0 vs 9.0 months) and 2L-OS (19 vs 20 months); no retesting did worse on PFS (5.0 months, P=0.005). Limitations: retrospective, ctDNA group n=49. Zhang W, Cheng F, Lin P et al., Front Oncol, 2026. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42856500/
