## Practice-changing

- **MEDIASTrial: endosonography with or without confirmatory mediastinoscopy for resectable NSCLC (randomised non-inferiority trial, J Clin Oncol 2023).**
  - **Design:** Patients with (suspected) resectable NSCLC, an indication for mediastinal staging and a negative systematic endosonography were randomised. 360 patients were assigned: 178 to immediate tumour resection with lymph node dissection and 182 to confirmatory mediastinoscopy and then resection. The comparator is the guideline standard of the time, confirmatory mediastinoscopy.
  - **Primary endpoint:** The rate of unforeseen N2 disease, with a non-inferiority margin of 8%. It was 8.8% after immediate resection vs 7.7% with mediastinoscopy first. The ITT difference was 1.03% (upper 95% CI 7.2%, P noninferior = .0144), and the per-protocol analysis agreed (difference 0.83%, upper CI 7.3%).
  - **Other findings:** Mediastinoscopy itself found metastases in 8.0% (95% CI 4.8–13.0). Major morbidity and 30-day mortality were 12.9% vs 15.4% (P = .49), so skipping mediastinoscopy also saves a procedure and some delay.
  - **Limitations:** Unforeseen N2 is a surrogate. The margin was justified by earlier data showing it did not compromise survival, but this trial does not report survival. The result depends on high-quality systematic endosonography of the kind done in the trial's expert centres.
  - **Since then:** The 2026 ERS/ESGE/ESTS guideline already in the index no longer recommends add-on mediastinoscopy after negative endosonography.
  - **Still open:** Applicability in Norway depends on local EBUS/EUS quality.
  - Bousema JE, Dijkgraaf MGW, van der Heijden EHFM et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37018653/

### lung-key-evidence / lung-top-journals / guidelines

- **ASCO living guidelines v2023.1, stage IV NSCLC with and without driver alterations.** These are updates to the two ASCO living guidelines. The abstracts only describe the living-guideline process and do not say which recommendations changed, so they cannot be rated practice-changing from this record. Singh N, Jaiyesimi IA, Ismaila N et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37023387/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37023367/
- **NCCN Guidelines Insights: NSCLC v2.2023.** This Insights paper focuses on perioperative (neoadjuvant and adjuvant) systemic therapy. The abstract does not state the changed recommendations. Ettinger DS, Wood DE, Aisner DL et al. J Natl Compr Canc Netw 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37015337/
- **EMPOWER-Lung 3 part 2, final OS (double-blind phase 3, n=466).** Cemiplimab plus platinum doublet vs placebo plus chemotherapy, in patients with no EGFR, ALK or ROS1 alteration and regardless of PD-L1 status. At 28.4 months median follow-up, median OS was 21.1 vs 12.9 months (HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.51–0.82) and PFS 8.2 vs 5.5 months (HR 0.55). Grade ≥3 TEAEs occurred in 48.7%. This confirms a regimen comparable to existing chemo-immunotherapy, against a chemotherapy-only comparator that is no longer standard. Makharadze T, Gogishvili M, Melkadze T et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37001859/
- **TRACERx: evolution and subclonal selection (Nature, 421 patients, 1,644 tumour regions).** Recent subclonal expansions and subclonal (not truncal) whole-genome doubling were associated with shorter DFS. 8% of adenocarcinomas in smokers lacked a tobacco signature and resembled never-smoker tumours. This is a mechanistic cohort with prognostic, not treatment, implications. Frankell AM, Dietzen M, Al Bakir M et al. Nature 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37046096/
- **TRACERx: evolution of metastases (Nature, 126 metastatic vs 144 non-metastatic tumours).** Metastases diverged early in 25% of cases and were seeded polyclonally in 32%, which was associated with extrathoracic recurrence. Single-region sampling misclassified most late divergence as early. Al Bakir M, Huebner A, Martínez-Ruiz C et al. Nature 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37046095/

### egfr-nsclc / nsclc-resistance-nextgen / nsclc-drivers

- **SBRT plus first-generation EGFR-TKI (randomised phase 2, n=62, closed early for slow accrual).** Patients with stage IV EGFR-mutant NSCLC with SD/PR after 3 months of gefitinib, erlotinib or icotinib were randomised to SBRT (30–50 Gy in 5 fractions) plus TKI or TKI alone. PFS was 17.6 vs 9.0 months (HR 0.52, 95% CI 0.31–0.89) and OS 33.6 vs 23.2 months (HR 0.53, 0.30–0.95), with no grade ≥3 toxicity. The trial is small, four-centre and closed early, and it used a first-generation TKI backbone rather than osimertinib. Peng P, Gong J, Zhang Y et al. Radiother Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37105304/
- **Furmonertinib CNS efficacy (post-hoc pooled analysis of two phase 2 studies, n=132 with CNS metastases).** In T790M-positive NSCLC with measurable CNS lesions, CNS ORR was 65% at 80 mg (n=34) and 85% at 160 mg (n=13). CNS PFS was 11.6 and 19.3 months respectively. The analysis is post-hoc with small dose groups, and the setting (second line after T790M) is now largely historical. Hu X, Zhang S, Ma Z et al. BMC Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37118803/
- **RELAY, TP53 exploratory analysis (phase 3).** Plasma TP53 mutation (42.7%) was a negative prognostic factor, especially in exon 8. Ramucirumab plus erlotinib improved PFS irrespective of TP53 status. The analysis is exploratory and uses a first-generation TKI backbone. Nishio M, Paz-Ares L, Reck M et al. Clin Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37076395/
- **TRAEMOS: T-DM1 plus osimertinib for HER2-overexpressing resistance (single-arm phase 1–2, n=27).** ORR at 12 weeks was 4% (1/27) and median PFS 2.8 months. This is a negative result for the strategy. Jebbink M, de Langen AJ, Monkhorst K et al. JTO Clin Res Rep 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37035409/
- **FDA Approval Summary: amivantamab for EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC.** This was an accelerated approval on 21 May 2021 for use after platinum, based on CHRYSALIS (ORR 40%, 95% CI 29–51; DoR 11.1 months), with Guardant360 CDx as companion diagnostic. It is a journal summary of an earlier decision. Chon K, Larkins E, Chatterjee S et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37022784/
- **GLORY: gumarontinib in METex14 NSCLC (single-arm phase 2, n=84, China and Japan).** ORR was 66% (95% CI 54–76): 71% in treatment-naive and 60% in pretreated patients. Oedema occurred in 80%, and grade ≥3 TEAEs in 54%. The trial is single-arm. Yu Y, Zhou J, Li X et al. EClinicalMedicine 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37096188/
- **CodeBreaK 100 2-year analysis (phase 1/2, n=174).** Sotorasib in pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC gave ORR 41%, DoR 12.3 months, PFS 6.3 months, OS 12.5 months and 2-year OS 33%. 23% had PFS ≥12 months, including some with STK11/KEAP1 alterations, which was associated with lower baseline ctDNA. The trial is single-arm, and CodeBreaK 200 later showed only a modest PFS gain over docetaxel. Dy GK, Govindan R, Velcheti V et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37098232/
- **KRYSTAL-1: adagrasib in other KRAS G12C solid tumours (phase 2 cohort, n=64).** ORR was 35.1% (pancreatic 33%, biliary 42%) and PFS 7.4 months. Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 27%. It is outside lung, but relevant to KRAS G12C drug development. Bekaii-Saab TS, Yaeger R, Spira AI et al. J Clin Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37099736/
- **BPI-9016M phase 1b (n=38).** This is a novel MET TKI tested in c-MET-overexpressing (n=34) and METex14 (n=4) NSCLC. TRAEs occurred in 92%. The study is small and mostly in the overexpression group. Hu X, Cui X, Wang Z et al. BMC Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37041472/
- **Adagrasib: first approval profile.** It covers the FDA accelerated approval on 12 December 2022 for KRAS G12C NSCLC. It is a drug profile, not a decision record. De SK. Curr Med Chem 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37005530/
- **Chinese consensus on BRAF-mutant advanced NSCLC (Chinese Anti-Cancer Association).** It covers diagnosis and treatment. The abstract gives no specific recommendations. Zhonghua Zhong Liu Za Zhi 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37078209/
- **Spanish SEAP/SEOM consensus on predictive biomarkers in NSCLC (update).** Testing for EGFR, BRAF, KRAS, MET, ALK, ROS1, NTRK, RET and PD-L1 is considered essential, NGS is recommended, and HER2 is named as an emerging target. It is a national consensus, not a guideline change with a stated new recommendation. Isla D, Lozano MD, Paz-Ares L et al. Rev Esp Patol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37061248/

### lung-ctdna-mrd / lung-screening

- **TRACERx ctDNA (Nature, 197 patients, 1,069 plasma samples).** Personalised tracking of about 200 mutations found ctDNA within 120 days after surgery in 25% of patients, including 49% of those who later relapsed. Serial surveillance identified relapse in another 20% of landmark-negative patients, and ctDNA-negative preoperative adenocarcinomas were indolent. It is observational and not interventional. Abbosh C, Frankell AM, Harrison T et al. Nature 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37055640/
- **DELFI cfDNA fragmentome in symptomatic patients (Copenhagen, n=296).** DELFI scores were higher with lung cancer (0.94 vs 0.19). Adding the score to age, smoking and symptoms raised AUC from 0.74 to 0.94. It is a proof-of-concept diagnostic study from a single Danish centre. Leal AIC, Mathios D, Jakubowski D et al. Chest 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37116747/
- **PM2.5 air pollution promotes EGFR-driven lung adenocarcinoma (Nature).** PM2.5 was associated with EGFR-mutant lung cancer incidence in 32,957 cases across four cohorts. Mouse models showed promotion via macrophage IL-1β. Oncogenic EGFR and KRAS mutations were found in normal lung tissue in 18% and 53% of 295 individuals. It is mechanistic and epidemiological, important for never-smoker aetiology. Hill W, Lim EL, Weeden CE et al. Nature 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37020004/
- **CanPredict (lung) risk model (Lancet Respir Med).** It was derived in 12.99 million people in QResearch, validated internally in 4.14 million and externally in 2.54 million (CPRD), and compared with seven models, including PLCOm2012 and LLP, for UK screening eligibility. The data are retrospective primary care records, and the model has no prospective screening outcomes yet. Liao W, Coupland CAC, Burchardt J et al. Lancet Respir Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37030308/

### lung-local-therapy

- **Sublobar resection in octogenarians with 2–4 cm NSCLC (prospective multicentre registry, 82 Japanese institutions, n=419).** 5-year OS was 54.7% after sublobar resection vs 66.8% after lobectomy (P = .09). Procedure was not independently prognostic (HR 0.8), and among patients fit for lobectomy OS was 67.5% vs 71.5%. Locoregional recurrence was 11% vs 7%. It is non-randomised with selection by fitness. Mimae T, Saji H, Nakamura H et al. Ann Thorac Surg 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37004802/

### lung-nordic / lung-repurposed

- **Antimuscarinics and lung cancer survival (Norwegian registry cohort, n=26,693).** It links the Cancer Registry of Norway and the Prescription Database, 2005–2018. Post-diagnostic use was associated with lower lung cancer-specific mortality (HR 0.84, 95% CI 0.77–0.92), only in chemotherapy-treated patients (HR 0.75), while peri-diagnostic use showed no association (HR 1.01). It is observational, post-diagnostic exposure is prone to immortal-time bias, and median follow-up was only 9 months. Sanjinez C, Botteri E, Støer NC et al. Lung Cancer 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37060880/
- **TNM-I trial sub-study: ML-based immune phenotypes (Ann Oncol, n=934, Norwegian prospective trial plus a retrospective cohort).** CD8-based phenotypes were 24% inflamed, 51% altered and 24% desert. KEAP1 and STK11 mutations were enriched in non-inflamed LUAD. The inflamed phenotype independently predicted longer disease-specific survival (HR 0.61) in the retrospective cohort. It is a biomarker study, not yet validated for treatment selection. Rakaee M, Andersen S, Giannikou K et al. Ann Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37100205/

### sclc / nsclc-immunotherapy

- **Real-world SCLC genomics (Cancer Discov, n=3,600).** It identifies STK11-mutant (1.7%) and TP53/RB1 wild-type (5.5%) subtypes. 4q12 amplification was associated with better OS and CCNE1 amplification with worse OS, and SCLC transformation was seen across several NSCLC driver groups. It is a genomic database study without treatment data. Sivakumar S, Moore JA, Montesion M et al. Cancer Discov 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37062002/
- **neoSCORE: two vs three cycles of neoadjuvant sintilimab plus chemotherapy.** It is a randomised trial in resectable NSCLC, published as a letter without abstract, so no results are given in this record. Shao M, Yao J, Wang Y et al. Signal Transduct Target Ther 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37032401/

### car-t-solid

- **GD2-CART01 in relapsed/refractory high-risk neuroblastoma (academic phase 1–2, n=27, NEJM).** These are third-generation GD2 CAR-T cells with an iCasp9 suicide switch. Overall response was 63% (9 CR, 8 PR). At the recommended dose, 3-year OS was 60% and EFS 36%. CRS occurred in 74% (mild in 95% of cases), the switch was activated once, and median persistence was 3 months (up to 30). It is single-arm with a small n. Del Bufalo F, De Angelis B, Caruana I et al. N Engl J Med 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37018492/
- **HBV-specific TCR-T in recurrent HCC after liver transplant (phase 1, n=6).** These are mRNA-electroporated, transiently functional TCR-T cells, HLA-restricted (A*02:01, A*11:01, B*58:01 or C*08:01) against HBsAg or HBcAg. Only grade 1 pyrexia occurred, with no CRS or neurotoxicity. There are very few patients, and efficacy data are limited. Yang F, Zheng X, Koh S et al. Hepatol Int 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37067675/

### thoracic-other

- **STYLE: sunitinib in pretreated B3 thymoma and thymic carcinoma (Simon two-stage phase 2, n=44).** The thymoma cohort closed after stage 1 (ORR 0%). Thymic carcinoma met its primary endpoint with ORR 21.7% (90% CI 9.0–40.4), PFS 8.8 months and OS 27.8 months. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 52% in thymic carcinoma. It is a small single-arm trial. Proto C, Manglaviti S, Lo Russo G et al. J Thorac Oncol 2023. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37094664/
